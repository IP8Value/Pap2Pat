The sequence listing includes nucleotide and amino acid sequences of SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins—spike (S), membrane (M), nucleocapsid (N), and envelope (E)—as well as regulatory elements, promoters, and expression cassettes used in the construction of recombinant attenuated poxvirus vectors. ## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to a composition for raising an immune response against coronavirus infection, particularly severe acute respiratory syndrome coronavirus 2 (SARS-CoV-2). More specifically, the invention provides genetically engineered attenuated poxvirus vectors—derived from vaccinia virus strains such as Modified Vaccinia Ankara (MVA) or Sementis Copenhagen Vaccinia (SCV)—that express one or more SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins, including the spike (S), membrane (M), nucleocapsid (N), and envelope (E) proteins. These compositions are designed to induce robust humoral and cellular immune responses, including neutralizing antibodies and T cell immunity, thereby preventing or mitigating SARS-CoV-2 infection and associated disease. The invention further encompasses methods of using these compositions as vaccines, either alone or in combination, and their application in medicament preparation for prophylactic or therapeutic purposes against coronavirus infections.

## BACKGROUND

The poxvirus family comprises large, double-stranded DNA viruses with complex replication cycles and broad host ranges. Within this family, the subfamily Chordopoxvirinae infects vertebrates and includes several genera of medical and veterinary importance, notably Orthopoxviridae and Avipoxviridae. Orthopoxviruses, such as vaccinia virus (VACV), variola virus (the causative agent of smallpox), and monkeypox virus, have been extensively studied due to their historical role in vaccination and emerging public health threats. Recombinant vaccinia-based viruses have long served as vaccine vectors owing to their capacity to accommodate large foreign DNA inserts, high immunogenicity, and ability to replicate in the cytoplasm without integrating into the host genome.

The poxvirus life cycle involves multiple stages of gene expression—early, intermediate, and late—regulated by distinct promoter types and host factors. A critical determinant of poxvirus host range is the presence of host range factors, which are viral proteins that counteract host antiviral defenses. Host range genes, such as K1L, C7L, and CP77, enable productive replication in specific cell types; their deletion often results in attenuation, rendering the virus replication-deficient in mammalian cells while permitting growth in complementing cell lines.

Historically, vaccinia virus strains like Dryvax and ACAM2000 were used as smallpox vaccines. Modified Vaccinia Ankara (MVA), developed through over 570 passages of VACV in chicken embryo fibroblasts (CEFs), is highly attenuated and replication-defective in human cells, making it a safe vector for recombinant vaccines. MVA has been evaluated in numerous clinical trials and is approved as a smallpox vaccine (IMVANEX/IMVAMUNE). However, MVA often requires heterologous prime-boost strategies to achieve strong immune responses in naïve individuals.

More recently, the Sementis Copenhagen Vaccinia (SCV) vector was developed from the Copenhagen strain of VACV. SCV contains a targeted deletion of the D13L gene, encoding the essential viral assembly protein D13, rendering it incapable of producing infectious progeny in standard mammalian cells. SCV is propagated in Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cells engineered to express D13 and the host range protein CP77, offering advantages over CEF-based systems, including scalability, regulatory familiarity, and serum-free production.

Coronaviruses are enveloped, positive-sense RNA viruses with genomes of approximately 30 kilobases. They possess four major structural proteins: the spike (S) glycoprotein, which mediates receptor binding and membrane fusion; the membrane (M) protein, which shapes the viral envelope and organizes virion assembly; the envelope (E) protein, involved in virion budding and pathogenesis; and the nucleocapsid (N) protein, which packages the viral RNA. Human coronaviruses include HCoV-229E, HCoV-OC43, HCoV-NL63, and HCoV-HKU1, which typically cause mild respiratory illness, as well as the more pathogenic SARS-CoV (2002–2003), MERS-CoV (2012–present), and SARS-CoV-2 (2019–present).

The SARS-CoV-2 pandemic, caused by a novel betacoronavirus, has resulted in millions of infections and deaths worldwide. SARS-CoV-2 transmits efficiently via respiratory droplets and aerosols, with a basic reproductive number (R₀) estimated between 2.5 and 3.5. The spike protein, particularly its receptor-binding domain (RBD) within the S1 subunit, binds to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2) on human cells, initiating infection. The M, E, and N proteins play critical roles in viral assembly, release, and immune evasion.

SARS-CoV-2 continues to evolve, with variants such as Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Delta, and Omicron exhibiting mutations that enhance transmissibility, immune escape, or virulence. Effective vaccines must therefore induce broad and durable immunity. Neutralizing antibodies targeting the spike protein, especially the RBD, can block viral entry. Additionally, T cell immunity—mediated by CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T cells—is crucial for viral clearance and long-term protection. Th1-polarized CD4⁺ T cells support B cell maturation and antibody class switching, while T follicular helper (Tfh) cells drive germinal center responses and high-affinity antibody production. A balanced Th1-biased humoral response is desirable, as Th2-skewed responses may contribute to vaccine-associated enhanced respiratory disease or antibody-dependent enhancement (ADE), though evidence for ADE in SARS-CoV-2 remains limited.

Vaccine development has focused on antigenic sequences that elicit protective immunity. The spike protein is the primary target due to its surface exposure and role in entry. However, the M, N, and E proteins also contain immunogenic T and B cell epitopes and may contribute to broader immune recognition. Compositions incorporating multiple SARS-CoV-2 antigens may enhance immunogenicity, promote cross-reactive responses against variants, and reduce the risk of immune escape.

## SUMMARY

The present invention provides a composition for the prevention of SARS-CoV-2 infection, comprising a genetically engineered attenuated poxvirus vector expressing one or more SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins. In a first aspect, the composition includes an attenuated poxvirus expressing the full-length SARS-CoV-2 spike protein. In a second aspect, the composition includes an attenuated poxvirus expressing the SARS-CoV-2 membrane protein. In a third aspect, the composition includes an attenuated poxvirus expressing the SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid protein. In a fourth aspect, the composition includes an attenuated poxvirus expressing the SARS-CoV-2 envelope protein.

In a fifth aspect, the composition comprises an attenuated poxvirus co-expressing spike and membrane proteins. In a sixth aspect, it co-expresses spike and nucleocapsid proteins. In a seventh aspect, it co-expresses spike and envelope proteins. In an eighth aspect, it co-expresses membrane and nucleocapsid proteins. In a ninth aspect, it co-expresses membrane and envelope proteins. In a tenth aspect, it co-expresses nucleocapsid and envelope proteins. In an eleventh aspect, it expresses all four structural proteins—S, M, N, and E—either from a single construct or multiple expression cassettes.

In a twelfth aspect, the composition comprises a mixture of two or more distinct attenuated poxvirus vectors, each expressing a different SARS-CoV-2 protein. In a thirteenth aspect, the composition includes virus-like particles (VLPs) assembled from SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins expressed by the poxvirus vector, mimicking native virion architecture.

In a fourteenth aspect, the invention provides a method of inducing an immune response in a subject, comprising administering an effective amount of the composition. In a fifteenth aspect, the method induces both neutralizing antibodies and T cell responses. In a sixteenth aspect, the method provides long-lasting immunity against SARS-CoV-2 and variants thereof.

In a seventeenth aspect, the composition resembles SARS-CoV-2 virus-like particles, enhancing antigen presentation and immunogenicity. In an eighteenth aspect, the composition is effective against other coronaviruses with high genetic similarity to SARS-CoV-2, such as SARS-CoV. In a nineteenth aspect, the composition is used in the preparation of a medicament for preventing coronavirus infection.

The genetically engineered attenuated vaccinia virus is preferably derived from the SCV or MVA strain. The composition may include a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier and is formulated for intramuscular, subcutaneous, or intradermal administration. The heterologous nucleic acid sequence encoding the SARS-CoV-2 polypeptide is inserted into the poxvirus genome at a site of deletion of one or more non-essential or immunomodulatory genes (e.g., A41L, C3L, A39R, B8R, or 87R-B8R locus) or into an intergenic region. The expression cassette comprises a poxvirus-compatible promoter (e.g., early/late synthetic promoter), the coding sequence, and a polyadenylation signal. Multiple expression cassettes may be used to express several antigens simultaneously.

Alternative embodiments include variations in insertion sites, gene deletions, promoter types, and antigen designs (e.g., stabilized prefusion spike, RBD-only, or truncated variants). The composition is designed to raise a robust, balanced immune response without inducing ADE or immunopathology, leveraging the safety and immunogenicity profile of attenuated poxvirus vectors.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

For the purposes of this invention, the term “comprise” is open-ended and includes but is not limited to the recited elements. “Consisting of” excludes any unrecited elements, while “consisting essentially of” permits only those elements that do not materially affect the basic and novel characteristics of the invention. Singular forms include plural referents unless context clearly indicates otherwise. The term “and/or” includes any and all combinations of the listed elements.

“Attenuation” refers to the reduction in virulence of a virus, achieved through genetic modification, such that it retains immunogenicity but is unable to cause significant disease. An “attenuated” poxvirus is replication-deficient or severely restricted in replication in human cells.

A “control element” or “control sequence” includes promoters, enhancers, polyadenylation signals, and other regulatory nucleic acid sequences that govern gene expression. “Corresponds to” or “corresponding to” means having sufficient sequence identity to perform a similar function or bind to the same target.

An “effective amount” is a quantity sufficient to induce a protective immune response. “Encode” or “encoding” refers to a nucleic acid sequence that directs the synthesis of a polypeptide. “Endogenous” denotes naturally occurring within the host or virus.

“Expressible” or “expressed” means that a gene is transcribed and, if applicable, translated into protein. A “gene” is a segment of DNA that encodes a functional product, including regulatory regions. A “heterologous nucleic acid sequence” is foreign to the poxvirus genome and encodes a non-poxvirus protein, such as a SARS-CoV-2 antigen. A “heterologous polypeptide” is the protein product thereof.

In a preferred embodiment, the heterologous DNA sequence encodes the SARS-CoV-2 spike, membrane, nucleocapsid, or envelope protein, or combinations thereof. A “protective immune response” includes neutralizing antibodies, CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T cell activation, and memory responses that prevent or reduce infection severity. A “neutralizing antibody response” specifically blocks viral entry or replication.

The poxvirus vector is propagated in mammalian cells, such as BC19A-12 or SD07-1 cell lines, which provide essential complementing functions. Homologous recombination is used to insert transgenes into the viral genome. “Operably connected” or “operably linked” means that a promoter is positioned to drive transcription of the downstream coding sequence.

An “open reading frame” (ORF) is a continuous stretch of codons that begins with a start codon and ends with a stop codon. “Polynucleotide” and “polypeptide” refer to chains of nucleotides or amino acids, respectively, regardless of length. “Recombinant” nucleic acids are artificially constructed or modified.

“Sequence identity” is determined by optimal alignment using algorithms such as BLAST or ClustalW, with identity calculated as the percentage of identical residues. A “signal sequence” or “signal peptide” directs protein secretion or membrane insertion. “Similarity” includes conservative amino acid substitutions.

A “subject,” “patient,” “host,” or “individual” is a mammal, preferably human, to whom the composition is administered. A “transgene” is a heterologous gene introduced into the vector. “Treatment” or “treating” includes prophylaxis and therapy.

“Wild-type,” “natural,” or “native” refers to the unmodified form of a gene or protein. “Viral infection” is the invasion and replication of a virus in host cells. “Virus-like particles” (VLPs) are non-infectious structures that mimic native virions but lack genomic material. “Variants” include natural isolates or engineered mutants with altered sequences.

Nucleic acid sequences with at least 80%, preferably 90% or 95% identity to the reference SARS-CoV-2 sequences are encompassed. Similarly, polypeptides with high amino acid identity are included. Sequences that “hybridize under stringent conditions” are considered equivalent.

A “deletion” is the removal of a segment of DNA. “Regulatory elements” include promoters, enhancers, and terminators. Chimeric constructs combine elements from different sources. Promoters may be constitutive, inducible, or viral-derived. Poxviral promoters include early, late, and early/late types. Compact synthetic promoters, such as those derived from the VACV p7.5 or p11 promoters, are preferred. Enhancer elements from SV40, RSV LTR, or human CMV may be used to boost expression. The CMV intron A sequence can enhance mRNA stability and translation.

The 3' non-translated sequence includes a polyadenylation signal, such as the VACV F17R or synthetic poly(A) signal. A selectable marker gene (e.g., GFP) may be included for screening but is preferably removed post-construction. Expression of poxvirus structural genes may be retained to support VLP formation.

SARS-CoV-2 has a ~30 kb RNA genome encoding 16 non-structural proteins and four structural proteins. Variants may carry mutations in spike (e.g., D614G, N501Y, E484K) that affect transmissibility or immune escape. The S1 subunit contains immunodominant T cell epitopes and the RBD.

The attenuated poxvirus is preferably derived from Orthopoxviridae, such as VACV Copenhagen or MVA. SCV is a preferred vector due to its safety and manufacturability. Deletions in genes such as D13L, K1L, A41L, C3L, and A39R enhance attenuation and immunogenicity. Heterologous DNA is inserted into intergenic regions or deletion sites (e.g., 87R-B8R locus). The SCV-SMX06 backbone, with deletions in A41L, D13L, and 87R-B8R, is exemplary.

Expression cassettes encode full-length or modified SARS-CoV-2 proteins, including prefusion-stabilized spike or RBD-only constructs. Co-expression of multiple antigens promotes VLP assembly and broad immunity. The composition is administered to induce humoral and cellular responses, providing protection against infection and disease.

### Construction of Vaccines

SCV-COVID19 vaccines were constructed by synthesizing expression cassettes for SARS-CoV-2 antigens, removing early transcription termination signals, and flanking them with control elements and endonuclease sites (e.g., NotI). These cassettes were cloned into homologous recombination (HR) plasmids containing viral genomic arms for targeted insertion. HR plasmids included a fluorescent selection marker (e.g., GFP) flanked by loxP sites for subsequent removal.

CHO cells were infected with SCV-SMX06 and transfected with NotI-digested HR plasmid. Fluorescent plaques were isolated, and recombinant virus was purified by limiting dilution. Cre recombinase-mediated excision removed the selection marker, yielding marker-free virus. Seed stocks were validated by PCR, sequencing, western blot, and immunostaining.

SCV-SMX06 is derived from the Copenhagen strain with deletions in A41L, D13L, and 87R-B8R. Transgenes were inserted into the 87R-B8R deletion site. Specific constructs included SCV-COVID19A (spike), SCV-COVID19B (membrane), SCV-COVID19C (nucleocapsid), SCV-COVID19D (envelope), SCV-COVID19E (spike + membrane), SCV-COVID19F (spike + nucleocapsid), SCV-COVID19G (spike + envelope), SCV-COVID19H (membrane + nucleocapsid), and SCV-COVID19I (all four proteins).

### Example 2

Mice were vaccinated with SCV-COVID19D or vector control. Serum samples were collected pre- and post-vaccination. S1-specific IgG was measured by ELISA, and neutralizing antibodies by pseudovirus or live-virus neutralization assays. SCV-COVID19D induced high titers of S1-specific IgG and neutralizing antibodies, with a Th1-biased subclass profile (IgG2a > IgG1), indicating a protective immune response.

### Example 3

T cell responses were assessed by ELISpot and intracellular cytokine staining. Splenocytes from vaccinated mice were stimulated with SARS-CoV-2 peptide pools. Robust IFN-γ, TNF-α, and IL-2 production was observed, confirming strong CD4⁺ and CD8⁺ T cell activation. Triple-cytokine-positive T cells indicated high-quality, polyfunctional responses.

### Conclusion

The results demonstrate that SCV-based SARS-CoV-2 vaccines induce potent humoral and cellular immunity, supporting their development as effective prophylactics.

### Example 4

Western blot and ELISA confirmed expression of SARS-CoV-2 proteins in infected cells. High levels of spike and nucleocapsid were detected, validating transgene expression and stability. ELISA showed antigen-specific antibody binding, confirming immunogenicity.

### Example 5

Inbred and outbred mice were vaccinated and tested for S1-specific IgG and neutralizing antibodies using cPass assay. Both groups mounted strong responses, with no significant difference, indicating robust immunogenicity across genetic backgrounds.

### Example 6

CD8⁺ and CD4⁺ T cell responses were analyzed by intracellular cytokine staining after peptide stimulation. Spike-specific triple-cytokine-positive CD8⁺ T cells, granzyme-B-producing CD8⁺ T cells, and IFN-γ-producing CD4⁺ T cells were detected, demonstrating comprehensive T cell immunity.

### Example 7

Pre-existing immunity to orthopoxviruses was evaluated by vaccinating mice with prior VACV exposure. SCV-COVID19 vaccines still induced strong antibody and T cell responses, indicating minimal interference. Prime-boost regimens enhanced immunity further. In aging mice, robust responses were maintained, supporting use in elderly populations. ELISpot and challenge studies confirmed prophylactic protection. Antigenic competition was not observed in multi-antigen formulations, and neutralization assays demonstrated functional protection. Cytolytic T lymphocyte activity was confirmed, underscoring the role of cellular immunity in viral clearance.
