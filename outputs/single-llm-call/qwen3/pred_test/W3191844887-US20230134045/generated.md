# DESCRIPTION

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

### 1. Field of the Invention

The present invention relates to a method and kit for evaluating prognosis in a patient diagnosed with breast cancer, particularly in those with luminal subtype tumors that are hormone receptor positive and human epidermal growth factor receptor type 2 negative. The invention provides a diagnostic framework grounded in the quantification of specific metabolic biomarkers—namely, pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2 and O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine—that correlate with long-term disease-free survival outcomes. This method enables clinicians to stratify patients according to their risk of recurrence following standard endocrine therapy, thereby guiding personalized treatment decisions such as the necessity for adjuvant chemotherapy or extended hormonal intervention. The invention further encompasses a diagnostic kit designed for routine clinical use, incorporating standardized reagents and protocols for immunohistochemical assessment of these biomarkers in formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue specimens. Unlike existing prognostic tools that rely primarily on tumor size, nodal status, or genomic signatures, the present invention introduces a metabolically informed approach that captures dynamic biochemical alterations intrinsic to tumor progression, offering enhanced predictive power independent of conventional clinicopathological parameters.

### 2. The Prior Art

Current methods for evaluating prognosis in breast cancer patients, particularly those with hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative disease, are limited by their inability to reliably predict late recurrences beyond five years post-diagnosis. Traditional tools such as the Nottingham Prognostic Index and the Clinical Treatment Score post-5 years integrate tumor size, lymph node involvement, and histological grade, yet they fail to account for underlying metabolic reprogramming that drives tumor aggressiveness and therapeutic resistance. While multigene expression assays like Oncotype DX, MammaPrint, and EndoPredict have improved risk stratification by analyzing proliferation-related gene signatures, these tests require specialized laboratory infrastructure, are cost-prohibitive in many healthcare settings, and do not directly reflect the functional metabolic state of the tumor. Furthermore, these genomic assays often yield intermediate-risk classifications that leave clinicians uncertain about treatment recommendations. Although some studies have implicated metabolic enzymes such as PKM2 in cancer progression, prior art has not established a clinically validated, reproducible method for assessing PKM2 expression in conjunction with O-GlcNAc modification as a combined prognostic indicator in luminal breast cancer. There remains a critical unmet need for an accessible, cost-effective, and biologically grounded diagnostic tool that can identify patients at high risk of recurrence despite favorable clinical features, thereby preventing both under-treatment and unnecessary chemotherapy exposure.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention introduces a novel method for evaluating prognosis in a patient with breast cancer by determining the expression levels of pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2 and O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine in a tissue sample obtained from the patient’s primary tumor. The method comprises the steps of obtaining a formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue specimen from the patient, performing immunohistochemical staining using specific antibodies directed against pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2 and O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine, quantifying the intensity and proportion of immunopositive cells to generate a Q score for each biomarker, and comparing the resulting Q scores to predefined thresholds to determine whether the patient exhibits high expression of either or both markers. The invention specifically identifies luminal breast cancer as the target subtype, with particular utility in patients whose tumors are hormone receptor positive and human epidermal growth factor receptor type 2 negative. The method further incorporates the Clinical Treatment Score post-5 years as a complementary clinical parameter to enhance prognostic accuracy. The invention also encompasses a diagnostic kit comprising a first antibody specific for pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2, a second antibody specific for O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine, a detection system compatible with standard immunohistochemistry protocols, and written instructions for performing the method and interpreting the results. The advantages of this invention include its compatibility with existing pathology workflows, its low cost relative to genomic assays, its ability to provide metabolic insight into tumor behavior, and its demonstrated capacity to identify high-risk patients who may benefit from intensified therapy despite favorable clinical characteristics, thereby improving clinical decision-making and patient outcomes.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PREFERRED EMBODIMENT

### Definition

For the purposes of this invention, data representation refers to the numerical quantification of biomarker expression derived from immunohistochemical staining, expressed as a Q score calculated by multiplying the percentage of immunopositive cells by the staining intensity score, where intensity is graded on a scale from zero to three. High expression levels of pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2 are defined as a Q score exceeding the mean Q score observed in the reference cohort of luminal breast cancer patients, while high expression levels of O-linked β-N-acetylglucosamine are similarly defined as a Q score above the cohort mean. The clinical treatment score post-5 years is a validated, web-based prognostic tool that integrates tumor size, histological grade, patient age, and number of positive lymph nodes to estimate the risk of distant recurrence five or more years after initiation of endocrine therapy, and is used in this invention as a continuous variable for multivariate analysis.

### High Expression of Pyruvate Kinase Isoenzyme M2 (PKM2) is Associated With Poor Clinical Outcome in Luminal Breast Cancer

The method of the invention is implemented following institutional review board approval and written informed consent from all patients whose tissue samples are utilized. Patient selection is restricted to those diagnosed with luminal breast cancer, defined by positive expression of estrogen receptor and/or progesterone receptor and absence of human epidermal growth factor receptor type 2 amplification. Tissue specimens are processed using standardized immunohistochemical protocols with a primary antibody specific for pyruvate kinase isoenzyme M2, followed by detection using a polymer-based system. Staining is evaluated by two blinded pathologists who record the percentage of positive tumor cells and the intensity of staining, which are then multiplied to yield a Q score. Survival analysis demonstrates that patients with high PKM2 expression exhibit significantly reduced disease-free survival compared to those with low expression, independent of tumor size or nodal status. This association is consistent across multiple validation cohorts and is most pronounced in luminal B subtype tumors, indicating that PKM2 overexpression reflects a biologically aggressive phenotype not fully captured by conventional clinicopathological parameters.

### High Expression of O-Linked β-N-Acetylglucosamine (O-GlcNAc) and PKM2 Can Predict Poor Prognosis of HR+/HER2- Breast Cancer

Immunohistochemical assessment is performed on serial tissue sections using validated antibodies against O-GlcNAc and PKM2, with antigen retrieval conducted via citrate buffer autoclaving. The Q score for each biomarker is calculated as described, and patients are stratified into low and high expression groups based on the mean Q score of the cohort. Kaplan-Meier survival curves reveal that high expression of either O-GlcNAc or PKM2 individually correlates with shorter disease-free survival, but the combination of both markers yields a statistically significant separation in survival outcomes, with patients exhibiting high levels of both biomarkers demonstrating the poorest prognosis. Subtype analysis confirms this association is strongest in luminal B tumors. The combined double-marker status is further validated through recurrence analysis, where high O-GlcNAc and PKM2 expression predicts early and late recurrence events with greater sensitivity than either marker alone. The resulting disease-free survival curve demonstrates a clear divergence between the double-high and double-low groups, with a hazard ratio exceeding two-fold. This finding establishes the synergistic prognostic value of these two metabolic markers in predicting tumor recurrence.

### Logistic Regression and Receiver Operating Characteristic (ROC) Curve Analysis

Multivariate logistic regression analysis is performed to evaluate the independent contribution of O-GlcNAc, PKM2, and clinical variables such as T stage, tumor size, and CTS5 to the prediction of disease-free survival. The analysis confirms that O-GlcNAc and PKM2 remain significant predictors even after adjustment for established clinical parameters. Receiver operating characteristic curve analysis is conducted to compare the diagnostic performance of individual and combined markers. The model incorporating O-GlcNAc, PKM2, and T stage achieves the highest area under the curve, surpassing models based on CTS5 alone or in combination with either biomarker. This indicates that the metabolic information provided by O-GlcNAc and PKM2 adds substantial predictive value beyond what is captured by anatomical staging or composite clinical scores. The optimal cutoff values for clinical application are determined through maximum likelihood estimation, and the resulting model is validated for reproducibility across independent patient cohorts. The invention thus provides a robust, statistically validated prognostic algorithm that integrates metabolic and clinical data to guide therapeutic decision-making.
