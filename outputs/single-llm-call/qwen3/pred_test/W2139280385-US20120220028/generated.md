# DESCRIPTION

## TECHNICAL FIELD

- relate to enhancer for promoter

The present invention relates to novel nucleic acid constructs and methods for enhancing the expression of foreign genes in eukaryotic cells, particularly in T lymphocytes and cells susceptible to herpesvirus infection. Specifically, the invention concerns the discovery and utilization of a highly active promoter derived from the major immediate-early region of human herpesvirus 6B, in combination with its naturally associated first intron, which together function as a potent transcriptional enhancer module. This enhancer-promoter system demonstrates superior activity compared to commonly used viral promoters such as the cytomegalovirus immediate-early promoter, particularly in immune cell types, and is suitable for use in gene therapy, vaccine development, and recombinant viral vector systems. The invention further encompasses engineered expression cassettes incorporating this enhancer-promoter unit, as well as vectors and pharmaceutical compositions designed for the targeted delivery and sustained expression of therapeutic or immunogenic polypeptides.

## BACKGROUND ART

- motivate gene therapy

Gene therapy has emerged as a transformative approach for the treatment of inherited disorders, infectious diseases, and malignancies by enabling the direct delivery and expression of therapeutic nucleic acids within target cells. The efficacy of such therapies is critically dependent on the ability to achieve robust, sustained, and cell-type-specific expression of the transgene. Viral promoters, particularly those derived from human cytomegalovirus (CMV), have been widely adopted due to their strong constitutive activity across a broad range of cell types. However, the utility of CMV-based promoters is limited in certain clinically relevant contexts, including T-cell-targeted interventions, where their activity is suboptimal or subject to silencing over time. This limitation has hindered the development of effective T-cell-based vaccines, adoptive cell therapies, and antiviral gene constructs that require high-level transgene expression in lymphoid compartments.

- limitations of prior art

Prior art systems relying on the CMV immediate-early promoter suffer from several critical shortcomings. First, the CMV promoter exhibits significantly reduced transcriptional activity in primary and immortalized T-cell lines compared to epithelial or fibroblast cell types, limiting its utility in immunotherapeutic applications. Second, prolonged expression from CMV-driven constructs is often compromised by epigenetic silencing mechanisms, including DNA methylation and histone deacetylation, which are particularly pronounced in lymphocytes. Third, the absence of intronic elements in many commercially available expression cassettes further diminishes mRNA stability and translational efficiency. While some studies have attempted to augment CMV promoter activity through the addition of synthetic enhancers or heterologous introns, these modifications have yielded inconsistent results and often fail to replicate the natural regulatory architecture of highly active viral promoters. There remains a critical unmet need for a promoter system that is both inherently strong in T cells and capable of sustaining high-level transgene expression without reliance on exogenous enhancer elements or complex genetic engineering.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

### Problems to be Solved by the Invention

- motivate enhancer for viral promoter

There exists a persistent challenge in the field of gene expression technology to identify and deploy viral promoter elements that are not only potent but also functionally optimized for specific cell lineages, particularly those of hematopoietic origin. The major immediate-early promoter of human herpesvirus 6B, despite its structural and functional homology to the CMV promoter, had not been systematically evaluated for its capacity to drive transgene expression in immune cells, nor had its potential to synergize with endogenous intronic sequences been explored. The lack of a promoter system that combines high activity in T cells with inherent resistance to silencing has impeded progress in developing next-generation gene therapies and viral vector platforms. The present invention addresses this gap by identifying a naturally occurring enhancer-promoter unit derived from HHV-6B that overcomes the limitations of prior art systems.

### Solutions to the Problems

- introduce intron for MIE gene

The invention provides a novel nucleic acid construct comprising the major immediate-early promoter of human herpesvirus 6B operatively linked to the first intron of the IE1 gene, wherein the intron is positioned immediately downstream of the transcriptional start site. This configuration results in a dramatic enhancement of promoter activity, particularly in T lymphocytes, surpassing the transcriptional output of the CMV promoter by up to tenfold in certain cell types.

- describe enhancer activity

The region of the HHV-6B MIE promoter spanning nucleotides -381 to -552 relative to the transcriptional start site contains a critical enhancer element that includes a functional NF-κB binding site. Deletion or mutation of this region results in a substantial reduction in promoter activity, confirming its essential role in transcriptional activation. The presence of the first intron further amplifies this activity, likely through the recruitment of transcriptional coactivators and stabilization of nascent mRNA transcripts.

- compare with CMV promoter

When compared directly to the cytomegalovirus immediate-early promoter in a panel of cell lines including Jurkat, SupT1, and Molt-3, the HHV-6B MIE promoter containing the first intron consistently demonstrated significantly higher luciferase reporter activity, with enhancements ranging from 2-fold to over 10-fold depending on cell type. In contrast, in non-lymphoid cells such as MRC-5 and MeWo, the activity levels were comparable, indicating that the enhanced activity is not universal but specifically advantageous in T-cell contexts.

- describe combination with 6MIE promoter

The invention further encompasses a modified promoter construct designated 6MIE, which corresponds to the full-length HHV-6B MIE promoter sequence including the first intron and the critical enhancer region. This 6MIE promoter is defined as a 1245-base-pair fragment extending from nucleotide -983 to +262 relative to the transcriptional start site, inclusive of the first intron. The 6MIE promoter exhibits superior performance in both transient and stable transfection assays and is compatible with integration into large viral genomes.

- summarize advantages

The 6MIE promoter system offers multiple advantages over existing technologies: it is naturally derived from a human herpesvirus, minimizing immunogenicity risks; it functions optimally in T cells without requiring synthetic enhancers; it resists silencing over extended culture periods; and it is compatible with recombinant viral vector systems such as varicella-zoster virus.

### Advantageous Effects of Invention

- summarize benefits

The invention delivers a transcriptional control element that significantly outperforms the CMV promoter in T-cell environments, enabling more efficient gene delivery for immunotherapies, T-cell engineering, and viral vector-based vaccines. The inclusion of the native first intron eliminates the need for artificial intronic sequences, simplifying construct design and reducing the risk of unintended splicing events. The system is scalable for use in both plasmid-based transfection and large-genome viral vectors, and its activity is maintained over time in culture, making it ideal for long-term expression applications. Furthermore, because the promoter is derived from a human virus, it is less likely to trigger innate immune responses compared to non-human viral promoters, enhancing its safety profile for clinical translation.
