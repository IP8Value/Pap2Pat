# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of biotechnology, specifically to methods and compositions for the diagnosis, prediction, and treatment of Alzheimer's disease (AD). More particularly, the invention provides biomarkers based on TRPC6 gene and protein expression levels, as well as therapeutic agents targeting TRPC6 activity to modulate amyloid-beta (Aβ) production. The invention further encompasses diagnostic kits and pharmaceutical compositions for AD management.  

## BACKGROUND  

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by cognitive decline, memory loss, and behavioral changes. The clinical manifestations of AD include the accumulation of amyloid plaques and neurofibrillary tangles in the brain, leading to synaptic dysfunction and neuronal death. The Amyloid Hypothesis posits that the accumulation of Aβ peptides, derived from the proteolytic cleavage of amyloid precursor protein (APP), is a central event in AD pathogenesis.  

Aβ generation occurs through sequential cleavage of APP by β-secretase and γ-secretase, producing various Aβ isoforms, including Aβ40 and Aβ42. The latter is particularly prone to aggregation and is considered a key contributor to AD pathology. Despite advances in understanding Aβ production, current diagnostic methods for AD remain limited, often detecting the disease only after significant neuronal damage has occurred. This delay restricts the effectiveness of therapeutic interventions.  

The need for early prediction and treatment of AD is critical, as existing therapies primarily address symptoms rather than underlying pathology. Clinical trials targeting γ-secretase, the enzyme responsible for the final cleavage step in Aβ production, have largely failed due to severe side effects. For example, γ-secretase inhibitors disrupt Notch signaling, leading to adverse effects such as gastrointestinal toxicity and impaired immune function. These limitations underscore the necessity for novel approaches that specifically target Aβ production without affecting other γ-secretase substrates.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a biomarker for the prediction or diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease based on TRPC6 gene and/or protein expression levels. Specifically, the invention involves detecting TRPC6 mRNA levels in peripheral blood cells, which correlate with AD risk. Reduced TRPC6 expression is associated with increased Aβ production, making it a valuable diagnostic indicator.  

The invention further provides a therapeutic agent for the treatment or prevention of AD, comprising TRPC6 protein or its derivatives. These agents modulate γ-secretase cleavage of APP, specifically reducing Aβ production without affecting other substrates such as Notch. The therapeutic approach includes administering polypeptides derived from TRPC6, particularly the transmembrane domain (TM2), which interacts with APP to inhibit Aβ generation.  

A diagnostic kit for AD prediction or diagnosis is also disclosed, comprising a nucleic acid chip or primers for detecting TRPC6 mRNA levels. The kit includes reagents for total RNA extraction, reverse transcriptase for cDNA synthesis, polymerase for PCR amplification, and instructions for comparing expression levels with normal controls. The detection method involves extracting mRNA from peripheral blood cells, quantifying TRPC6 mRNA levels via real-time PCR, and correlating these levels with AD risk.  

Pharmaceutical compositions containing TRPC6 agonists or active fragments are provided for therapeutic use. These compositions are formulated for administration via various routes, including oral, intravenous, or intranasal delivery, and are designed to enhance TRPC6 expression or activity, thereby reducing Aβ accumulation.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### TRPC6 mRNA in Peripheral Blood Cells  

TRPC6 mRNA levels in peripheral blood cells serve as a biomarker for AD risk. Studies demonstrate that reduced TRPC6 expression correlates with increased Aβ production and AD progression. The invention leverages this correlation by providing a non-invasive diagnostic method based on blood samples.  

### TRPC6 Protein Structure and Function  

TRPC6 is a non-selective cation channel protein with six transmembrane domains and cytoplasmic N- and C-terminals. The TM2 domain (residues 487–507) is critical for its interaction with APP and inhibition of Aβ production. TRPC6 functions independently of its channel activity, acting instead through protein-protein interactions to modulate γ-secretase cleavage of APP.  

### APOE Gene and AD  

The APOE gene, particularly the APOE4 allele, is a major genetic risk factor for sporadic AD. The invention explores the relationship between APOE genotypes and TRPC6 expression, revealing that APOE3 carriers exhibit higher TRPC6 mRNA levels, which may confer protective effects against Aβ accumulation.  

### Pharmaceutical Composition and Therapeutic Use  

The invention provides pharmaceutical compositions containing TRPC6 polypeptides or agonists. These compositions are formulated for targeted delivery to the brain, where they enhance TRPC6 activity and reduce Aβ production. Methods for preparing these compositions include conjugating TRPC6-derived peptides to cell-penetrating motifs, such as TAT, to facilitate blood-brain barrier penetration.  

### Medication Screening Method  

A screening method for identifying compounds that modulate TRPC6 expression or activity is disclosed. Candidates are tested for their ability to upregulate TRPC6 mRNA or protein levels and inhibit Aβ production in vitro and in vivo. High-throughput assays using HEK293APP stable cells or primary neurons are employed to evaluate compound efficacy.  

### Advantages of the Invention  

The present invention offers several advantages:  
1. **Early Diagnosis**: TRPC6 mRNA levels provide an objective biomarker for early AD detection, enabling intervention before significant neuronal damage occurs.  
2. **Specificity**: TRPC6-derived therapeutics selectively inhibit γ-secretase cleavage of APP, avoiding side effects associated with broad-spectrum γ-secretase inhibitors.  
3. **Non-Invasive Testing**: Peripheral blood-based diagnostics eliminate the need for invasive brain imaging or cerebrospinal fluid analysis.  

### General Methods  

The invention includes protocols for RNA extraction, reverse transcription, and real-time PCR to quantify TRPC6 mRNA. Protein extraction and Western blotting are used to assess TRPC6 protein levels. Immunohistochemistry and ELISA are employed for Aβ quantification in cell and animal models.  

### Animal Models  

APP/PS1 transgenic mice and TRPC6-overexpressing mice are used to validate the therapeutic effects of TRPC6 modulation. Behavioral tests, such as the Morris Water Maze, demonstrate improved cognitive function in treated animals.  

### TRPC6 Agonists and Activators  

Compounds that upregulate TRPC6 expression or mimic its activity are identified through screening assays. These include small molecules, peptides, and biologics that enhance TRPC6-APP interaction or stabilize TRPC6 protein.  

### Summary  

The invention provides a comprehensive approach to AD management, encompassing diagnostics, therapeutics, and screening methods. By targeting TRPC6, the invention addresses the unmet need for specific and early interventions in AD.  

## EXAMPLE 1  

Blood samples were collected from AD patients and age-matched controls. Total RNA was extracted from peripheral blood cells and reverse-transcribed into cDNA. Real-time fluorescence quantitative PCR revealed significantly lower TRPC6 mRNA levels in AD patients compared to controls. ROC curve analysis confirmed the diagnostic accuracy of TRPC6 mRNA levels, with no significant difference observed in TRPC5 mRNA levels. TRPC6 mRNA levels also correlated with Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE) scores, further validating their utility as a biomarker for early-stage AD.  

## EXAMPLE 2  

The relevance between TRPC6 mRNA levels and Aβ accumulation was assessed in HEK293APP cells and APP/PS1 mice. Reduced TRPC6 expression correlated with increased Aβ levels in both models, confirming TRPC6 as an ideal biomarker for early AD diagnosis.  

## EXAMPLE 3  

TRPC6 overexpression in HEK293APP stable cells and primary cortical neurons significantly reduced Aβ levels. Similarly, TRPC6 transgenic APP/PS1 mice exhibited lower Aβ accumulation and improved cognitive performance, demonstrating the therapeutic potential of TRPC6 modulation.  

## EXAMPLE 4  

TRPC6 specifically inhibited γ-secretase cleavage of APP (C99) without affecting Notch cleavage. In vitro assays using purified C99 and γ-secretase confirmed that TRPC6-derived peptides selectively block APP processing.  

## EXAMPLE 5  

Truncation analysis identified the 437–507 aa region of TRPC6 as crucial for reducing Aβ levels. Further refinement revealed the 487–507 aa (TM2 domain) as the core inhibitory sequence for γ-secretase cleavage of APP.  

## EXAMPLE 6  

The fusion peptide TAT-TM2, containing the TM2 domain, reduced Aβ levels in HEK293APP cells, primary neurons, and APP/PS1 mice. Intraperitoneal administration of TAT-TM2 significantly lowered brain Aβ levels, confirming its therapeutic efficacy.  

## EXAMPLE 7  

A high-throughput screen identified Compound C as a TRPC6 expression upregulator. Treatment with Compound C increased TRPC6 mRNA levels and reduced Aβ production in cellular models.  

## EXAMPLE 8  

APOE genotype analysis revealed higher TRPC6 mRNA levels in APOE3 carriers compared to APOE4 carriers. Cell experiments confirmed that APOE3 enhances TRPC6 expression in Jurkat cells and primary neurons, suggesting a protective mechanism against AD.  

## EXAMPLE 9  

TAT-TM2 specifically inhibited γ-secretase cleavage of APP (C99) without affecting Notch processing. In vitro and in vivo studies demonstrated the selectivity and efficacy of TAT-TM2 as a therapeutic agent for AD.  

This patent application provides a foundation for developing TRPC6-based diagnostics and therapeutics, addressing critical gaps in AD management.
