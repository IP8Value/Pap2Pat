# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to bone morphogenetic proteins (BMPs), particularly to designed BMP proteins with modified biological activities. More specifically, the invention provides modified BMP proteins exhibiting reduced susceptibility to inhibition by Noggin and Noggin-like proteins, along with nucleic acids encoding such modified proteins, methods for their production, and pharmaceutical compositions containing them.

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Bone morphogenetic proteins constitute a distinct subfamily within the transforming growth factor-beta (TGF-β) superfamily of proteins. These secreted signaling molecules play crucial roles in embryonic development and tissue homeostasis, particularly in bone and cartilage formation. BMPs signal through binding to specific cell surface receptors, initiating intracellular signaling cascades that regulate gene expression and cellular differentiation.

The BMP signaling pathway is tightly regulated by extracellular antagonists, including Noggin, which binds directly to BMPs and prevents their interaction with receptors. While this antagonistic regulation is essential for proper spatial and temporal control of BMP activity during development, it presents a significant limitation for therapeutic applications of BMPs. The natural feedback loop wherein BMPs induce Noggin expression creates a self-limiting system that reduces their therapeutic efficacy.

Current clinical use of recombinant BMPs requires administration of supraphysiological doses to overcome this inhibitory regulation, leading to increased costs and potential side effects. There exists a need for BMP variants that maintain biological activity while exhibiting reduced susceptibility to inhibition by endogenous antagonists like Noggin.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides designed bone morphogenetic proteins with modified amino acid sequences that confer reduced susceptibility to inhibition by Noggin. These modified BMP proteins maintain the ability to bind and activate BMP receptors while exhibiting decreased binding affinity for Noggin or altered binding kinetics that allow receptor displacement.

The invention encompasses modified versions of various BMP subfamily members including BMP-2, BMP-4, BMP-5, BMP-6, BMP-7, GDF-5, GDF-6, and GDF-7, each containing specific amino acid substitutions that reduce Noggin inhibition. Particularly, the invention provides modified BMP-7 proteins containing substitutions at positions corresponding to Lys60 of BMP-6, including single substitutions such as E60K, as well as multiple substitutions combining E60K with other modifications.

The invention further provides chimeric proteins combining elements of TGF-β superfamily proteins with wild-type BMP-6 or BMP-9 sequences, wherein the chimeras exhibit resistance to Noggin inhibition. Specific embodiments include an 80-1 chimera containing residues 45-80 of BMP-6 within a BMP-7 backbone.

Nucleic acid sequences encoding all described modified BMP and GDF proteins are provided, along with expression vectors containing these sequences. Methods for producing the modified proteins include culturing host cells transformed with the nucleic acids and purifying the expressed proteins.

Pharmaceutical compositions containing the modified BMP proteins are described, formulated for various administration routes including parenteral, oral, and local delivery. Methods for modulating inhibition of BMP activity by Noggin or Noggin-like proteins are provided, comprising administering the modified BMP proteins to a patient in need thereof.

The invention further provides methods for inducing bone or cartilage growth in patients by administering the modified BMP proteins, including treatments for bone fractures, osteoporosis, and cartilage injuries. Additional therapeutic applications include treatments for kidney, liver, lung, and neurological disorders where BMP signaling modulation is beneficial.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to modified bone morphogenetic proteins engineered to exhibit reduced susceptibility to inhibition by natural BMP antagonists, particularly Noggin. Through detailed structure-function analysis, key residues influencing Noggin binding have been identified and modified to create BMP variants with improved therapeutic potential.

### Bone Morphogenetic Proteins

Bone morphogenetic proteins belong to the TGF-β superfamily of cysteine knot cytokines. The BMP subfamily includes over 20 members in humans, with BMP-2 through BMP-15 representing the classical members. These proteins share a conserved structure comprising a signal peptide, prodomain, and mature domain containing the characteristic cysteine knot motif.

BMPs are synthesized as precursor proteins that undergo proteolytic processing to release the mature, biologically active dimer. The mature domain typically contains 6-7 conserved cysteine residues that form intramolecular disulfide bonds creating the cysteine knot structure, plus an additional cysteine involved in dimer formation.

BMP signal transduction occurs through binding to specific type I and type II serine/threonine kinase receptors at the cell surface. This binding initiates intracellular Smad-dependent and Smad-independent signaling pathways that regulate gene expression. BMP activity is modulated by interactions with various extracellular components including co-receptors, matrix proteins, and antagonist proteins.

### Interactions Between BMPs and Their Antagonists

Noggin represents a major class of BMP antagonists that bind directly to BMPs and prevent receptor interactions. Structural studies reveal that Noggin binds across both monomers of the BMP dimer, covering the receptor binding interfaces. Other important BMP antagonists include members of the DAN family (Gremlin, Cerberus) and Chordin/SOG proteins.

The present invention stems from the observation that different BMPs exhibit varying degrees of susceptibility to Noggin inhibition. While BMP-2, -4, and -7 show strong inhibition by Noggin, BMP-6 and -9 demonstrate marked resistance. Through comparative analysis, key structural differences between these BMPs were identified and exploited to engineer modified BMPs with reduced Noggin sensitivity.

Particular attention was given to the region surrounding position 60 (BMP-6 numbering) in finger 1 of the mature domain. In BMP-6, this position is occupied by lysine (K60), while BMP-7 has glutamic acid (E60). Substitution of E60K in BMP-7 conferred significant resistance to Noggin inhibition while maintaining receptor binding and signaling capability.

Additional modifications were made to create modified BMP-7 variants with multiple substitutions, including:
- Single substitution: E60K
- Double substitutions: R48Q/E60K, E60K/Y65N
- Triple substitution: R48Q/E60K/Y65N
- Quadruple substitution: R48Q/E60K/Y65N/Y78H

Similar modifications were applied to other BMP family members:
- BMP-2: P36K
- BMP-4: corresponding position substitutions
- BMP-5: corresponding position substitutions
- GDF-5: corresponding position substitutions
- GDF-6: corresponding position substitutions
- GDF-7: corresponding position substitutions

Chimeric proteins were also created by replacing segments of BMP-7 with corresponding regions from BMP-6. The 80-1 chimera, containing residues 45-80 of BMP-6 within BMP-7, showed Noggin resistance comparable to wild-type BMP-6 while maintaining BMP-7's expression characteristics.

### Generating Modified BMPs

The modified BMPs of the invention are defined as non-naturally occurring polypeptides having at least 80% sequence identity to a wild-type BMP protein, wherein the modifications reduce Noggin inhibition while maintaining receptor binding and signaling activity. Preferred embodiments maintain at least 90% sequence identity outside the modified regions.

Positional numbering follows the mature protein sequence after cleavage of the prodomain. For example, in mature BMP-7, E60 corresponds to residue 60 of the processed protein (residue 136 of the full-length precursor).

The modified BMPs may include additional modifications such as glycosylation variants, pegylation, or fusion proteins. The core invention resides in the specific amino acid substitutions that confer Noggin resistance while preserving biological activity.

### Preparing Nucleic Acids Encoding Modified BMPs

Nucleic acids encoding the modified BMP proteins were prepared using standard molecular biology techniques. Wild-type BMP cDNA sequences served as templates for site-directed mutagenesis to introduce specific amino acid changes. Chimeric constructs were created by overlap extension PCR, combining segments from different BMP genes.

### Expression Vectors

The nucleic acids were cloned into mammalian expression vectors containing:
- Strong promoters (CMV, EF1α)
- Selection markers (neomycin, hygromycin)
- Secretion signals
- Epitope tags (optional)
- Polyadenylation signals

Both transient and stable expression systems were developed, with the latter preferred for large-scale production.

### Transfection/Transformation

Expression vectors were introduced into host cells using:
- Lipid-based transfection reagents
- Electroporation
- Viral transduction
- Stable integration methods

### Hosts for Expression of Modified BMPs

Appropriate host cells include:
- Mammalian cells (HEK293, CHO, COS)
- Insect cells (Sf9, Sf21)
- Yeast (Pichia pastoris)
- Bacterial systems (for non-glycosylated versions)

### Expression Methods

Modified BMPs were produced by:
- Culturing transformed host cells in appropriate media
- Maintaining optimal culture conditions (temperature, CO2)
- Harvesting conditioned media containing secreted proteins
- Monitoring expression levels by ELISA or activity assays

### Purification

The modified BMP proteins were purified using:
- Ammonium sulfate precipitation
- Immobilized metal affinity chromatography (IMAC)
- Ion exchange chromatography
- Hydrophobic interaction chromatography
- Size exclusion chromatography
- HPLC final polishing

The purification process yielded proteins >95% pure as assessed by SDS-PAGE and mass spectrometry. Purified proteins were characterized for:
- Molecular weight
- Purity
- Biological activity
- Stability
- Endotoxin levels

## EXAMPLES

### BMP Induction of Alkaline Phosphatase Activity

A panel of BMPs was tested for ability to induce alkaline phosphatase (ALP) activity in ROS 17/2.8 osteosarcoma cells. BMP-6 showed highest potency (EC50 = 14 ng/ml), followed by BMP-7, BMP-4, and BMP-2. This differential activity profile suggested intrinsic differences in signaling capacity or regulation among BMP family members.

### Noggin Inhibition of a Panel of Exemplary BMPs and Related Proteins

BMPs were tested for susceptibility to Noggin inhibition using a BRE-luciferase reporter assay in A549-BRE cells. BMP-4 showed greatest inhibition (IC50 = 300 ng/ml Noggin), followed by BMP-2 (IC50 = 500 ng/ml), BMP-7 (IC50 = 800 ng/ml), and BMP-6 (IC50 > 5000 ng/ml). Similar results were obtained in primary human mesenchymal stem cells measuring endogenous gene expression.

### BMP-6 Induction of Downstream Genes in Primary Human Bone Marrow-Derived Mesenchymal Stem Cells is Less Susceptible to Noggin Inhibition

In hMSCs, BMP-6 induction of Id-1, Dlx-5, Msx-2, and Noggin itself was significantly less inhibited by Noggin compared to BMP-7. While Noggin reduced BMP-7-induced gene expression by 70-90%, it only marginally affected BMP-6 responses (10-30% inhibition).

### BMP-7 Variant Proteins with Marked Resistance to Noggin Inhibition

Based on sequence comparisons between BMP-6 and BMP-7, several BMP-7 variants were created:
1. Chimeric proteins replacing segments of BMP-7 with BMP-6 sequences:
   - 40-1 chimera (residues 1-40 from BMP-6)
   - 80-1 chimera (residues 45-80 from BMP-6)
   - 90-1 chimera (residues 90-120 from BMP-6)

The 80-1 chimera showed Noggin resistance similar to wild-type BMP-6 while maintaining good expression levels.

2. Point mutants at position corresponding to BMP-6 K60:
   - E60K single mutant showed significantly reduced Noggin inhibition
   - Additional mutants combining E60K with other substitutions (R48Q, Y65N, Y78H) showed incremental improvements

### Example 5

Detailed analysis of position 60 variants:
- BMP-7 E60K mutant showed 3-fold reduction in Noggin sensitivity
- BMP-2 P36K mutant showed 2-fold reduction
- Multiple substitutions in BMP-7 (E60K/Y65N/Y78H) showed 5-fold reduction
- All mutants maintained receptor binding and signaling activity

In vivo testing in rabbit ulna defect model:
- BMP-7 E60K induced more robust bone formation than wild-type BMP-7 at equal doses
- Bone volume/tissue volume ratios were 1.8-fold higher for E60K
- Mechanical strength of regenerated bone was significantly improved

These examples demonstrate that strategic modification of BMP sequences can yield proteins with reduced Noggin sensitivity while maintaining or improving therapeutic potential. The specific modifications described herein provide a foundation for developing improved BMP-based therapeutics.
