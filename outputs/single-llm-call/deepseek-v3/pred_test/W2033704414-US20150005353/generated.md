# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to novel inhibitors of sphingosine kinase-2 (SphK2), particularly 5-alkylidenethiazolidine-2,4-dione and 3-alkylidene-1,3-dihydroindol-2-one derivatives, and their use in treating diseases associated with SphK2 activity, such as cancer. Sphingosine-1-phosphate (S1P), a bioactive lipid metabolite, plays a critical role in cellular processes including proliferation, migration, and apoptosis. S1P is synthesized from sphingosine by two isoenzymes, SphK1 and SphK2, which exhibit distinct subcellular localizations and functional roles.  

SphK1 is predominantly cytosolic and is associated with promoting cell survival and tumor progression, whereas SphK2 localizes to the nucleus, endoplasmic reticulum, and mitochondria, with emerging roles in histone acetylation and mitochondrial function. Despite structural homology, SphK2 has been implicated in both pro-apoptotic and pro-survival mechanisms, depending on cellular context. The development of selective SphK2 inhibitors is essential to elucidate its biological functions and therapeutic potential.  

Existing SphK inhibitors, such as ABC294640 and FTY720 derivatives, exhibit limited potency or selectivity. Non-selective inhibitors like dimethylsphingosine (DMS) affect both SphK isoforms, complicating mechanistic studies. Thus, there remains an unmet need for potent, selective SphK2 inhibitors with adaptable chemical scaffolds for therapeutic and research applications.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides compounds of Formula I and Formula II, which selectively inhibit SphK2. Formula I comprises a 5-alkylidenethiazolidine-2,4-dione core, wherein R1 is an alkoxyl substituent with 4, 7, or 8 carbon atoms, and R2–R5, V, X, Y, Z, and W are defined substituents. Formula II derivatives include 3-alkylidene-1,3-dihydroindol-2-one scaffolds, with R10–R18 and X as specified.  

Key embodiments include:  
1. **K145 (Compound 7)**: 3-(2-Aminoethyl)-5-[3-(4-butoxyphenyl)propylidene]thiazolidine-2,4-dione, exhibiting an IC50 of 4.30 µM against SphK2 and no activity against SphK1 at 10 µM.  
2. **KLIII016 (Compound 31)** and **KLII157 (Compound 32)**: Additional Formula I variants with demonstrated SphK2 inhibition.  

The compounds are useful for:  
- Treating cancers (e.g., leukemia, breast adenocarcinoma) by inhibiting SphK2-mediated S1P production.  
- Reducing phosphorylation of ERK and Akt, downstream effectors of SphK2 signaling.  
- Oral or intraperitoneal administration, with demonstrated in vivo efficacy in xenograft models.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### Compounds of Formula I  
- **R1**: Alkoxyl groups (e.g., butoxy, heptyloxy) critical for SphK2 binding.  
- **R2–R5**: Hydrogen, halogen, or alkyl substituents modulating solubility and potency.  
- **V, X, Y, Z**: Structural moieties enhancing competitive binding to the sphingosine pocket.  
- **W**: Preferably NH2 for hydrogen bonding with Asp344 in SphK2.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions  
Formulations may include:  
- **Additives**: Acidifying agents (e.g., citric acid), alkalinizing agents (e.g., sodium bicarbonate), and emulsifiers (e.g., polysorbate 80).  
- **Dosage**: 15–50 mg/kg/day, with oral bioavailability confirmed in murine models.  

### Methods of Use  
1. **Cancer Treatment**: Administering Formula I compounds (e.g., K145) to reduce tumor growth in U937 leukemia or JC mammary adenocarcinoma xenografts.  
2. **SphK2 Inhibition**: Measuring S1P reduction via ESI-MS/MS or FTY720 phosphorylation assays.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Synthesis of Compound 26  
4-Butoxybenzaldehyde was condensed with Meldrum’s acid, followed by reduction to yield 3-(4-butoxyphenyl)propionaldehyde (Intermediate 10). Condensation with Boc-protected thiazolidine-2,4-diamine and deprotection afforded K145 (72% yield, purity >95% by HPLC).  

### Example 2: In Vitro Assays  
K145 inhibited SphK2 (IC50 = 4.30 µM) without affecting SphK1 or ceramide kinase. Lineweaver-Burk analysis confirmed competitive inhibition with sphingosine (Ki = 6.4 µM).  

### Example 3: In Vivo Efficacy  
- **U937 Xenografts**: 15 mg/kg i.p. K145 reduced tumor weight by 44.2% (vs. vehicle) over 17 days.  
- **Oral Administration**: 50 mg/kg K145 achieved 51.25% tumor growth inhibition.  

### Example 4: NMR Characterization  
KLIII016 (31): 1H NMR (DMSO-d6) δ 7.15 (d, J = 8.5 Hz, 2H), 3.92 (t, J = 6.4 Hz, 2H), 1.69 (m, 2H).
