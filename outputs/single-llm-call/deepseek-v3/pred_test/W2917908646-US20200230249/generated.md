# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of peptidomimetic foldamers, specifically peptide-amino urea hybrid compounds that mimic the structure and function of native peptides while exhibiting improved pharmaceutical properties. These hybrid compounds combine segments of natural amino acid peptides with synthetic oligourea residues, creating novel molecular architectures with enhanced metabolic stability, prolonged half-life, and retained biological activity. The invention particularly relates to glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) oligourea hybrid compounds useful for treating metabolic disorders including diabetes mellitus, obesity, and related conditions.  

## BACKGROUND  

Protein-protein interactions represent fundamental biological processes that regulate cellular functions and pathways. Therapeutic modulation of these interactions holds significant potential for treating various diseases. Peptides have emerged as valuable tools for targeting protein-protein interactions due to their ability to bind with high specificity and affinity to medium and large active sites. However, natural peptides suffer from several limitations that restrict their therapeutic utility, including poor membrane permeability, rapid enzymatic degradation, short in vivo half-life, and low bioavailability.  

Foldamers have attracted considerable interest as potential solutions to overcome the limitations of natural peptides. Oligoureas represent a particularly promising class of foldamers that combine three-dimensional structural similarity to peptides with enhanced metabolic stability. These synthetic oligomers maintain water solubility and functional flexibility while adopting defined secondary, tertiary, and quaternary structures based on their sequences. Importantly, the oligourea backbone demonstrates remarkable resistance to proteolytic degradation while maintaining compatibility with peptide α-helix structures.  

The invention addresses the unmet need for peptidomimetic compounds that preserve the biological activity of native peptides while overcoming their pharmacological limitations. By strategically replacing segments of natural peptides with oligourea residues, the hybrid compounds of the invention achieve superior pharmacokinetic profiles without compromising target engagement. This approach proves particularly valuable for peptide hormones like glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1), which suffers from extremely short half-life in vivo due to rapid degradation by dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) and neutral endopeptidase 24.11 (NEP 24.11).  

## SUMMARY  

The invention provides novel peptide-amino urea hybrid foldamer compounds that combine segments of natural peptides with synthetic oligourea residues. These hybrid compounds maintain the biological activity of the parental peptides while exhibiting improved pharmaceutical properties including enhanced proteolytic resistance and prolonged in vivo half-life.  

The hybrid compounds feature a structure where four consecutive amino acid residues in a native peptide sequence are replaced by three oligourea residues (XuXuXu), creating a peptide-oligourea chimera. This substitution maintains the overall helical structure and side chain presentation of the native peptide while conferring protease resistance to the oligourea segment. The invention particularly encompasses glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) oligourea hybrids where various tetrapeptide segments are replaced by oligourea triads.
