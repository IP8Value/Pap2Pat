# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to engineered polypeptides comprising variant Fc regions with reduced effector function while retaining desirable biophysical and pharmacological properties. More specifically, the invention provides polypeptides, including antibodies and antigen-binding fragments thereof, that incorporate specific amino acid substitutions in the Fc region to substantially eliminate binding to Fc gamma receptors (FcγRs) and complement component C1q while maintaining binding to neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) and Protein A (ProA). The engineered polypeptides exhibit improved developability characteristics, including resistance to acid-induced aggregation and thermal stability comparable to wild-type IgG1.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Biologics, particularly therapeutic monoclonal antibodies, have become indispensable tools in modern medicine for treating various diseases, including cancer, autoimmune disorders, and infectious diseases. Antibodies offer several advantages, including high specificity and affinity for their targets, long serum half-life, and the ability to engage immune effector functions. However, these effector functions, while beneficial in some therapeutic contexts, can lead to unintended adverse effects such as inflammation, tissue damage, and cytotoxicity.  

The Fc region of an antibody mediates interactions with Fc receptors (FcRs) and complement proteins, which are critical for effector functions such as antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC), antibody-dependent cell-mediated phagocytosis (ADCP), and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC). The Fc gamma receptors (FcγRs) include activating receptors (FcγRI, FcγRIIa, FcγRIIc, and FcγRIIIa) that bind to the hinge and upper CH2 domains of the Fc region. These interactions trigger immune responses that can be undesirable in certain therapeutic applications, particularly for T cell-engaging bispecific antibodies.  

The neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) plays a crucial role in antibody recycling, contributing to the extended serum half-life of IgG antibodies. FcRn binds to the Fc region in a pH-dependent manner, with stronger binding under acidic conditions (pH < 6.5) in the endosome. Additionally, the complement system is activated through the binding of C1q to the Fc region, initiating the classical complement pathway and leading to CDC.  

Efforts to modulate effector function have focused on modifying the Fc region to reduce or eliminate binding to FcγRs and C1q while preserving FcRn and ProA binding. Known modifications include substitutions in the lower hinge region (e.g., L234A/L235A, termed "LALA") and CH2 domain (e.g., P329G). However, some existing Fc variants exhibit undesirable biophysical properties, such as increased sensitivity to acid-induced aggregation or reduced thermal stability.  

There remains a need for Fc variants that combine complete elimination of effector function with wild-type-like developability characteristics, including stability, solubility, and resistance to aggregation. The present invention addresses this need by providing novel Fc variants, including L234A/L235A/P329A (LALA-PA) and L234A/L235E/P329G (LALE-PG), which exhibit superior properties compared to existing variants.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides polypeptides comprising variant Fc regions with reduced effector function, characterized by specific amino acid substitutions that substantially eliminate binding to FcγRs and C1q while retaining binding to FcRn and ProA. The polypeptides include antibodies, antigen-binding fragments, and Fc fusion proteins.  

Key embodiments of the invention include polypeptides with Fc variants having reduced affinity for FcγRs and C1q. In particular, the LALA-PA variant (L234A, L235A, P329A) and the LALE-PG variant (L234A, L235E, P329G) demonstrate complete abrogation of effector function under assay conditions while maintaining desirable biophysical properties.  

The LALA-PA polypeptide exhibits properties including negligible binding to FcγRI, FcγRIIa, FcγRIIc, and FcγRIIIa, as well as C1q, while retaining high-affinity binding to FcRn and ProA. The LALE-PG polypeptide similarly eliminates effector function but incorporates a glutamate substitution at position 235, which may further reduce residual binding to FcγRs under highly avid conditions.  

Optional features of the polypeptides include additional modifications to enhance stability, solubility, or pharmacokinetic properties. The polypeptides may be formulated into pharmaceutical compositions for therapeutic use, optionally in combination with other active agents.  

Methods of producing the polypeptides include recombinant expression in host cells, such as mammalian cells (e.g., HEK or CHO cells), followed by purification using standard techniques, including Protein A affinity chromatography. The polypeptides may be produced as full-length antibodies, antigen-binding fragments (e.g., Fab, scFv), or Fc fusion proteins.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

As used herein, the term "about" refers to a numerical value that may vary by ±10% without altering the function or properties of the described embodiment. The EU numbering system is used to designate amino acid residues in the Fc region, as described in Edelman et al., Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA 63, 78-85 (1969).  

The terms "comprising," "consisting of," and "consisting essentially of" are used in their standard patent law meanings. "Comprising" permits the inclusion of additional elements, while "consisting of" excludes any unspecified elements. "Consisting essentially of" allows for minor modifications that do not materially affect the invention's properties.  

The variant Fc domain of the invention provides advantages over wild-type Fc, including reduced effector function while maintaining FcRn and ProA binding. A "variant Fc" refers to an Fc region with one or more amino acid substitutions, deletions, or insertions relative to a parent Fc. The Fc region comprises the CH2 and CH3 domains, including the lower hinge region, which is critical for FcγR binding.  

The "lower hinge" of the Fc region spans residues 233-238 (EU numbering) and is a primary binding site for FcγRs. A "parent Fc" is the unmodified Fc region from which variants are derived, typically from human IgG1, IgG2, or IgG4.  

Fc variants with decreased effector function are defined by their reduced binding to FcγRs and C1q. "Effector function" encompasses ADCC, ADCP, and CDC, mediated by FcγRs and complement proteins. The Fc gamma receptor (FcγR) family includes activating receptors (FcγRI, FcγRIIa, FcγRIIc, FcγRIIIa) and inhibitory receptors (FcγRIIb).  

FcRn, the neonatal Fc receptor, is a heterodimeric protein that binds IgG at acidic pH to prolong serum half-life. Fc variants with desirable therapeutic properties retain FcRn binding while eliminating interactions with activating FcγRs and C1q.  

Antibody-dependent cell-mediated cytotoxicity (ADCC) is a mechanism by which antibodies recruit immune cells to kill target cells. ADCC is mediated by FcγRIIIa (CD16a) on natural killer (NK) cells. Antibody-dependent cell-mediated phagocytosis (ADCP) involves the engulfment of antibody-coated targets by macrophages via FcγRs. Complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) is initiated by C1q binding to the Fc region, leading to target cell lysis.  

In vitro and in vivo cytotoxicity assays are used to assess effector function. Fc receptor binding assays, such as surface plasmon resonance (SPR) and flow cytometry, measure affinity for FcγRs and FcRn. ADCC activity is assessed using NK cell-mediated killing assays, while CDC activity is measured using complement activation assays.  

The L234A/L235A/P329A (LALA-PA) and L234A/L235E/P329G (LALE-PG) variants are characterized by substitutions in the CH2 domain of the human IgG1 Fc region. Testing for reduced FcγR binding and developability includes biophysical assays such as hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC), size-exclusion chromatography (SEC), and thermal stability measurements.  

An "Fc fusion protein" comprises an Fc region linked to a heterologous protein or peptide. An "antibody" is a protein comprising at least one antigen-binding domain, including full-length immunoglobulins and antigen-binding fragments (e.g., Fab, scFv). A "monoclonal antibody" is derived from a single clone and binds a specific epitope.  

Antigen-binding fragments include Fab, F(ab')2, scFv, and single-domain antibodies. A "multispecific antibody" binds two or more distinct epitopes, while a "bispecific antibody" binds two different epitopes. An "epitope" is the specific region of an antigen recognized by an antibody.  

Antibody structure comprises heavy and light chains, each with variable (VH, VL) and constant (CH, CL) regions. The variable regions include complementarity-determining regions (CDRs) and framework regions (FRs). Antibody classes (IgG1, IgG2, IgG3, IgG4) differ in effector function and stability.  

"Developability" refers to the suitability of an antibody for manufacturing and therapeutic use. Desirable developability characteristics include high solubility, stability, and low aggregation propensity. Undesirable characteristics include polyspecificity, self-interaction, and acid-induced aggregation.  

Methods to ascertain developability include polyspecificity reagent (PSR) binding assays, affinity-capture self-interaction nanoparticle spectroscopy (AC-SINS), and dynamic scanning fluorimetry (DSF). Polyspecific antibodies exhibit off-target binding, while improved expressability refers to high yields during recombinant production.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Antibodies  

Anti-CD3 antibody variants were generated by introducing LALA-PA and LALE-PG substitutions into the Fc region of the IgG1 antibody ADI-26906. Variants were expressed in HEK cells and purified by Protein A affinity chromatography. Binding to CD3 was confirmed by biolayer interferometry (BLI), demonstrating retained antigen-binding affinity.  

### Example 2: THP-1 Cell Binding and C1q Binding to Antibodies  

Antibody variants were tested for binding to THP-1 cells (expressing FcγRI and FcγRII) under standard and highly avid conditions. Precomplexing with CD3-BSA enhanced detection sensitivity. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants showed no detectable binding to THP-1 cells, in contrast to wild-type IgG1.  

C1q binding was assessed using a FACS-based assay with biotinylated CD3 peptide-loaded Dyna Beads. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants exhibited no C1q binding, while wild-type IgG1 showed strong binding.  

### Example 3: FcγR Binding by Antibodies  

Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) was used to measure FcγR binding kinetics. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants showed no detectable binding to FcγRI, FcγRIIa, or FcγRIIIa, while retaining FcRn binding comparable to wild-type IgG1.  

### Example 4: Developability Assays  

Polyspecificity reagent (PSR) binding was assessed using CHO cell-derived membrane proteins. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants exhibited low PSR binding scores, comparable to wild-type IgG1.  

Hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC) retention times were unchanged, indicating no increase in hydrophobicity. AC-SINS analysis showed minimal self-interaction propensity.  

Thermal stability was measured by differential scanning fluorimetry (DSF). LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants exhibited CH2 melting temperatures (Tm) within 2°C of wild-type IgG1. pH stress assays demonstrated resistance to acid-induced aggregation, with <3% monomer loss after low pH exposure.  

### Example 5: ADCC Activity Assay  

ADCC activity was assessed using NK cell-mediated killing of target cells. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants showed no ADCC activity, while wild-type IgG1 induced significant target cell lysis.  

### Example 6: Complement Activity Assay  

CDC activity was measured using complement-mediated lysis of target cells. LALA-PA and LALE-PG variants exhibited no CDC activity, whereas wild-type IgG1 triggered robust complement activation.  

The invention thus provides Fc variants with fully silenced effector function and superior developability, enabling safer and more effective therapeutic antibodies.
