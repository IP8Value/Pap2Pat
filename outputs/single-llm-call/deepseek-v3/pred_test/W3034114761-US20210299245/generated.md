## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to immunogenic compositions for raising an immune response against coronavirus infection in a subject. More particularly, the invention provides recombinant attenuated poxvirus vectors encoding one or more SARS-CoV-2 antigens, including spike protein, membrane protein, nucleocapsid protein, and envelope protein, for use in vaccine formulations. The compositions are capable of inducing both humoral and cellular immune responses against SARS-CoV-2 and related coronaviruses.

## BACKGROUND

The Poxviridae family comprises large, complex DNA viruses that replicate in the cytoplasm of infected cells. Within this family, the Chordopoxvirinae subfamily includes viruses that infect vertebrates, with the Orthopoxviridae and Avipoxviridae genera being of particular interest for vaccine development. Poxviruses have been extensively studied as vaccine vectors due to their ability to accommodate large inserts of heterologous DNA and their capacity to induce robust immune responses.

Recombinant vaccinia-based viruses have demonstrated utility as vaccine vectors, with several poxvirus vectors currently in clinical use or development. The poxvirus life cycle involves early gene expression for immune evasion and DNA replication, followed by intermediate and late gene expression for virion assembly. Host range factors determine the species specificity of different poxvirus strains, with host range genes playing critical roles in viral replication in specific cell types.

Historically, various vaccinia strains have been used as smallpox vaccines, including the New York City Board of Health (NYCBH) strain, the Lister strain, and the Copenhagen strain. Modified Vaccinia Ankara (MVA), derived from the Ankara strain through extensive passage in chicken embryo fibroblasts, represents an attenuated vaccinia strain that has been widely studied as a vaccine vector. MVA was developed as a safer smallpox vaccine and has been shown to be replication-deficient in mammalian cells while maintaining immunogenicity. The MVA vector has been used to express heterologous molecules from various pathogens, demonstrating its versatility as a vaccine platform.

The Sementis Copenhagen Vector (SCV) represents an improved vaccinia virus vector system based on the Copenhagen strain. SCV offers several advantages over traditional poxvirus vectors, including enhanced safety through targeted gene deletions and improved manufacturing in Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cell lines rather than primary chicken embryo fibroblasts. The SCV system has demonstrated efficacy in expressing multiple heterologous antigens and inducing protective immune responses.

Coronaviruses are enveloped, positive-sense single-stranded RNA viruses belonging to the Coronaviridae family. The coronavirus structure comprises four major structural proteins: spike (S), membrane (M), envelope (E), and nucleocapsid (N). The coronavirus life cycle begins with viral attachment via the spike protein, followed by membrane fusion and release of the viral genome into the host cell cytoplasm. Viral replication produces new genomic RNA and subgenomic mRNAs that encode structural and accessory proteins.

Human coronaviruses include several pathogens of clinical significance. Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus (SARS-CoV) emerged in 2002, followed by Middle East Respiratory Syndrome Coronavirus (MERS-CoV) in 2012. The COVID-19 pandemic, caused by SARS-CoV-2, has highlighted the urgent need for effective coronavirus vaccines. SARS-CoV-2 demonstrates efficient human-to-human transmission with a reproductive number (R0) estimated between 2-3 in the absence of interventions.

The SARS-CoV-2 spike protein mediates viral entry through binding to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2) receptors. The spike protein comprises two functional subunits: S1, containing the receptor-binding domain, and S2, mediating membrane fusion. The membrane protein is the most abundant structural component and plays key roles in virion assembly and budding. The envelope protein contributes to viral pathogenesis through ion channel activity, while the nucleocapsid protein packages the viral RNA genome.

SARS-CoV-2 has demonstrated significant evolutionary capacity, with multiple mutations leading to variants of concern. These variants may exhibit altered transmission characteristics, disease severity, or immune evasion. Neutralizing antibodies targeting the spike protein represent a critical component of protective immunity, while T cell responses, including both CD4 and CD8 T cells, contribute to viral clearance. Th1 polarized CD4 T cell responses are particularly important for coordinating effective immunity, including support for T follicular helper cells and germinal center responses.

Vaccine development against SARS-CoV-2 has focused primarily on the spike protein due to its surface exposure and role in viral entry. However, inclusion of additional structural proteins (membrane, envelope, and nucleocapsid) may provide broader immune coverage and address potential immune escape variants. The immunogenicity of these proteins varies, with the nucleocapsid protein being particularly immunogenic for T cell responses. Careful vaccine design must consider potential antibody-dependent enhancement of disease, where suboptimal antibody responses might exacerbate infection.

## SUMMARY

The present invention provides compositions and methods for preventing SARS-CoV-2 infection through induction of protective immune responses. In a first aspect, the invention provides a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein. A second aspect provides a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 membrane protein. A third aspect provides a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid protein. A fourth aspect provides a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 envelope protein.

Further aspects of the invention include combinations of these vectors, including: a fifth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding both the SARS-CoV-2 spike and membrane proteins; a sixth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike and nucleocapsid proteins; a seventh aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike and envelope proteins; an eighth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 membrane and nucleocapsid proteins; a ninth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 membrane and envelope proteins; a tenth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid and envelope proteins; an eleventh aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike, membrane, and nucleocapsid proteins; a twelfth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike, membrane, and envelope proteins; and a thirteenth aspect providing a composition comprising an attenuated poxvirus vector encoding all four SARS-CoV-2 structural proteins.

The invention further provides methods of inducing immune responses, including: a fourteenth aspect providing a method of inducing an immune response against SARS-CoV-2 comprising administering to a subject the composition of any preceding aspect; a fifteenth aspect providing a method of inducing both humoral and cellular immune responses against SARS-CoV-2 comprising administering the composition; and a sixteenth aspect providing a method of inducing sterilizing immunity against SARS-CoV-2 comprising administering the composition.

Additional aspects include: a seventeenth aspect providing a composition that resembles SARS-CoV-2 virus-like particles when expressed in host cells; an eighteenth aspect providing a composition effective against coronaviruses with genetic similarity to SARS-CoV-2; and a nineteenth aspect providing the use of the composition in the preparation of a medicament for preventing SARS-CoV-2 infection.

The invention specifically contemplates genetically engineered attenuated vaccinia virus vectors, particularly SCV-based vectors, expressing one or more SARS-CoV-2 antigens. These compositions may include nucleic acid sequences encoding SARS-CoV-2 polypeptides inserted into deleted open reading frames (ORFs) of immune modulatory genes or intergenic regions of the attenuated vaccinia virus genome. The vectors may contain deletions of one or more genes, with heterologous sequences inserted into the deletion sites. Expression cassettes encoding SARS-CoV-2 polypeptides may be single or multiple, and the compositions may include pharmaceutically acceptable carriers.

The compositions are designed to raise immune responses comprising neutralizing antibodies and T cell responses against SARS-CoV-2 antigens. The genetically engineered attenuated vaccinia virus vectors may be used in methods for preventing SARS-CoV-2 infection by administering to subjects in need thereof. Alternative embodiments include variations in insertion sites, gene deletions, expression cassette design, and combinations of SARS-CoV-2 antigens to optimize immune responses.

Expression of poxvirus structural genes follows the viral replication cycle, with early genes involved in immune evasion and DNA replication, and late genes encoding virion components. Emerging SARS-CoV-2 variants may contain mutations in the spike protein, particularly the S1 subunit containing the receptor-binding domain. The SCV vector represents a particularly preferred embodiment, derived from the Copenhagen strain with targeted deletions to enhance safety while maintaining immunogenicity. Expression of SARS-CoV-2 structural and non-structural proteins in host cells may lead to spontaneous assembly of virus-like particles (VLPs) that mimic native virions. Methods of inducing immune responses involve administering the compositions via various routes including intramuscular, subcutaneous, or intradermal injection. Preferred attenuated poxviruses include modified orthopoxviruses with specific gene deletions. One exemplary vector has deletions of D13L (essential for virion assembly) and K1L (host range factor), rendering it replication-defective in normal cells while allowing propagation in complementing cell lines. ### Construction of Vaccines

The SCV-COVID19 vaccines are constructed through a series of well-defined molecular biology steps. Initial design involves removal of early transcription termination signals from SARS-CoV-2 gene sequences to ensure proper expression in the poxvirus context. Synthetic expression cassettes are designed for each antigenic transgene, incorporating appropriate control elements and flanking endonuclease recognition sites for subsequent cloning steps. Specific examples of expression cassettes include those driving SARS-CoV-2 spike protein expression under the control of a synthetic early/late promoter with optimized Kozak sequence and 3' transcription termination signals.

The transgene expression cassettes are inserted into homologous recombination (HR) plasmids containing vaccinia virus flanking sequences to target specific genomic loci. HR plasmid components typically include vaccinia homology arms (500-1000 bp), selectable marker genes (e.g., GFP or antibiotic resistance), and bacterial propagation elements. Specific examples of HR cassettes include those targeting the A41L or D13L deletion sites in the SCV genome.

Preparation of HR plasmids for virus construction involves large-scale plasmid purification followed by restriction digestion to linearize the recombination cassette. Homologous recombination is performed by infecting complementing mammalian cells (e.g., CHO-D13L/CP77) with SCV-SMX06 helper virus and transfecting with Not I-digested HR plasmid. Following incubation, recombinant viruses are identified by fluorescent marker expression or plaque morphology, then purified from parent virus through multiple rounds of plaque purification.

Selection genes are removed from recombinant viruses through intramolecular recombination between flanking direct repeats, facilitated by serial passage in the absence of selection pressure. Enrichment and purification of virus without selection markers involves plaque purification and PCR verification of marker loss. Virus seed stocks are validated by PCR and DNA sequencing across insertion sites, as well as Western blot or immunostaining to confirm antigen expression.

The construction strategy leverages SCV derivation from the Copenhagen strain through targeted gene deletions and host range adaptations. SCV-SMX06 represents a further modified version with additional deletions to enhance safety and increase capacity for heterologous gene insertion. Transgene insertion typically occurs at deletion sites of non-essential genes, with options for single or multiple antigen expression.

Variations of SCV-COVID19 vaccines include different combinations of SARS-CoV-2 antigens. Recombinant virus SCV-COVID19A encodes only the spike protein, while SCV-COVID19B co-expresses spike and membrane proteins. SCV-COVID19C incorporates spike, membrane, and envelope proteins, while SCV-COVID19D includes all four structural proteins (spike, membrane, envelope, and nucleocapsid). Further variants (SCV-COVID19E through SCV-COVID19I) explore alternative promoters, gene orders, and inclusion of non-structural proteins to optimize immune responses.
