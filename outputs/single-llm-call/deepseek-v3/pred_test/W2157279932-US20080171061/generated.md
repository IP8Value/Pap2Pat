# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND  

Human endogenous retrovirus (HERV) sequences represent remnants of ancient retroviral infections that have become fixed in the human genome through germline integration. These sequences comprise approximately 8.29% of the human genome and are normally transcriptionally silent due to truncation mutations or suppression by host defense mechanisms such as APOBEC3 proteins. However, recent studies have demonstrated that certain HERV families retain significant protein-coding capacity and can be reactivated under specific conditions, particularly during HIV-1 infection.  

When HIV-1 infects a host cell, it creates an intracellular environment conducive to viral replication by compromising cellular defense mechanisms. The HIV-1 protein Vif, for example, counteracts APOBEC proteins that would otherwise suppress retroviral activity. Additionally, some HERV transcripts contain sequences recognized by HIV-1 Rev protein, facilitating their nuclear export in infected cells. This altered cellular environment leads to detectable levels of HERV RNA in the plasma of HIV-1-infected individuals, suggesting active HERV protein production.  

### Literature  

Prior research has established that HIV-1-infected cells present both viral and self-antigens through HLA class I molecules, potentially including peptides derived from reactivated HERV sequences. While cytotoxic CD8+ T cells normally recognize and eliminate HIV-1-infected cells through detection of viral peptides, the presentation of HERV-derived peptides could theoretically prime an additional immune response against endogenous retroviral elements. Previous studies have reconstructed infectious hybrids from certain HERV families, confirming their retained coding capacity. Other investigations have detected elevated HERV RNA levels in HIV-1-positive individuals compared to uninfected controls, suggesting HIV-1-mediated reactivation of endogenous retroviral elements.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to isolated HERV polypeptides, immunogenic compositions comprising such polypeptides, and methods of using these compositions to stimulate immune responses. The invention specifically encompasses polypeptides derived from various HERV families including but not limited to HERV-K, HERV-L, and HERV-H. These polypeptides may be used individually or in combination to elicit cellular immune responses, particularly CD8+ T cell responses, against cells expressing HERV products.  

The invention further provides pharmaceutical formulations containing HERV polypeptides and methods for monitoring patient responses to these immunogenic compositions. The disclosed polypeptides and compositions have applications in treating HIV-1 infection, various cancers, and autoimmune disorders by modulating immune responses to endogenous retroviral elements.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

The invention provides novel HERV polypeptides and compositions for stimulating immune responses against cells expressing endogenous retroviral elements. These compositions have therapeutic applications in HIV-1 infection, cancer, and autoimmune disorders by targeting HERV products expressed in diseased cells.  

The immunogenic compositions comprise one or more HERV polypeptides capable of eliciting cellular immune responses, particularly CD8+ T cell responses. Pharmaceutical formulations containing these polypeptides may be administered to stimulate protective immunity or modulate pathological immune reactions. Methods for monitoring patient responses to treatment include measuring cytokine production and cytotoxic activity against HERV-expressing targets.  

### Isolated HERV Polypeptides  

The invention encompasses isolated polypeptides derived from various HERV families, including HERV-K, HERV-L, and HERV-H. These polypeptides are selected based on their ability to stimulate immune responses when presented by HLA molecules. The isolated polypeptides may comprise naturally occurring HERV sequences or modified variants designed to enhance immunogenicity while maintaining specificity for endogenous retroviral elements.  

### HERV Polypeptides  

HERV polypeptides of the invention typically range from 8-30 amino acids in length, corresponding to potential HLA class I binding epitopes. Representative sequences include but are not limited to those derived from HERV-K gag, pol, and env regions, HERV-L open reading frames, and conserved regions of HERV-H proteins.  

Specific examples include polypeptides sharing at least 70% sequence identity with native HERV proteins while retaining immunogenic properties. Sequence variations may include conservative substitutions that do not substantially alter antigenic characteristics. Additional polypeptides may be derived from regions showing homology between different HERV families or between HERV and exogenous retroviral sequences.  

### Fusion Proteins  

The invention includes fusion proteins combining HERV polypeptides with heterologous sequences to enhance immunogenicity or facilitate purification. Fusion partners may include immunoglobulin constant regions, protein tags, or immunostimulatory domains. The HERV polypeptide may be attached to the fusion partner directly or through a peptide linker.  

### Multimerized HERV Polypeptides  

Multimerized forms of HERV polypeptides are provided to increase avidity and immunogenicity. These may comprise dimers, trimers, or higher-order multimers connected through peptide linkers or chemical cross-linking. Flexible linker sequences such as (GGGGS)n may be used to connect multiple HERV polypeptide units while maintaining structural flexibility.  

### Carriers  

The HERV polypeptides may be conjugated to carriers including but not limited to keyhole limpet hemocyanin, bovine serum albumin, or synthetic nanoparticles. Conjugation methods include chemical cross-linking through reactive groups or genetic fusion. Lipidated forms of HERV polypeptides may be prepared by attaching fatty acid chains to enhance immunogenicity.  

### Compositions  

Pharmaceutical compositions may contain single HERV polypeptides or combinations targeting multiple endogenous retroviral elements. These compositions typically include pharmaceutically acceptable excipients and may be formulated for various routes of administration.  

### Production of HERV Polypeptides  

HERV polypeptides may be produced by chemical synthesis or recombinant expression in bacterial, yeast, or mammalian systems. Purification methods include affinity chromatography, size exclusion, and reverse-phase HPLC.  

### Immunogenic Compositions Comprising a HERV Polypeptide  

The immunogenic compositions contain one or more HERV polypeptides capable of stimulating T cell responses. These compositions may be designed to preferentially elicit CD8+ T cell responses against cells expressing HERV products.  

### Adjuvants  

Suitable adjuvants include Freund's complete and incomplete adjuvants, cytokines, monophosphoryl lipid A, CpG oligonucleotides, and saponin-based formulations. The compositions may include pharmaceutically acceptable carriers, buffers, and stabilizers.  

### Treatment Methods  

The invention provides methods for treating HIV-1 infection by administering immunogenic HERV polypeptide compositions to stimulate immune responses against infected cells. Similar approaches may be applied to cancer treatment by targeting HERV expression in tumor cells. For autoimmune disorders, the compositions may be used to modulate pathological immune responses.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

HIV-1-positive and negative volunteers were screened for T cell responses to HERV peptides. ELISPOT analysis detected strong interferon-γ responses to HERV peptides in HIV-1-positive but not negative individuals. 51Cr release assays demonstrated cytotoxic activity of HERV-specific CD8+ T cells against peptide-pulsed targets. These responses inversely correlated with HIV-1 viral load, suggesting a protective role.
