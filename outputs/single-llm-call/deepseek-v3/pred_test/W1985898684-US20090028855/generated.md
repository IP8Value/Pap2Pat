# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to novel compounds and methods for inhibiting the Akt protein kinase pathway. Akt, also known as protein kinase B (PKB), plays a critical role in cellular processes including proliferation, survival, metabolism, and angiogenesis. Akt activation occurs through a multi-step process involving phosphorylation at two key residues: threonine 308 (Thr308) and serine 473 (Ser473). While Thr308 phosphorylation is mediated by phosphoinositide-dependent kinase 1 (PDK1), the kinase responsible for Ser473 phosphorylation remains less clearly defined, with several candidate enzymes proposed.  

The phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K)/Akt pathway is negatively regulated by the tumor suppressor PTEN, which dephosphorylates phosphatidylinositol (3,4,5)-trisphosphate (PIP3). Loss of PTEN function leads to constitutive Akt activation, observed in numerous human malignancies. Aberrant Akt signaling contributes to tumor development through multiple mechanisms, including promotion of cell survival, inhibition of apoptosis, and stimulation of cell proliferation.  

Several Akt inhibitors have been developed, including lipid-based compounds targeting the pleckstrin homology (PH) domain and ATP-competitive kinase inhibitors. However, many existing inhibitors suffer from limited efficacy, poor selectivity, or unacceptable toxicity profiles. There remains an unmet need for potent, selective Akt inhibitors with favorable pharmacological properties for therapeutic applications.  

## BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides novel compounds and compositions for inhibiting Akt signaling, particularly the compound designated API-1 (pyrido[2,3-d]pyrimidine). API-1 demonstrates selective inhibition of Akt kinase activity and phosphorylation in living cells through a unique mechanism involving binding to the PH domain of Akt.  

The invention encompasses methods for inhibiting cancer cell proliferation by administering effective amounts of API-1 or related compounds to subjects in need thereof. API-1 shows particular efficacy against cancer cells exhibiting elevated Akt activity, including those with PTEN mutations or constitutive Akt activation. The compound inhibits tumor growth in vivo while showing minimal effects on cells with normal Akt signaling.  

Pharmaceutical compositions containing API-1 are provided for therapeutic use, including formulations suitable for oral, parenteral, and topical administration. The invention further provides methods for treating oncological disorders by targeting the Akt pathway, either as monotherapy or in combination with other chemotherapeutic agents.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides compounds having the general structure of pyrido[2,3-d]pyrimidine derivatives, particularly the compound designated API-1. These compounds demonstrate specific inhibition of the Akt/PKB signaling pathway through binding to the PH domain of Akt proteins.  

Preferred embodiments include API-1 and its pharmaceutically acceptable salts, hydrates, and stereoisomers. The compounds effectively inhibit both wild-type Akt and constitutively active mutants, including the E17K-AKT1 mutant observed in human cancers. Inhibition occurs through prevention of Akt membrane translocation rather than direct kinase domain blockade.  

Pharmaceutical compositions according to the invention comprise API-1 or related compounds formulated with pharmaceutically acceptable carriers. Suitable formulations include tablets, capsules, pills, troches, and liquid preparations for oral administration; sterile solutions for injection; and topical preparations containing solid or liquid carriers. Compositions may include adjuvants such as thickeners, flavoring agents, and preservatives.  

The alkyl and alkoxy groups in the compounds may be saturated or unsaturated, linear or branched, and optionally substituted. Halogen substituents include fluorine, chlorine, bromine, and iodine. The term "optionally substituted" refers to groups that may bear one or more substituents including hydroxyl, amino, carboxyl, and sulfhydryl groups.  

Therapeutic applications focus on the anti-proliferative properties of the compounds, particularly in treating oncological disorders characterized by aberrant Akt signaling. Administration methods include oral, intravenous, intramuscular, subcutaneous, and topical routes. Effective dosages may be extrapolated from animal models to human subjects based on body surface area calculations.  

The invention provides methods for inhibiting cancer cell survival or proliferation by contacting cells with API-1 or administering it to subjects. Treatment protocols may combine API-1 with other chemotherapeutic agents including platinum compounds, taxanes, antimetabolites, and immunotherapeutic agents.  

Kits according to the invention contain API-1 compositions in suitable containers with instructions for use. The methods benefit various mammalian species including humans, domesticated animals, farm animals, and exotic species. The term "patient" or "subject" refers to any animal recipient of the therapeutic methods.  

In vitro applications include screening assays for Akt inhibitors and research tools for studying Akt signaling pathways. The compounds show particular utility in studying PH domain function and membrane translocation mechanisms.  

### MATERIALS AND METHODS  

Cell lines used in developing the invention included standard cancer cell lines available from ATCC and previously characterized lines. Screening utilized the NCI Diversity Set of 2300 compounds to identify Akt inhibitors.  

Primary screening measured inhibition of Akt2-transformed NIH3T3 cell growth compared to vector controls. Secondary assays evaluated in vitro protein kinase activity, cell survival, and apoptosis induction. The nude mouse xenograft model assessed antitumor activity by measuring tumor volume changes following API-1 administration.  

### EXAMPLE 1  

The need for selective Akt inhibitors motivated development of small molecule compounds targeting this pathway. Screening the NCI Diversity Set identified API-1 as a potent Akt/PKB pathway inhibitor. Characterization revealed that API-1 inhibits Akt kinase activity and phosphorylation (Thr308 and Ser473) in living cells without directly inhibiting recombinant Akt in vitro.  

API-1 demonstrates selective Akt inhibition through binding to the PH domain, preventing membrane translocation. This unique mechanism distinguishes API-1 from ATP-competitive kinase inhibitors and accounts for its specificity.  

### EXAMPLE 2  

Investigations confirmed that API-1 does not inhibit upstream activators of Akt, including PI3K and PDK1. In vitro kinase assays showed no effect of API-1 on PI3K or PDK1 activity, and immunoblotting revealed no changes in PDK1 phosphorylation patterns following API-1 treatment.  

### EXAMPLE 3  

Selectivity profiling demonstrated that API-1 does not inhibit other AGC kinase family members including PKA, PKC, and SGK. Furthermore, API-1 showed no effect on other oncogenic survival pathways mediated by ERK, JNK, p38, or STAT3.  

### EXAMPLE 4  

API-1 effectively inhibits constitutively active Akt mutants, including myristoylated Akt and the E17K-AKT1 mutant. The compound reduces phosphorylation of downstream Akt targets such as GSK3β, mTOR, and FOXO3a, confirming pathway inhibition.  

### EXAMPLE 5  

API-1 preferentially inhibits cell growth and induces apoptosis in cancer cells with elevated Akt activity. Cells expressing wild-type Akt or having low Akt levels show significantly less response to API-1 treatment, demonstrating its selectivity.  

### EXAMPLE 6  

In vivo studies using nude mouse xenograft models showed API-1 significantly inhibits growth of tumors with elevated Akt activity while having minimal effect on tumors with normal Akt levels. Treated tumors exhibited reduced Akt phosphorylation without changes in total Akt protein.
