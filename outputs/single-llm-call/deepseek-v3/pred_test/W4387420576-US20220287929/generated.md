## DESCRIPTION  

### FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of dermatological compositions and methods for topical skin treatment. More specifically, the invention pertains to novel lipid-based capsule formulations designed for targeted delivery of active ingredients to counteract oxidative stress and signs of aging in human skin.  

### BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Skin care products have long been utilized to maintain skin health and combat visible signs of aging. These products typically contain active ingredients that provide biological activity beneficial to skin structure and function. Among the most critical biological activities are antioxidant effects that neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS).  

ROS are naturally occurring molecules that play essential roles in cellular signaling and homeostasis. However, when ROS concentrations exceed cellular defense mechanisms, oxidative stress occurs, leading to deleterious effects on skin components. The oxygen radical absorbance capacity (ORAC) is a standardized measure of a substance's ability to neutralize oxygen radicals.  

The damage caused by ROS manifests through several biochemical pathways. Lipid peroxidation occurs when free radicals attack polyunsaturated fatty acids in cell membranes, leading to cellular dysfunction. Protein damage results from oxidation of amino acid side chains and protein backbone modifications, disrupting enzymatic activity and structural integrity. DNA damage occurs through oxidation of nucleic acids, particularly affecting telomeres and accelerating cellular senescence.  

Current antioxidant products typically utilize vitamins such as A, C, and E to combat oxidative stress. However, these known products have significant drawbacks. Free vitamins often demonstrate poor stability and skin penetration, while existing encapsulation technologies frequently fail to provide adequate protection or controlled release of active ingredients. Many current formulations also lack specificity for ROS-rich environments in aged or damaged skin.  

### BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a novel solution to these limitations through a specialized capsule formulation with high affinity for reactive oxygen species. The invention comprises a lipid particle matrix capable of entrapping both lipophilic and hydrophilic active ingredients while demonstrating exceptional ROS-targeting capabilities.  

The skin care product of the invention offers several advantages over existing solutions. The capsule matrix provides a reliable and easy-to-implement delivery system that protects active ingredients from degradation while ensuring their controlled release. The manufacturing process is cost-competitive with conventional encapsulation methods, yet yields superior performance characteristics.  

The capsule composition includes a unique blend of natural waxes and polyunsaturated fatty acids that confer high ROS affinity. The process for preparing these capsules involves melting solid lipid components, solubilizing active ingredients, and forming stable microemulsions through controlled emulsification and sieving techniques.  

When incorporated into topical treatments such as serums, creams, or lotions, the invention provides multiple benefits including enhanced skin hydration, improved barrier function, and significant reduction in visible signs of aging. Clinical studies demonstrate superior performance compared to both free vitamins and conventionally encapsulated actives.  

### DESCRIPTION  

The present invention relates to skin care products and methods for their use in combating oxidative stress and aging. The skin care product comprises specialized capsules suspended in a dermatologically acceptable carrier.  

The capsule composition features a lipid particle matrix consisting of a carefully balanced mixture of natural waxes including jojoba wax, mimosa wax, and sunflower seed wax. These waxes form the lipophilic solid part of the matrix, providing structural integrity to the capsules. The matrix further contains polyunsaturated fatty acids derived from safflower oil, constituting the lipophilic liquid portion that confers fluidity and ROS affinity.  

The matrix composition includes between 20-40% polyunsaturated fatty acids by weight, ensuring optimal ROS scavenging capacity. The lipophilic solid portion constitutes 30-50% of the matrix, while surfactants such as decyl glucoside and suspension agents comprise 5-15% of the formulation. Humectants are incorporated at 2-10% to maintain capsule hydration.  

Active ingredients are embedded within this matrix, including both water-soluble compounds like sodium ascorbyl phosphate (vitamin C derivative) and lipophilic actives such as retinyl palmitate (vitamin A derivative). Antioxidant agents are strategically positioned within the matrix to provide comprehensive protection against oxidative damage.  

In an alternate embodiment, the skin care product features a modified matrix composition with enhanced thermal properties. The lipid component in this embodiment includes wax blends with melting points between 45-60°C, allowing for easier processing while maintaining stability at skin temperature. The lipid component mixture in this version may incorporate additional natural butters to optimize texture and spreadability.  

The matrix composition demonstrates exceptionally high ROS affinity, defined as the ability to preferentially localize in oxidative stress environments and neutralize free radicals. This is achieved through the unsaturated fatty acid content which serves as oxidation targets, combined with strategically positioned antioxidant agents that regenerate the matrix's scavenging capacity.  

The oxidation-stable matrix maintains integrity even under prolonged oxidative stress, as demonstrated by iodine index measurements below 60 g/100g. Excipients such as stabilizers and emulsifiers are incorporated at optimal levels to preserve matrix functionality without compromising its ROS affinity.  

The method for making the capsules involves several precise steps. First, the solid lipophilic portion is melted at 50-60°C under controlled stirring. Active ingredients are then solubilized or dispersed into this melted phase. Separately, an aqueous solution containing hydrophilic actives and surfactants is prepared at 45-50°C. The lipid and aqueous phases are combined under high-shear mixing to form a uniform emulsion, which is then screened through a 25μm mesh to ensure consistent particle size. The emulsion is rapidly cooled to solidify the lipid particles, which are then dried to remove excess water.  

An alternate preparation method involves direct incorporation of the capsules into final product formulations such as serums or creams. This streamlined process reduces manufacturing steps while maintaining product quality. The finished skin care product is packaged in air-tight containers to preserve capsule integrity.  

The capsules demonstrate exceptional antioxidant capacity, with ORAC values significantly exceeding conventional vitamin formulations. This enhanced activity stems from both the matrix composition and the synergistic combination of encapsulated active ingredients. Clinical studies confirm superior performance in improving skin hydration, barrier function, and visible signs of aging compared to market alternatives.
