# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates generally to the field of cancer therapy. More specifically, the invention provides compositions and methods for treating cancer by targeting the chemokine CCL20 and its receptor CCR6. The invention encompasses neutralizing antibodies against CCL20, pharmaceutical compositions containing such antibodies, and methods for treating various cancers including prostate cancer, leukemia, and colon cancer by inhibiting CCL20-mediated tumor growth, vascularization, and metastasis.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Chemokines are a family of small pro-inflammatory cytokines primarily responsible for directional migration (chemotaxis) of lymphocytes to lymphoid tissues and recruitment of leukocytes to sites of infection or tissue damage. Beyond chemotaxis, chemokines participate in diverse biological processes including angiogenesis, hematopoiesis, lymphopoiesis, and embryogenesis.  

Cancer cells exploit chemokine receptor signaling to facilitate key steps in primary tumor development and metastatic progression. Among chemokine receptors, CXCR4 is uniquely expressed across a broad spectrum of cancers including gliomas, breast tumors, leukemias, uterine cancer, Burkitt's lymphoma, neuroblastomas, and pancreatic cancer. CXCR4 activation promotes tumor progression through mechanisms that remain incompletely understood.  

CCL20 (macrophage inflammatory protein-3α) is a CC-type chemokine that specifically binds CCR6. While constitutively expressed at low levels in normal tissues, CCL20 expression increases in response to inflammatory stimuli. CCL20 has been detected in various malignancies including colorectal, lung, pancreatic, and breast cancers, as well as gliomas, leukemias, lymphomas, and melanomas.  

The role of CCL20 in cancer remains controversial. Some studies suggest CCL20 may suppress tumors by attracting dendritic cells to induce antitumor immunity. However, accumulating evidence indicates CCL20 frequently promotes tumor growth and invasiveness. For example, CCL20 overexpression in pancreatic carcinoma stimulates neoplastic cell growth and invasion. Similarly, hepatocellular carcinoma tissues show elevated CCL20 expression correlating with tumor grade.  

Despite these advances, the relationship between CXCR4 and CCL20 in cancer progression remains unexplored. Moreover, current cancer therapies inadequately target chemokine-mediated tumor growth mechanisms. There exists an unmet need for novel therapeutic approaches that disrupt chemokine signaling pathways driving tumor progression.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides compositions and methods for treating cancer by neutralizing CCL20 activity. Surprisingly, we discovered that CXCR4 upregulates CCL20 expression in cancer cells, establishing a novel CXCR4/CCL20 axis in tumor progression. Our findings demonstrate CCL20 promotes tumor growth, adhesion, vascularization, and invasion through autocrine/paracrine mechanisms.  

The invention encompasses CCL20-neutralizing antibodies, particularly monoclonal antibody MAB360, that specifically inhibit CCL20 binding to CCR6. These antibodies effectively suppress CCL20-mediated tumor growth and metastasis in various cancers including prostate cancer, leukemia, and colon cancer.  

Pharmaceutical compositions containing CCL20-neutralizing antibodies are provided for therapeutic administration. The antibodies may be formulated with pharmaceutically acceptable carriers for intravenous, subcutaneous, or other routes of administration.  

Methods of treating cancer comprise administering a therapeutically effective amount of CCL20-neutralizing antibodies to subjects with CCR6- and/or CXCR4-expressing tumors. The antibodies inhibit tumor growth, progression, vascularization, and metastasis while promoting tumor regression. Target cancers include but are not limited to prostate cancer, glioma, leukemia, uterine cancer, and colon cancer.  

The invention further provides kits containing CCL20-neutralizing antibodies with instructions for therapeutic use. Diagnostic methods assess CCL20 and CCR6 expression levels to identify patients likely to respond to anti-CCL20 therapy.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides anti-CCL20 antibodies for treating CCL20-dependent cancers. The antibodies specifically bind CCL20, blocking its interaction with CCR6 and inhibiting downstream signaling. Antibody MAB360 serves as an exemplary CCL20-neutralizing antibody, though the invention encompasses other antibodies with similar neutralizing activity.  

The antibodies are useful for treating cancers expressing CCR6 and/or CXCR4, including prostate cancer, glioma, leukemia, uterine cancer, and colon cancer. Administration inhibits tumor growth and progression by disrupting CCL20-mediated autocrine/paracrine signaling. The antibodies also suppress tumor vascularization by blocking CCL20-induced angiogenesis.  

Pharmaceutical compositions contain CCL20-neutralizing antibodies formulated for parenteral administration, typically by injection or infusion. Liquid formulations may include buffers, stabilizers, and other excipients. Dosage regimens provide therapeutically effective amounts based on tumor type, stage, and patient characteristics.  

The antibodies may be administered as monotherapy or combined with other anticancer agents. Treatment protocols involve regular administration until achieving therapeutic response, which may include tumor regression or stabilized disease.  

Antibody structures include intact immunoglobulins, antigen-binding fragments (Fab, F(ab')2), single-chain Fv (scFv), and other engineered variants. Antibodies may be murine, chimeric, humanized, or fully human. Production methods include hybridoma technology, phage display, and recombinant DNA techniques.  

Humanized antibodies are particularly preferred for therapeutic use. CDR grafting techniques transplant murine complementarity-determining regions into human framework regions. Further engineering optimizes affinity and reduces immunogenicity. Fully human antibodies can be generated using transgenic mice or phage display libraries.  

The antibodies specifically bind CCL20 with high affinity, typically exhibiting dissociation constants (Kd) below 10 nM. Neutralizing activity is demonstrated by in vitro assays measuring inhibition of CCL20-induced cell adhesion or proliferation. In vivo efficacy is shown by tumor growth inhibition in xenograft models.  

Pharmaceutical kits contain sterile preparations of CCL20-neutralizing antibodies with instructions for therapeutic use. Kits may include diagnostic reagents for assessing CCL20/CCR6 expression to guide treatment decisions.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Experimental Procedures  

Cell Culture: Prostate cancer cell lines (PC3, LNCaP, 22Rv1, DU145), leukemia cell lines (NB4, HL-60), and colon carcinoma cell line HT-29 were maintained in standard media. CXCR4-overexpressing PC3 clones were generated by transduction and single-cell cloning.  

RNA Analysis: Total RNA was extracted using TRIzol reagent. Reverse transcription produced cDNA for semi-quantitative and real-time PCR. Primer sets amplified CCL20, CCR6, CXCR4, and β-actin sequences.  

Cell Proliferation Assays: Cells were cultured with varying CCL20 concentrations. Viable cells were counted after propidium iodide staining. Alternatively, proliferation was assessed by BrdU incorporation.  

Cell Adhesion Assays: Cancer cells were allowed to adhere to fibronectin- or collagen I-coated plates. Non-adherent cells were washed away, and adherent cells were quantified.  

ELISA: CCL20 protein levels in cell supernatants and lysates were measured using commercial ELISA kits according to manufacturer protocols.  

Flow Cytometry: Cell surface expression of CCR6 and CXCR4 was analyzed using fluorophore-conjugated antibodies and flow cytometric analysis.  

RNA Interference: CXCR4 expression was silenced using Dicer-substrate siRNA duplexes. Transfection efficiency was verified by flow cytometry and PCR.  

Xenograft Models: SCID/bg mice were injected subcutaneously with cancer cells. Tumor size was measured regularly. Some mice received anti-CCL20 antibody treatments.  

Immunohistochemistry: Formalin-fixed tumor sections were stained for CCL20, CCR6, and CXCR4 using specific antibodies. Staining intensity was scored by independent evaluators.  

### Example 1  

CCL20 and CCR6 expression was characterized in prostate cell lines. Only PC3 cells expressed both CCR6 receptor and secreted CCL20 protein. CCL20 stimulation increased PC3 cell proliferation (1.75-fold at 50 ng/mL) and adhesion to collagen I (2.2-fold at 50 ng/mL). These effects were abolished by pertussis toxin, indicating G protein-coupled receptor mediation.  

CXCR4 overexpression in PC3 cells increased CCL20 mRNA (41-fold) and protein secretion (50-fold). CXCL12 stimulation further enhanced CCL20 production in a dose-dependent manner. Silencing CXCR4 reduced both constitutive and CXCL12-stimulated CCL20 expression, confirming CXCR4 regulates CCL20 production.  

### Example 2  

In xenograft models, CCL20-overexpressing PC3 cells formed larger, more vascularized tumors than control cells. MRI analysis showed significantly higher vascular functionality (ΔSo2 values) in CCL20-expressing tumors. Histological examination revealed extensive angiogenesis and tissue invasion in CCL20-positive tumors.  

### Example 3  

Anti-CCL20 antibody treatment inhibited CCL20-induced cell adhesion in vitro. In vivo, antibody therapy delayed tumor appearance and reduced growth of both CCL20-overexpressing and CXCR4-overexpressing prostate tumors. Treated tumors showed extensive necrosis and reduced vascularization.  

### Example 4  

Immunohistochemical analysis of human prostate tumors revealed coexpression of CCL20 and CCR6 in most samples (65-96%). High CCL20 expression correlated with advanced Gleason scores, suggesting clinical relevance of the CCL20/CCR6 axis in prostate cancer progression. Similar results were observed for CXCR4 and CCL20 coexpression.  

This detailed description provides enabling disclosure for practicing the invention, including specific embodiments and experimental support. The examples demonstrate therapeutic efficacy of CCL20-neutralizing antibodies across multiple cancer types, particularly those expressing CCR6 and/or CXCR4.
