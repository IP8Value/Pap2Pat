# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates generally to the field of immunology and autoimmune disorders. More specifically, the invention provides compositions and methods for treating inflammatory muscle diseases and other autoimmune conditions through administration of Insulin-like 6 (Insl6) protein or functional fragments thereof. The invention further encompasses diagnostic methods for identifying subjects amenable to treatment with Insl6 agents based on detection of Insl6 expression levels in biological samples.

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Myositis refers to a group of rare autoimmune disorders characterized by chronic inflammation of skeletal muscles. The major clinical subtypes include polymyositis, dermatomyositis and inclusion body myositis, each presenting with progressive muscle weakness and elevated serum creatine kinase levels. Histopathological examination reveals inflammatory cell infiltration in affected muscle tissue, with distinct patterns among the different subtypes. In dermatomyositis, CD4+ T cells and B cells predominantly localize in perivascular regions, while polymyositis features endomysial infiltration of CD8+ T cells that invade non-necrotic muscle fibers.  

Current treatment options for inflammatory myopathies remain limited and often inadequate. Standard immunosuppressive therapies including corticosteroids, methotrexate and intravenous immunoglobulin demonstrate variable efficacy across patient populations. Moreover, prolonged use of these agents frequently leads to significant adverse effects. The development of targeted therapies has been hindered by incomplete understanding of disease pathogenesis. Emerging evidence suggests regulatory T cell (Treg) dysfunction contributes to disease progression, though the molecular mechanisms underlying this immune dysregulation remain poorly characterized.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides comprehensive compositions and methods centered on the therapeutic and diagnostic applications of Insulin-like 6 (Insl6) protein in autoimmune disorders. The detailed description that follows elaborates on various embodiments of the invention, supported by experimental data demonstrating the efficacy of Insl6 in ameliorating autoimmune myositis.  

### Insl6 Protein and Its Variants  

The invention encompasses native Insl6 polypeptides as well as functional variants and fragments thereof. Insl6 belongs to the insulin/relaxin family of peptide hormones characterized by distinct A and B chains connected by disulfide bonds. The full-length protein undergoes post-translational processing to generate mature bioactive forms. The invention includes all naturally occurring isoforms as well as recombinant forms produced through genetic engineering techniques.  

Functional fragments of Insl6 retain at least one biological activity of the full-length protein, such as the ability to suppress T cell proliferation or reduce pro-inflammatory cytokine production. Exemplary fragments comprise the B-domain, A-domain, or combinations thereof while maintaining proper disulfide bonding. The invention further encompasses derivatives including PEGylated forms, glycosylated variants, and other modifications that enhance stability or bioavailability.  

### Fusion Proteins  

The invention provides fusion proteins wherein Insl6 or its functional fragments are conjugated to heterologous polypeptides. In preferred embodiments, Insl6 is fused to immunoglobulin Fc domains, particularly human IgG1 Fc, to prolong serum half-life and enhance therapeutic efficacy. The Fc moiety may be linked directly or through flexible peptide linkers. Other fusion partners include serum albumin, purification tags, or targeting domains that direct tissue-specific delivery.  

### Regulation of T-Regulatory Cells  

A key mechanism underlying the therapeutic effects of Insl6 involves modulation of regulatory T cell (Treg) activity. Experimental data demonstrate that Insl6 promotes Treg-mediated suppression of effector T cell responses, thereby restoring immune homeostasis. This activity proves particularly beneficial in Th1-dominant autoimmune conditions where Treg dysfunction contributes to pathogenesis. The invention encompasses methods for enhancing Treg function through Insl6 administration, either as monotherapy or in combination with other immunomodulatory agents.  

### Treatment of Autoimmune Diseases  

The invention provides specific methods for treating various autoimmune disorders using Insl6 agents. Inflammatory myopathies represent a primary indication, with demonstrated efficacy in experimental models of polymyositis and dermatomyositis. Treatment protocols involve administering therapeutically effective doses of Insl6 protein, fragments, or nucleic acids encoding same, with regimens tailored to disease severity and patient characteristics.  

Additional treatable conditions include but are not limited to rheumatoid arthritis, multiple sclerosis, inflammatory bowel disease, systemic lupus erythematosus, and type 1 diabetes. The broad immunomodulatory activity of Insl6 renders it effective across diverse autoimmune pathologies characterized by excessive inflammatory responses.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions  

The invention encompasses various formulations of Insl6 agents suitable for clinical administration. These include sterile injectable solutions, lyophilized powders for reconstitution, sustained-release preparations, and oral dosage forms. Pharmaceutical carriers may be selected to optimize stability, bioavailability, and tissue targeting. Exemplary formulations incorporate excipients such as buffers, surfactants, preservatives, and isotonicity agents.  

### Diagnostic Applications  

The invention provides methods for diagnosing autoimmune diseases and identifying treatment-responsive subjects through Insl6 detection. Immunoassays quantify Insl6 protein levels in muscle biopsies or other biological samples, while nucleic acid-based tests measure transcript abundance. Decreased Insl6 expression relative to reference values indicates disease presence or progression, while restoration of normal levels correlates with therapeutic response.  

### Gene Therapy Approaches  

The invention encompasses gene therapy methods wherein nucleic acids encoding Insl6 are delivered to patients using viral or non-viral vectors. Suitable expression systems include adenoviral, lentiviral, and adeno-associated viral vectors engineered for tissue-specific transduction. Regulatory elements such as muscle-specific promoters may be incorporated to direct localized production. Non-viral delivery methods include lipid nanoparticles, electroporation, and hydrodynamic injection.

## EXAMPLES  

The following examples illustrate specific embodiments of the invention without limiting its scope.  

### Example 1  

An experimental autoimmune myositis (EAM) model was established in C57BL/6 mice by immunization with skeletal muscle myosin-binding protein C. Insl6-overexpressing transgenic mice showed significantly reduced inflammation and improved motor function compared to wild-type controls. Histological analysis revealed decreased CD4+ and CD8+ T cell infiltration in muscle tissue.  

Construction of an Fc-Insl6 fusion protein enabled hydrodynamic delivery in Insl6-deficient mice. Treated animals exhibited ameliorated disease symptoms, including reduced pro-inflammatory cytokine levels and improved Rotarod performance. These findings corroborate the therapeutic potential of Insl6 in inflammatory myopathies.
