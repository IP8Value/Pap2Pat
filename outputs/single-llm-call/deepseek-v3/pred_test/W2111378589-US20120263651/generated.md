## TECHNICAL FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of molecular biology and biochemistry, specifically to single-stranded DNA (ssDNA) aptamers that selectively bind to the nuclear factor-kappa B (NF-κB) subunit RelA. More particularly, the invention provides novel ssDNA aptamers, methods for their isolation, and their applications in diagnostic, therapeutic, and analytical techniques, including the quantification of activated RelA in biological samples.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Single-stranded DNA aptamers are synthetic oligonucleotides that bind with high affinity and specificity to target molecules, including proteins, small molecules, and cells. These aptamers are selected through an iterative process of binding and enrichment, often using techniques such as Systematic Evolution of Ligands by Exponential Enrichment (SELEX). Due to their high specificity and stability, ssDNA aptamers have found widespread applications in therapeutics, diagnostics, and analytical assays.  

Therapeutic applications of ssDNA aptamers include their use as antagonists to block protein-protein interactions or as delivery vehicles for targeted drug delivery. Diagnostic applications leverage their ability to detect and quantify biomarkers in complex biological samples. Analytical applications include their use in affinity purification and mass spectrometry-based quantification of target proteins.  

Previous patents and applications have described various aspects of ssDNA aptamers. For example, U.S. Patent Application No. 20080261225 discloses methods for generating aptamers that bind to specific protein targets. U.S. Pat. No. 6,867,289 describes the use of aptamers in diagnostic assays, while U.S. Pat. No. 7,309,786 covers therapeutic applications of aptamers for modulating cellular pathways. Despite these advances, there remains a need for aptamers that specifically target activated forms of transcription factors such as NF-κB/RelA, which play a central role in inflammatory and immune responses.  

NF-κB/RelA is a key regulator of gene expression in response to inflammatory stimuli. Its activity is tightly controlled by interactions with inhibitory proteins such as IκBα, which sequester RelA in the cytoplasm in an inactive state. Upon stimulation, IκBα is degraded, allowing RelA to translocate to the nucleus and activate target genes. Current methods for quantifying activated RelA are limited by their inability to distinguish between free RelA and IκBα-bound RelA. The present invention addresses this gap by providing ssDNA aptamers that selectively bind to activated RelA, enabling its precise quantification and isolation.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides single-stranded DNA (ssDNA) aptamers that specifically bind to the NF-κB subunit RelA. These aptamers are useful for the quantification, isolation, and modulation of RelA activity in vitro and in vivo.  

One embodiment of the invention is the ssDNA aptamer designated P028F4, which binds to RelA with high affinity (KD = 6.4 × 10^−10 M) and competes with natural double-stranded NF-κB binding sites for RelA binding. P028F4 selectively recognizes the activated, IκBα-free form of RelA, making it a valuable tool for studying RelA activation dynamics.  

The invention further provides methods for quantifying RelA using P028F4 in combination with stable isotope dilution-selected reaction monitoring mass spectrometry (SID-SRM-MS). This approach enables the precise measurement of activated RelA in biological samples, overcoming limitations of traditional immunoassays.  

Additional embodiments include methods for enriching RelA using biotin-conjugated P028F4 and streptavidin magnetic beads, methods for releasing bound RelA from aptamer complexes, and assays for detecting released RelA. The invention also encompasses methods for modulating the innate immune response by administering compositions comprising P028F4 or its derivatives to inhibit NF-κB-mediated gene expression.  

The aptamer P028F4 is represented by SEQ ID NO: 9, which exhibits homology to SEQ ID NO: 21. The invention includes variants of P028F4 with modified bases or backbones that retain binding affinity for RelA.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Introduction  

The invention provides ssDNA aptamers that bind to the NF-κB subunit RelA, methods for their isolation, and their applications in research, diagnostics, and therapeutics. The aptamer P028F4 was isolated through a genetic enrichment protocol and characterized for its binding properties and functional effects.  

### Definitions  

As used herein, the following terms have the specified meanings:  

- **Aptamer**: A single-stranded DNA or RNA oligonucleotide that binds to a specific target molecule.  
- **RelA**: The p65 subunit of NF-κB, a transcription factor involved in inflammatory and immune responses.  
- **Activated RelA**: RelA that is not bound to IκBα and is capable of binding DNA or other ligands.  
- **SID-SRM-MS**: Stable isotope dilution-selected reaction monitoring mass spectrometry, a quantitative proteomics technique.  

### ssDNA Aptamer P028F4  

The aptamer P028F4 was selected from a DNA library containing a 30-base random sequence flanked by primer-binding sites. After 12 rounds of selection, P028F4 was identified as a high-affinity binder to RelA. The variable region of P028F4 contains the sequence GGGAC, which matches half of the natural NF-κB binding site.  

P028F4 binds to recombinant RelA (amino acids 1-313) with a dissociation constant (KD) of 6.4 × 10^−10 M, as determined by surface plasmon resonance. The aptamer competes with double-stranded NF-κB DNA for RelA binding, suggesting it interacts with the DNA-binding domain of RelA.  

### Applications of P028F4  

#### Enrichment and Quantification of RelA  

P028F4 is used in an affinity isolation-mass spectrometric quantification assay to enrich activated RelA from biological samples. The assay involves:  
1. Incubating cell extracts with biotinylated P028F4.  
2. Capturing the aptamer-RelA complex on streptavidin magnetic beads.  
3. Releasing bound RelA for quantification by SID-SRM-MS.  

This method significantly improves the signal-to-noise ratio for RelA detection, enabling precise quantification of activated RelA in complex samples.  

#### Modulation of Innate Immune Response  

P028F4 inhibits TNFα-induced NF-κB-dependent gene expression by blocking RelA binding to target promoters. In cell-based assays, P028F4 reduces the expression of genes such as Groβ and TNFAIP3/A20, which are regulated by NF-κB.  

#### Analytical Methods  

The invention provides methods for:  
- Isolating RelA using P028F4 and streptavidin beads.  
- Quantifying RelA by SID-SRM-MS.  
- Detecting post-translational modifications of RelA, such as phosphorylation at Ser536.  

### Experimental Procedures  

#### Recombinant Protein Expression  

RelA (amino acids 1-313) and IκBα were expressed as GST fusion proteins in E. coli and purified using glutathione affinity chromatography.  

#### Aptamer Binding Studies  

Binding affinity was measured by nitrocellulose filter binding assays and surface plasmon resonance. Competition experiments confirmed P028F4's specificity for RelA over IκBα-bound RelA.  

#### Cell-Based Assays  

A549 cells were transfected with P028F4 or control aptamers and stimulated with TNFα. RelA binding to target promoters was assessed by chromatin immunoprecipitation (ChIP), and gene expression was quantified by RT-PCR.  

#### Mass Spectrometry  

RelA was quantified using SID-SRM-MS with stable isotope-labeled peptides as internal standards. The assay was optimized for sensitivity and specificity, with a linear dynamic range spanning three orders of magnitude.  

### Utility of the Invention  

The invention has broad applications in:  
- **Research**: Studying NF-κB activation dynamics and protein-protein interactions.  
- **Diagnostics**: Quantifying activated RelA in patient samples for inflammatory diseases.  
- **Therapeutics**: Developing aptamer-based inhibitors of NF-κB for treating inflammation or cancer.  

### Conclusion  

The ssDNA aptamer P028F4 is a powerful tool for isolating, quantifying, and modulating RelA activity. Its high specificity for activated RelA enables precise measurements of NF-κB signaling in health and disease. The invention encompasses P028F4, its derivatives, and methods for its use in research, diagnostics, and therapeutics.
