# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD  

The present invention relates to antibodies for cancer treatment. More particularly, the invention provides anti-TIGIT antibodies and methods for their use in treating cancer, either as monotherapy or in combination with other therapeutic agents such as anti-PD1 antibodies.  

## BACKGROUND  

The TIGIT protein (T cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains) is a member of the immunoglobulin superfamily receptors. TIGIT is expressed on activated CD8+ and CD4+ T cells, natural killer (NK) cells, and regulatory T cells (Tregs) in both mice and humans. The structure of TIGIT comprises an extracellular immunoglobulin variable (IgV) domain, a transmembrane domain, and an intracellular immunoreceptor tyrosine-based inhibitory motif (ITIM).  

TIGIT expression is upregulated in the tumor microenvironment across multiple malignancies. High TIGIT expression on tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes correlates with poor patient survival. TIGIT frequently co-expresses with PD-1 and other inhibitory receptors such as TIM-3 and LAG-3 on exhausted CD8+ T cells and Tregs within tumors.  

Functionally, TIGIT binds with high affinity to its ligand PVR (CD155) and with lower affinity to PVRL2 (CD112). Through these interactions, TIGIT inhibits T cell and NK cell functions via multiple mechanisms, including direct inhibitory signaling, competition with the stimulatory receptor DNAM-1/CD226 for ligand binding, enhancement of Treg immunosuppressive functions, and promotion of tolerogenic dendritic cells.  

In the context of immune responses, TIGIT plays a critical role in regulating immune cell activity. The protein contributes to immune cell dysfunction in cancer by suppressing anti-tumor immunity. TIGIT is also implicated in viral infections, where it may contribute to immune evasion by pathogens.  

PD1/PDL1 checkpoint inhibitors have revolutionized cancer immunotherapy. However, limitations exist with single-agent checkpoint blockade, including acquired resistance and incomplete response rates. The co-expression of TIGIT with PD-1 in tumors suggests that combination therapy targeting both pathways may overcome these limitations. The present invention addresses this need by providing anti-TIGIT antibodies for use in combination with anti-PD1 therapy.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides anti-TIGIT antibodies for cancer treatment. The antibodies comprise heavy chain and light chain variable regions that specifically bind human TIGIT. In particular embodiments, the heavy chain variable region comprises complementarity determining regions (CDRs) with specific amino acid sequences, while the light chain variable region similarly comprises defined CDR sequences.  

The antibodies bind human TIGIT with high affinity, exhibiting equilibrium dissociation constants (Kd) in the nanomolar range as measured by surface plasmon resonance. The invention further provides antibodies with human constant regions, including IgG1 constant regions with wild-type Fc domains capable of mediating effector functions.  

Methods of treating cancer using the anti-TIGIT antibodies are disclosed. The methods include administering an effective amount of an anti-TIGIT antibody, optionally in combination with an anti-PD1 antibody. Specific anti-PD1 antibodies suitable for combination therapy include pembrolizumab and nivolumab. The methods are applicable to various cancer types, including but not limited to non-small cell lung cancer, nasopharyngeal carcinoma, esophageal squamous cell carcinoma, and cervical cancer.  

Dosing regimens for the anti-TIGIT antibodies are provided, including specific schedules for administration. The invention further contemplates combination therapies incorporating chemoradiotherapy alongside the antibody treatments.

### Anti-TIGIT Antibodies  

The invention provides anti-TIGIT antibodies characterized by specific heavy and light chain variable region sequences. These antibodies bind human TIGIT with high affinity and block its interaction with ligands PVR and PVRL2.  

The antibodies may comprise mutations in framework regions to improve stability or binding properties. The antibodies are useful for treating cancers where TIGIT contributes to immune suppression in the tumor microenvironment.  

### Anti-PD1 Antibodies  

The invention contemplates combination therapies using anti-TIGIT antibodies with anti-PD1 antibodies. Suitable anti-PD1 antibodies include pembrolizumab and nivolumab, which bind PD1 and block its interaction with PDL1.  

### Further Alteration of the Framework of Fc Region  

The Fc region of anti-TIGIT antibodies may be modified to alter effector functions. Amino acid substitutions can modify affinity for Fc receptors or complement components. Such modifications may enhance antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) or reduce complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC).  

### Antibody Production  

Anti-TIGIT antibodies may be produced by recombinant expression in host cells. Polynucleotides encoding the antibody chains are inserted into expression vectors under control of suitable promoters. Both prokaryotic and eukaryotic host cells may be used, with mammalian cells preferred for full-length antibody production.  

### Methods of Detection and Diagnosis  

The invention provides methods for detecting TIGIT in biological samples, useful for diagnosing disorders associated with TIGIT expression. These methods involve comparing TIGIT levels in test samples with control samples.  

### Methods of Treatment  

The anti-TIGIT antibodies are useful for treating TIGIT-associated disorders, particularly cancers. Administration may be via parenteral, intrapulmonary, or intranasal routes. Dosing schedules and combination regimens with other therapeutic agents are provided.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions and Formulations  

Pharmaceutical compositions comprising anti-TIGIT antibodies are disclosed. These compositions include pharmaceutically acceptable carriers and may be formulated for sustained release. Sterile formulations for injection are particularly contemplated.  

## EXAMPLE  

### Example 1: Generation of Anti-TIGIT Monoclonal Antibody  

Anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibodies were generated using hybridoma technology. Mice were immunized with TIGIT recombinant protein, and splenocytes were fused with murine myeloma cells. Hybridoma clones were screened by ELISA and flow cytometry for TIGIT binding.  

### Example 2: Cloning and Sequence Analysis of TIGIT Antibodies  

Variable regions of selected antibodies were cloned and sequenced. Sequence analysis revealed specific CDR sequences responsible for TIGIT binding.  

### Example 3: Affinity Determination of Purified Murine Anti-TIGIT Antibodies by SPR  

Surface plasmon resonance measured binding kinetics of anti-TIGIT antibodies. The lead antibody exhibited sub-nanomolar affinity for human TIGIT.  

### Example 4: Humanization of the Murine Anti-Human TIGIT mAb  

The murine antibody was humanized by CDR grafting onto human framework regions. The humanized antibody retained high affinity binding to TIGIT.  

### Example 5: Binding Activities to Native TIGIT  

The humanized antibody bound effectively to native TIGIT expressed on cell surfaces.  

### Example 6: Blockade of TIGIT-Ligand Interactions  

The antibody effectively blocked TIGIT binding to both PVR and PVRL2 ligands.  

### Example 7: Activation of CMV-Specific Human T Cells  

The anti-TIGIT antibody enhanced interferon-gamma production by CMV-specific T cells.  

### Example 8: Enhanced NK Cell-Mediated Cytotoxicity  

The antibody augmented NK cell cytotoxicity against target cells expressing TIGIT ligands.  

### Example 9: Trogocytosis of TIGIT Receptor  

The antibody induced FcγR-mediated trogocytosis, reducing TIGIT surface expression on target cells.
