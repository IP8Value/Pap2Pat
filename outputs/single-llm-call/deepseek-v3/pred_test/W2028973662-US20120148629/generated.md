# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of nutritional compositions containing probiotics for modulating allergic responses. More specifically, the invention provides a complete nutritional composition comprising at least one Lactococcus lactis strain that demonstrates efficacy in secondary prevention of allergies by reducing allergic symptoms in sensitized individuals without affecting primary sensitization pathways.  

## BACKGROUND TO THE INVENTION  

Allergic diseases have shown a dramatic increase in prevalence over recent decades, particularly in pediatric populations. The development of allergies follows a progression known as the atopic march, wherein early sensitization to food allergens often precedes respiratory or contact allergies later in life. The immune system's development plays a critical role in this process, with Th2-skewed responses driving allergic sensitization through cytokines such as IL-4, IL-5, and IL-13.  

Prevention of allergies can be achieved at different levels, including primary prevention aimed at avoiding initial sensitization and secondary prevention focused on managing symptoms after sensitization has occurred. Current strategies for modulating sensitization and symptoms include dietary interventions, pharmacotherapy, and probiotic supplementation. Among food allergens, cow's milk, eggs, and wheat are common triggers, while respiratory allergens such as pollen further exacerbate the atopic march.  

Lactococci, particularly Lactococcus lactis strains, have shown promise in exerting anti-allergic effects through immune modulation. Probiotic cultures have been investigated for their ability to suppress skin allergies and reduce allergic reactions by influencing local immune responses in the gastrointestinal tract. However, existing solutions fail to address the nutritional needs of infants and other vulnerable populations while simultaneously providing clinically meaningful relief from allergy symptoms.  

There remains an unmet need for a complete nutritional composition that integrates specific probiotic strains capable of modulating allergic reactions without interfering with sensitization pathways. Such a composition must provide essential nutrients while delivering therapeutic benefits through carefully selected Lactococcus lactis strains that demonstrate targeted effects on secondary allergy prevention.  

## Prevention of Allergies can be Achieved on Different Levels:  

Primary prevention refers to interventions that prevent the initial development of allergic sensitization in at-risk individuals. Secondary prevention involves strategies to reduce symptoms in already sensitized patients by modulating immune responses to allergens. The present invention focuses on modulating sensitization through immunological pathways while simultaneously addressing symptom management in affected individuals.  

Food allergens such as cow's milk proteins and respiratory allergens like pollen trigger distinct immune responses that contribute to the atopic march. Lactococci, particularly Lactococcus lactis strains, have demonstrated anti-allergic effects in preclinical models by altering cytokine profiles associated with Th2 responses. Specific probiotic cultures have shown efficacy in suppressing skin allergy manifestations and reducing symptom severity in food allergy models.  

The nutritional needs of infants and other vulnerable groups require formulations that provide complete nutrition while addressing allergic symptoms. Complex matrices containing hydrolyzed proteins, prebiotics, and probiotics offer a multifaceted approach to modulating allergic reactions. The invention fulfills the need for a nutritional composition that delivers both essential nutrients and therapeutic benefits through selected Lactococcus lactis strains.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides a complete nutritional composition comprising at least one Lactococcus lactis strain selected for its ability to promote secondary prevention of allergies. The composition reduces symptoms of food and respiratory allergies without affecting primary sensitization pathways. Key components include L. lactis NCC 2287 (CNCM I-4154) and optionally L. lactis NCC 2180, combined with prebiotics such as fructooligosaccharides and inulin to create a synergistic effect.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The invention is directed to patients susceptible to or suffering from allergic conditions, including young patients with food allergies and individuals with airborne or contact allergies. A complete nutritional composition according to the invention provides all essential macro- and micronutrients while incorporating specific probiotic strains that modulate allergic responses.  

Symptoms of allergies include scratching, diarrhea, labored respiration, and anaphylaxis, mediated through biochemical pathways involving cytokines such as IL-13 and chemokines including CCL11 and CCL17. Primary prevention of allergies aims to prevent initial sensitization, whereas secondary prevention focuses on reducing symptoms in sensitized individuals. The weaning period represents a critical window for nutritional interventions targeting immune system development.  

Sensitization refers to the process by which the immune system develops Th2-skewed responses to allergens. The invention provides an immune boost through specific probiotic strains without altering sensitization pathways. A probiotic is defined as a live microorganism that confers health benefits when administered in adequate amounts, while a prebiotic comprises non-digestible food ingredients that stimulate beneficial gut bacteria.  

### Effect of the Composition:  

The nutritional composition exerts its effect specifically on secondary prevention of allergies by reducing symptoms of food allergy without influencing sensitization. Clinical manifestations of allergies, including scratching, diarrhea, and respiratory distress, are mediated through biochemical pathways involving mast cell activation and cytokine release. The composition demonstrates no effect on sensitization markers such as OVA-specific IgE but significantly influences immune responses by reducing IL-13 production in the ileum.  

Long-term administration of the composition shows sustained effects on reducing allergy symptoms while maintaining nutritional adequacy. The invention modulates allergic responses through localized effects in the gastrointestinal tract, particularly the ileum, where it reduces expression of Th2-associated chemokines CCL11 and CCL17. This targeted action distinguishes the invention from broad-spectrum immunomodulators that affect both sensitization and symptom pathways.  

### Composition of the Invention or for Use in the Invention:  

The complete nutritional composition comprises a probiotic strain of the Lactococcus genus, specifically L. lactis NCC 2287 (CNCM I-4154) deposited at the CNCM under accession number I-4154. An alternative strain, L. lactis NCC 2180 (SL60), may be included in combination or as a substitute. The probiotic is present in an amount between 1×10^6 to 1×10^10 CFU per serving, preferably 5×10^8 CFU/mL when administered in liquid formulations.  

The probiotic may be mixed with the nutritional composition in live form or subjected to treatments that improve stability, including microencapsulation or lyophilization. Inactivated probiotic preparations retain efficacy through immunomodulatory components present in bacterial cell walls. The composition further includes prebiotics selected from oligosaccharides, particularly fructooligosaccharide and inulin mixtures, present in amounts between 0.1-10% by weight.  

Apple extract may be incorporated to provide synergistic effects with the probiotic through polyphenol content. Protein components comprise hydrolyzed proteins from sources including cow's milk, soy, or rice, treated with proteases to reduce allergenicity while maintaining nutritional value. The complete nutritional profile includes carbohydrates, fats, vitamins, and minerals tailored to the target population's requirements.  

### Target Group:  

The primary target group comprises young patients with food allergies, particularly infants and children during the weaning period. The composition is formulated as infant formula, cereal, or baby food suitable for starter or follow-up feeding regimens. Secondary target populations include patients with airborne or contact allergies and non-human mammals exhibiting allergic conditions.  

Fragile, hospitalized, and elderly adults benefit from the nutritional and immunomodulatory properties of the composition. Formulations include adapted milk powders for children, yoghurt drinks for children and adults, and specialized foods for pets. The invention addresses nutritional and therapeutic needs across developmental stages from infancy through adulthood.  

## Experimental Results, Section 1:  

Probiotics were tested for effects on allergic sensitization using an in vitro system evaluating cytokine profiles in human PBMC. Th2-skewing was induced in PBMC cultures, with cytokine production measured by ELISA. L. lactis NCC 2287 demonstrated induction of IFNγ and IL-10 while inhibiting IL-5 secretion, confirmed at the mRNA level through quantitative PCR.  

In vivo evaluation utilized a mouse food allergy model sensitized with OVA. L. lactis NCC 2287 administration during the management phase reduced clinical symptoms significantly compared to controls (P=0.038). Serum MMCP-1 levels, a marker of mast cell activation, showed reduction trends in treated animals. Ex vivo restimulation of lymphocytes from treated mice revealed modulated immune responses characterized by decreased IL-13 production.  

## Experimental Results, Section 2:  

Evaluation of L. lactis NCC 2287 on respiratory allergies demonstrated consistent induction of IFNγ and IL-10 in PBMC cultures from allergic donors. The strain significantly inhibited IL-5 levels in cultures from grass pollen-allergic individuals compared to non-allergic controls (P<0.05). Comparative analysis confirmed the strain's beneficial effects extend to respiratory allergies through similar immunomodulatory mechanisms observed in food allergy models.  

## Analytical Methods:  

### Reagents and Bacterial Biomass:  
Bacterial biomass was produced by culturing L. lactis NCC 2287 under standard conditions, harvested during stationary phase, washed, and preserved in glycerol at -80°C.  

### Isolation and Culture of Th2-Skewed Human PBMC:  
PBMC were isolated from human donors and cultured under conditions inducing Th2 cytokine phenotypes. Cultures were supplemented with L. lactis NCC 2287 to evaluate immunomodulatory effects.  

### Cytokine ELISA:  
Cytokine levels including IL-4, IL-5, IL-10, IL-13, and IFNγ were quantified using standardized ELISA protocols with appropriate controls and calibration curves.  

### Evaluation of ELISA Results:  
Data were normalized to control conditions and analyzed using appropriate statistical methods including Bonferroni correction for multiple comparisons.  

### Quantitative Gene Expression Levels by Real-Time PCR:  
RNA was extracted from stimulated PBMC and analyzed using TaqMan assays for Th1/Th2 cytokine genes, normalized to housekeeping genes (GAPDH, β-actin).  

## OVA Food Allergy Mouse Model:  
BALB/c mice were sensitized with OVA and cholera toxin, followed by oral challenge. Nutritional intervention with L. lactis NCC 2287 was administered during prevention or management phases.  

### Serum MMCP-1:  
MMCP-1 levels were quantified in serum by ELISA as a marker of mast cell activation following allergic challenge.  

### Isolation and Culture of Mesenteric Lymph Node (MLN) Lymphocytes:  
MLN cells were isolated post-challenge and cultured with OVA to evaluate recall immune responses.  

### Cytokines in Supernatant of Mesenteric Lymph Node Cell and Splenocyte Cultures:  
Multiplex cytokine analysis was performed on culture supernatants to characterize Th1/Th2 balance.  

### Low Density Array Analyses of Gene Expression in Intestine:  
RNA from ileum and Peyer's patches was analyzed using custom arrays focusing on immune-related genes.
