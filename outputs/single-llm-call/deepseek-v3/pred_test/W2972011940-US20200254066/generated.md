## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The progressive decline in thymic function with age, known as thymic involution, contributes significantly to immunosenescence, increasing susceptibility to infections, cancer, autoimmune disorders, and systemic inflammation. Prior attempts to restore thymic function have relied on growth hormone (GH) administration, which has demonstrated thymotrophic effects in animal models and human immunodeficiency virus (HIV) patients. However, GH therapy presents substantial limitations, including its diabetogenic effects, which induce hyperinsulinemia and increase the risk of metabolic syndrome and type II diabetes.  

Prior art methods, such as those described by Fahy, have explored GH administration in combination with dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA) to mitigate insulin resistance. However, these approaches fail to address the increased cancer risk associated with elevated insulin-like growth factor-1 (IGF-1) levels, a downstream effector of GH. DHEA alone has been shown to improve glucose tolerance but does not counteract the oncogenic potential of GH-induced IGF-1 elevation. Furthermore, DHEA supplementation has been linked to prostate cancer risk, complicating its long-term use.  

A critical concern in thymus regeneration is the potential exacerbation of "inflammaging," a chronic low-grade inflammatory state associated with aging. GH administration may trigger cytokine storms or amplify existing inflammation, counteracting the intended immunorestorative benefits. Clinical trials have demonstrated that uncontrolled GH therapy can lead to adverse metabolic and proliferative effects, underscoring the need for a balanced therapeutic approach.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a novel pharmaceutical composition and method for reversing human aging by regenerating thymic function while minimizing adverse metabolic and oncogenic effects. The invention comprises a combination of GH (or a GH releaser), DHEA, and metformin, administered in a timed-release formulation to optimize immune reconstitution and metabolic stability.  

Key advantages of the invention include:  
- Restoration of youthful CD4/CD8 T-cell ratios, critical for immune competence.  
- Mitigation of GH-induced hyperinsulinemia through synergistic DHEA and metformin co-administration.  
- Prevention of age-related changes, including systemic inflammation, cancer risk, cardiovascular disease, and epigenetic aging.  

The pharmaceutical dosage form includes GH or a GH secretagogue, DHEA, and metformin in ratios that maintain insulin sensitivity while promoting thymic regeneration. The invention further addresses age-related biomarkers, such as CD38-positive monocyte reduction, IL-7 and IL-21 modulation, and improvements in bone marrow density and thymic fat-free fraction (TFFF).  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Pharmaceutical Composition and Administration  

The invention provides a unified pharmaceutical composition comprising:  
1. **GH or GH Releaser**: Administered at 0.015 mg/kg body weight, 3-4 times weekly, to stimulate IGF-1 production while avoiding supraphysiological levels. GH releasers include ghrelin mimetics, GHRH analogs, or synthetic secretagogues.  
2. **DHEA**: Dosed at 50 mg daily to counteract GH-induced insulin resistance and support adrenal function.  
3. **Metformin**: Administered at 500-2000 mg daily in divided or timed-release form to preserve glucose tolerance and mitigate hyperinsulinemia.  

### Method of Administration  

1. **Initial Phase**: GH monotherapy for one week to assess individual insulin response.  
2. **Adaptation Phase**: Introduction of DHEA (week 2) followed by metformin (week 3) to stabilize metabolic parameters.  
3. **Maintenance Phase**: Personalized dosing adjustments based on IGF-1 levels, CD4/CD8 ratios, and glycemic control.  

### Key Therapeutic Outcomes  

- **Thymic Regeneration**: MRI-confirmed increase in TFFF, correlating with naïve T-cell expansion.  
- **Epigenetic Age Reversal**: Reduction in GrimAge and Horvath clock measurements, indicating biological age regression.  
- **Metabolic Stability**: Controlled insulin and glucose levels despite GH administration.
