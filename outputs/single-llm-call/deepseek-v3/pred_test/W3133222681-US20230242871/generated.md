## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Primordial germ cells (PGCs) serve as the precursors to eggs and sperm in mammalian embryos, playing a critical role in reproduction and the transmission of genetic and epigenetic information across generations. While the developmental origins of mouse PGCs have been extensively characterized, the precise origins and specification mechanisms of human PGCs remain poorly understood due to the inaccessibility of early human embryos during weeks 2-3 of embryogenesis when PGCs first emerge.  

Previous studies have proposed competing models for human PGC specification, with some suggesting they originate from posterior epiblast or primitive streak/mesoderm precursors, while others propose an independent lineage from nearby precursors or even dorsal amnion origins based on studies in non-human primates. Current methods for generating human PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) from pluripotent stem cells (PSCs) involve complex 3D culture systems with prolonged exposure to various signaling factors, presenting challenges in reproducibility, scalability, and efficiency.  

There remains an unmet need in the art for a robust, simplified in vitro platform that can efficiently generate human PGCLCs while providing insights into the molecular mechanisms governing human germline specification. The present invention addresses these limitations by providing a defined monolayer culture system that enables rapid, efficient production of human PGCLCs with high purity through temporal modulation of key signaling pathways.  

## BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides methods for forming primordial germ cells (PGCs) from pluripotent stem cells in vitro. The methods involve contacting a population of pluripotent stem cells with a WNT agonist and TGFβ agonist for a defined period to generate posterior epiblast cells, followed by removal of these agonists and addition of a WNT inhibitor to promote PGC specification. The resulting PGCs can be isolated using a unique cell surface marker profile of CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP-.  

The invention further provides methods for isolating PGCs from heterogeneous cell populations using fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) based on the CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP- signature. Additionally, the invention encompasses methods for treating infertility by administering the in vitro-derived PGCs to patients in need thereof. Various aspects of the PGCs are described, including their transcriptional profile, protein expression patterns, and functional characteristics that resemble native human fetal PGCs.

### Methods  

The invention provides a simplified 2D in vitro platform for generating human PGCs from pluripotent stem cells. This platform serves as a valuable research tool for investigating key lineage intermediates and extracellular signals involved in PGC specification, while also enabling applications in drug discovery, disease modeling, and infertility treatments.  

The method involves first forming posterior epiblast cells by contacting a pluripotent stem cell population with a WNT agonist (e.g., CHIR99021 at 3 μM) and TGFβ agonist (e.g., Activin-A at 100 ng/mL) for approximately 12 hours in serum-free medium. This brief exposure generates an intermediate posterior epiblast population that co-expresses pluripotency factors (OCT4, NANOG) and primitive streak markers (MIXL1, BRACHYURY).  

Following posterior epiblast formation, the WNT and TGFβ agonists are removed and replaced with a WNT inhibitor (e.g., XAV939 at 1 μM) along with BMP4 (40 ng/mL), SCF (100 ng/mL), and EGF (50 ng/mL) to promote PGC specification. The entire differentiation process is completed within 3.5 days in monolayer culture, yielding a population containing 20-30% PGCs that can be further purified using cell surface markers.  

The PGCs are isolated using fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) based on their unique surface marker profile of CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP-. This signature distinguishes PGCs from both undifferentiated pluripotent stem cells and other differentiated cell types in the culture. The purified PGCs express characteristic markers including NANOS3, TFAP2C, PRDM1, and SOX17 while lacking expression of somatic lineage markers.  

The methods further encompass variations in culture conditions, including different concentrations of signaling modulators (e.g., BMP4 from 10-100 ng/mL, XAV939 from 0.5-5 μM), alternative culture media formulations, and modifications to the temporal sequence of signaling pathway activation and inhibition. The platform has been demonstrated to work across multiple human embryonic stem cell (hESC) and induced pluripotent stem cell (iPSC) lines with consistent efficiency.  

### Cell Compositions  

The invention provides cell culture compositions comprising WNT-activated posterior epiblast cells and purified CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP- PGC populations. These compositions are maintained in defined serum-free media under controlled culture conditions (37°C, 5% CO2) on extracellular matrix-coated surfaces.  

The posterior epiblast cell composition is characterized by co-expression of pluripotency factors (OCT4, NANOG) and primitive streak markers (MIXL1, BRACHYURY) following 12-hour exposure to WNT and TGFβ agonists. The PGC composition demonstrates high purity (>95%) when isolated using the CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP- signature and exhibits transcriptional profiles closely resembling native human fetal PGCs.  

### METHODS OF USE  

The PGCs generated by the disclosed methods have multiple applications, including but not limited to: studying human germline development, modeling genetic diseases affecting fertility, screening compounds for effects on germ cell formation and function, and developing cell-based therapies for infertility. The purified PGC populations serve as valuable tools for investigating the molecular mechanisms of human germline specification and the effects of environmental factors on germ cell development.  

### EXAMPLES  

The following examples illustrate specific embodiments of the invention:  

Example 1 describes the optimized monolayer differentiation protocol using H1 hESCs. Cells were treated with 100 ng/mL Activin-A, 3 μM CHIR99021, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 12 hours to generate posterior epiblast intermediates, followed by culture with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours, then 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for an additional 24 hours, culminating in final culture with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours. This protocol yielded approximately 30% NANOS3+ PGCs within 3.5 days.  

Example 2 demonstrates the discovery and validation of the CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP- surface marker signature through high-throughput screening of 369 cell surface markers. FACS purification using this signature enabled isolation of >95% pure PGC populations from differentiated cultures across multiple hESC and iPSC lines.  

Example 3 presents single-cell RNA-sequencing analysis comparing in vitro-derived PGCs to human fetal PGCs, showing strong transcriptional similarity between the two populations. The in vitro PGCs most closely resembled early-stage fetal germ cells (FGC1) in their expression of pluripotency factors (OCT4, NANOG) and germline markers (NANOS3, TFAP2C).  

## EMBODIMENTS  

The invention encompasses various embodiments including but not limited to:  

1. A method for forming primordial germ cells (PGCs) comprising:  
   a) contacting a pluripotent stem cell population with a WNT agonist and TGFβ agonist for about 12 hours to form posterior epiblast cells;  
   b) removing the WNT agonist and TGFβ agonist;  
   c) contacting the posterior epiblast cells with a WNT inhibitor; and  
   d) culturing the cells under conditions suitable for PGC formation.

5. The method of any preceding embodiment, wherein the WNT inhibitor is XAV939 at a concentration of about 1 μM.  

6. The method of any preceding embodiment, further comprising contacting the cells with BMP4 at about 40 ng/mL, SCF at about 100 ng/mL, and EGF at about 50 ng/mL during PGC formation.  

7. The method of any preceding embodiment, wherein the PGCs are formed within about 3.5 days of differentiation.  

8. A composition comprising isolated human primordial germ cells characterized by CXCR4+/PDGFRα-/GARP- cell surface marker expression.  

9. The composition of embodiment 8, wherein the PGCs express NANOS3, TFAP2C, PRDM1, and SOX17.  

10. A method of treating infertility comprising administering to a patient in need thereof an effective amount of PGCs produced by the method of any of embodiments 1-7.
