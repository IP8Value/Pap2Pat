# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to polypeptides comprising modified Fc regions that exhibit enhanced binding affinity to the inhibitory Fcγ receptor IIb (FcγRIIb) while maintaining or decreasing binding to activating Fcγ receptors, particularly FcγRIIa. The invention further relates to methods of producing such polypeptides, pharmaceutical compositions containing them, and their use in therapeutic applications for treating immunological inflammatory diseases, autoimmune disorders, and other conditions where selective engagement of FcγRIIb provides therapeutic benefit.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Antibodies have emerged as important pharmaceutical agents for treating various diseases. The stability and effector functions of antibodies are largely mediated through interactions between the Fc region and Fc gamma receptors (FcγRs). The FcγR protein family includes activating receptors (FcγRIa, FcγRIIa, FcγRIIIa) and the inhibitory receptor FcγRIIb. The hinge region and CH2 domain of antibodies play critical roles in FcγR binding, particularly through interactions modulated by the sugar chain attached to Asn at position 297.  

FcγRIIb is uniquely expressed on B cells and functions as an immunomodulatory receptor by suppressing B cell activation through phosphorylation of immunoreceptor tyrosine-based inhibition motifs (ITIMs) and recruitment of SHIP phosphatase. This suppresses primary immune responses and inflammatory cascades. FcγRIIb is also expressed on dendritic cells, macrophages, and other immune cells where it inhibits activating FcγR functions. Deficiencies in FcγRIIb are associated with various human autoimmune diseases.  

Recent developments have engineered Fc variants with enhanced FcγRIIb binding for therapeutic applications. However, current variants often simultaneously increase binding to FcγRIIa, particularly the R131 allotype, which poses risks of thromboembolism and rapid clearance. There remains an unmet need for Fc variants that selectively enhance FcγRIIb binding while maintaining or decreasing binding to FcγRIIa.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

### Problems to be Solved by the Invention  

Current Fc variants that enhance FcγRIIb binding suffer from several limitations. Many variants simultaneously increase binding to FcγRIIa, particularly the R131 allotype, which may lead to thromboembolic complications and rapid clearance from plasma. Existing variants also lack sufficient selectivity between FcγRIIb and FcγRIIa due to their high structural homology. There remains a need for Fc variants that provide:  
1) Enhanced binding to FcγRIIb  
2) Maintained or decreased binding to FcγRIIa (both R131 and H131 allotypes)  
3) Preserved pharmacokinetic properties  
4) Suitable stability for pharmaceutical development  

### Means for Solving the Problems  

The invention provides polypeptide variants comprising altered Fc regions with:  
1) Enhanced FcγRIIb-binding activity (KD ratio of FcγRIIb:FcγRIIa ≤0.5)  
2) Maintained or decreased FcγRIIa-binding activity  
3) Maintained or decreased FcγRIa and FcγRIIIa-binding activity  

Specific amino acid alterations include substitutions at positions 233, 237, 238, 268, 271, 296, and 330 (EU numbering), particularly:  
- P238D  
- E233D  
- G237D  
- H268D  
- P271G  
- Y296D  
- A330R  

The polypeptides may be IgG antibodies (preferably IgG1 or IgG4) or Fc fusion proteins. Methods for producing these polypeptides include:  
1) Introducing specific amino acid substitutions into the Fc region  
2) Screening variants for enhanced FcγRIIb binding and selectivity  
3) Combining multiple alterations to optimize properties  

The invention further provides:  
1) Pharmaceutical compositions containing the polypeptides  
2) Therapeutic agents for immunological inflammatory diseases  
3) Agents for treating autoimmune disorders  
4) Antiviral agents  
5) Kits for therapeutic use  

### Effects of the Invention  

The Fc variants of the invention provide:  
1) Selective enhancement of FcγRIIb binding (>200-fold increase)  
2) Maintained or decreased binding to FcγRIIa (avoiding thromboembolic risks)  
3) Preserved pharmacokinetics and stability  
4) Enhanced therapeutic efficacy in agonistic antibody applications  
5) Reduced risk of platelet activation and rapid clearance  

## MODE FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION  

The invention provides polypeptides with modified Fc regions that exhibit enhanced binding to FcγRIIb. Fcγ receptors include FcγRI (CD64), FcγRIIA (CD32A), FcγRIIB (CD32B), FcγRIIIA (CD16A), and FcγRIIIB (CD16B). The FcγRIIA allotypes include R131 and H131 variants.  

Parent polypeptides include naturally-occurring IgGs (IgG1, IgG2, IgG3, IgG4). The Fc region spans residues 216-446 (EU numbering). Binding activity is determined by surface plasmon resonance (SPR) using a 1:1 binding model to calculate KD values.  

Polypeptides with decreased FcγR-binding activity exhibit:  
- KD ratio (variant:parent) ≥1.5 for FcγRIIa  
- KD value ≥1×10^-6 M for FcγRIIa  

Polypeptides with enhanced FcγRIIb-binding activity exhibit:  
- KD ratio (parent:variant) ≥2 for FcγRIIb  
- KD value ≤1×10^-7 M for FcγRIIb  

Preferred embodiments combine multiple alterations, such as:  
1) P238D + E233D + G237D + H268D + P271G + A330R (V12 variant)  
2) P238D + E233D + P271G + A330R  
3) P238D + G237D + H268D + P271G  

Additional alterations may improve:  
1) FcRn binding (e.g., M252Y, S254T, T256E)  
2) Stability (e.g., S239D, I332E)  
3) Heterogeneity (e.g., N297A, N297Q)  

### <Substitution>  

Amino acid substitutions include conservative (e.g., hydrophobic to hydrophobic) and non-conservative changes (e.g., hydrophobic to charged). Post-translational modifications include glycosylation at Asn297 in eukaryotic cells or non-glycosylated forms in prokaryotic cells.  

### <Antibody>  

Antibodies include monoclonal, polyclonal, humanized, chimeric, and phage-derived forms. Variable regions may be modified while maintaining antigen binding. Constant regions include human IgG1 and IgG4 heavy chains and κ or λ light chains.  

Fc fusion proteins combine the Fc region with therapeutic proteins (e.g., cytokines, receptors). Bispecific antibodies engage two distinct antigens.  

Polypeptides are produced by:  
1) Cloning heavy and light chain genes into expression vectors  
2) Transfecting host cells (e.g., CHO, HEK293)  
3) Purifying antibodies from culture supernatants  

### <Pharmaceutical Compositions>  

Compositions contain:  
1) Polypeptides (0.1-100 mg/mL)  
2) Pharmaceutically acceptable carriers (e.g., saline, buffers)  
3) Excipients (e.g., surfactants, stabilizers)  

Administration routes include intravenous, subcutaneous, and intramuscular injection. Dosages range from 0.1-20 mg/kg depending on the condition.  

Therapeutic applications include:  
1) Suppressing B cell activation and autoantibody production  
2) Inhibiting mast cell and basophil degranulation  
3) Treating autoimmune diseases (e.g., lupus, rheumatoid arthritis)  
4) Preventing antibody responses to therapeutic proteins  
5) Antiviral therapy by suppressing antibody-dependent enhancement  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

A comprehensive mutagenesis screen evaluated over 500 Fc variants for binding to FcγRIIa (R131 and H131) and FcγRIIb. Only P238D and L328E substitutions enhanced FcγRIIb binding while decreasing binding to both FcγRIIa allotypes. P238D showed superior selectivity, with a 217-fold increase in FcγRIIb affinity and reduced binding to activating FcγRs.  

### Example 2  

SPR analysis of Fc variants demonstrated that P238D exhibited:  
- KD(FcγRIIb) = 2.1×10^-8 M (217-fold improvement vs IgG1)  
- KD(FcγRIIaR131) = 1.4×10^-6 M (no enhancement)  
- KD(FcγRIIaH131) = undetectable binding  

### Example 3  

Combining P238D with additional substitutions (E233D, G237D, H268D, P271G, A330R) created the V12 variant with:  
- KD(FcγRIIb) = 6.2×10^-9 M (62-fold improvement vs P238D)  
- Maintained selectivity over FcγRIIa  

### Example 4  

X-ray crystallography of Fc(P238D)/FcγRIIb revealed:  
1) Conformational changes in the CH2 domain loops  
2) Novel hydrogen bonds between Gly237 and FcγRIIb Tyr160  
3) Salt bridge between Asp270 and FcγRIIb Arg131  

These interactions explain the selectivity for FcγRIIb over FcγRIIa.  

### Example 5  

Pharmacokinetic studies in hFcRn transgenic mice showed V12 had:  
- Comparable FcRn binding to IgG1 (KD = 1.4 μM at pH6.0)  
- Similar half-life (2.6 days vs 2.8 days for IgG1)  

### Example 6  

Platelet activation assays demonstrated:  
1) S267E/L328F variant activated platelets from R/R131 donors  
2) V12 variant showed no platelet activation  
This confirms the safety advantage of selective FcγRIIb binding.  

### Example 7  

The V12 variant enhanced agonistic activity of anti-CD137 antibody, increasing IFN-γ production by >5-fold compared to IgG1. This demonstrates therapeutic utility in cancer immunotherapy.  

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  

The Fc variants of the invention have broad therapeutic applications, including:  
1) Autoimmune disease treatment  
2) Cancer immunotherapy  
3) Prevention of anti-drug antibodies  
4) Inflammatory disorder management  

The selective FcγRIIb engagement provides efficacy while minimizing risks associated with FcγRIIa activation.
