# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to engineered antibody Fc variants with selectively enhanced binding affinity to the inhibitory Fcγ receptor IIb (FcγRIIb) relative to activating Fcγ receptors, including FcγRIIa allotypes. More specifically, the invention provides novel amino acid substitutions in the Fc region of human IgG1 that significantly improve binding selectivity for FcγRIIb while minimizing binding to FcγRIIaR131 and FcγRIIaH131. The engineered antibodies are useful for therapeutic applications requiring enhanced FcγRIIb engagement, such as agonistic activity against tumor necrosis factor receptor (TNFR) superfamily members, suppression of B-cell activation, and modulation of dendritic cell function.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

### PRIOR ART DOCUMENTS  

Previous efforts to engineer antibody Fc regions have primarily focused on enhancing binding to activating Fcγ receptors (e.g., FcγRI, FcγRIIa, FcγRIIIa) to improve antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) for cancer immunotherapy. For example, substitutions such as S298A/E333A/K334A (Stavenhagen et al., 2007) and glycoengineering to reduce fucosylation (Mossner et al., 2010) have been employed to increase FcγRIIIa binding.  

In contrast, engineering efforts targeting the inhibitory FcγRIIb have been limited. The S267E/L328F variant (Chu et al., 2008) was reported to enhance FcγRIIb binding by 355-fold but also increased binding to FcγRIIaR131 to a similar extent, raising concerns about platelet activation and thromboembolic risks (Smith et al., 2012). Other variants, such as P238D and L328E, were identified but provided only modest improvements in selectivity.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

### Problems to be Solved by the Invention  

Existing Fc-engineered antibodies lack sufficient selectivity for FcγRIIb over FcγRIIaR131, which may lead to adverse effects such as platelet activation and rapid clearance in vivo. There is a need for Fc variants that exclusively enhance FcγRIIb binding without increasing affinity for activating FcγRs, particularly FcγRIIaR131.  

### Means for Solving the Problems  

The invention provides novel Fc variants, including:  
1. The **P238D substitution**, which selectively enhances FcγRIIb binding while reducing affinity for FcγRIIaR131 and FcγRIIaH131.  
2. The **V12 variant (E233D/G237D/H268D/P271G/Y296D/A330R on a P238D backbone)**, which increases FcγRIIb affinity by 217-fold without increasing binding to FcγRIIa allotypes.  
3. Structural insights into the FcγRIIb binding mechanism, revealing novel interactions (e.g., Gly237-Tyr160 hydrogen bond) that confer selectivity.  

### Effects of the Invention  

The engineered antibodies exhibit:  
- **Enhanced therapeutic activity**: Improved agonism for TNFR superfamily antibodies (e.g., anti-CD137).  
- **Reduced side effects**: No platelet activation or aggregation due to minimal FcγRIIa binding.  
- **Favorable pharmacokinetics**: Comparable half-life to wild-type IgG1 due to preserved FcRn binding.  
- **Stability**: Maintained structural integrity at high concentrations (100 mg/mL).  

## MODE FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION  

### Substitution  

The P238D substitution disrupts the hydrophobic core of the CH2 domain, inducing conformational changes that enable selective FcγRIIb engagement. Key interactions include:  
- A hydrogen bond between Gly237 (CH2-A) and Tyr160 (FcγRIIb).  
- A salt bridge between Asp270 (CH2-B) and Arg131 (FcγRIIb).  

### Antibody  

The antibodies comprise a human IgG1 Fc region with the following substitutions:  
- **P238D** (baseline selectivity).  
- **V12 variant (E233D/G237D/H268D/P271G/Y296D/A330R)** for enhanced affinity.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions  

Formulations may include:  
- **Antibodies at 10–200 mg/mL** in histidine buffer (pH 6.0).  
- **Stabilizers** (e.g., sucrose, polysorbate 80) to prevent aggregation.  

### Asparagine: Asn (N)  

The N-linked glycosylation site at Asn297 is preserved to maintain FcγR binding and effector functions.  

### Glutamine: Gln (O)  

No modifications to Gln residues are required for FcγRIIb selectivity.  

### Glycine: Gly (G)  

The G237D substitution in the V12 variant introduces a critical hydrogen bond with FcγRIIb Tyr160.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Screening of Fc Variants  

A library of 500+ Fc variants was screened using surface plasmon resonance (SPR). P238D and L328E were identified as the only substitutions that enhanced FcγRIIb binding while reducing affinity for FcγRIIa allotypes.  

### Example 2: Structural Analysis of P238D  

X-ray crystallography (2.6 Å resolution) revealed asymmetric binding of Fc(P238D) to FcγRIIb, with novel interactions at the CH2-A (Gly237-Tyr160) and CH2-B (Asp270-Arg131) interfaces.  

### Example 3: Development of the V12 Variant  

Combinatorial mutagenesis on the P238D backbone yielded the V12 variant, which showed a 217-fold increase in FcγRIIb affinity (KD = 0.8 nM) without enhancing FcγRIIa binding.  

### Example 4: Pharmacokinetic Studies  

In hFcRn transgenic mice, the V12 variant exhibited a half-life (2.6 days) comparable to wild-type IgG1 (2.8 days), confirming preserved FcRn binding.  

### Example 5: Platelet Aggregation Assay  

Preformed immune complexes (ICs) with the V12 variant did not activate platelets from FcγRIIaR131 or FcγRIIaH131 donors, unlike the S267E/L328F variant.  

### Example 6: Agonistic Activity Enhancement  

The V12 variant increased IFN-γ production by anti-CD137 antibody (clone 1D8) by 5-fold in a co-culture of CTLL-2 and Raji cells.  

### Example 7: Stability Assessment  

After 4 weeks at 25°C, the V12 variant showed <0.5% high-molecular-weight (HMW) species formation, similar to wild-type IgG1.  

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  

The engineered antibodies are applicable to:  
1. **Cancer immunotherapy**: Enhancing agonistic activity of anti-TNFR antibodies (e.g., anti-CD40, anti-DR5).  
2. **Autoimmune diseases**: Suppressing B-cell activation via FcγRIIb-BCR coengagement.  
3. **Allergic disorders**: Inhibiting IgE-mediated mast cell degranulation.  

The V12 variant’s selectivity and stability make it suitable for large-scale pharmaceutical production and clinical development.
