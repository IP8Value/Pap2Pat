# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND  

The field of regenerative medicine has long sought improved methods for culturing human epidermal keratinocytes (HEKs) for therapeutic applications. Current standard techniques rely on co-culture with murine 3T3-J2 fibroblast feeder layers, a method originally developed by Rheinwald and Green. This approach suffers from significant limitations including exposure to zoonotic pathogens, immunogenic agents, and undefined culture components classified as high-risk ancillary materials by regulatory agencies.  

Prior attempts to develop defined culture systems have proven inadequate, often still requiring initial expansion on feeder layers or utilizing undefined supplements like bovine pituitary extract. The scientific literature describes unsuccessful efforts using conditioned media, human feeder cells, protein coatings, or combinations of extracellular matrix components. These methods fail to provide a truly defined, xeno-free system capable of supporting long-term keratinocyte expansion while maintaining progenitor characteristics.  

A critical unmet need exists for a chemically defined, animal-free culture system that can reliably expand functional keratinocytes without compromising cell quality or introducing regulatory concerns. Such a system would enable safer and more widely applicable epithelial stem cell therapies for conditions ranging from severe burns to genetic skin disorders.  

## BRIEF DESCRIPTION  

The present invention provides a breakthrough solution to these longstanding challenges through the discovery that specific laminin isoforms can effectively support human epidermal keratinocyte culture without feeder layers. The invention encompasses compositions and methods utilizing laminin-511 (LN-511) or laminin-421 (LN-421) as substrate coatings in defined culture media to propagate functional keratinocytes.  

This fully human, chemically defined system demonstrates equivalent or superior performance compared to traditional 3T3 co-culture methods across multiple parameters including:  
- Population doubling rates  
- Colony-forming efficiency  
- Maintenance of progenitor markers  
- Genetic stability  
- Capacity for epidermal stratification  
- In vivo graft functionality  

The invention further includes therapeutic applications using keratinocytes expanded according to the disclosed methods, as well as kits containing the essential components for practicing the invention.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

The present invention provides a paradigm shift in human epidermal keratinocyte culture technology by identifying specific laminin isoforms that can replace murine feeder layers while maintaining all critical cellular functions. The detailed embodiments below describe the composition and implementation of this innovative system.  

**Laminin Substrates**  
The culture system utilizes purified human laminin-511 (α5β1γ1 trimer) or laminin-421 (α4β2γ1 trimer) as substrate coatings. These laminins are applied to tissue culture surfaces at concentrations between 0.1-10 μg/cm², with optimal performance observed at 2.5 μg/cm². The laminins may be derived from recombinant production or purified from human tissues, with recombinant forms preferred for consistency and regulatory compliance.  

**Culture Media**  
Keratinocytes are maintained in chemically defined media such as Keratinocyte Growth Medium-Chemically Defined (KGM-CD). The media contains no animal-derived components, cholera toxin, or other high-risk ancillary materials. Essential components include:  
- Basal medium (e.g., DMEM/F12)  
- Defined growth factors (EGF, insulin)  
- Trace elements and lipids  
- Buffering systems  

**Cell Culture Methods**  
Primary keratinocytes are isolated from human skin samples using enzymatic digestion (dispase followed by trypsin) and directly plated on laminin-coated surfaces without prior expansion on feeder layers. Cells are maintained at 37°C in humidified incubators with 5-10% CO₂. Passaging is performed using enzyme-free dissociation reagents to preserve surface receptors.  

The system supports long-term culture (≥10 passages) while maintaining:  
- Typical keratinocyte morphology  
- Expression of basal markers (KRT5, KRT14, p63)  
- Normal karyotype  
- High colony-forming efficiency  
- Capacity for terminal differentiation  

**Quality Control Parameters**  
The invention includes defined quality metrics for cultured keratinocytes:  
- ≥90% positivity for basal progenitor markers by flow cytometry  
- Population doubling time ≤48 hours  
- Colony-forming efficiency ≥20%  
- Normal stratified epidermis formation in organotypic culture  
- Successful engraftment in animal models  

### EXAMPLES  

**Example 1: Primary Keratinocyte Isolation and Culture**  
Human epidermal keratinocytes were isolated from surgical skin samples using dispase/trypsin digestion. Cells were directly plated on LN-511 or LN-421 coated plates (2.5 μg/cm²) in KGM-CD medium at 9×10⁴ cells/cm². Cultures reached confluence within 5-7 days and were passaged at 1×10⁴ cells/cm². Population doublings were calculated at each passage, demonstrating equivalent growth kinetics to 3T3 co-culture controls.  

**Example 2: Phenotypic Characterization**  
Flow cytometry analysis of passage 3 keratinocytes cultured on laminins showed:  
- 92.3% ± 3.1% KRT14+ cells (LN-511)  
- 90.8% ± 2.7% KRT14+ cells (LN-421)  
- 94.1% ± 2.9% p63+ cells (both conditions)  
These results confirm maintenance of progenitor phenotype comparable to feeder layer cultures.  

**Example 3: Organotypic Culture**  
Keratinocytes expanded on laminin substrates formed fully stratified epidermis when seeded on de-epidermalized dermis and raised to air-liquid interface. Histology revealed normal stratification with:  
- Continuous basal layer  
- Distinct spinous and granular layers  
- Compact stratum corneum  
Immunostaining confirmed appropriate expression of differentiation markers (KRT1, KRT10) in upper layers.  

**Example 4: In Vivo Grafting**  
Flap transplantation in nude mice demonstrated functional engraftment of laminin-cultured keratinocytes. Grafts showed:  
- Human-specific Ku80+ epidermis at 14 days  
- Normal thickness and stratification  
- Continuous laminin-332 deposition at dermal-epidermal junction  
- Appropriate expression of differentiation markers  

## SUMMARY  

The present invention provides the first fully defined, xeno-free system for human epidermal keratinocyte culture that matches or exceeds the performance of traditional feeder layer methods. Key advantages include:  

1. Elimination of high-risk ancillary materials (murine feeders, bovine serum, cholera toxin)  
2. Consistent, reproducible culture conditions  
3. Maintenance of progenitor characteristics through multiple passages  
4. Normal differentiation capacity in vitro and in vivo  
5. Compliance with current good manufacturing practices  

This breakthrough enables safer, more widely applicable cell therapies for:  
- Severe burns  
- Genetic skin disorders (e.g., epidermolysis bullosa)  
- Chronic wounds  
- Cosmetic and reconstructive applications  

The invention represents a fundamental advancement in epithelial cell biology with far-reaching implications for regenerative medicine.
