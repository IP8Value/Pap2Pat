# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of peptide therapeutics and specifically to novel peptide-oligourea hybrid compounds that exhibit enhanced stability and prolonged pharmacological activity. More particularly, the invention concerns glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) analogues incorporating oligourea segments that demonstrate improved resistance to proteolytic degradation while maintaining receptor binding affinity. The invention further encompasses pharmaceutical compositions containing these hybrid compounds and methods for treating metabolic disorders including type 2 diabetes mellitus, obesity, and related conditions using these compounds.  

## BACKGROUND  

Peptide therapeutics have emerged as important pharmaceutical agents due to their high specificity and potency in targeting various biological pathways. However, native peptides suffer from significant limitations including poor membrane permeability, rapid proteolytic degradation, and short in vivo half-lives, which restrict their therapeutic utility. GLP-1, an important incretin hormone, exemplifies these challenges as it has a very short half-life of only 2-3 minutes in vivo due to rapid cleavage by proteases such as dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) and neutral endopeptidase 24.11 (NEP 24.11).  

Various strategies have been employed to improve the pharmacokinetic properties of GLP-1 analogues, including sequence modifications, conjugation to albumin-binding molecules, fusion to large proteins, side chain cross-linking, and residue substitutions. While these approaches have yielded clinically useful GLP-1 receptor agonists, there remains a need for improved peptide therapeutics with enhanced stability and prolonged duration of action.  

Oligoureas represent a promising class of foldamers that can mimic peptide secondary structures while offering advantages such as protease resistance and metabolic stability. The oligourea backbone maintains a helical conformation compatible with peptide α-helices and can be interfaced with peptide segments without disrupting helix propagation. However, the potential of peptide-oligourea hybrids as therapeutic agents, particularly for GLP-1 receptor activation, has not been previously demonstrated.  

## SUMMARY  

The present invention provides novel peptide-oligourea hybrid compounds that address the limitations of native peptides by combining the biological activity of peptide sequences with the enhanced stability of oligourea segments. Specifically, the invention discloses GLP-1 analogues wherein segments of four consecutive amino acids are replaced by three ureido residues (oligourea triads), creating hybrid molecules with preserved receptor binding affinity and improved proteolytic resistance.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  

1. GLP-1-oligourea hybrid compounds comprising at least one oligourea triad replacement of four consecutive amino acids in the GLP-1 sequence, particularly in the helical region spanning residues 9-31.  

2. Specific hybrid compounds demonstrating potent GLP-1 receptor agonist activity, including GG2[YuEuAu]14-17 (Compound 9) which shows equal or greater potency than native GLP-1.  

3. Hybrid compounds exhibiting significantly prolonged in vivo activity, particularly GG2[AuAuAu]18-21 (Compound 14), GG2[AuAuAu]28-31 (Compound 22), and GG2[YuEuAuAuAuAu]14-21 (Compound 23), which maintain glucose-lowering effects for up to 6 hours compared to 1 hour for native GLP-1.  

4. Hybrid compounds showing enhanced stability in proteolytic degradation assays, with some analogues demonstrating >3.8-fold improvement in plasma half-life compared to native GLP-1.  

5. A protraction strategy combining the oligourea hybrid approach with fatty acid conjugation, exemplified by hybrid 24 (semaglutide-[AuAuAu]28-31), which shows improved stability for potential oral administration.  

The invention further provides pharmaceutical compositions containing these hybrid compounds and methods for treating metabolic disorders by administering these compounds to patients in need thereof. The improved pharmacokinetic properties of these hybrids enable less frequent dosing and potentially oral administration routes compared to existing peptide therapeutics.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

The present invention is based on the unexpected finding that strategic replacement of peptide segments with oligourea triads in GLP-1 can yield hybrid compounds that maintain receptor binding affinity while gaining substantial improvements in proteolytic stability and duration of pharmacological action. The detailed description provides specific embodiments and experimental data supporting these findings.  

### Design Principles  

The peptide-oligourea hybrids of the invention were designed based on structural modeling showing that three ureido residues (oligourea triad) can effectively mimic four consecutive amino acids in an α-helical conformation. This replacement maintains the overall helical structure while introducing protease-resistant linkages. Side chain positioning was optimized through systematic scanning with alanine-mimicking oligourea triads (Alau triads) followed by reintroduction of native side chains at critical interaction points.  

Key interaction residues identified for GLP-1 receptor binding (D9, Y13, E15, F22, and I23) were preserved or appropriately mimicked in the hybrid designs. The glycine substitution at position 2 (G2) was incorporated to prevent DPP-4 cleavage. Structural modeling guided the selection of side chain attachment points on the oligourea backbone to best mimic native peptide side chain projections.  

### Exemplary Compounds  

The invention encompasses numerous specific GLP-1-oligourea hybrid compounds, with particular embodiments including:  

1. GG2[DuαAuAu]9-12 (Compound 2): Incorporates an oligourea triad at positions 9-12 with a shifted aspartate mimic (Duα) to preserve the critical D9 interaction.  

2. GG2[YuEuAu]14-17 (Compound 9): Features a triad replacement at positions 14-17 with native-like tyrosine and glutamate side chains, demonstrating superior potency (EC50 = 0.18 nM).  

3. GG2[AuAuAu]18-21 (Compound 14): Contains a simple Alau triad at positions 18-21, showing excellent plasma stability (t1/2 = 38 min vs 16 min for native GLP-1) and prolonged in vivo activity.  

4. GG2[FuαIuAu]22-25 (Compound 16): Incorporates critical phenylalanine and isoleucine mimics at positions 22-23 with shifted side chain attachments.  

5. GG2[AuAuAu]28-31 (Compound 22): Demonstrates exceptional plasma stability (t1/2 >60 min) and sustained glucose-lowering effects up to 6 hours post-administration.  

6. GG2[YuEuAuAuAuAu]14-21 (Compound 23): Features two consecutive oligourea triads replacing eight amino acids (positions 14-21), maintaining good activity (EC50 = 3.4 nM) despite extensive modification.  

7. Semaglutide-[AuAuAu]28-31 (Compound 24): Combines the oligourea hybrid approach with fatty acid protraction, showing improved stability in pancreatin degradation assays (t1/2 = 3.2 h vs 0.63 h for semaglutide).  

### Biological Activity  

The hybrid compounds were evaluated in comprehensive biological assays:  

1. **In Vitro Potency**: cAMP production assays in βTC6 cells expressing human GLP-1R demonstrated that many hybrids maintained nanomolar potency, with Compound 9 (0.18 nM) exceeding native GLP-1-G2 (0.24 nM). Even extensively modified hybrids like Compound 23 (3.4 nM) retained significant activity.  

2. **Proteolytic Stability**: NEP 24.11 degradation assays showed that Compounds 22 (4.5 h) and 23 (6.1 h) had significantly improved stability versus native GLP-1-G2 (3.6 h). Mouse plasma stability assays revealed even greater improvements, with Compound 22 showing >60 min half-life versus 16 min for native peptide.  

3. **In Vivo Activity**: Intravenous administration in mice demonstrated prolonged glucose-lowering effects for Compounds 14, 22, and 23, maintaining activity for 4-6 hours versus 1 hour for native GLP-1. Intraperitoneal glucose tolerance tests (IPGTT) confirmed sustained activity up to 6 hours post-dosing for these hybrids.  

4. **Pharmacokinetics**: Compound 22 showed significantly prolonged plasma exposure compared to native GLP-1 after intravenous administration in mice, correlating with its enhanced proteolytic stability.  

5. **Protracted Hybrids**: Compound 24 (semaglutide-oligourea hybrid) maintained 48-hour glucose-lowering activity in db/db mice comparable to semaglutide while showing improved stability in pancreatin assays, suggesting potential for oral administration.  

### Pharmaceutical Applications  

The peptide-oligourea hybrids of the invention are useful for treating various metabolic disorders including:  

1. Type 2 diabetes mellitus - via sustained GLP-1 receptor activation enhancing glucose-dependent insulin secretion.  

2. Obesity - through GLP-1-mediated appetite suppression and weight loss effects.  

3. Non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH) - via metabolic improvements and potential direct hepatic effects.  

4. Cardiovascular diseases - through GLP-1's cardioprotective actions.  

5. Cognitive disorders - potentially benefiting from GLP-1's neuroprotective effects.  

The hybrids may be formulated for various administration routes including subcutaneous, intravenous, and potentially oral delivery. Pharmaceutical compositions may include appropriate carriers, excipients, and stabilizers suitable for peptide-based therapeutics.  

### Examples  

#### Example 1: Synthesis of GLP-1-Oligourea Hybrids  

Compounds were synthesized using solid-phase techniques on Sieber amide resin with microwave-assisted coupling. Fmoc-protected amino acids were coupled using PyBOP/DIEA, while oligourea building blocks were coupled using similar conditions. After chain assembly, compounds were cleaved from resin using TFA/triisopropylsilane/water (95:2.5:2.5) and purified by preparative HPLC.  

#### Example 2: In Vitro Potency Assay  

βTC6 cells expressing human GLP-1R were incubated with test compounds in HBSS/HEPES buffer with IBMX. After 10 min, cells were lysed and cAMP production was measured using HTRF detection with europium cryptate-labeled anti-cAMP antibody and D2-labeled cAMP. Concentration-response curves were generated to determine EC50 values.  

#### Example 3: Proteolytic Stability Assays  

1. **NEP 24.11 Assay**: Compounds (304 μM) were incubated with NEP 24.11 (20 μg/mL) in HEPES buffer at 20°C. Aliquots were taken at various times, quenched with TFA, and analyzed by HPLC to determine degradation half-lives.  

2. **Mouse Plasma Assay**: Compounds (5 μM) were incubated in mouse plasma/PBS (1:1) at 37°C. Samples were taken at intervals, precipitated with acetonitrile, and analyzed by LC-MS to determine stability.  

#### Example 4: In Vivo Pharmacodynamics  

1. **Blood Glucose Studies**: Normal mice received intravenous doses (5 μg/mouse) of test compounds. Blood glucose was monitored for 6 hours post-dosing in fed conditions.  

2. **IPGTT Studies**: Fasted mice were dosed intravenously (200 μg/kg) followed by intraperitoneal glucose challenge (2 g/kg) at various times (5 min to 4 h) post-dosing. Blood glucose was monitored for 2 hours post-challenge.  

3. **db/db Mouse Study**: Diabetic mice received single intraperitoneal doses (200 μg/kg) of protracted hybrids (e.g., Compound 24) with blood glucose monitored for 48 hours.  

#### Example 5: Pharmacokinetic Study  

Mice received intravenous doses (1 mg/kg) of test compounds. Blood samples were taken at various times, processed to plasma, and analyzed by LC-MS/MS to determine plasma concentration profiles and pharmacokinetic parameters.  

The examples demonstrate that strategic incorporation of oligourea triads into GLP-1 creates hybrid compounds with improved pharmaceutical properties while maintaining biological activity. These hybrids represent a significant advance in peptide therapeutic development with potential applications across multiple disease areas.
