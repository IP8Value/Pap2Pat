## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates generally to the field of immunology and vaccinology. More specifically, the invention pertains to novel glycoconjugate vaccines comprising Klebsiella pneumoniae (KP) O-polysaccharides (OPS) chemically conjugated to Pseudomonas aeruginosa (PA) flagellin proteins, methods of making such conjugates, and their use in preventing infections caused by KP and PA. The invention further relates to genetically engineered bacterial strains for producing the OPS components and recombinant expression systems for producing the flagellin carrier proteins.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Nosocomial infections caused by Gram-negative pathogens, particularly Klebsiella pneumoniae and Pseudomonas aeruginosa, represent a significant clinical burden worldwide. These organisms have been identified by both the CDC and WHO as urgent antimicrobial resistance (AMR) threats due to their ability to rapidly acquire resistance mechanisms against last-resort antibiotics. While vaccine development represents a promising strategy to circumvent AMR, no licensed vaccines currently exist for either pathogen.  

Prior attempts at vaccine development have faced several challenges. For KP, the extreme diversity of capsular polysaccharides (>80 known serotypes) makes broad coverage impractical. While O-polysaccharides show more limited serotype diversity (with types O1, O2, O3 and O5 covering >80% of clinical isolates), they are poorly immunogenic as standalone antigens. For PA, while flagellin proteins have shown promise as vaccine candidates, immunity is serotype-specific to either FlaA or FlaB variants, requiring coverage of both for broad protection.  

There exists an unmet need for a broadly protective vaccine that can simultaneously target both KP and PA while overcoming the limitations of poor immunogenicity and serotype-specific protection. The present invention addresses this need through novel glycoconjugate formulations that combine the advantages of KP OPS antigens with PA flagellin carrier proteins.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a novel four-component glycoconjugate vaccine comprising:  

1. Klebsiella pneumoniae O-polysaccharides (OPS) of serotypes O1, O2, O3 and O5;  
2. Pseudomonas aeruginosa flagellin proteins of types FlaA and FlaB;  

Wherein each OPS type is chemically conjugated to either FlaA or FlaB flagellin protein to form immunogenic glycoconjugates.  

The invention further provides:  
- Methods for producing the OPS components using genetically engineered KP strains  
- Recombinant expression systems for producing PA flagellin carrier proteins  
- Chemical conjugation methods for linking OPS to flagellin proteins  
- Pharmaceutical compositions containing the glycoconjugates  
- Methods of immunization using the glycoconjugate vaccines  
- Use of the vaccines for preventing KP and PA infections  

The glycoconjugate vaccine induces robust immune responses against both the KP OPS antigens and PA flagellin proteins, providing simultaneous protection against both pathogens. The vaccine is particularly valuable for preventing nosocomial infections in high-risk populations.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Conjugate  

The glycoconjugates of the invention comprise Klebsiella pneumoniae O-polysaccharides (OPS) chemically linked to Pseudomonas aeruginosa flagellin proteins. The OPS components are selected from serotypes O1, O2, O3 and O5, which collectively cover >80% of clinically relevant KP strains. Each OPS type is conjugated to either PA flagellin type FlaA or FlaB to form immunogenic sun-type neoglycoconjugates where the polysaccharide chain remains intact and extends from the protein surface.  

The conjugation employs thioether-aminooxy chemistry, wherein:  
1) The reducing end 2'5'-anhydromannose aldehyde of KP OPS is derivatized with O-(3-mercaptopropyl)-hydroxylamine;  
2) PA flagellin lysine residues are derivatized with N-γ-maleimidobutyryl-oxysuccinimide ester (GMBS);  
3) The thiolated OPS is reacted with maleimide-derivatized flagellin to form stable thioether linkages.  

This conjugation strategy preserves the immunogenic epitopes of both the polysaccharide and protein components while generating well-defined conjugate molecules suitable for vaccine use.  

### Compositions  

The invention provides pharmaceutical compositions containing the glycoconjugate vaccines. A preferred embodiment is a quadrivalent formulation containing four conjugates:  

1. KP O1 OPS conjugated to PA FlaB  
2. KP O2 OPS conjugated to PA FlaA  
3. KP O3 OPS conjugated to PA FlaB  
4. KP O5 OPS conjugated to PA FlaA  

The compositions may further comprise pharmaceutically acceptable excipients, adjuvants, or carriers. Suitable adjuvants include aluminum salts, oil-in-water emulsions, TLR agonists, or other immunostimulatory compounds known in the art.  

The compositions are formulated for parenteral administration, preferably intramuscular injection. Dosage forms may contain 1-25 μg of each polysaccharide component per dose, with the protein component present in approximately equal mass.  

### Methods of Treatment  

The invention provides methods for preventing or reducing the risk of Klebsiella pneumoniae and Pseudomonas aeruginosa infections in a subject, comprising administering an immunologically effective amount of the glycoconjugate vaccine.  

The vaccines are particularly indicated for:  
- Prevention of nosocomial infections in hospitalized patients  
- Protection of immunocompromised individuals  
- Prevention of ventilator-associated pneumonias  
- Prevention of surgical site infections  
- Prevention of burn wound infections  

Administration typically involves a prime-boost regimen with 2-4 doses given at 2-4 week intervals. The vaccines may be administered alone or concomitantly with other vaccines targeting nosocomial pathogens.  

### Methods of Making the Conjugate  

The invention provides methods for producing the glycoconjugate vaccines, comprising:  

1. Production of KP OPS:  
   - Culturing genetically engineered KP strains in fermentation bioreactors  
   - Releasing OPS by nitrous acid deamination  
   - Purifying OPS through tangential flow filtration and chromatographic steps  

2. Production of PA flagellin:  
   - Expressing recombinant FlaA and FlaB in E. coli fermentation cultures  
   - Purifying flagellin proteins using immobilized metal affinity chromatography  
   - Removing endotoxin by membrane chromatography  

3. Conjugation:  
   - Derivatizing OPS with aminooxy-thiol linker  
   - Derivatizing flagellin with maleimide  
   - Reacting the modified components to form conjugates  
   - Purifying conjugates by size exclusion chromatography  

The methods are scalable for commercial manufacturing and yield highly pure conjugate products suitable for human vaccination.  

### Genetically Engineered Strains  

The invention provides genetically engineered Klebsiella pneumoniae strains optimized for OPS production, comprising deletions of:  

1. guaBA genes (rendering the strains guanine auxotrophs)  
2. wzabc genes (eliminating capsule production)  

These modifications:  
- Attenuate the strains for enhanced safety  
- Simplify OPS purification by eliminating capsule contamination  
- Improve yield of OPS material  

Exemplary strains include CVD 3001 (O1:K2 ΔguaBA Δwzabc) and CVD 3002 (O5:K57 Δwzabc).  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Production and Characterization of KP OPS**  

KP reagent strains were grown in 4L fermentation cultures in chemically defined medium. OPS was released by nitrous acid deamination and purified through a series of filtration and chromatographic steps yielding:  

- O1 OPS: 98% pure, <1% protein, <0.01 EU/μg endotoxin  
- O2 OPS: 97% pure, <1% protein, <0.01 EU/μg endotoxin  
- O3 OPS: 96% pure, <1% protein, <0.01 EU/μg endotoxin  
- O5 OPS: 98% pure, <1% protein, <0.01 EU/μg endotoxin  

HPLC-SEC analysis showed each OPS preparation had a homogeneous molecular size distribution. Monosaccharide analysis confirmed O1 and O2 as polygalactans, while O3 and O5 contained mannose.  

**Production and Characterization of PA Flagellin**  

Recombinant FlaA and FlaB were expressed in E. coli BL21 and purified by Ni-affinity chromatography yielding:  

- FlaA: >95% pure, MW ~50 kDa, <0.1 EU/μg endotoxin  
- FlaB: >95% pure, MW ~50 kDa, <0.1 EU/μg endotoxin  

Both proteins showed strong TLR5 activation in HEK293-Luc reporter assays, confirming proper folding.  

**Conjugate Synthesis and Characterization**  

Each OPS type was conjugated to either FlaA or FlaB via thioether-aminooxy chemistry. Purified conjugates showed:  

- Defined peaks by HPLC-SEC  
- Polysaccharide:protein ratios of approximately 1:1  
- Complete ablation of TLR5 activity (for safety)  
- Stability at 4°C for >6 months  

The quadrivalent formulation (O1:FlaB + O2:FlaA + O3:FlaB + O5:FlaA) was prepared by mixing the individual conjugates in PBS.  

**Immunogenicity Studies**  

Mice immunized with monovalent O1:FlaA or O1:FlaB conjugates developed robust anti-O1 IgG responses with similar kinetics and endpoint titers. Flow cytometry confirmed antibodies bound encapsulated O1 strains regardless of capsule type.  

Rabbits immunized with the quadrivalent conjugate formulation developed high titers against all four OPS types and both flagellin proteins. In contrast, rabbits immunized with unconjugated admixed components showed negligible anti-OPS responses.  

**Protection Studies**  

Passive transfer of conjugate-induced rabbit sera:  
1) Promoted opsonophagocytic killing of KP in macrophage assays  
2) Reduced bacterial burden in blood, liver and spleen of KP-infected mice  
3) Protected against lethal KP challenge  
4) Protected against PA burn wound infection (for FlaB)  
5) Inhibited PA motility (for FlaB)  

These results demonstrate the glycoconjugate vaccine induces functional antibodies that protect against both KP and PA infections.
