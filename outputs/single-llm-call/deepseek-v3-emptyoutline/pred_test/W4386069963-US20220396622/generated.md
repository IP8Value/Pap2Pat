# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to engineered immunoglobulin G (IgG) antibodies with silenced effector functions while retaining antigen-binding capacity and favorable biophysical properties. More specifically, the invention pertains to modified IgG1 antibodies comprising specific mutations in the lower hinge and CH2 regions that eliminate binding to Fc gamma receptors (FcγRs) and complement component C1q, while preserving binding to neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) and Protein A (ProA). The invention further provides methods for producing these antibodies and their therapeutic applications in conditions where reduced effector function is desirable, such as T cell-engaging bispecific antibody therapies.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Therapeutic antibodies have become indispensable in modern medicine for treating various diseases, including cancer, autoimmune disorders, and infectious diseases. Their success stems from high specificity, strong target binding affinity, long serum half-life, and the ability to engage immune effector functions. However, these effector functions—mediated through interactions with FcγRs and complement—can lead to unintended adverse effects, including inflammation, tissue damage, and cytotoxicity.  

Effector functions such as antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) are triggered when the Fc region of an antibody binds to FcγRs or C1q, respectively. While these functions are beneficial in some therapeutic contexts, they can be detrimental in others, particularly with T cell-engaging bispecific antibodies, where effector function may cause off-target immune activation.  

Current strategies to reduce effector function include using IgG2 or IgG4 subclasses, which exhibit weaker FcγR and complement binding compared to IgG1. However, IgG2 and IgG4 antibodies suffer from biophysical instability, including increased sensitivity to acid-induced aggregation, which complicates manufacturing and purification.  

Previous attempts to engineer IgG1 antibodies with silenced effector functions have involved mutations in the lower hinge (e.g., L234A/L235A or "LALA") or CH2 domain (e.g., P329G). While these mutations reduce effector function, they often do not completely eliminate it under highly avid conditions, and some combinations negatively impact stability.  

Thus, there remains a need for IgG1 variants that completely abrogate effector function while maintaining FcRn and ProA binding, as well as WT-like biophysical properties suitable for large-scale manufacturing.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides modified IgG1 antibodies with silenced effector functions through specific mutations in the lower hinge and CH2 regions. Key embodiments include combinations such as:  
- L234A/L235A-P329G (LALA-PG)  
- L234A/L235A-P329A (LALA-PA)  
- L234A/L235E-P329G (LALE-PG)  
- L234A/L235A-S267K (LALA-SK)  

These variants exhibit the following advantageous properties:  
1. **Complete elimination of FcγR binding**, even under highly avid conditions, as demonstrated by THP-1 cell binding assays.  
2. **Abolished C1q binding**, preventing complement activation.  
3. **Retained FcRn binding**, ensuring normal serum half-life.  
4. **Preserved ProA binding**, enabling standard purification processes.  
5. **WT-like biophysical stability**, including resistance to acid-induced aggregation, as assessed by differential scanning fluorimetry (DSF) and pH 3.5 stress size-exclusion chromatography (SEC).  

The invention further encompasses pharmaceutical compositions comprising these antibodies, methods of producing them, and their use in treating diseases where reduced effector function is beneficial, such as cancers or autoimmune disorders.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Antibody Structure and Engineering Principles  

The IgG1 antibody comprises two heavy chains and two light chains, each containing variable (VH, VL) and constant (CH1-3, CL) domains. The Fc region, consisting of the CH2 and CH3 domains, mediates interactions with FcγRs, C1q, FcRn, and ProA.  

Key regions for effector function modulation include:  
- **Lower hinge (residues 234–237)**: Primary binding site for FcγRs. Mutations here (e.g., L234A/L235A) reduce but do not fully eliminate binding.  
- **CH2 domain (residues 265–331)**: Contains secondary interaction sites for FcγRs and C1q. Mutations here (e.g., P329G) further disrupt binding.  

### Mutations and Their Effects  

The invention combines lower hinge mutations (LALA, LALE) with CH2 mutations (P329G, P329A, S267K) to achieve complete effector silencing. Key findings include:  

1. **FcγR Binding**:  
   - LALA alone reduces binding but does not eliminate it under highly avid conditions (e.g., immune complexes).  
   - Adding P329G, P329A, or S267K abolishes binding even in high-avidity assays.  

2. **C1q Binding**:  
   - LALA alone eliminates detectable C1q binding in standard assays.  
   - CH2 mutations (e.g., P329G) further ensure no residual complement activation.  

3. **FcRn and ProA Binding**:  
   - All tested variants retain WT-like binding to FcRn and ProA, critical for pharmacokinetics and manufacturability.  

4. **Biophysical Stability**:  
   - DSF shows that LALA-P329G, LALA-P329A, and LALE-P329G maintain CH2 melting temperatures (Tm) close to WT (~67°C).  
   - pH 3.5 stress SEC confirms minimal aggregation, unlike destabilizing mutations (e.g., D265A or P331S).  

### Production and Characterization  

The antibodies are produced via transient transfection in HEK cells and purified using ProA chromatography. Characterization includes:  
- **Binding assays**: Biolayer interferometry (BLI) for FcRn/ProA; SPR for FcγRs/C1q; FACS for cell-based avidity.  
- **Biophysical profiling**: AC-SINS (self-interaction), HIC (hydrophobicity), SEC (purity), and DSF (thermal stability).  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Antibodies  

A panel of anti-CD3ε IgG1 antibodies (ADI-26906 backbone) was engineered with lower hinge (LALA, LALE) and CH2 (P329G, P329A, S267K) mutations. All variants were expressed in HEK293 cells and purified to >95% monomericity by SEC.  

### Example 2: THP-1 Cell Binding and C1q Binding to Antibodies  

THP-1 cells (expressing FcγRI/II) were incubated with antibody variants (100 nM) or immune complexes (preformed with CD3ε-BSA). Binding was detected by flow cytometry. Results showed:  
- LALA-P329G, LALA-P329A, and LALE-P329G eliminated binding under both conditions.  
- C1q binding was undetectable for all variants in Dynal bead assays.  

### Example 3: FcγR Binding by Antibodies  

SPR assays quantified binding to FcγRI (CD64), FcγRIIa (CD32a), and FcγRIIIa (CD16a):  
- WT IgG1 showed strong binding (KD ~10 nM for CD64).  
- LALA-P329G and LALE-P329G reduced binding to baseline levels.  

### Example 4: Developability Assays  

Biophysical profiling revealed:  
- **AC-SINS**: No increased self-interaction (Δλ < 5 nm vs. WT).  
- **HIC**: Retention times matched WT, indicating unchanged hydrophobicity.  
- **SEC**: >95% monomericity post-pH 3.5 stress (vs. 85% for destabilized controls).  

### Example 5: ADCC Activity Assay  

ADCC was measured using NK cell-mediated lysis of CD3ε+ target cells:  
- WT IgG1 induced robust lysis (~60% at 10 nM).  
- LALA-P329G and LALE-P329G showed no activity above background.  

### Example 6: Complement Activity Assay  

CDC was assessed by C3 deposition on target cells:  
- WT IgG1 triggered strong complement activation.  
- All variants showed no C3 deposition, confirming silenced CDC.  

### Conclusion  

The engineered IgG1 variants of the invention (e.g., LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G) represent a significant advance by combining complete effector silencing with WT-like stability, enabling safer and more manufacturable therapeutics.
