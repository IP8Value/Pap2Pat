# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of pharmaceutical compositions and methods for treating neurological disorders, particularly epilepsy. More specifically, the invention pertains to the synergistic combination of perampanel and decanoic acid (DA) for inhibiting α-amino-3-hydroxy-5-methyl-4-isoxazolepropionic acid (AMPA) receptor activity and controlling seizures. The invention encompasses novel therapeutic formulations, methods of administration, and treatment regimens that leverage the distinct yet complementary mechanisms of action of these compounds to achieve enhanced efficacy with reduced side effects compared to monotherapy approaches.  

## BACKGROUND TO THE INVENTION  

Epilepsy affects approximately 50 million people worldwide, with nearly one-third of cases remaining resistant to existing pharmacological treatments. Current antiepileptic drugs (AEDs) often exhibit limited efficacy or intolerable side effects at therapeutic doses. Perampanel (Fycompa), a noncompetitive AMPA receptor antagonist, has demonstrated clinical utility as an adjunctive treatment for partial-onset and primary generalized tonic-clonic seizures. However, its clinical use is constrained by dose-dependent adverse effects including dizziness, somnolence, and behavioral changes.  

Medium chain triglycerides (MCTs), particularly those containing decanoic acid, have shown promise in managing drug-resistant epilepsy through ketogenic diets. Recent evidence suggests that decanoic acid exerts direct antiseizure effects through AMPA receptor inhibition at a binding site distinct from that of perampanel. While both compounds independently modulate AMPA receptor activity, their potential synergistic interaction has not been previously explored or exploited for therapeutic benefit.  

There exists an unmet need for improved antiepileptic therapies that provide enhanced seizure control while minimizing adverse effects. The present invention addresses this need through the discovery and development of synergistic combinations of perampanel and decanoic acid, which unexpectedly demonstrate superior AMPA receptor inhibition and seizure suppression compared to either compound administered alone.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides pharmaceutical compositions and methods for treating seizure disorders through the combined administration of perampanel and decanoic acid. The inventors have discovered that these compounds act synergistically to inhibit AMPA receptor activity, resulting in enhanced seizure control at lower individual doses than required for monotherapy. This synergistic interaction stems from the compounds binding to distinct sites on AMPA receptors - perampanel at the S1-M1 and S2-M4 linker regions and decanoic acid at the M3 region.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  
1) Pharmaceutical compositions comprising perampanel and decanoic acid in synergistic ratios  
2) Methods of treating epilepsy by co-administering perampanel and decanoic acid  
3) Treatment regimens that achieve enhanced efficacy with reduced side effects  
4) Formulations suitable for oral, parenteral, or other routes of administration  
5) Dosage protocols that maintain therapeutic plasma concentrations of both active compounds  

The invention is supported by experimental data demonstrating:  
- Synergistic inhibition of recombinant AMPA receptor currents  
- Enhanced suppression of seizure-like activity in animal models  
- Effective control of epileptiform activity in human brain tissue  
- Reduced effective doses compared to monotherapy

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### Combination  

The invention is based on the unexpected discovery that perampanel and decanoic acid exhibit synergistic inhibition of AMPA receptors when administered in combination. This synergy arises from the compounds binding to distinct sites on the AMPA receptor complex - perampanel at the S1-M1 and S2-M4 linker regions and decanoic acid at the M3 region. The simultaneous binding at these non-overlapping sites produces an inhibitory effect greater than the sum of their individual effects.  

The combination provides several therapeutic advantages:  
1) Enhanced seizure control at lower individual doses  
2) Reduced incidence and severity of dose-dependent side effects  
3) Potential to overcome drug resistance in refractory epilepsy  
4) Flexible dosing regimens adaptable to patient needs  

The synergistic effect has been demonstrated through:  
- Isobolographic analysis showing combination points below the additive line  
- Reduced IC50 values for both compounds in combination  
- Enhanced suppression of seizure activity in multiple models  

### Decanoic Acid and Compositions Comprising the Same  

Decanoic acid (C10:0) is a medium-chain fatty acid that may be derived from natural sources or synthetically produced. For therapeutic use, decanoic acid may be administered as:  
- The free fatty acid  
- Pharmaceutically acceptable salts (e.g., sodium decanoate)  
- Esters or other derivatives with improved pharmacokinetic properties  
- Components of medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs)  

Compositions may contain decanoic acid in pure form or as part of MCT oils containing 30-100% decanoic acid by weight. The decanoic acid component may be formulated with perampanel in single combination products or as separate coordinated formulations.  

### Perampanel  

Perampanel (2-(2-oxo-1-phenyl-5-pyridin-2-yl-1,2-dihydropyridin-3-yl)benzonitrile) is a noncompetitive AMPA receptor antagonist approved for epilepsy treatment. The invention utilizes perampanel in its free base form or as pharmaceutically acceptable salts, solvates, or polymorphs. Perampanel may be formulated for oral administration (tablets, capsules, solutions) or other routes (parenteral, transdermal).  

In combination with decanoic acid, perampanel demonstrates:  
- 3-5 fold increase in potency  
- Enhanced efficacy at lower doses  
- Improved therapeutic index  

### Treatment  

The invention provides methods for treating seizure disorders including:  
- Partial-onset seizures  
- Generalized tonic-clonic seizures  
- Drug-resistant epilepsy  
- Epileptic syndromes  

Treatment involves administering perampanel and decanoic acid in amounts that produce synergistic therapeutic effects. Typical daily doses may include:  
- Perampanel: 0.5-8 mg (preferably 2-4 mg)  
- Decanoic acid: 100-1000 mg (preferably 300-600 mg)  

The combination therapy may be used as:  
- First-line treatment for newly diagnosed epilepsy  
- Adjunctive therapy in treatment-resistant cases  
- Alternative to ketogenic diet in appropriate patients  

### Administration  

The compounds may be administered:  
- Simultaneously in single combination formulations  
- Sequentially as separate formulations  
- Via the same or different routes of administration  

Preferred administration routes include:  
- Oral (tablets, capsules, liquids)  
- Parenteral (intravenous, intramuscular)  
- Enteral (nasogastric, gastrostomy)  

Dosing schedules may be:  
- Once or twice daily for maintenance  
- Acute dosing for seizure clusters  
- Titrated based on therapeutic response  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1—Methods  

Experimental protocols were established to evaluate the synergistic effects of perampanel and decanoic acid:  

1. AMPA Receptor Electrophysiology:  
Recombinant AMPA receptors (GluA1/2 and GluA2/3 combinations) were expressed in Xenopus laevis oocytes. Glutamate-induced currents were recorded in the presence of varying concentrations of perampanel, decanoic acid, or combinations thereof.  

2. Animal Seizure Models:  
Pentylenetetrazol (PTZ)-induced seizure-like activity was measured in rat hippocampal slices. Epileptiform discharges were recorded before and after application of test compounds.  

3. Human Brain Slice Studies:  
Epileptiform activity was induced in neocortical slices from epilepsy patients undergoing surgical resection. The effects of test compounds on local field potentials were quantified.  

Data analysis included:  
- Calculation of IC50 values using Hill plots  
- Isobolographic analysis for synergy determination  
- Statistical comparisons using ANOVA and post-hoc tests  

### Example 2—Direct Inhibition of AMPA Receptors by Perampanel and Decanoic Acid  

Electrophysiological studies demonstrated:  

1. Perampanel alone inhibited AMPA receptor currents with IC50 values of:  
- 6.27 μmol/L for GluA1/2  
- 8.11 μmol/L for GluA2/3  

2. Decanoic acid alone showed IC50 values of:  
- 1.16 mmol/L for GluA1/2  
- 0.52 mmol/L for GluA2/3  

3. In combination:  
- 1 μmol/L perampanel reduced DA IC50 to 0.21 mmol/L (GluA1/2) and 0.15 mmol/L (GluA2/3)  
- 4 μmol/L perampanel further reduced DA IC50 to 0.10 mmol/L and 0.12 mmol/L respectively  
- Similar potentiation was observed for perampanel in the presence of DA  

Isobolographic analysis confirmed synergistic interaction, with combination data points falling below the additive line for both receptor subtypes.  

### Example 3—Effects of Perampanel and Decanoic Acid on Seizure Models  

1. Animal Model Results:  
- DA alone reduced seizure activity at ≥300 μmol/L  
- 100 nmol/L perampanel + 10 μmol/L DA reduced activity to 76.8% of baseline (vs 95.6% for perampanel alone)  
- 500 nmol/L perampanel + DA further reduced activity to 69.6%  
- DA IC50 decreased from 352 μmol/L (alone) to 122 μmol/L with 500 nmol/L perampanel  

2. Human Tissue Results:  
- Neither DA (100 μmol/L) nor perampanel (0.5 μmol/L) alone significantly reduced epileptiform activity  
- The combination dramatically reduced activity to 144.7 μV2 from baseline 1690 μV2  
- Complete abolition of pathological network activity was observed
