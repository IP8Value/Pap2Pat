# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates generally to compositions and methods for treating autoimmune diseases and immune-related disorders. More particularly, the invention provides Insulin-Like 6 (Insl6) agents, including Insl6-Fc fusion proteins, pharmaceutical compositions containing Insl6 agents, and methods of using such compositions for the treatment of inflammatory myopathies such as polymyositis and dermatomyositis. The invention further encompasses gene therapy approaches for delivering Insl6 to subjects in need thereof, as well as kits containing Insl6 agents for therapeutic use.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Autoimmune myopathies, including polymyositis and dermatomyositis, represent a group of rare but debilitating inflammatory muscle disorders characterized by chronic muscle inflammation, weakness, and elevated creatine kinase levels. Current treatment options for these conditions remain limited and often provide incomplete relief. The pathogenesis of these disorders involves aberrant immune activation, particularly infiltration of CD4+ and CD8+ T cells into muscle tissue, leading to persistent inflammation and impaired muscle regeneration.  

While corticosteroids and immunosuppressants are commonly employed, these treatments carry significant side effects and fail to address the underlying regenerative deficits. There exists an urgent need for novel therapeutic approaches that can simultaneously modulate the immune response while promoting muscle repair. The insulin-like protein family has emerged as a promising source of factors with both anti-inflammatory and tissue-regenerative properties.  

Insulin-Like 6 (Insl6) is a member of the insulin/relaxin superfamily that has recently been implicated in muscle regeneration. Previous studies have demonstrated that Insl6 expression increases following muscle injury and that adenoviral-mediated overexpression can enhance muscle repair in acute injury models. However, the potential therapeutic utility of Insl6 in chronic inflammatory conditions, particularly autoimmune myopathies, has not been explored. Furthermore, the development of stable, biologically active Insl6 formulations suitable for clinical use has not been previously described.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention addresses these unmet needs by providing novel Insl6-based therapeutics and methods for treating autoimmune and immune-related diseases. The invention is based on the surprising discovery that Insl6 not only promotes muscle regeneration but also possesses potent immunomodulatory properties, particularly the ability to suppress T cell activation and inflammatory cytokine production.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  
1. Isolated Insl6 agents comprising native Insl6 polypeptides, biologically active fragments thereof, and Fc-fusion proteins incorporating Insl6 sequences  
2. Pharmaceutical compositions containing Insl6 agents formulated for parenteral administration  
3. Methods of treating autoimmune myopathies by administering Insl6 agents to reduce inflammation and promote muscle regeneration  
4. Gene therapy approaches for sustained Insl6 expression in subjects  
5. Kits containing Insl6 agents with instructions for therapeutic use  

The invention demonstrates that Insl6 deficiency exacerbates experimental autoimmune myositis, while Insl6 overexpression ameliorates disease severity. These effects correlate with reduced T cell infiltration and decreased expression of inflammatory cytokines in affected muscle tissue. Importantly, the invention further shows that Insl6 levels are reduced in human myositis patients compared to controls, suggesting that Insl6 supplementation may have therapeutic value in clinical settings.

As used herein, the term "Insl6" refers to Insulin-Like 6 protein from any mammalian species, including human Insl6 (UniProt ID Q9Y5R6) and mouse Insl6 (UniProt ID Q9JKR6), as well as biologically active variants thereof sharing at least 80% sequence identity with native Insl6. The term encompasses full-length polypeptides, mature forms lacking signal sequences, and fragments retaining biological activity.  

"Treating" or "treatment" refers to therapeutic interventions that ameliorate symptoms, slow disease progression, or induce remission in autoimmune or immune-related disorders.  

"Pharmaceutically acceptable carrier" denotes non-toxic vehicles suitable for administering therapeutic agents to subjects, including buffers, excipients, stabilizers, and preservatives.  

"Subject" means any mammal requiring treatment, particularly humans diagnosed with or at risk for autoimmune myopathies.  

### Insulin-Like 6 (Insl6) Agents  

The invention provides isolated Insl6 agents for therapeutic use. These include:  

1. Native Insl6 polypeptides isolated from natural sources or produced recombinantly  
2. Biologically active fragments comprising at least 50 contiguous amino acids of full-length Insl6  
3. Variant polypeptides with amino acid substitutions, deletions, or additions that retain biological activity  
4. Fusion proteins combining Insl6 sequences with heterologous domains such as Fc regions  

Preferred Insl6 agents demonstrate the ability to:  
- Inhibit T cell proliferation by at least 30% in Jurkat cell assays  
- Reduce IL-2 production by activated T cells  
- Decrease inflammatory cytokine expression in muscle tissue  
- Improve motor function in experimental autoimmune myositis models  

### Insl6-Fc Fusion Proteins  

A particularly advantageous embodiment comprises Insl6-Fc fusion proteins. These chimeric molecules combine the N-terminal Fc domain of human IgG1 with full-length or truncated Insl6 sequences. The Fc domain provides several benefits:  

1. Extended serum half-life through FcRn-mediated recycling  
2. Dimerization via Fc-Fc interactions enhancing receptor binding  
3. Simplified purification using Protein A/G affinity chromatography  

Exemplary Insl6-Fc constructs include:  
- Human IgG1 Fc (residues 216-446) linked to mouse Insl6 (residues 24-147)  
- Human IgG1 Fc linked to human Insl6 (residues 25-148)  
- Variants with flexible linker peptides (e.g., (GGGGS)n) between domains  

These fusion proteins are produced by:  
1. Cloning Insl6 cDNA into mammalian expression vectors containing Fc sequences  
2. Transfecting host cells (e.g., CHO, HEK293)  
3. Purifying secreted fusion proteins from culture media

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Insl6-Fc Fusion Protein Production and Characterization**  

The Fc-tagged Insl6 construct was generated by fusing human IgG1 Fc to mouse Insl6 cDNA cloned into pLEV113 mammalian expression vector. HEK293 cells were transfected and stable pools selected. The fusion protein was purified from conditioned media using Protein A affinity chromatography.  

Analytical characterization showed:  
- Molecular weight: ~45 kDa by SDS-PAGE  
- Purity: >95% by SEC-HPLC  
- Bioactivity: 40% inhibition of Jurkat cell proliferation at 100 nM  

**Experimental Autoimmune Myositis Treatment**  

C57BL/6 mice were immunized with skeletal C-protein fragment to induce myositis. Treatment groups received:  
1. Insl6-Fc (5 mg/kg, twice weekly)  
2. Vehicle control  

After 2 weeks, Insl6-Fc treated mice showed:  
- 60% improvement in Rotarod performance  
- 70% reduction in CD8+ T cell infiltration  
- 50% decrease in TNF-α mRNA levels  

These results demonstrate the therapeutic potential of Insl6-Fc in autoimmune myositis.
