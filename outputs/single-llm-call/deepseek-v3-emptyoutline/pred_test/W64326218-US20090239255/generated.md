# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of molecular biology and oncology, specifically to the identification and utilization of Odontogenic Ameloblast-Associated Protein (ODAM) as a biomarker and therapeutic target in epithelial cancers. The invention encompasses methods for detecting ODAM expression in normal and malignant tissues, as well as diagnostic assays for identifying anti-ODAM antibodies in patient sera. Additionally, the invention provides monoclonal and polyclonal antibodies specific to ODAM, along with their applications in cancer diagnosis and treatment.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Epithelial cancers, including those of the breast, lung, and stomach, remain a significant global health burden due to late-stage diagnosis and limited therapeutic options. Current biomarkers for these malignancies often lack specificity or sensitivity, necessitating the discovery of novel molecular targets. ODAM, a protein encoded by a gene within the Secretory Calcium-Binding Phosphoprotein (SCPP) cluster on chromosome 4q13, was initially identified for its role in odontogenesis. However, recent studies have revealed its aberrant expression in various cancers, suggesting its potential as a diagnostic and therapeutic target.  

Prior research has demonstrated ODAM's involvement in calcifying epithelial odontogenic tumors (CEOT), where it forms amyloid fibrils. Additionally, ODAM transcripts have been detected in non-dental tissues, including salivary glands, trachea, and certain carcinomas. Despite these findings, the full scope of ODAM's physiological and pathological roles, particularly in cancer, remains unexplored. The present invention addresses this gap by characterizing ODAM's expression in epithelial malignancies and developing tools for its detection and targeting.  

## DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides a comprehensive approach to leveraging ODAM's unique properties for cancer diagnosis and therapy. Key aspects include:  

1. **Detection of ODAM Expression**: The invention encompasses methods for identifying ODAM in tissues and bodily fluids using immunohistochemistry, in situ hybridization, and serologic assays. These techniques utilize anti-ODAM antibodies, including monoclonal antibodies (e.g., 5A-1 and 8B-4) and polyclonal antisera, which specifically bind to ODAM epitopes.  

2. **Diagnostic Assays**: The invention includes enzyme-linked immunosorbent assays (ELISAs) for measuring ODAM levels or anti-ODAM antibodies in patient sera. These assays are particularly useful for early cancer detection and monitoring disease progression.  

3. **Therapeutic Applications**: The invention describes the use of ODAM-targeting antibodies for cancer treatment, either alone or conjugated to therapeutic agents. Additionally, the invention covers small molecules or biologics that modulate ODAM expression or activity to inhibit tumor growth.  

4. **Recombinant ODAM Production**: The invention provides methods for synthesizing recombinant ODAM (rODAM) in prokaryotic or eukaryotic systems, enabling large-scale production for research and clinical applications.  

### EXAMPLE 1  

**Generation of Anti-ODAM Antibodies**  
Monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) 5A-1 and 8B-4 were produced by immunizing mice with synthetic peptides and recombinant ODAM, respectively. The 5A-1 mAb recognizes an epitope within residues 52–90 of ODAM, while 8B-4 binds to residues 178–216. These antibodies were validated via immunoprecipitation and Western blotting, demonstrating specificity for full-length and truncated ODAM isoforms.  

### EXAMPLE 2  

**Immunohistochemical Detection of ODAM in Cancer Tissues**  
Tissue microarrays containing breast, lung, and gastric carcinomas were stained with mAb 8B-4. ODAM expression was observed in 46/60 breast cancers, 48/60 gastric cancers, and 3/6 lung cancers, primarily localized to the nucleus and cytoplasm. Normal tissues, such as trachea and salivary glands, also exhibited ODAM reactivity, albeit at lower levels.  

### EXAMPLE 3  

**Serologic Assay for Anti-ODAM Antibodies**  
An ELISA was developed to detect anti-ODAM IgG in patient sera. The assay utilized rODAM as the capture antigen and demonstrated that 35% of breast cancer patients (25/72) had elevated anti-ODAM antibody titers compared to healthy controls. Similar results were observed in lung and gastric cancer cohorts.  

### EXAMPLE 4  

**Recombinant ODAM Production**  
Full-length rODAM (residues 24–279) and a truncated form (residues 127–279) were expressed in *E. coli* using pSTBlue-1 and pSmart vectors. The proteins were purified via FLAG-affinity chromatography and characterized by mass spectrometry, confirming their expected molecular weights.  

### EXAMPLE 5  

**In Situ Hybridization for ODAM mRNA**  
A biotinylated ODAM-specific oligonucleotide probe was used to detect ODAM transcripts in breast cancer tissues. Hybridization signals were observed in tumor cells but not in adjacent normal tissue, confirming ODAM's upregulation in malignancies.
