## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The specification of primordial germ cells (PGCs) represents a fundamental biological process essential for reproduction and transmission of genetic information across generations. While significant progress has been made in understanding murine PGC development, the mechanisms underlying human PGC specification remain incompletely characterized due to ethical and technical challenges in studying early human embryos. Current approaches for generating human PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) from pluripotent stem cells (PSCs) typically involve complex three-dimensional culture systems requiring extended differentiation periods and high concentrations of growth factors. These methods often yield heterogeneous cell populations with limited efficiency, complicating downstream applications and analyses. There exists a pressing need for simplified, reproducible, and efficient systems to generate homogeneous populations of human PGCLCs that accurately recapitulate the molecular and functional characteristics of native PGCs. The present invention addresses these limitations through novel monolayer culture methods and improved cell surface marker identification for PGCLC purification.

## BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides methods for efficiently generating human primordial germ cell-like cells (PGCLCs) from pluripotent stem cells (PSCs) in monolayer culture. The invention encompasses a simplified two-dimensional differentiation protocol requiring only 3.5 days to produce PGCLCs, representing a significant improvement over existing three-dimensional culture methods. A key aspect of the invention involves temporally dynamic modulation of WNT signaling, wherein initial activation followed by precise inhibition enhances PGCLC specification efficiency. The methods demonstrate robust performance across multiple human embryonic stem cell (hESC) and induced pluripotent stem cell (hiPSC) lines, with typical yields of 20-30% PGCLCs and peak efficiencies reaching 73.2%.

The invention further discloses novel cell surface markers (CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-) that enable highly specific identification and purification of human PGCLCs by fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS). Single-cell RNA sequencing confirms that cells purified using this surface marker profile exhibit 97.2% homogeneity and closely resemble native human fetal PGCs at the transcriptional level. The invention also provides insights into the continuous expression of pluripotency factors during the transition from pluripotent stem cells to germline fate, challenging previous models of transient pluripotency factor downregulation during PGC specification.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

### Definitions  

For purposes of this invention, the following terms shall have the meanings specified:

"Pluripotent stem cells (PSCs)" refers to cells capable of differentiating into all cell types of an organism, including human embryonic stem cells (hESCs), induced pluripotent stem cells (hiPSCs), and other pluripotent cell types maintained in culture.

"Primordial germ cell-like cells (PGCLCs)" refers to in vitro derived cells that recapitulate key molecular and functional characteristics of native primordial germ cells, including expression of markers such as NANOS3, PRDM1, TFAP2C, SOX17, and OCT4.

"Posterior epiblast" refers to an intermediate cell state during differentiation characterized by co-expression of pluripotency factors (OCT4, NANOG) and primitive streak markers (MIXL1, BRACHYURY, FGF8, NODAL).

"Temporally dynamic WNT modulation" refers to the sequential activation and inhibition of WNT signaling at specific timepoints during differentiation to promote PGCLC specification.

"CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-" refers to the cell surface marker profile that specifically identifies human PGCLCs, enabling their purification from heterogeneous cell populations.

### Methods  

The invention provides optimized methods for differentiating human pluripotent stem cells into primordial germ cell-like cells in monolayer culture. The differentiation protocol comprises two principal phases conducted over 3.5 days.

In the first phase, undifferentiated hPSCs are exposed to posteriorizing signals for 12 hours to generate posterior epiblast intermediates. This step utilizes a defined medium (aRB27) supplemented with 100 ng/mL Activin, 3 μM CHIR99021 (a WNT agonist), and 10 μM Y-27632 (a ROCK inhibitor). The 12-hour duration represents a critical parameter, as extended exposure (24 hours) promotes primitive streak formation at the expense of PGCLC specification.

The second phase involves sequential treatment with specific growth factors and signaling modulators. Cells are initially treated for 24 hours with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 1 μM XAV939 (a WNT inhibitor), and 10 μM Y-27632. This is followed by 24 hours treatment with 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632. The final 24-hour period employs 40 ng/mL BMP4, 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632. All treatments are conducted in aRB27 basal medium.

The invention demonstrates that explicit WNT inhibition during the second phase (using XAV939) provides a ~3-fold improvement in PGCLC specification compared to simply withholding WNT agonists. This highlights the importance of controlling endogenous WNT signals during differentiation. The protocol achieves peak PGCLC formation by day 3.5 of differentiation, with typical yields of 20-30% NANOS3+ PGCLCs.

### Cell Compositions  

The invention provides novel cell compositions comprising purified populations of human PGCLCs identified by the surface marker profile CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-. These cell populations exhibit high purity (97.2% homogeneous by single-cell RNA sequencing) and express characteristic PGC markers including POU5F1 (OCT4), NANOG, TFCP2L1, PRDM1 (BLIMP1), NANOS3 and TFAP2C (AP2γ).

The PGCLC compositions are substantially free of endodermal (FOXA2-, HHEX-) and extraembryonic (CDX2-) cell types. Transcriptional profiling confirms that the PGCLCs closely resemble early-stage human fetal PGCs (FGC1), expressing pluripotency genes (POU5F1, NANOG) and PGC-specific markers (NANOS3, SOX17, PRDM1, TFAP2C) while lacking markers of later germ cell differentiation stages.

The invention further encompasses intermediate cell compositions generated during the differentiation process, particularly the posterior epiblast cells produced after 12 hours of differentiation. These intermediates co-express pluripotency factors (OCT4, NANOG) and primitive streak markers (MIXL1, BRACHYURY, FGF8, NODAL) at levels distinct from both undifferentiated PSCs and primitive streak cells.

### METHODS OF USE  

The methods and compositions of the invention have multiple applications in research and therapeutic development:

1. **Germ Cell Development Studies**: The simplified monolayer system enables detailed investigation of human PGC specification mechanisms, including the roles of WNT signaling dynamics and continuous pluripotency factor expression.

2. **Infertility Research**: The invention provides a platform for studying genetic and epigenetic causes of infertility by enabling generation of patient-specific PGCLCs from hiPSCs.

3. **Drug Screening**: The homogeneous PGCLC populations allow screening of compounds that may affect human germ cell development or treat reproductive disorders.

4. **Assisted Reproductive Technologies**: The methods may eventually contribute to developing in vitro derived gametes for treating infertility, pending further maturation of the PGCLCs.

5. **Disease Modeling**: Patient-specific PGCLCs can model germ cell disorders and screen potential therapeutic interventions.

### EXAMPLES  

**Example 1: Optimized Monolayer Differentiation Protocol**

Human pluripotent stem cells (H9 hESC line) were maintained in mTeSR1 medium on Matrigel-coated plates. For differentiation, cells were treated for 12 hours with 100 ng/mL Activin, 3 μM CHIR99021, and 10 μM Y-27632 in aRB27 basal medium to generate posterior epiblast intermediates. Subsequently, cells were treated sequentially with: (1) 40 ng/mL BMP4, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours; (2) 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours; and (3) 40 ng/mL BMP4, 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours. Flow cytometry analysis at day 3.5 revealed 28.5% NANOS3-mCherry+ PGCLCs.

**Example 2: Cell Surface Marker Identification**

A high-throughput screen of 369 cell surface markers was performed on differentiated SOX17-GFP+ PGCLCs versus SOX17-GFP- non-PGCLCs and undifferentiated hPSCs. CXCR4 was identified as the most specific positive marker for PGCLCs, while PDGFRα and GARP served as negative markers for excluding mesodermal cells. FACS purification using the CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP- profile yielded 97.2% pure PGCLCs by single-cell RNA sequencing.

**Example 3: Cross-Line Validation**

The monolayer differentiation protocol was applied to five additional hESC/hiPSC lines (both male and female). The method consistently generated CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP- PGCLCs across all lines (average 36.7±22.6%, peak 73.2%). Purified PGCLCs from all lines expressed hallmark PGC markers without substantial endodermal or mesodermal marker expression.

### EMBODIMENTS  

1. A method for generating human primordial germ cell-like cells (PGCLCs) from pluripotent stem cells (PSCs) in monolayer culture, comprising:
   a) differentiating human PSCs in monolayer culture by exposing them to posteriorizing signals for approximately 12 hours to generate posterior epiblast cells;
   b) subsequently treating the cells with BMP4 and a WNT inhibitor for approximately 24 hours;
   c) further treating the cells with SCF, EGF and a WNT inhibitor for approximately 24 hours; and
   d) finally treating the cells with BMP4, SCF, EGF and a WNT inhibitor for approximately 24 hours;
   whereby PGCLCs are generated within approximately 3.5 days of differentiation.

2. The method of embodiment 1, wherein the posteriorizing signals comprise Activin, a WNT agonist, and a ROCK inhibitor.

3. The method of embodiment 2, wherein the WNT agonist is CHIR99021.

4. The method of any of embodiments 1-3, wherein the WNT inhibitor is XAV939.

5. The method of any of embodiments 1-4, wherein the PGCLCs are identified by the cell surface marker profile CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-.

6. A composition comprising purified human primordial germ cell-like cells (PGCLCs) identified by the cell surface marker profile CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-.

7. The composition of embodiment 6, wherein the PGCLCs express one or more markers selected from the group consisting of NANOS3, PRDM1, TFAP2C, SOX17, and OCT4.

8. The composition of embodiment 6 or 7, wherein the PGCLCs are at least 95% pure as determined by single-cell RNA sequencing.

9. A method of identifying human primordial germ cell-like cells (PGCLCs), comprising detecting the cell surface marker profile CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP-.

10. The method of embodiment 9, further comprising purifying the CXCR4+PDGFRα-GARP- cells by fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS).

11. An in vitro population of human posterior epiblast cells generated by treating pluripotent stem cells with posteriorizing signals for approximately 12 hours, wherein the cells co-express OCT4, NANOG, and primitive streak markers.
