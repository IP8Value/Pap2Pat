# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to immunostimulatory oligodeoxynucleotides (ODNs) and their use as Toll-like receptor (TLR) 9 agonists, particularly for inducing interferon-alpha (IFN-α) and interleukin-6 (IL-6) production. More specifically, the invention provides modified A/D-type CpG ODNs that exhibit improved solubility, stability, and immunostimulatory activity while avoiding uncontrolled aggregation. The disclosed ODNs are useful as vaccine adjuvants, immunotherapeutic agents, and inducers of innate immune responses.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Immunostimulatory CpG oligodeoxynucleotides (ODNs) are synthetic DNA sequences containing unmethylated CpG motifs that activate the innate immune system via TLR9. These ODNs have been classified into four types based on their structural and functional properties: A/D, B/K, C, and P-type. A/D-type ODNs, characterized by a phosphodiester backbone with a poly-G tail at the 3′ end, are potent inducers of IFN-α production in plasmacytoid dendritic cells (pDCs). However, their clinical application has been hindered by their tendency to form G-quadruplex structures, leading to uncontrolled aggregation and precipitation.  

Previous attempts to stabilize A/D-type ODNs have included the introduction of thermolytic protective groups to prevent premature aggregation while allowing G-quadruplex formation in vivo. However, these approaches have not yet been successfully translated into clinical use. B/K-type ODNs, which have a fully phosphorothioate backbone, exhibit strong IL-6 induction but weak IFN-α responses, limiting their utility in applications requiring robust type I interferon signaling.  

There remains a need for improved A/D-type ODNs that retain high IFN-α induction while avoiding aggregation-related stability issues. The present invention addresses this need by providing modified A/D-type ODNs with enhanced solubility and controlled immunostimulatory properties.  

## SUMMARY OF INVENTION  

### Solution to Problem  

The present invention provides modified A/D-type CpG ODNs that overcome the limitations of prior art by incorporating a phosphorothioate polynucleotide tail at the 3′ end. This modification prevents uncontrolled aggregation while maintaining high IFN-α induction. Key aspects of the invention include:  

1. **Non-aggregating A/D-type ODNs**: The addition of a phosphorothioate polydeoxynucleotide tail (e.g., dAs40) to the 3′ end of A/D-type ODNs prevents G-quadruplex-mediated aggregation without compromising immunostimulatory activity.  
2. **CpG motif retention**: The immunostimulatory activity of the modified ODNs remains dependent on the presence of an unmethylated CpG motif, ensuring TLR9-dependent signaling.  
3. **Improved solubility**: The modified ODNs exhibit excellent solubility in saline, facilitating clinical formulation and administration.  
4. **Enhanced adjuvanticity**: The disclosed ODNs demonstrate superior vaccine adjuvant properties compared to conventional A/D-type and B/K-type ODNs in vivo.  

### Advantageous Effects of Invention  

The modified A/D-type ODNs of the invention provide several advantages over existing immunostimulatory ODNs:  

- **Controlled physical properties**: The phosphorothioate tail prevents uncontrolled aggregation, ensuring uniform product quality and ease of manufacturing.  
- **High IFN-α induction**: The modified ODNs retain the strong IFN-α induction characteristic of A/D-type ODNs, making them suitable for applications requiring robust innate immune activation.  
- **Stable formulation**: The ODNs can be lyophilized and readily reconstituted in saline, simplifying clinical use.  
- **Versatile applications**: The ODNs are effective as vaccine adjuvants, monotherapeutic immunomodulators, and inducers of protective immune responses against infections.  

## DESCRIPTION OF EMBODIMENTS  

The invention encompasses modified A/D-type CpG ODNs comprising:  

1. **Core sequence**: A palindromic CpG-containing sequence (e.g., the 12-mer core of D35).  
2. **Phosphorothioate tail**: A polynucleotide tail (e.g., dAs40) attached to the 3′ end, wherein the tail is fully or partially phosphorothioated to prevent aggregation.  

Preferred embodiments include:  

- **D35-dAs40**: A modified D35 ODN with a 3′ dAs40 tail.  
- **D35core-dAs40**: A truncated D35 core sequence (lacking the G-hexamer) with a 3′ dAs40 tail.  

The ODNs may further include additional modifications such as 5′ or 3′ extensions, alternative polynucleotide tails (e.g., dTs40, dCs40), or partial phosphorothioation to fine-tune immunostimulatory activity.  

### EXAMPLES  

**Example 1: IFN-α and IL-6 Induction in Human PBMCs**  
Human peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) were stimulated with D35, D35-dAs40, or D35core-dAs40. ELISA analysis confirmed that D35-dAs40 and D35core-dAs40 induced IFN-α and IL-6 at levels comparable to or exceeding those of unmodified D35, despite lacking G-quadruplex formation.  

**Example 2: Solubility in Saline**  
Lyophilized D35-dAs40 and D35core-dAs40 were readily dissolved in saline, whereas unmodified D35 formed insoluble aggregates. The modified ODNs remained stable in solution for over one month at 4°C.  

**Example 3: Adjuvant Activity in Cynomolgus Monkeys**  
Monkeys immunized with influenza split vaccine plus D35-dAs40 or D35core-dAs40 exhibited stronger and more consistent antibody responses compared to those receiving unmodified D35 or B/K-type K3.  

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  

The modified A/D-type ODNs of the invention are suitable for industrial-scale production and clinical use. Applications include:  

- **Vaccine adjuvants**: Enhancing immune responses to viral, bacterial, or parasitic vaccines.  
- **Immunotherapy**: Treating infections (e.g., Leishmaniasis) or cancers by stimulating IFN-α-dependent immunity.  
- **Drug formulations**: Lyophilized or liquid formulations for subcutaneous, intramuscular, or mucosal administration.  

The invention thus provides a significant advancement in the development of stable, effective immunostimulatory ODNs for therapeutic and prophylactic applications.
