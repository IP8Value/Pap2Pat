# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND  

Mucosal immune responses provide superior protection against infectious diseases, yet there remains a significant unmet need for adjuvants capable of effectively stimulating cell-mediated immunity at mucosal surfaces. Current vaccine strategies face substantial challenges in delivering immunogenic proteins to mucosal tissues while preserving antigen integrity and immunogenicity. The hostile microenvironment of mucosal surfaces often degrades protein antigens before they can be taken up by antigen-presenting cells such as dendritic cells. Furthermore, existing adjuvants frequently induce excessive inflammation while failing to generate robust Th1-type cellular immunity required for clearance of intracellular pathogens.  

Chlamydia trachomatis infections exemplify this challenge, representing one of the most prevalent sexually transmitted infections worldwide with approximately 92 million new cases annually. Despite extensive research efforts, no vaccine has successfully elicited the necessary mucosal Th1 immune responses to prevent chlamydial infections. The major outer membrane protein (MOMP) of Chlamydia represents a promising vaccine candidate due to its immunogenic properties, but conventional delivery methods have failed to generate adequate protective immunity. There exists a critical need for novel vaccine delivery platforms that can protect antigens during mucosal delivery, target antigen-presenting cells, and stimulate appropriate immune responses without causing excessive inflammation.  

## SUMMARY  

The present invention provides recombinant vault nanoparticles engineered as mucosal vaccine delivery platforms. These modified vault particles comprise: (1) a major vault protein (MVP) scaffold forming the vault structure; (2) an Fc-binding peptide domain displayed on the vault exterior; and (3) at least one immunogenic protein encapsulated within the vault interior. The vault nanoparticles demonstrate unexpected properties as "smart adjuvants" capable of stimulating protective mucosal immunity while limiting inflammatory responses.  

Key advantages of the invention include: (1) preservation of antigen integrity during mucosal delivery; (2) enhanced uptake by dendritic cells; (3) induction of Th1-polarizing cytokines without excessive inflammation; (4) activation of inflammasome pathways independent of Toll-like receptor signaling; and (5) generation of protective immunity at distant mucosal sites following nasal administration. The vault platform has been specifically demonstrated using Chlamydia MOMP as the model antigen, showing superior protection against genital challenge compared to existing delivery methods.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

The recombinant vault nanoparticles of the invention comprise three principal components that confer their unique immunological properties:  

1. **Vault Scaffold Structure**: The vault structure is formed by multiple copies of major vault protein (MVP), which self-assembles into barrel-shaped nanoparticles approximately 400 Å wide and 700 Å long. The MVP may optionally include stabilizing modifications such as cysteine-rich tags (cp-MVP). The vault structure provides a protective cavity capable of encapsulating immunogenic proteins while presenting exterior surface domains for cellular targeting.  

2. **Fc-Binding Exterior Domain**: The C-terminus of MVP is modified to display an Fc-binding peptide (e.g., protein A-derived "z" peptide) on the vault exterior. This domain mediates enhanced binding to immunoglobulins (particularly IgG) without interfering with Fc receptor interactions, facilitating uptake by dendritic cells.  

3. **Encapsulated Immunogenic Proteins**: The vault interior encapsulates one or more immunogenic proteins fused to a vault-targeting domain (e.g., mINT domain of VPARP). The immunogenic protein may be any antigen of interest, with demonstrated efficacy shown for Chlamydia MOMP. The vault cavity protects these proteins from degradation while maintaining their immunogenic epitopes.  

The vault nanoparticles are produced through recombinant expression in insect cells using baculovirus vectors. Following expression, the Fc-modified MVP self-assembles into vault structures, and immunogenic proteins fused to targeting domains are incorporated into the vault interior through specific binding interactions. The resulting particles are purified by density gradient centrifugation.  

Unexpectedly, protein-loaded vaults (but not empty vaults) activate the Nalp3 inflammasome pathway, leading to caspase-1 activation and IL-1β secretion without triggering Toll-like receptor signaling. This unique mechanism underlies their "smart adjuvant" properties - stimulating Th1-polarizing cytokines (IL-12, IFNγ) and chemokines (CXCL10) while avoiding production of pro-inflammatory mediators (TNFα, IL-6, CXCL2) associated with tissue damage.  

For vaccination, the vault nanoparticles are administered mucosally, preferably intranasally. This route takes advantage of the interconnected nature of mucosal immune systems, allowing nasal immunization to generate protective immunity at distant mucosal sites such as the genital tract. The vault platform has been specifically shown to protect against Chlamydia muridarum genital challenge following intranasal administration, reducing bacterial burden more effectively than live infection or other vaccine approaches.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Construction and Characterization of MOMP-Containing Vault Nanoparticles**  

Recombinant baculoviruses were generated encoding: (1) cp-MVP-z (cysteine-modified MVP with C-terminal Fc-binding "z" peptide); and (2) MOMP-mINT (Chlamydia MOMP fused to the vault-targeting mINT domain). Sf9 insect cells were co-infected with these viruses to produce vault nanoparticles containing encapsulated MOMP.  

Purified MOMP-vaults were characterized by:  
- Negative stain electron microscopy confirming vault structure integrity  
- Western blot verifying MOMP incorporation using anti-MOMP antibodies  
- ELISA demonstrating enhanced IgG binding via the "z" peptide  
- Size exclusion chromatography showing uniform particle size distribution  

The MOMP-vaults maintained the immunogenic properties of MOMP while gaining the protective and adjuvant properties of the vault platform.  

### Example 2  

**Dendritic Cell Uptake and Activation**  

Immature bone marrow-derived dendritic cells (BMDCs) were incubated with:  
- Media control  
- Fluorescent control particles (FITC-dextran)  
- GFP-containing vaults (GL-vaults)  

Flow cytometry and microscopy demonstrated superior internalization of vault nanoparticles compared to controls. MOMP-vaults induced DC maturation characterized by:  
- Significant IL-12p70 production (Th1 polarization)  
- Increased IL-10 secretion (regulatory balance)  
- Elevated CXCL10 secretion (Th1 cell recruitment)  
- No increase in inflammatory cytokines (TNFα, IL-6)  

Notably, this activation occurred without detectable Toll-like receptor stimulation, indicating a novel mechanism of action.  

### Example 3  

**Inflammasome Activation Mechanism**  

Protein-loaded vaults (MOMP-vaults and GL-vaults) induced:  
- Caspase-1 activation (Western blot)  
- IL-1β maturation and secretion (Luminex assay)  
- No TLR pathway activation (NFκB reporter assay)  

This pattern matched known inflammasome activators like alum but occurred through vault-specific pathways. The findings explain the selective cytokine induction profile that enables Th1 immunity without excessive inflammation.  

### Example 4  

**Protective Efficacy Against Chlamydial Infection**  

Mice were immunized intranasally with:  
- MOMP-vaults (200 μg, 3 doses)  
- Control GL-vaults  
- Live C. muridarum (positive control)  

Following vaginal challenge with C. muridarum:  
- MOMP-vault immunization reduced bacterial burden by >1 log compared to controls  
- Protection exceeded that from live infection immunization  
- Induced MOMP-specific serum IgG without vault autoantibodies  
- Generated Th1 cells in iliac lymph nodes and genital tract  
- Increased IFNγ and CXCL10 in genital tissues  

These results demonstrate the vault platform's unique ability to generate protective mucosal immunity against challenging pathogens like Chlamydia.
