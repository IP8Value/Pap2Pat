# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The aging process in humans is accompanied by a progressive decline in immune function, a phenomenon known as immunosenescence. A primary driver of immunosenescence is thymic involution, the age-related degeneration of the thymus gland, which results in diminished production of naïve T-cells and a collapse of the T-cell receptor (TCR) repertoire. This decline in immune function is associated with increased susceptibility to infections, cancer, autoimmune diseases, and chronic inflammatory conditions, all of which contribute to age-related morbidity and mortality.  

Existing interventions aimed at mitigating immunosenescence have largely focused on isolated aspects of immune dysfunction, such as cytokine supplementation or stem cell therapies. However, these approaches have not demonstrated systemic reversal of aging biomarkers, particularly epigenetic age, which serves as a robust indicator of biological aging. Furthermore, prior attempts to rejuvenate thymic function using recombinant human growth hormone (rhGH) have been limited to specific populations, such as HIV patients, and have not addressed the broader implications of immune restoration in healthy aging individuals.  

There remains a critical unmet need for a therapeutic regimen capable of reversing thymic involution, restoring immune function, and mitigating systemic aging biomarkers in healthy aging adults. The present invention addresses this need by providing a novel combination therapy that not only regenerates thymic tissue but also induces epigenetic age reversal, thereby offering a comprehensive approach to combating age-related decline.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a method for reversing immunosenescence and systemic aging in humans through a combination therapy comprising recombinant human growth hormone (rhGH), dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA), and metformin. This therapeutic regimen is designed to regenerate thymic tissue, restore immune function, and reverse epigenetic aging markers, as measured by established epigenetic clocks.  

The invention is based on the discovery that administration of rhGH in combination with DHEA and metformin induces significant regeneration of thymic functional mass, as evidenced by increased thymic fat-free fraction (TFFF) in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scans. Additionally, the treatment reduces pro-inflammatory monocytes, increases the lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio (LMR), and decreases PD-1-positive CD8 T-cells, indicative of improved immune function. Remarkably, the therapy also reverses epigenetic age, as demonstrated by reductions in multiple epigenetic clock measurements, including the Horvath, Hannum, PhenoAge, and GrimAge clocks.  

The method involves administering rhGH at a dose of approximately 0.015 mg/kg body weight, combined with 50 mg DHEA and 500 mg metformin, with individualized adjustments based on patient response. The treatment is typically administered over a period of 12 months, with optional extensions or repetitions to sustain therapeutic benefits. The invention further includes monitoring biomarkers such as insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1), insulin sensitivity, and epigenetic age to optimize dosing and assess efficacy.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides a pharmaceutical composition and method for reversing immunosenescence and systemic aging in humans. The composition comprises three active agents: recombinant human growth hormone (rhGH), dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA), and metformin. Each component contributes uniquely to the therapeutic effects, and their combination synergistically enhances thymic regeneration, immune restoration, and epigenetic age reversal.  

**Recombinant Human Growth Hormone (rhGH):**  
rhGH is administered to stimulate thymic epithelial cell proliferation and hematopoiesis, thereby reversing thymic involution. The preferred dosage is approximately 0.015 mg/kg body weight, administered subcutaneously 3–4 times per week. rhGH increases circulating levels of insulin-like growth factor 1 (IGF-1), which promotes thymic regeneration and immune cell production. However, rhGH alone can induce hyperinsulinemia, which is counteracted by the inclusion of DHEA and metformin in the regimen.  

**Dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA):**  
DHEA is administered at a dose of 50 mg daily to mitigate the diabetogenic effects of rhGH and to provide additional anti-aging benefits. DHEA improves insulin sensitivity, reduces inflammation, and supports immune function. Its inclusion in the regimen ensures that the therapeutic effects of rhGH are not offset by adverse metabolic consequences.  

**Metformin:**  
Metformin is administered at a dose of 500 mg daily to further enhance insulin sensitivity and act as a calorie restriction mimetic. Metformin has been shown to extend lifespan in animal models and is hypothesized to contribute to the epigenetic age reversal observed in the present invention. Its combination with rhGH and DHEA creates a balanced metabolic environment conducive to thymic regeneration and systemic aging reversal.  

**Administration and Monitoring:**  
The treatment begins with a one-week lead-in period of rhGH alone to assess individual insulin responses. In the second week, DHEA is added to evaluate its insulin-suppressing effects. By the third week, metformin is introduced, and dosages are individualized based on patient-specific responses to IGF-1, insulin, and other biomarkers. Regular monitoring of thymic fat-free fraction (TFFF), immune cell subsets, and epigenetic age is conducted to adjust dosing and assess therapeutic progress.  

**Mechanisms of Action:**  
The invention operates through multiple interconnected mechanisms:  
1. **Thymic Regeneration:** rhGH stimulates thymic epithelial cell proliferation and increases thymic functional mass, as measured by TFFF.  
2. **Immune Restoration:** The therapy reduces exhausted PD-1+ CD8 T-cells, increases naïve T-cell populations, and improves the lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio (LMR), indicative of enhanced immune function.  
3. **Epigenetic Age Reversal:** The combination of rhGH, DHEA, and metformin induces regression of epigenetic age across multiple clocks, suggesting systemic reversal of biological aging.  
4. **Metabolic Optimization:** DHEA and metformin counteract rhGH-induced hyperinsulinemia, ensuring that the metabolic environment supports rather than hinders therapeutic effects.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

A clinical trial was conducted with nine healthy male volunteers aged 51–65 years to evaluate the effects of the invention. Participants received rhGH (0.015 mg/kg), DHEA (50 mg), and metformin (500 mg) for 12 months. Thymic regeneration was assessed via MRI, showing a statistically significant increase in thymic fat-free fraction (TFFF) (p = 8.57 × 10^−17). Epigenetic age reversal was observed across all four epigenetic clocks, with an average reduction of 2.5 years at 12 months.  

### Example 2  

In the same trial, immune cell analysis revealed a significant decline in CD38-positive monocytes (p = 0.01) and an increase in the lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio (LMR) (p = 0.018). These changes persisted for 6 months after treatment discontinuation, indicating durable immune restoration.  

### Example 3  

One volunteer exhibited a baseline thymic fat-free fraction (TFFF) of 12%, which increased to 24% after 12 months of treatment. This improvement correlated with a reduction in epigenetic age of 3.1 years, as measured by the GrimAge clock.  

### Example 4  

Two volunteers with abnormally low baseline TFFF (high thymic fat content) showed no significant improvement in TFFF, suggesting that the invention is most effective in individuals with residual thymic tissue.  

### Example 5  

Serum analysis demonstrated a decline in C-reactive protein (CRP), a marker of inflammation, reaching statistical significance by 9–12 months (p < 0.05). This supports the anti-inflammatory effects of the invention.  

### Example 6  

Epigenetic age regression accelerated after 9 months of treatment, with the rate of reversal increasing from −1.56 years/year to −6.48 years/year (p < 0.005). This suggests that prolonged treatment may enhance therapeutic benefits.  

### Example 7  

The GrimAge clock, which predicts human life expectancy, showed no regression of epigenetic age reversal after treatment discontinuation, with a persistent gain of 2.1 years at 18 months.  

### Example 8  

No adverse effects on prostate-specific antigen (PSA) levels were observed, and insulin levels remained stable due to the inclusion of DHEA and metformin.  

### Example 9  

Six months after treatment discontinuation, improvements in immune parameters (e.g., LMR) and epigenetic age were partially sustained, supporting the potential for long-term benefits with periodic treatment cycles.  

## SUMMARY  

The invention provides a groundbreaking method for reversing immunosenescence and systemic aging in humans through a combination of rhGH, DHEA, and metformin. This therapy regenerates thymic tissue, restores immune function, and reverses epigenetic age, offering a comprehensive approach to combating age-related decline. Clinical trials have demonstrated its safety and efficacy, with durable improvements observed in both immune and epigenetic biomarkers. The invention represents a significant advancement in the field of aging intervention, with potential applications in preventive medicine, longevity research, and the treatment of age-related diseases.
