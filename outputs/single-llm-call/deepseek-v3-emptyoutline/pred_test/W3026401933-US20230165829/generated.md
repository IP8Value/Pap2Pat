# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of oncology and molecular biology, particularly to compositions and methods for inhibiting RAS protein activation in cancer cells. More specifically, the invention discloses novel compounds derived from flavaglines that effectively inhibit KRAS activation by disrupting the interaction between prohibitins (PHB1/PHB2), KRAS, and specific phospholipids in the plasma membrane. The invention further provides pharmaceutical compositions containing these compounds and methods for treating KRAS-driven cancers.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

RAS proteins are small GTPases that function as molecular switches regulating cellular signal transduction pathways. The RAS family comprises three main isoforms (KRAS, HRAS, and NRAS) that exhibit 82-90% sequence identity. These proteins cycle between inactive GDP-bound and active GTP-bound states, with their activation regulated by GTPase-activating proteins (GAPs) and guanine nucleotide exchange factors (GEFs). Oncogenic mutations in RAS genes, particularly at codons 12, 13, and 61, prevent GAP-mediated GTP hydrolysis, leaving RAS constitutively active in its GTP-bound state.  

KRAS mutations are frequently identified in pancreatic, lung, and colon carcinomas, making it a prime therapeutic target. Current approaches to target mutant KRAS have focused on direct inhibitors like ARS-1620 that specifically target the KRAS G12C mutant. However, these inhibitors show limited efficacy against other KRAS mutants and can lead to feedback reactivation of wild-type RAS through receptor tyrosine kinase activation. Moreover, the high affinity of GTP for RAS (in the picomolar range) makes direct inhibition of GTP loading challenging.  

Prohibitins (PHB1 and PHB2) are evolutionarily conserved proteins known to regulate mitochondrial function and cristae morphogenesis. Recent studies have shown that prohibitins also localize to plasma membrane subdomains where they form functional oligomers that serve as signaling platforms. PHB1 has been shown to directly bind to CRAF kinase and is required for its activation. Previous attempts to target the PHB1-CRAF interaction with compounds like fluorizoline and rocaglamide showed limited success as activated BRAF(V600E) could bypass this inhibition.  

There remains an unmet need for effective inhibitors that can target multiple KRAS mutants without being bypassed by alternative signaling pathways and that can overcome the high affinity of GTP for RAS proteins.  

## DISCLOSURE OF INVENTION  

The present invention provides novel compounds and methods for inhibiting KRAS activation by targeting the prohibitin-KRAS-phospholipid complex at the plasma membrane. The invention is based on the unexpected discovery that a specific subset of flavaglines can potently inhibit KRAS GTP loading at nanomolar concentrations, regardless of the specific KRAS mutation.  

The flavagline compounds of the invention are characterized by a cyclopenta[b]benzofuran core structure with specific substituents that enable selective inhibition of KRAS activation. These compounds disrupt the formation of KRAS nanoclusters at the plasma membrane by interfering with the prohibitin-dependent segregation of phosphatidylserine (PS) and phosphatidic acid (PA), which are critical for maintaining KRAS in its active conformation.  

Unlike prior art compounds that target specific KRAS mutants (e.g., G12C inhibitors) or the PHB1-CRAF interaction, the compounds of the invention directly prevent GTP loading to KRAS by disrupting its membrane localization and nanoclustering. This mechanism is effective against all tested KRAS mutants (including G12V, G12D, and G12C) and shows efficacy in both in vitro models and autochthonous mouse models of KRAS-driven cancers.  

### R8 is —H, —OCH3, —Br, —F, —Cl;  

In preferred embodiments, the flavagline compounds of the invention feature specific substitutions at the R8 position that are critical for anti-KRAS activity. The R8 position may be selected from —H, —OCH3, —Br, —F, or —Cl. Experimental data demonstrate that suppression of the methoxy group at this position is highly detrimental to anti-KRAS activity, while introduction of bromine or chlorine at this position enhances activity.  

### R8 is —H, —OCH3, —Br, —F, —Cl;  

Further embodiments of the invention include flavagline compounds where the R8 position is specifically —H, —OCH3, —Br, —F, or —Cl. Structure-activity relationship studies reveal that these specific substitutions at the R8 position are essential for maintaining the compound's ability to disrupt KRAS nanoclustering and inhibit GTP loading. The presence of these substituents correlates with the compound's ability to stabilize the PHB1-RAS complex, as demonstrated by cellular thermal shift assays (CETSA).  

## MODES FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION  

The invention may be practiced through several embodiments, including but not limited to:  

1. Pharmaceutical compositions comprising effective amounts of the flavagline compounds described herein, particularly rocaglamide, FL3, FL10, FL13, FL15, FL19, FL23, FL32, FL37, FL40, and FL42. These compositions may be formulated for oral, intravenous, or intraperitoneal administration, with preferred dosages in the range of 2.5 mg/kg body weight for in vivo applications.  

2. Methods for treating KRAS-driven cancers by administering the compounds to patients in need thereof. The treatment is particularly effective against pancreatic, lung, and colon carcinomas harboring various KRAS mutations. The compounds show synergistic effects when combined with existing KRAS G12C inhibitors like ARS-1620.  

3. Diagnostic methods using the compounds to detect KRAS activation states in patient samples through techniques like NanoBiT assays or FLIM-FRET imaging.  

4. Methods for inhibiting KRAS nanoclustering by administering compounds that disrupt the interaction between prohibitins, KRAS, and phospholipids (PS and PA) at the plasma membrane. This is achieved through direct binding of the flavagline compounds to the PHB1-KRAS complex, as confirmed by biochemical assays and electron microscopy.  

5. Use of the compounds in autochthonous mouse models of KRAS-driven non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), where treatment with rocaglamide at 2.5 mg/kg body weight significantly reduced lung tumor nodules without observable liver or kidney toxicity.  

The invention is further illustrated by the following experimental data: NanoBiT assays demonstrated that rocaglamide inhibits KRAS activation with an IC50 of 24.8 nM, achieving 50% inhibition within 50 minutes at 100 nM concentration. The compounds showed efficacy against multiple KRAS mutants (G12V, G12D, G12C) and all three RAS isoforms, though nanoclustering inhibition was specific to KRAS. Cellular assays confirmed that the compounds prevent KRAS clustering at membrane curvatures, as shown by nanobar substrate experiments.  

The flavagline compounds of the invention represent a significant advancement over prior art by providing:  
1) Broad-spectrum inhibition of multiple KRAS mutants  
2) Nanomolar potency in both in vitro and in vivo models  
3) A novel mechanism targeting KRAS nanoclustering rather than direct GTP binding  
4) Favorable toxicity profile in animal models  
5) Potential for combination therapies with existing KRAS inhibitors
