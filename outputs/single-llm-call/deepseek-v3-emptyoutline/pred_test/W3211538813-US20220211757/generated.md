# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of immunotherapy, specifically to compositions and methods for generating allogeneic invariant natural killer T (iNKT) cells derived from hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) for use in cancer treatment. More particularly, the invention provides an in vitro culture system for producing large quantities of engineered iNKT cells that exhibit potent antitumor activity while avoiding graft-versus-host disease (GvHD) and host immune rejection. The invention further encompasses chimeric antigen receptor (CAR)-engineered versions of these cells and methods of using them for off-the-shelf allogeneic cell therapy against various cancers.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Immunotherapy has emerged as a transformative approach for cancer treatment, with T-cell-based therapies showing remarkable clinical success. Chimeric antigen receptor (CAR)-engineered T cells and tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) have demonstrated efficacy against certain hematologic malignancies. However, current T-cell therapies predominantly rely on autologous approaches, wherein T cells are harvested from individual patients, engineered ex vivo, and reinfused. This process is time-consuming, costly, and may not be feasible for patients with lymphopenia or rapidly progressing disease.  

Allogeneic cell therapies derived from healthy donors offer potential solutions to these limitations by enabling large-scale production of standardized cell products. However, conventional αβ T cells pose significant risks of GvHD due to histocompatibility leukocyte antigen (HLA) incompatibility, necessitating additional gene editing to eliminate endogenous T-cell receptor (TCR) expression. Natural killer (NK) cells, which lack GvHD risk, have been explored as alternative allogeneic effectors but often exhibit limited persistence and antitumor potency compared to T cells.  

Invariant natural killer T (iNKT) cells represent a unique lymphocyte population that combines features of both T cells and NK cells. iNKT cells recognize lipid antigens presented by CD1d molecules and demonstrate potent antitumor activity through multiple mechanisms, including direct cytotoxicity, cytokine production, and modulation of the tumor microenvironment. Importantly, iNKT cells do not induce GvHD, making them attractive candidates for allogeneic therapy. However, human blood contains extremely low frequencies of iNKT cells (0.001%-1%), posing substantial challenges for large-scale production.  

Previous attempts to generate iNKT cells through TCR engineering of hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) followed by in vivo reconstitution have been reported. However, such approaches cannot produce mature allogeneic iNKT cells suitable for off-the-shelf applications. There remains an unmet need for robust methods to manufacture clinically relevant quantities of allogeneic iNKT cells with consistent quality, purity, and potency for cancer immunotherapy.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides solutions to the aforementioned limitations by disclosing an in vitro culture system for generating allogeneic HSC-engineered iNKT (AlloHSC-iNKT) cells. The invention encompasses:  

1. A method for producing AlloHSC-iNKT cells comprising:  
   a) Transducing CD34+ hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) with a lentiviral vector encoding an iNKT TCR and optionally a suicide gene;  
   b) Differentiating the transduced HSCs into iNKT cells using an artificial thymic organoid (ATO) culture system; and  
   c) Expanding the differentiated iNKT cells using α-galactosylceramide (αGC) stimulation.  

2. The AlloHSC-iNKT cells produced by this method, which exhibit:  
   - High purity (>99% iNKT TCR+) with minimal contaminating conventional T cells  
   - CD8+ or double-negative (DN) phenotype associated with enhanced cytotoxicity  
   - Potent antitumor activity through multiple mechanisms (NK receptors, iNKT TCR, and optional CAR)  
   - Resistance to graft-versus-host disease (GvHD)  
   - Reduced immunogenicity due to low HLA-I/II expression  

3. CAR-engineered versions of AlloHSC-iNKT cells (AlloCAR-iNKT) that exhibit enhanced tumor targeting through combined NK/TCR/CAR mechanisms.  

4. HLA-ablated universal HSC-iNKT (UHSC-iNKT) cells generated by CRISPR-Cas9 editing of B2M and CIITA genes to eliminate HLA-I/II expression, further reducing host immune rejection.  

5. Pharmaceutical compositions comprising the AlloHSC-iNKT cells and methods of using them for treating various cancers, including hematologic malignancies and solid tumors.  

The invention provides significant advantages over existing approaches, including: scalable production from limited HSC sources (e.g., one cord blood donor can yield sufficient cells for 500-5,000 doses); intrinsic GvHD safety without requiring additional gene editing; multiple tumor-targeting mechanisms; and resistance to host immune rejection. These features make the AlloHSC-iNKT platform particularly suitable for developing off-the-shelf allogeneic cancer immunotherapies.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides comprehensive methods for generating and utilizing allogeneic HSC-engineered iNKT (AlloHSC-iNKT) cells for cancer immunotherapy. The detailed description that follows will enable persons skilled in the art to understand and practice the invention.  

### 1. Source Cells and Initial Engineering  

The production process begins with CD34+ hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) obtained from cord blood or granulocyte colony-stimulating factor (G-CSF)-mobilized peripheral blood. These cells are transduced with a lentiviral vector (Lenti/iNKT-sr39TK) encoding:  
- An iNKT TCR comprising invariant Vα24-Jα18 paired with Vβ11 chains  
- A suicide gene (sr39TK) for safety control, enabling ganciclovir (GCV)-mediated elimination if needed  

Transduction efficiency typically exceeds 50%, as measured by intracellular TCR expression 72 hours post-transduction. The suicide gene provides an important safety mechanism while serving as a positron emission tomography (PET) reporter for tracking cell persistence in vivo.  

### 2. In Vitro Differentiation System  

The invention employs a two-stage culture system to differentiate transduced HSCs into mature AlloHSC-iNKT cells:  

**Stage 1 - ATO Differentiation (8 weeks):**  
Transduced HSCs are cultured in artificial thymic organoids (ATOs) comprising:  
- MS5 stromal cells expressing Delta-like ligand 4 (DLL4)  
- Serum-free medium (RB27) supplemented with FLT3-L and IL-7  

This 3D culture system mimics thymic microenvironments, supporting HSC differentiation through characteristic developmental stages (DN→DP→CD8SP/DN). The culture yields >100-fold expansion with >50% iNKT cell purity (iNKT TCR+TCRαβ+).  

**Stage 2 - αGC Expansion (2-3 weeks):**  
ATO-derived cells are dissociated and stimulated with αGC-loaded, irradiated PBMCs in medium containing IL-7 and IL-15. This stage achieves additional 100-1,000-fold expansion, yielding final products with >99% iNKT purity.  

The complete process generates approximately 5×10^11 cells from cord blood HSCs (sufficient for 500-5,000 doses at 10^8-10^9 cells/dose) or 3×10^13 cells from mobilized peripheral blood HSCs (30,000-300,000 doses).  

### 3. Cell Characteristics  

AlloHSC-iNKT cells exhibit distinctive features that confer therapeutic advantages:  

**Phenotype:**  
- Surface markers: CD45RO+CD161+CCR4+CCR5+CXCR3+ (memory/effector phenotype)  
- Co-receptors: Predominantly CD8SP/DN (>99%), associated with Th1/cytotoxic function  
- Minimal HLA-I/II expression (resists host T-cell rejection)  
- High NKG2D/DNAM-1 (activating) and low KIR (inhibitory) receptor expression  

**Functionality:**  
- Robust proliferation (>10-fold) upon αGC stimulation  
- Th1-biased cytokine production (high IFN-γ, TNF-α, IL-2; low IL-4, IL-17)  
- Abundant cytotoxic molecules (perforin, granzyme B)  
- Triple tumor-targeting mechanisms:  
  1) NK receptors (e.g., NKG2D, DNAM-1) recognizing stress ligands  
  2) iNKT TCR recognizing CD1d-presented lipids  
  3) Engineered CAR when applicable  

**Transcriptional Profile:**  
- Master regulators: High PLZF (ZBTB16), T-bet (TBX21); low GATA3, RORγt  
- Effector genes: Elevated IFN-γ, TNF, granzymes, perforin  
- Homing receptors: High CCR1-6, CXCR3 (tumor infiltration)  

### 4. CAR Engineering  

AlloHSC-iNKT cells can be further engineered to express chimeric antigen receptors (CARs) against tumor targets (e.g., BCMA for multiple myeloma). CAR engineering occurs after Stage 2 expansion using retroviral vectors, achieving >80% transduction efficiency without compromising cell yield or phenotype.  

CAR-engineered AlloHSC-iNKT (AlloCAR-iNKT) cells exhibit:  
- Enhanced tumor killing through combined NK/TCR/CAR mechanisms  
- Resistance to antigen escape (multiple targeting pathways)  
- Maintained GvHD safety and low immunogenicity  

### 5. HLA Ablation for Universal Cells  

To further reduce immunogenicity, the invention provides methods to generate HLA-ablated universal HSC-iNKT (UHSC-iNKT) cells by CRISPR-Cas9 editing of:  
- B2M (abolishes HLA-I expression)  
- CIITA (abolishes HLA-II expression)  

Editing is performed on transduced HSCs prior to ATO culture, achieving >50% double-negative cells that can be MACS-purified to >97% purity. UHSC-iNKT cells and their CAR-engineered derivatives:  
- Are resistant to host T-cell-mediated rejection  
- Maintain antitumor potency and safety features  
- Represent true "off-the-shelf" universal therapeutics  

### 6. Therapeutic Applications  

The AlloHSC-iNKT platform is suitable for treating various cancers, particularly:  
- Hematologic malignancies (leukemia, lymphoma, myeloma)  
- Solid tumors (melanoma, lung, prostate cancers)  

Dosing regimens may involve:  
- 10^8-10^9 cells/kg administered intravenously  
- Optional αGC co-stimulation to enhance persistence  
- GCV activation of suicide switch if toxicity occurs  

Preclinical studies demonstrate superior efficacy compared to conventional CAR-T or NK cells, with complete tumor eradication in xenograft models of melanoma and multiple myeloma.  

### EXAMPLES  

The following examples illustrate specific embodiments of the invention without limiting its scope.  

**Example 1: Production of AlloHSC-iNKT Cells**  
CD34+ HSCs from cord blood were transduced with Lenti/iNKT-sr39TK (MOI=10) and cultured in ATOs for 8 weeks, followed by αGC expansion for 2 weeks. The process yielded 5.2×10^11 cells from 5×10^6 HSCs (104-fold expansion) with 99.3% iNKT purity (n=12 donors).  

**Example 2: Antitumor Activity**  
In a melanoma xenograft model, AlloHSC-iNKT cells (1.2×10^7) mediated superior tumor regression compared to PBMC-NK cells (85% vs. 45% reduction at day 21; p<0.001). Frozen-thawed AlloHSC-iNKT retained full activity, while NK cell potency declined >50%.  

**Example 3: CAR Engineering**  
BCMA-CAR-engineered AlloHSC-iNKT cells eliminated MM.1S myeloma cells more effectively than conventional CAR-T cells in high tumor burden settings (70% vs. 30% survival at 4 months; p<0.01), without causing GvHD.  

**Example 4: HLA Ablation**  
B2M/CIITA-edited UHSC-iNKT cells showed undetectable IFN-γ production in MLR assays (vs. 450 pg/mL for unedited cells; p<0.0001) while maintaining tumor killing capacity.  

## ILLUSTRATIVE VECTOR SEQUENCES  

The invention encompasses the following representative vector sequences:  

1. **Lenti/iNKT-sr39TK**:  
   - iNKT TCRα (Vα24-Jα18) - F2A linker - iNKT TCRβ (Vβ11) - P2A linker - sr39TK suicide gene

These vectors enable efficient gene delivery while maintaining optimal cell viability and function. Alternative configurations using different linkers, promoters, or reporter genes fall within the scope of the invention.
