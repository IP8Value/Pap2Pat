# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of immunotherapy, specifically to monoclonal antibodies targeting TIGIT (T-cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains) and methods of using such antibodies for the treatment of cancer. The invention encompasses anti-TIGIT antibodies, including humanized variants, compositions comprising such antibodies, and methods for their use in modulating immune responses, particularly in combination with anti-PD-1 antibodies.  

## BACKGROUND  

TIGIT is an inhibitory receptor expressed on activated T cells, regulatory T cells (Tregs), and natural killer (NK) cells. It binds with high affinity to its ligands PVR (CD155) and PVRL2 (CD112), thereby suppressing immune responses. TIGIT plays a critical role in immune evasion by tumors, as its expression is upregulated in the tumor microenvironment (TME) and correlates with poor clinical outcomes.  

Current immune checkpoint inhibitors, such as anti-PD-1/PD-L1 antibodies, have demonstrated clinical efficacy but exhibit limited response rates in many cancers. There is a need for novel immunotherapies that enhance anti-tumor immunity by targeting additional inhibitory pathways, such as TIGIT. Several anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) are in clinical development, but their optimal Fc effector functions remain unclear. The present invention addresses this gap by providing an anti-TIGIT antibody with a wild-type IgG1 Fc domain, which exhibits superior effector functions compared to Fc-silent variants.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides an anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibody, designated BGB-A1217, which binds human TIGIT with high affinity and blocks its interaction with ligands PVR and PVRL2. BGB-A1217 is derived from a murine antibody (mu1217) and humanized via CDR grafting. The antibody comprises a wild-type IgG1 Fc domain, enabling potent FcγR-mediated effector functions, including antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC), NK cell activation, and trogocytosis-mediated TIGIT removal.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  
- Anti-TIGIT antibodies with variable regions derived from mu1217 and human framework regions.  
- Antibodies with wild-type IgG1 Fc (BGB-A1217) or effector-silent Fc (BGB-A1217MF).  
- Methods of using anti-TIGIT antibodies to enhance T-cell and NK-cell activity.  
- Pharmaceutical compositions comprising anti-TIGIT antibodies, optionally combined with anti-PD-1 antibodies.  
- Methods of treating cancer by administering anti-TIGIT antibodies alone or in combination with PD-1 blockade.  

Preclinical data demonstrate that BGB-A1217 exhibits superior anti-tumor efficacy compared to Fc-silent variants, mediated by Treg depletion, NK activation, and trogocytosis. The antibody also synergizes with anti-PD-1 therapy, enhancing T-cell responses in vitro and in vivo.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### Definitions  

- **TIGIT**: T-cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains, a checkpoint inhibitory receptor.  
- **PVR (CD155)** and **PVRL2 (CD112)**: Ligands of TIGIT that mediate immune suppression.  
- **FcγRs**: Fc gamma receptors that bind the Fc region of antibodies, mediating effector functions.  
- **ADCC**: Antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, an immune mechanism where FcγR-bearing cells kill antibody-bound target cells.  
- **Trogocytosis**: FcγR-mediated removal of cell-surface antigens via endocytosis.  

### Anti-TIGIT Antibodies  

The invention provides anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibodies, particularly BGB-A1217, which binds human TIGIT with high affinity (KD = 0.135 nM). The antibody blocks TIGIT-PVR and TIGIT-PVRL2 interactions, relieving immune suppression. BGB-A1217 is derived from murine clone mu1217 and humanized to reduce immunogenicity.  

### Anti-PD-1 Antibodies  

The invention encompasses combination therapies using anti-TIGIT antibodies (e.g., BGB-A1217) with anti-PD-1 antibodies (e.g., BGB-A317). Synergistic effects include enhanced IFN-γ production and tumor growth inhibition in preclinical models.  

### Further Alteration of the Framework of Fc Region  

The Fc region of BGB-A1217 may be modified to enhance or reduce effector functions. For example, mutations (e.g., L234A/L235A) can ablate FcγR binding (BGB-A1217MF), while afucosylation can enhance ADCC.  

### Antibody Production  

Anti-TIGIT antibodies are produced via recombinant expression in mammalian cells (e.g., CHO cells). Purification involves protein A/G chromatography and polishing steps (e.g., size-exclusion chromatography).  

### Methods of Detection and Diagnosis  

Anti-TIGIT antibodies are useful for detecting TIGIT expression in tumors via immunohistochemistry (IHC) or flow cytometry. High TIGIT expression may predict responsiveness to therapy.  

### Methods of Treatment  

The invention provides methods for treating cancer (e.g., melanoma, lung cancer) by administering anti-TIGIT antibodies alone or with anti-PD-1 therapy. Dosing regimens may include intravenous or subcutaneous administration.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions and Formulations  

Pharmaceutical compositions comprise anti-TIGIT antibodies in buffered solutions (e.g., PBS, pH 7.4) with stabilizers (e.g., polysorbate 80). Lyophilized formulations are also provided.  

### Pharmaceutical Compositions and Kits  

Kits include anti-TIGIT antibodies, optionally with anti-PD-1 antibodies, and instructions for use in cancer therapy.  

## EXAMPLE  

### Example 1: Generation of Anti-TIGIT Monoclonal Antibody  

Hybridoma technology was used to generate murine anti-TIGIT antibody mu1217. Variable regions were sequenced and humanized via CDR grafting to create BGB-A1217.  

### Example 2: Cloning and Sequence Analysis of TIGIT Antibodies  

The heavy and light chain variable regions of mu1217 were cloned into expression vectors for human IgG1 production. Sequence analysis confirmed correct humanization.  

### Example 3: Affinity Determination of Purified Murine Anti-TIGIT Antibodies by SPR  

Surface plasmon resonance (SPR) confirmed high-affinity binding of BGB-A1217 to human TIGIT (KD = 0.135 nM).  

### Example 4: Humanization of the Murine Anti-Human TIGIT mAb mu1217  

Framework regions of mu1217 were replaced with human germline sequences, retaining CDRs. The humanized antibody (BGB-A1217) exhibited comparable binding to mu1217.  

### Example 5: Binding Activities of Different Versions of 1217 to Native TIGIT  

BGB-A1217 and BGB-A1217MF showed identical TIGIT binding but differed in FcγR interactions.  

### Example 6: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies Block the Interactions of TIGIT with its Ligands PVR and PVR-L2  

Ligand competition assays demonstrated complete blockade of TIGIT-PVR/PVRL2 by BGB-A1217 (IC50 = 4.53 nM and 1.69 nM, respectively).  

### Example 7: Activation of CMV-Specific Human T Cells by Anti-TIGIT Antibodies  

BGB-A1217 enhanced IFN-γ secretion by CMV-specific T cells in a dose-dependent manner.  

### Example 8: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies Enhanced NK Cell-Mediated Cytotoxicity  

BGB-A1217, but not BGB-A1217MF, activated NK cells and induced ADCC against TIGIT+ cells.  

### Example 9: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies can Reduce the Surface Expression of TIGIT Receptor Via FcγR-Mediated Trogocytosis  

BGB-A1217 induced FcγR-dependent removal of TIGIT from T cells, a mechanism absent in BGB-A1217MF.
