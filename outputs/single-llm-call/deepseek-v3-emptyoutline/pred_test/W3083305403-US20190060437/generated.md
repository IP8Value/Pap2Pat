# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of synthetic carbohydrate vaccines, particularly stabilized glycomimetics of Neisseria meningitidis serogroup A (MenA) capsular polysaccharide (CPS) and their use in conjugate vaccines. More specifically, the invention provides carbaMenA oligomers comprising 8 repeating units (DP8) that are chemically stabilized through replacement of the ring oxygen with a methylene group, along with methods for their synthesis and conjugation to carrier proteins. The invention further encompasses acetylated variants of these carbaMenA DP8 oligomers that demonstrate enhanced immunogenicity and mimicry of the natural MenA CPS, as well as vaccine formulations containing these glycomimetic conjugates.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Vaccination remains the most effective strategy for preventing infectious diseases caused by encapsulated bacteria such as Neisseria meningitidis. While glycoconjugate vaccines based on natural polysaccharides have achieved clinical success, these biomolecules suffer from inherent instability due to hydrolytic cleavage of phosphodiester linkages in aqueous environments. This is particularly problematic for MenA CPS, which contains labile (1→6)-linked 2-acetamido-2-deoxy-α-D-mannopyranosyl phosphate repeating units that readily degrade under physiological conditions.  

Previous attempts to stabilize MenA antigens have included:  
1) Chemical synthesis of natural MenA oligosaccharide fragments  
2) Generation of C-phosphono analogs where the anomeric oxygen is replaced with a methylene group  
3) Preparation of carba-sugar analogs with ring-oxygen substitution  

However, these approaches have yielded only short oligomers (up to 3 repeating units) with limited immunogenicity. Moreover, while conjugation to carrier proteins like CRM197 can improve stability, the resulting vaccines still require cold chain storage and have suboptimal shelf lives in liquid formulations. There remains an unmet need for fully synthetic, hydrolytically stable MenA glycomimetics that can:  
a) Accurately mimic the natural polysaccharide's antigenic structure  
b) Induce robust bactericidal antibody responses  
c) Maintain stability in liquid formulations without refrigeration  

## DISCLOSURE OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention overcomes these limitations through the design and synthesis of carbaMenA oligomers containing 8 repeating units (DP8) that demonstrate:  

1) Exceptional hydrolytic stability compared to natural MenA CPS, with no detectable degradation after 56 days at 37°C in pH 7 buffer  
2) Effective mimicry of native MenA CPS antigenic structure, as evidenced by:  
   - Binding to bactericidal anti-MenA monoclonal antibodies  
   - Recognition by polyclonal sera raised against natural MenA conjugates  
3) Capacity to induce functional immune responses when conjugated to carrier proteins  

A critical advancement is the introduction of random O-acetylation at C3 and C4 positions of the carbaMenA DP8 structure, which:  
- Improves antigenic mimicry by 4 orders of magnitude compared to non-acetylated analogs  
- Enables generation of bactericidal antibody titers equivalent to natural MenA conjugate vaccines  
- Maintains the enhanced stability of the carba-sugar scaffold

## MODES FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION  

The invention may be practiced through the following non-limiting examples:  

**Example 1: Synthesis of CarbaMenA DP8**  
1. Prepare carbasugar building block 9 from 3,4,6-tri-O-acetyl-D-glucal  
2. Deprotect to form diol 11 (78% yield over two steps)  
3. Install DMTr protecting group to give 12 (91% yield)  
4. Convert to phosphoramidite 13 (94% yield)  
5. Initiate synthesis with aminohexanol spacer using phosphoramidite 14  
6. Perform iterative couplings with building block 13 using:  
   - Dicyanoimidazole activation  
   - CSO oxidation  
   - TCA deprotection  
7. Purify intermediates by size-exclusion chromatography  
8. Conduct global deprotection using:  
   - Aqueous ammonia for cyanoethyl removal  
   - Hydrogenolysis for benzyl ether cleavage  
9. Obtain final carbaMenA DP8 in 40% overall yield from 12  

**Example 2: Acetylation of CarbaMenA DP8**  
1. Protect linker amine with Boc group  
2. Treat with Ac2O/imidazole at 40°C for 9 days  
3. Monitor acetylation progress by NMR  
4. Remove Boc group with TFA  
5. Purify to obtain product with 75% acetylation (56% yield)  

**Example 3: Conjugation to CRM197**  
1. Activate oligomer amine with di-N-hydroxysuccinimidyl adipate  
2. Purify by acetone precipitation  
3. React with CRM197 at pH 7.2 (40:1 molar ratio)  
4. Purify conjugate by tangential flow filtration  
5. Characterize by MALDI-TOF (saccharide/protein ratio 10-17:1)  

**Example 4: Immunogenicity Testing**  
1. Immunize BALB/c mice with 2 μg doses at days 1, 14, 28  
2. Measure antibody titers by ELISA against:  
   - Conjugate itself  
   - Natural MenA CPS  
   - Deacetylated MenA CPS  
3. Assess bactericidal activity using:  
   - Rabbit complement (rSBA)  
   - Human complement (hSBA)  
4. Results show:  
   - Acetylated carbaMenA DP8 conjugate induces GMT of X  
   - Bactericidal titers equivalent to natural MenA conjugate controls  

**Example 5: Stability Testing**  
1. Incubate carbaMenA DP8 and natural MenA fragments in pH 7 buffer at 37°C  
2. Monitor degradation by 31P NMR over 56 days  
3. Results demonstrate:  
   - No detectable hydrolysis of carbaMenA DP8  
   - Natural MenA avDP~15 degrades to avDP 12.8 by day 28  
   - Deacetylated natural MenA degrades to avDP 7 by day 28
