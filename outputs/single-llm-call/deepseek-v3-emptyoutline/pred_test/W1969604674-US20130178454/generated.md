# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to compositions and methods for selectively increasing testosterone levels in a subject while minimizing undesirable effects on prostate growth. More specifically, the invention pertains to pharmaceutical compositions comprising testosterone or its analogues or derivatives in combination with ornithine decarboxylase (ODC) inhibitors, which enhance anabolic effects on skeletal muscle while sparing the prostate. The invention further provides methods of treating low testosterone disorders, including hypogonadism and age-related functional decline, using such compositions.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Testosterone is a critical androgen that regulates muscle mass, strength, and physical function in men. However, testosterone therapy is associated with undesirable prostatic effects, including benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH) and increased risk of prostate cancer. Current selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs) aim to mitigate these effects but often exhibit incomplete selectivity or suboptimal efficacy.  

Polyamine biosynthesis, regulated by ornithine decarboxylase (ODC), plays a key role in prostate growth. Testosterone upregulates ODC activity in the prostate but not in skeletal muscle, suggesting that ODC inhibition could selectively block testosterone’s prostatic effects while preserving its anabolic benefits. The irreversible ODC inhibitor α-difluoromethylornithine (DFMO) has been used clinically for other indications, supporting its repurposing in combination with testosterone.  

There remains an unmet need for therapies that harness testosterone’s anabolic effects without compromising prostate safety. The present invention addresses this need by combining testosterone with ODC inhibitors to achieve tissue-selective androgen action.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides compositions and methods for increasing testosterone levels in a subject while minimizing prostate stimulation. Key aspects include:  

1. **Compositions**: Pharmaceutical formulations comprising testosterone (or its analogues/derivatives) and an ODC inhibitor (e.g., DFMO), optionally with pharmaceutically acceptable carriers.  
2. **Methods**: Administering the compositions to subjects with low testosterone levels to increase muscle mass and strength without promoting prostate growth.  
3. **Formulations**: Oral, injectable, or transdermal delivery systems optimized for co-administration of testosterone and ODC inhibitors.  
4. **Therapeutic Applications**: Treatment of hypogonadism, age-related functional decline, and other low testosterone disorders in at-risk populations (e.g., older men or prostate cancer survivors).  

The invention is supported by preclinical data demonstrating that DFMO blocks testosterone-induced prostate growth while preserving its anabolic effects on muscle.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### DEFINITIONS  

- **Testosterone**: Includes testosterone propionate, enanthate, cypionate, undecanoate, and other esterified or non-esterified forms, as well as synthetic androgens (e.g., methyltestosterone) and selective androgen receptor modulators (SARMs).  
- **ODC Inhibitors**: Compounds that inhibit ornithine decarboxylase activity, including DFMO, derivatives thereof, and other irreversible or reversible ODC inhibitors.  
- **Low Testosterone Disorders**: Conditions characterized by serum testosterone levels below 300 ng/dL, including hypogonadism, age-related functional decline, and androgen deficiency syndromes.  

### Testosterone  

Testosterone and its derivatives are administered at doses sufficient to restore physiological levels (e.g., 2.5–10 mg/day for oral formulations or 50–200 mg/week for injectables). Preferred analogues include testosterone undecanoate for oral use and testosterone enanthate for intramuscular injection.  

### Compositions Comprising Testosterone or Analogues or Derivatives Thereof and ODC Inhibitors  

The compositions comprise:  
1. **Testosterone Component**: 0.1–500 mg of testosterone or equivalent molar amounts of analogues/derivatives.  
2. **ODC Inhibitor Component**: 1–1000 mg of DFMO or equivalent doses of other ODC inhibitors.  
3. **Optional Additives**: Carriers (e.g., sesame oil for injectables), stabilizers, or absorption enhancers (e.g., cyclodextrins for oral formulations).  

Exemplary ratios of testosterone to ODC inhibitor range from 1:1 to 1:100 (w/w).  

### Method for Increasing Testosterone Levels in a Subject  

The method comprises administering to a subject in need thereof a therapeutically effective amount of a composition as described herein. Key steps include:  
1. **Diagnosis**: Confirming low testosterone levels via serum testing.  
2. **Dosing**: Adjusting testosterone and ODC inhibitor doses based on age, weight, and clinical response.  
3. **Monitoring**: Assessing muscle mass (e.g., DEXA scans) and prostate-specific antigen (PSA) levels periodically.  

### Oral Formulations  

Preferred oral formulations include:  
- **Testosterone undecanoate** (40–240 mg/day) in lipid-based capsules.  
- **DFMO** (100–500 mg/day) as tablets or granules.  
- **Combination tablets** with enteric coatings to enhance stability.  

### Doses and Administration Regimens  

- **Testosterone**: 50–200 mg intramuscularly every 2–4 weeks *or* 2.5–10 mg orally daily.  
- **DFMO**: 200–1000 mg/day orally or 7.5–15 mg/day intraperitoneally.  
- **Duration**: Continuous or cyclic regimens (e.g., 4–12 weeks on/off).  

### Low Testosterone Disorders and Subjects Amenable to Treatment  

Target populations include:  
1. **Hypogonadal Men**: With total testosterone <300 ng/dL and symptoms (e.g., fatigue, reduced libido).  
2. **Aging Men**: With sarcopenia or functional decline.  
3. **Prostate Cancer Survivors**: After radical prostatectomy, provided no residual disease is present.  

### Other Uses of the Methods and PDE8 Inhibitors Dual PDE8/PDE4 Inhibitor Compounds  

The compositions may further include PDE8 or dual PDE8/PDE4 inhibitors to enhance muscle anabolism synergistically.  

### Kits  

Kits comprise:  
1. **Testosterone and ODC inhibitor** in separate or combined dosage forms.  
2. **Instructions** for use and dose titration.  
3. **Monitoring tools** (e.g., PSA test strips).  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Preclinical Efficacy of Testosterone + DFMO in Castrated Mice**  
- **Methods**: Castrated male mice received:  
  - Group 1: Vehicle (control).  
  - Group 2: Testosterone (15 μg/day).  
  - Group 3: Testosterone + DFMO (15 μg/day each).  
- **Results**:  
  - Group 2 showed restored prostate and levator ani weights.  
  - Group 3 exhibited restored levator ani weight but no prostate growth.  
- **Conclusion**: DFMO selectively blocks testosterone’s prostatic effects.
