# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of cardioprotective compositions, specifically to acidic polysaccharides derived from Panax ginseng (ginseng) and their use in preventing or reducing myocardial injury caused by ischemia-reperfusion (I/R) or hypoxia-reoxygenation (H/R) conditions. More particularly, the invention provides an isolated acidic polysaccharide fraction, designated as AP1, which exhibits potent cardioprotective effects by modulating mitochondrial function, reducing oxidative stress, and activating the RISK (Reperfusion Injury Salvage Kinase) signaling pathway. The invention further encompasses pharmaceutical compositions, functional food supplements, and therapeutic methods utilizing AP1 for the treatment or prevention of ischemic heart disease (IHD) and related cardiovascular conditions.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Ischemic heart disease (IHD) remains the leading cause of global mortality, accounting for approximately 7.4 million deaths annually. A critical factor in IHD-related myocardial damage is the deprivation of oxygen and nutrients due to coronary occlusion. While timely reperfusion can mitigate short-term mortality, it often exacerbates long-term cardiac remodeling due to oxidative stress and inflammation triggered during reperfusion. Current therapeutic strategies focus on minimizing reperfusion injury, but existing pharmacological interventions often suffer from adverse effects, limiting their suitability for prolonged use.  

Functional foods and herbal supplements have gained attention as alternative or adjunctive therapies due to their favorable safety profiles. Ginseng (Panax ginseng C.A. Meyer), a traditional Chinese medicinal herb, has been extensively studied for its cardiovascular benefits, including reduction of myocardial infarct size and improvement of lipid profiles. While ginsenosides—glycosidic compounds in ginseng—have been the primary focus of research, polysaccharides from ginseng have been largely overlooked despite demonstrated immunomodulatory, antioxidative, and anticancer properties. Prior art has not established the cardioprotective efficacy of ginseng polysaccharides or their mechanisms of action, particularly in the context of I/R injury.  

There exists an unmet need for novel cardioprotective agents that can effectively mitigate reperfusion injury while minimizing adverse effects. The present invention addresses this need by identifying and characterizing AP1, an acidic polysaccharide fraction from ginseng, which exhibits superior cardioprotective properties through unique mechanisms involving mitochondrial preservation and RISK pathway activation.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides an isolated acidic polysaccharide fraction (AP1) derived from Panax ginseng, wherein AP1 comprises glucose (76.31%), galactose (12.70%), galacturonic acid (5.64%), arabinose (3.72%), and glucuronic acid (1.64%), with an apparent molecular weight of approximately 3.8 × 10^4 Da. AP1 is characterized by its ability to:  
1. Reduce cardiomyocyte apoptosis and necrosis during H/R or I/R injury, as evidenced by decreased lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) and creatine kinase (CK) release.  
2. Attenuate oxidative stress by lowering malondialdehyde (MDA) levels, enhancing superoxide dismutase (SOD) activity, and suppressing reactive oxygen species (ROS) generation.  
3. Preserve mitochondrial function by maintaining mitochondrial membrane potential (MMP), adenosine triphosphate (ATP) production, and oxygen consumption rate (OCR).  
4. Activate the RISK pathway via glucocorticoid receptor (GR) and estrogen receptor (ER) signaling, leading to phosphorylation of PI3K/Akt, ERK1/2, and endothelial nitric oxide synthase (eNOS), with subsequent nitric oxide (NO) production.  

The invention further encompasses:  
- Pharmaceutical compositions comprising AP1 and a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier, formulated for oral, intravenous, or intramuscular administration.  
- Functional food supplements containing AP1 for cardiovascular health maintenance.  
- Methods for preventing or treating I/R injury in a subject by administering a therapeutically effective amount of AP1.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### 1. Composition and Isolation of AP1  
AP1 is isolated from Panax ginseng through a bioassay-guided fractionation process comprising the following steps:  
1. **Ethanol Extraction**: Ginseng roots are extracted with ethanol to obtain a crude extract (RSE).  
2. **High-Speed Counter-Current Chromatography (HSCCC)**: RSE is fractionated using a butanol-water (1:1) solvent system, yielding bioactive fractions.  
3. **Stepwise Ethanol Precipitation**: Active fractions are precipitated with 70% ethanol to enrich polysaccharides.  
4. **Ion-Exchange Chromatography**: The precipitate is further purified using a Toyopearl DEAE 650 M column, eluting with NaCl gradients to isolate AP1.  

AP1 is characterized as an acidic heteroglycan with 90.28% carbohydrate content, 17.93% uronic acid, and 9.85% protein. Its monosaccharide composition and molecular weight are confirmed by high-performance gel permeation chromatography (HPGPC) and HPLC.  

### 2. Cardioprotective Mechanisms  
AP1 exerts cardioprotection through multimodal mechanisms:  

#### a) Mitochondrial Protection  
- **MMP Preservation**: AP1 prevents H/R-induced MMP dissipation, as measured by rhodamine 123 fluorescence.  
- **ATP Restoration**: AP1 enhances ATP production by 2.5-fold compared to H/R-injured controls.  
- **OCR Improvement**: AP1 normalizes basal respiration, ATP-linked respiration, and maximal respiratory capacity in Seahorse assays.  

#### b) Antioxidant Effects  
- AP1 reduces intracellular and mitochondrial ROS by 40–60% (measured via DCFH-DA and mitoSOX probes).  
- It lowers MDA levels by 35% and increases SOD activity by 25%, indicating attenuated lipid peroxidation.  

#### c) RISK Pathway Activation  
- AP1 upregulates GR and ER expression, triggering PI3K/Akt and ERK1/2 phosphorylation.  
- This cascade activates eNOS, increasing NO production by 3-fold, which inhibits apoptosis and stabilizes mitochondrial function.  

### 3. Therapeutic Applications  
AP1 is effective in:  
- **Acute Myocardial Infarction**: Administered during reperfusion to limit infarct size.  
- **Chronic IHD**: Used as a maintenance therapy to prevent recurrent ischemic events.  
- **Cardiac Surgery**: Applied perioperatively to reduce ischemia-reperfusion injury.  

Dosages range from 12.5 to 200 μg/mL in vitro, with equivalent human doses of 0.1–10 mg/kg/day.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Isolation and Characterization of AP1  
1. Ginseng roots were extracted with 70% ethanol, concentrated, and fractionated by HSCCC.  
2. The active fraction was precipitated with 70% ethanol, yielding a polysaccharide-rich precipitate.  
3. Further purification via DEAE chromatography yielded AP1, which was dialyzed and lyophilized.  
4. HPGPC confirmed AP1’s molecular weight (3.8 × 10^4 Da), and HPLC identified its monosaccharide profile.  

### Example 2: In Vitro Cardioprotection in H9c2 Cells  
1. H9c2 cardiomyocytes were subjected to 3 h hypoxia followed by 4 h reoxygenation (H/R).  
2. Pretreatment with AP1 (200 μg/mL) increased cell viability by 80% (vs. 45% in H/R controls).  
3. AP1 reduced LDH release by 60% and caspase-3/7 activity by 50%, confirming antiapoptotic effects.  
4. Mechanistic studies showed AP1 suppressed JNK/p38 phosphorylation by 70%, while enhancing eNOS activity 3-fold.
