# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of targeted protein degradation therapeutics, specifically to novel Proteolysis Targeting Chimeras (PROTACs) designed for the simultaneous degradation of Smad3 and stabilization of hypoxia-inducible factor-2α (HIF-2α). More particularly, the invention discloses a bifunctional PROTAC molecule comprising a Smad3-binding small molecule compound (SMC), a von Hippel-Lindau (VHL)-binding SMC, and a linker connecting the two moieties. This PROTAC exhibits dual therapeutic effects by hijacking the ubiquitin-proteasome pathway (UPP) to degrade Smad3—a core mediator of fibrosis—while concurrently stabilizing HIF-2α, a renoprotective factor. The invention further encompasses methods of synthesizing and optimizing the PROTAC, pharmaceutical compositions containing the PROTAC, and its use in treating renal fibrosis, chronic kidney disease (CKD), and renal anemia.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Proteolysis Targeting Chimeras (PROTACs) represent a revolutionary therapeutic modality that induces targeted protein degradation via the ubiquitin-proteasome system. A typical PROTAC consists of three components: (1) a ligand binding to a protein of interest (POI), (2) a recognition moiety for an E3 ubiquitin ligase, and (3) a linker connecting these two parts. By recruiting an E3 ligase to the POI, PROTACs facilitate polyubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation of the target protein.  

Despite their promise, conventional PROTACs face limitations. For instance, excessive recruitment of E3 ligases may disrupt the degradation of the ligase’s natural substrates, potentially leading to unintended biological consequences. Additionally, many PROTACs rely on inhibitors as POI-binding moieties, which can be ineffective for targets like Smad3, whose activity depends on phosphorylation. Traditional Smad3 inhibitors block phosphorylation, rendering them incompatible with PROTAC-mediated ubiquitination.  

Smad3 is a central effector of the TGF-β signaling pathway and a key driver of fibrosis in chronic kidney disease. Conversely, HIF-2α, regulated by the E3 ligase VHL, exerts renoprotective effects by promoting erythropoiesis and angiogenesis. Prior attempts to target these pathways separately—such as HIF prolyl hydroxylase (PHD) inhibitors for HIF stabilization or Smad3 inhibitors for fibrosis—have shown partial efficacy but are hampered by off-target effects or incomplete pathway modulation.  

There remains an unmet need for a therapeutic agent capable of simultaneously degrading Smad3 and stabilizing HIF-2α to address the multifactorial pathology of CKD. The present invention fulfills this need by providing a rationally designed PROTAC that leverages the interdependence of these targets.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides a heterobifunctional PROTAC molecule comprising:  
1. A Smad3-binding small molecule compound (SMC) as the POI-targeting moiety;  
2. A VHL-binding SMC as the E3 ligase-recruiting moiety; and  
3. A linker covalently connecting the two moieties, preferably a succinic acid [(CH2COOH)2] linker.  

Key advantages of the invention include:  
- **Dual Mechanism**: The PROTAC degrades Smad3 via the UPP while stabilizing HIF-2α by sequestering VHL, its natural E3 ligase. This dual action combats fibrosis (via Smad3 degradation) and enhances renal repair (via HIF-2α stabilization).  
- **Optimized Linker and Chirality**: The PROTAC incorporates a succinic acid linker and an S-configured Smad3-binding SMC, which collectively enhance ubiquitination efficiency and potency.  
- **Tissue Specificity**: The PROTAC’s activity is confined to tissues with high Smad3 and VHL expression (e.g., kidneys), minimizing off-target effects.  
- **Therapeutic Versatility**: The PROTAC ameliorates renal fibrosis, preserves kidney function, and corrects renal anemia in preclinical models.  

The invention further encompasses:  
- Methods of synthesizing the PROTAC, including linker optimization and chiral configuration selection.  
- Pharmaceutical compositions comprising the PROTAC and a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier.  
- Methods of treating renal fibrosis, CKD, or renal anemia by administering the PROTAC.  

## EMBODIMENTS  

### Example 1  
**PROTAC Design and Synthesis**  

The PROTAC was synthesized by conjugating:  
1. A Smad3-binding SMC (identified via virtual screening and surface plasmon resonance) at its optimal linkage site.  
2. A VHL-binding SMC (e.g., a hydroxyproline derivative) at its carboxyl terminus.  
3. A succinic acid linker [(CH2COOH)2], selected after comparative testing against a polyethylene glycol (PEG) linker.  

**Step 1: Linker Optimization**  
Two PROTAC variants were synthesized:  
- **PROTAC-PEG**: Longer, flexible linker.  
- **PROTAC-(CH2COOH)2**: Shorter, rigid linker.  

In vitro testing in NRK-49F fibroblasts demonstrated that PROTAC-(CH2COOH)2 induced Smad3 degradation at lower concentrations (effective dose: 25 nM) compared to PROTAC-PEG, confirming the superiority of the succinic acid linker.  

**Step 2: Chirality Selection**  
The Smad3-binding SMC exists as R and S enantiomers. PROTAC-S (S-configured) showed higher potency in degrading Smad3 and stabilizing HIF-2α than PROTAC-R, as validated by Western blotting in renal cells.  

**Step 3: Structural Validation**  
The final PROTAC (750.8 Da) was characterized by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC) and mass spectrometry (MS), confirming >95% purity and correct molecular weight.  

### Example 2  
**In Vitro Efficacy**  

**Ubiquitination and Degradation Assay**  
ACHN cell lysates (high VHL expression) were incubated with recombinant Smad3 and PROTAC. Key findings:  
- Dose-dependent Smad3 degradation (Fig. 1B).  
- Proteasome inhibitor MG132 rescued Smad3 degradation and revealed polyubiquitination bands (Fig. 1C), confirming UPP dependence.  

**Cell-Type Specificity**  
- In NRK-49F fibroblasts, PROTAC (25 nM) degraded Smad3 and stabilized HIF-2α but not HIF-1α (Fig. 2D–E).  
- In HK2 tubular cells, PROTAC degraded Smad3 without affecting HIF-1α due to compensatory E3 ligases (e.g., RACK1, HAF) (Fig. 2F).  

### Example 3  
**In Vivo Therapeutic Effects**  

**Renal Fibrosis Models**  
1. **Unilateral Ureteral Obstruction (UUO)**:  
   - PROTAC (30–90 mg/kg, i.p.) reduced collagen deposition, α-SMA expression, and macrophage infiltration while restoring peritubular capillaries (Fig. 4D–G).  
   - Serum VEGF levels increased, confirming HIF-2α-mediated angiogenesis (Fig. 4H).  

2. **5/6 Nephrectomy (5/6Nx)**:  
   - PROTAC (24 mg/kg, s.c., biweekly) preserved renal function (lower serum creatinine/BUN) (Fig. 5F–H).  
   - Corrected renal anemia by elevating erythropoietin (EPO) and improving iron utilization (Fig. 5I–N).  

**Safety Profile**  
- No toxicity was observed in mice at doses up to 300 mg/kg (Fig. 3A–E).  
- Pharmacokinetics revealed rapid clearance (T1/2 = 1.58 h after s.c. dosing) and tissue distribution favoring kidneys (Fig. 3F–G).
