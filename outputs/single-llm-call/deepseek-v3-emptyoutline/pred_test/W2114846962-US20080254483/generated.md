# DESCRIPTION  

## 1. FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of medical diagnostics, particularly to methods and compositions for detecting renal cell carcinoma (RCC). More specifically, the invention provides novel biomarkers and immunoassay techniques for identifying RCC through detection of elevated levels of matrix metalloproteinase-7 (MMP-7) in biological samples. The invention further encompasses monoclonal antibodies specifically binding to MMP-7, diagnostic kits containing said antibodies, and methods for using these components in clinical settings for early detection, prognosis, and treatment monitoring of RCC.  

## 2. BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

Renal cell carcinoma represents a significant clinical challenge due to the lack of reliable biomarkers for early detection and monitoring. Current diagnostic methods rely primarily on imaging techniques that often detect tumors only at advanced stages, when treatment options are limited. The medical community has long sought specific molecular markers that could enable non-invasive detection of RCC through simple blood tests.  

Matrix metalloproteinases have been studied in various cancers, but their potential as circulating biomarkers for RCC has not been previously established. While MMP-7 expression has been observed in tumor tissues, the art has failed to recognize its presence and diagnostic value in serum. Prior attempts to identify serum markers for RCC have focused on other proteins, none of which have demonstrated sufficient sensitivity and specificity for clinical use.  

The present inventors have discovered that pro-MMP-7 serves as an unexpectedly sensitive and specific marker for RCC when detected in serum. This finding was made possible through a novel combination of proteomic analysis of RCC cell line secretomes with serological screening using patient sera. The development of specific monoclonal antibodies recognizing both pro- and active forms of MMP-7 has enabled the creation of highly sensitive detection assays with superior performance characteristics compared to existing diagnostic methods.  

## 3. SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides a method for detecting renal cell carcinoma in a subject comprising measuring the level of matrix metalloproteinase-7 (MMP-7) in a biological sample from the subject, wherein an elevated level of MMP-7 compared to a control indicates the presence of RCC. The biological sample may be serum, plasma, or other bodily fluids.  

The invention further provides isolated monoclonal antibodies that specifically bind to MMP-7, particularly antibodies that recognize both pro-MMP-7 and active MMP-7 forms. These antibodies are useful for developing diagnostic assays and kits. The preferred embodiments include antibody clones designated 01S, 04S, 21S and 29S, which demonstrate high affinity and specificity for MMP-7.  

Additionally, the invention encompasses diagnostic kits containing the anti-MMP-7 antibodies and reagents for performing immunoassays to detect MMP-7 levels. The kits may include calibration standards, detection reagents, and instructions for use in diagnosing RCC.  

The methods of the invention demonstrate superior diagnostic performance, with sensitivity of approximately 93% and specificity of 75% for detecting RCC in tested samples. The invention thus fulfills an unmet need in clinical oncology by providing the first reliable serum marker for RCC detection and monitoring.  

## MMP-7  

Matrix metalloproteinase-7 (MMP-7), also known as matrilysin, is a member of the matrix metalloproteinase family that plays important roles in tissue remodeling and cancer progression. The inventors have discovered that MMP-7 exists in two distinct forms in RCC cell line supernatants: a 29 kDa pro-enzyme (pro-MMP-7) and a 19 kDa active form. Surprisingly, only the pro-MMP-7 form was detected in serum samples from both healthy individuals and RCC patients, suggesting this may be the predominant circulating form.  

The immunogenic properties of pro-MMP-7 make it particularly suitable as a diagnostic marker. The inventors have identified specific peptide sequences within pro-MMP-7 (FYLYDSETK and VIEIMQKPR) that contribute to its antigenicity and can be targeted by diagnostic antibodies. These epitopes appear to be uniquely exposed in the context of RCC, as demonstrated by the specific immunoreactivity of RCC patient sera with pro-MMP-7 but not with control sera.  

The measurement of serum pro-MMP-7 levels provides clinically significant information. In healthy controls, the median serum pro-MMP-7 concentration is approximately 2.13 g/L (range 0.17-3.5 g/L). In contrast, RCC patients show significantly elevated levels with a median of 7.56 g/L (range 3.12-30.5 g/L). This elevation is observed in both localized (median 7.26 g/L) and metastasized (median 7.87 g/L) RCC, indicating the marker's potential utility across disease stages.  

## 4. EXPERIMENTAL SECTION AND EXAMPLES  

### Example 1: Identification of pro-MMP-7 as RCC Marker  

The CAL54 RCC cell line was cultured in serum-free DMEM medium for 48 hours to collect secreted proteins. The secretome was analyzed by two-dimensional electrophoresis (2DE) followed by immunoblotting with pooled sera from RCC patients or healthy controls. Computer-assisted comparison identified 12 immunoreactive spots specific to RCC patient sera, one of which (spot S4) was identified as pro-MMP-7 by mass spectrometry.  

Peptide mass fingerprinting confirmed the identity of pro-MMP-7, with two characteristic peptides (FYLYDSETK and VIEIMQKPR) matching residues 42-50 and 78-86 of human pro-MMP-7 (Swiss-Prot acc. no. P09237). This demonstrated that pro-MMP-7 is specifically recognized by antibodies present in RCC patient sera but not in healthy controls.  

### Example 2: Development of Anti-MMP-7 Monoclonal Antibodies  

Mice were immunized with recombinant human MMP-7 to generate monoclonal antibodies. Four clones (01S, 04S, 21S and 29S) were selected based on their ability to recognize both pro-MMP-7 (29 kDa) and active MMP-7 (19 kDa) in Western blots of CAL54 supernatant.  

Specificity testing showed all four clones recognized recombinant MMP-7. Clone 01S was particularly effective for detecting pro-MMP-7 in serum samples, while clone 29S showed strong reactivity with both forms. These antibodies were purified by protein A affinity chromatography for use in diagnostic assays.  

### Example 3: Serum MMP-7 Detection by Western Blot  

Western blot analysis of serum samples from RCC patients and healthy controls was performed using the monoclonal antibodies. A 29 kDa band corresponding to pro-MMP-7 was consistently detected in RCC patient sera but was faint or absent in control sera. No 19 kDa band (active MMP-7) was observed in any serum sample, indicating pro-MMP-7 is the predominant circulating form.  

### Example 4: Development of TRACE Immunoassay  

A time-resolved amplified cryptate emission (TRACE) immunoassay was developed using cryptate-conjugated clone 01S antibody and AF647-labeled clone 29S antibody. The assay demonstrated excellent performance characteristics:  
- Linear correlation coefficient (r²) >0.99  
- Intra-assay CV of 4.2% at 3.1 g/L  
- Average recovery of 96% (range 89-109%)  
- Assay time of 50 minutes  

### Example 5: Clinical Validation  

The TRACE immunoassay was used to measure serum MMP-7 levels in:  
- 30 healthy controls (median 2.13 g/L, range 0.17-3.5 g/L)  
- 19 localized RCC patients (median 7.26 g/L, range 3.12-27.9 g/L)  
- 11 metastasized RCC patients (median 7.87 g/L, range 4.25-30.5 g/L)  

Statistical analysis showed highly significant differences (P<0.0001) between RCC patients and controls. The assay demonstrated 93% sensitivity (95% CI 78%-99%) and 75% specificity (59%-87%) for RCC detection.  

### Example 6: Diagnostic Kit Preparation  

A diagnostic kit was prepared containing:  
- Cryptate-conjugated clone 01S antibody (0.35 mg/L)  
- AF647-labeled clone 29S antibody (5 mg/L)  
- MMP-7 calibrators (0.5-40 g/L)  
- Assay buffer  
- Instruction manual for automated analysis on Brahms Kryptor system  

The kit enables rapid, standardized measurement of serum MMP-7 levels for RCC diagnosis and monitoring.
