# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  
The present invention relates to the field of lipid mediators and their therapeutic applications in regulating inflammation and angiogenesis. Specifically, the invention pertains to the use of aspirin-triggered lipoxin A4 analog (ATLa), resolvin D1 (RvD1), and resolvin E1 (RvE1) for the treatment of inflammatory corneal neovascularization (CNV) and related conditions. These lipid mediators function as dual anti-inflammatory and pro-resolving agents, effectively suppressing hemangiogenesis (HA) while modulating immune responses in ocular tissues.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  
Corneal neovascularization (CNV) is a pathological condition characterized by the abnormal growth of blood vessels into the cornea, often resulting from inflammatory or hypoxic stimuli. This condition can lead to vision impairment and blindness if left untreated. Current therapeutic approaches primarily target vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) signaling, but these treatments are often limited by incomplete efficacy or adverse effects.  

Lipoxins and resolvins are endogenous lipid mediators derived from polyunsaturated fatty acids, known for their roles in resolving inflammation and promoting tissue repair. Aspirin-triggered lipoxin A4 (ATL) and its analogs, as well as resolvins such as RvD1 and RvE1, have demonstrated potent anti-inflammatory properties. However, their specific effects on corneal angiogenesis and the underlying molecular mechanisms remain underexplored.  

There exists a need for novel therapeutic agents that not only suppress pathological angiogenesis but also modulate inflammatory responses without inducing systemic side effects. The present invention addresses this need by demonstrating the efficacy of ATLa, RvD1, and RvE1 in reducing CNV through direct inhibition of VEGF-mediated angiogenesis and resolution of innate immune responses.  

## BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  
The invention provides methods for treating inflammatory corneal neovascularization (CNV) using lipid mediators, including aspirin-triggered lipoxin A4 analog (ATLa), resolvin D1 (RvD1), and resolvin E1 (RvE1). These compounds act through specific receptors (Fpr-rs2 for ATLa and ChemR23 for RvE1) expressed in corneal epithelial cells, stromal keratocytes, and infiltrating immune cells.  

Key findings of the invention include:  
1. **Reduction of Immune Cell Infiltration**: ATLa, RvD1, and RvE1 significantly decrease neutrophil and macrophage infiltration into inflamed corneas.  
2. **Suppression of Pro-Inflammatory Cytokines**: These mediators downregulate mRNA expression of TNF-α, IL-1α, IL-1β, VEGF-A, VEGF-C, and VEGFR2.  
3. **Inhibition of Hemangiogenesis (HA)**: All three compounds suppress suture-induced and IL-1β-induced HA, with ATLa showing additional efficacy in directly inhibiting VEGF-A-mediated angiogenesis.  
4. **Selective Action on Lymphangiogenesis (LA)**: Unlike HA, lymphatic vessel growth remains unaffected by treatment, indicating specificity in angiogenic regulation.  

The invention further encompasses pharmaceutical compositions comprising ATLa, RvD1, or RvE1 for ocular administration, including subconjunctival injection, topical application, or sustained-release formulations.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### Results  

#### Lipoxin A4 and Resolvin E1 Receptor Expression in the Cornea  
The receptors for ATLa (Fpr-rs2) and RvE1 (ChemR23) were identified in corneal epithelial and stromal-endothelial layers via RT-PCR. Notably, infiltrating CD11b⁺ immune cells in inflamed corneas also expressed these receptors, confirming their role in mediating the effects of ATLa and RvE1.  

#### Reduction of Neutrophil and Macrophage Infiltration  
Treatment with ATLa, RvD1, or RvE1 led to a marked decrease in neutrophil and macrophage infiltration into suture-induced inflamed corneas compared to vehicle controls. This effect was observed as early as 24 hours post-treatment and persisted at 72 hours.  

#### Suppression of Inflammatory Cytokines and Angiogenic Factors  
Real-time PCR analysis revealed significant reductions in mRNA levels of TNF-α, IL-1β, VEGF-A, VEGF-C, and VEGFR2 in treated corneas. Notably, IL-1α expression remained unchanged, suggesting selective modulation of pro-inflammatory pathways.  

#### Inhibition of Corneal Neovascularization  
Slit lamp biomicroscopy and immunohistochemical staining demonstrated that ATLa, RvD1, and RvE1 suppressed suture-induced HA without affecting lymphatic vessel growth. ATLa exhibited superior efficacy in directly inhibiting VEGF-A-induced angiogenesis, distinguishing it from RvD1 and RvE1.  

### Discussion  

The findings establish ATLa, RvD1, and RvE1 as potent regulators of corneal inflammation and angiogenesis. Their mechanisms include:  
- **Resolution of Innate Immunity**: By reducing neutrophil and macrophage infiltration, these mediators shorten the duration of inflammation.  
- **Modulation of VEGF Signaling**: Downregulation of VEGF-A and VEGFR2 expression contributes to the suppression of HA.  
- **Receptor-Specific Actions**: ATLa’s unique ability to inhibit VEGF-A-induced angiogenesis suggests additional signaling pathways independent of immune modulation.  

These lipid mediators offer a novel therapeutic strategy for CNV, combining anti-inflammatory and anti-angiogenic properties with minimal off-target effects.  

### Methods  

#### Animal Models  
- **Suture-Induced CNV**: Three interrupted nylon sutures were placed intrastromally in BALB/c mice to induce inflammatory CNV.  
- **Corneal Micropocket Assay**: Hydron pellets containing IL-1β or VEGF-A were implanted to assess HA and LA.  

#### Treatment Protocols  
- Compounds (100 ng/10 μL) were administered via subconjunctival injection every 48 hours.  
- Controls received vehicle (normal saline).  

#### Molecular Analyses  
- **RT-PCR and Real-Time PCR**: Used to quantify receptor expression (Fpr-rs2, ChemR23) and cytokine/angiogenic factor mRNA levels.  
- **Immunohistochemistry**: Corneas were stained with anti-CD31 (blood vessels) and anti-LYVE-1 (lymphatics) for morphometric analysis.  

### Lipoxins  
Lipoxins, particularly ATLa, exhibit enhanced stability and prolonged activity compared to endogenous LXA4 due to structural modifications that resist enzymatic degradation. Their dual role in resolving inflammation and inhibiting angiogenesis positions them as superior candidates for treating ocular neovascular disorders.
