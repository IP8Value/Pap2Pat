# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of immunology and neuropharmacology, specifically to novel peptide antagonists of interleukin-1 receptor type I (IL-1RI) and their use in treating inflammatory conditions. More particularly, the invention provides synthetic peptide fragments derived from the N-terminal domain of interleukin-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra) that competitively inhibit IL-1 signaling through IL-1RI. The invention further encompasses pharmaceutical compositions containing these peptides and methods for their use in treating peripheral and neuroinflammatory disorders including but not limited to rheumatoid arthritis, diabetes, neurodegenerative diseases, and autoimmune encephalomyelitis.  

## BACKGROUND OF INVENTION  

Interleukin-1 (IL-1) is a key mediator of acute and chronic inflammatory responses and has been implicated in tissue dysfunction and destruction across various pathological conditions. The IL-1 family consists of two agonist isoforms, IL-1α and IL-1β, which signal through IL-1RI. Upon binding to IL-1RI, the interleukin-1 receptor accessory protein (IL-1RAcP) is recruited, triggering nuclear factor κB (NF-κB) and mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) signaling cascades that lead to the expression and release of inflammatory mediators.  

Current IL-1 antagonists include IL-1Ra, IL-1-neutralizing antibodies, and the IL-1 trap, a fusion protein between soluble IL-1RI and IL-1RAcP. Anakinra (IL-1Ra) is a recombinant protein that competitively blocks IL-1 actions without agonist activity. While effective for certain inflammatory conditions, these biologics face limitations including poor blood-brain barrier (BBB) penetration, short therapeutic duration, and injection site reactions. There remains an unmet need for low-molecular-weight IL-1 antagonists with improved pharmacokinetic properties and BBB penetration for treating neuroinflammatory disorders.  

## SUMMARY OF INVENTION  

The present invention provides novel peptide antagonists of IL-1RI, collectively termed Ilantide, derived from the N-terminal domain of human IL-1Ra involved in IL-1RI interactions. The peptides competitively inhibit IL-1 binding to IL-1RI and subsequent inflammatory signaling.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  
1. A synthetic peptide comprising the sequence SGRKSSKMQA (Ilantide) or its functional variants  
2. Multimeric forms of Ilantide, particularly tetrameric dendrimers (Ilantide-t)  
3. Pharmaceutical compositions containing Ilantide peptides  
4. Methods for treating inflammatory conditions by administering effective amounts of Ilantide  

The peptides demonstrate high affinity binding to IL-1RI, inhibition of NF-κB activation, and suppression of tumor necrosis factor α (TNF-α) secretion. Notably, Ilantide crosses the BBB and shows efficacy in models of neuroinflammation including Alzheimer's-like dementia and experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis. Compared to existing IL-1 blockers, Ilantide offers advantages of synthetic production, improved stability, and enhanced CNS penetration.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Inflammation  

Inflammation is a complex biological response to harmful stimuli characterized by immune cell activation and release of inflammatory mediators. Chronic inflammation underlies numerous pathological conditions including autoimmune diseases, metabolic disorders, and neurodegenerative diseases. The interleukin-1 family plays a central role in mediating inflammatory responses through activation of IL-1 receptor signaling pathways.  

### Interleukins  

Interleukins are cytokines that regulate immune and inflammatory responses. IL-1α and IL-1β are proinflammatory cytokines that bind IL-1RI, initiating downstream signaling cascades. IL-1Ra is a natural antagonist that binds IL-1RI without recruiting IL-1RAcP, thereby blocking IL-1 signaling. Dysregulation of IL-1 signaling contributes to various inflammatory disorders, making IL-1RI an important therapeutic target.  

### Peptide Fragments of the Present Invention  

The invention provides peptide fragments derived from the IL-1Ra N-terminal domain that retain IL-1RI binding capability. The core peptide sequence is SGRKSSKMQA (Ilantide), corresponding to residues involved in IL-1RI interaction. The peptide may be modified at termini or contain conservative amino acid substitutions that preserve binding activity.  

### Synthetic Preparation  

Ilantide peptides are synthesized using fluorenylmethyloxycarbonyl (Fmoc) solid-phase peptide synthesis on TentaGel resin. After cleavage from resin, peptides are purified by gel filtration chromatography to ≥85% purity as determined by HPLC. Multimeric forms are prepared by coupling monomers to lysine backbones.  

### Compound of the Present Invention  

The primary compound is the Ilantide peptide (SGRKSSKMQA) which binds IL-1RI with high affinity (Kd ≈ 2.75 × 10^-8 M). Surface plasmon resonance analysis confirms specific binding to IL-1RI comparable to IL-1Ra. The peptide competitively inhibits IL-1β binding to IL-1RI and subsequent signaling.  

### Multimeric Compound  

Multimeric forms, particularly tetrameric Ilantide (Ilantide-t), demonstrate enhanced efficacy. Ilantide-t shows superior inhibition of IL-1β-induced apoptosis (93% inhibition) compared to monomeric form (49% inhibition). The multimeric structure improves pharmacokinetic properties including plasma half-life (~2 hours) and BBB penetration.  

### Methods  

The invention provides methods for:  
1. Inhibiting IL-1 signaling by administering Ilantide peptides  
2. Treating inflammatory conditions by targeting IL-1RI  
3. Preventing neuronal damage by blocking neuroinflammatory responses  

### Pharmaceutical Formulation  

Pharmaceutical compositions contain Ilantide peptides in pharmaceutically acceptable carriers. Formulations may include solutions for injection, lyophilized powders, or sustained-release preparations. The peptides may be combined with other anti-inflammatory agents.  

### Dosage  

Effective doses range from 1-100 mg/kg, with preferred dose of 10 mg/kg administered subcutaneously. Treatment regimens depend on condition severity, with daily administration for acute inflammation and intermittent dosing for chronic conditions.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

Surface plasmon resonance analysis demonstrated Ilantide-t binds IL-1RI with Kd of 2.75 × 10^-8 M, comparable to IL-1Ra (4.8 × 10^-8 M) and superior to IL-1β (1.71 × 10^-7 M). Ilantide-t dose-dependently inhibited IL-1β binding to IL-1RI.  

### Example 2  

In HEK-Blue IL-1β cells, Ilantide-m and Ilantide-t dose-dependently inhibited IL-1β-induced NF-κB activation, with complete inhibition at 10^-6 M. Scrambled control peptides showed no effect.  

### Example 3  

Ilantide treatment (10^-6 M) reduced IL-1β-induced TNF-α secretion by AMJ2-C8 macrophages by 85%. The effect was comparable to IL-1Ra and superior to scrambled peptide controls.  

### Example 4  

In cerebellar granule neurons, Ilantide-t (10^-7 M) increased cell survival by 65% and stimulated neurite outgrowth by 2.5-fold compared to controls, demonstrating neuroprotective effects.  

### Example 5  

Ilantide-t (8.68 × 10^6 nM) inhibited IL-1β-induced pancreatic islet apoptosis by 93%, exceeding the 64% inhibition by anakinra at 8.68 × 10^4 nM.  

### Example 6  

Pharmacokinetic studies showed Ilantide-t (10 mg/kg s.c.) reached plasma concentrations of 5,816 ng/ml at 20 minutes with plasma half-life of ~2 hours. CSF concentrations reached 125 ng/ml at 30 minutes post-injection.  

### Example 7  

In collagen-induced arthritis, daily Ilantide-t treatment (10 mg/kg) from day 8-15 post-immunization significantly reduced clinical scores compared to vehicle (p<0.05).  

### Example 8  

Ilantide-t (10 mg/kg) counteracted LPS-induced social behavior depression and attenuated body weight loss in rats. The effect was accompanied by increased IL-10 plasma levels.  

### Example 9  

In Aβ25-35-treated rats, Ilantide-t (10 mg/kg) restored social memory deficits, with recognition ratio returning to control levels (p<0.05 vs Aβ/vehicle).  

The complete specification demonstrates that Ilantide peptides represent novel, effective IL-1 antagonists with advantages over existing therapeutics, particularly for neuroinflammatory conditions. The examples provide evidence of efficacy across multiple disease models supporting broad therapeutic applications.
