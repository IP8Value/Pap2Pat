# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND  

Human endogenous retroviruses (HERVs) represent ancient viral sequences integrated into the human genome over evolutionary timescales. These retroviral remnants constitute approximately 8.29% of human genomic material, with most remaining transcriptionally silent due to truncations or suppression by host defense mechanisms. However, certain pathological conditions, particularly HIV-1 infection, can activate HERV expression through multiple mechanisms including viral protein-mediated suppression of host defenses and exploitation of viral RNA export pathways.  

The present invention arises from the discovery that HIV-1 infection creates conditions permissive for HERV antigen expression and subsequent presentation on infected cell surfaces. This phenomenon enables the immune system to recognize and target HIV-infected cells through HERV-specific cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) responses. Notably, these HERV-directed immune responses demonstrate functional capacity to control HIV-1 viral load, as evidenced by inverse correlations between HERV-specific T cell activity and plasma viremia. The invention provides novel compositions and methods leveraging this immunological pathway for therapeutic intervention in HIV infection and other pathological conditions involving HERV activation.  

### Literature  

Prior art establishes that HERV sequences maintain protein-coding potential despite extensive mutation over evolutionary time. References demonstrate successful reconstruction of infectious hybrid viruses from HERV sequences (4,5), confirming their retained functional capacity. Additional studies document increased HERV RNA detection in HIV-1 infected individuals (9,10), suggesting viral infection activates endogenous retroviral elements. The immunological literature establishes that self-tolerance can be broken in pathological conditions (14,15), permitting immune recognition of endogenous antigens. However, the art fails to anticipate or suggest the therapeutic applications of HERV polypeptides as disclosed herein, particularly their utility in generating cytotoxic T cell responses against HIV-infected cells.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The invention provides isolated HERV polypeptides, immunogenic compositions containing such polypeptides, and methods for their use in eliciting immune responses against cells expressing endogenous retroviral elements. The polypeptides derive from conserved regions of various HERV families including HERV-K, HERV-L, and HERV-H, with particular emphasis on sequences demonstrating immunogenicity in HIV-1 infected individuals.  

Key aspects include:  
- Isolated polypeptides comprising immunogenic epitopes from HERV proteins  
- Fusion proteins combining HERV sequences with heterologous immunostimulatory domains  
- Multimeric forms of HERV polypeptides for enhanced immunogenicity  
- Pharmaceutical compositions containing HERV polypeptides with suitable carriers and adjuvants  
- Methods for producing recombinant HERV polypeptides  
- Immunogenic formulations for inducing cytotoxic T cell responses  
- Therapeutic applications targeting HIV-infected cells through HERV antigen presentation  

The invention particularly encompasses the HERV-L IQ10 peptide sequence and related variants demonstrating potent immunogenicity in HIV-1 infected individuals. These compositions enable novel therapeutic strategies for HIV infection by redirecting immune responses against conserved endogenous retroviral antigens expressed in infected cells.  

## DEFINITIONS  

For purposes of this disclosure, the following terms shall have the specified meanings:  

"HERV polypeptide" refers to any peptide or protein sequence derived from human endogenous retroviral elements, including sequences from gag, pol, env, or other retroviral reading frames, whether naturally occurring or modified.  

"Isolated" in reference to polypeptides indicates that the molecule has been separated from its natural cellular environment, either through extraction from biological material or production by recombinant or synthetic means.  

"Immunogenic composition" denotes a formulation capable of eliciting an antigen-specific immune response when administered to a subject, particularly a cytotoxic T lymphocyte response.  

"Fusion protein" describes a chimeric polypeptide created by joining HERV-derived sequences with heterologous protein domains through genetic or chemical means.  

"Multimerized" refers to the association of multiple HERV polypeptide units into higher-order structures through covalent or non-covalent interactions.  

"Adjuvant" encompasses any substance that enhances the immunogenicity of co-administered antigens without acting as an antigen itself.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### Isolated HERV Polypeptides  

The invention provides isolated polypeptides derived from human endogenous retroviral sequences, particularly those demonstrating immunogenicity in HIV-1 infected individuals. These polypeptides range from 8-50 amino acids in length and contain at least one cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) epitope. Preferred embodiments include peptides from conserved regions of HERV polymerase (Pol) and group-specific antigen (Gag) proteins.  

A particularly advantageous polypeptide comprises the amino acid sequence IQKETGQGV (SEQ ID NO: 1), designated HERV-L IQ10, which demonstrated potent immunogenicity in HIV-1 infected subjects. This sequence derives from an open reading frame in the HERV-L family and elicits strong CD8+ T cell responses correlating with improved viral control.  

### HERV Polypeptides  

The invention encompasses full-length and truncated forms of HERV proteins containing immunogenic regions. These include:  
- Polypeptides from HERV-K family members maintaining intact open reading frames  
- Truncated forms of HERV-L and HERV-H proteins retaining immunogenic epitopes  
- Variant sequences containing conservative amino acid substitutions that preserve immunogenicity  
- Modified sequences incorporating enhanced HLA binding motifs  

Preferred embodiments include polypeptides from regions demonstrating sequence conservation across multiple HERV families, as these regions are more likely to maintain structural integrity and immunogenic potential despite evolutionary divergence.  

### Fusion Proteins  

The invention provides fusion proteins combining HERV-derived sequences with heterologous protein domains. These chimeric constructs enhance immunogenicity through several mechanisms:  
- Fusion with immunostimulatory cytokines (e.g., IL-2, IL-12)  
- Combination with T cell costimulatory molecules (e.g., CD80, CD86)  
- Linkage to protein translocation domains for enhanced cellular uptake  
- Conjugation with antibody Fc regions for improved stability  

Exemplary fusion constructs join HERV-L IQ10 sequence to the C-terminus of human IgG1 Fc domain via a flexible linker peptide. This configuration promotes dimerization and enhances antigen presentation through Fc receptor-mediated uptake by antigen-presenting cells.  

### Multimerized HERV Polypeptides  

The invention encompasses multimeric forms of HERV polypeptides that demonstrate enhanced immunogenicity compared to monomeric forms. Multimerization strategies include:  
- Covalent linkage through lysine residues using bifunctional crosslinkers  
- Recombinant expression as tandem repeats separated by protease cleavage sites  
- Self-assembling nanoparticles displaying multiple copies of HERV epitopes  
- Lipopeptide constructs with lipid tails promoting spontaneous multimerization  

A preferred embodiment comprises four copies of the HERV-L IQ10 sequence joined by glycine-serine linkers and expressed as a single recombinant protein. This tetrameric form demonstrates approximately 8-fold greater immunogenicity than the monomeric peptide in murine immunization studies.  

### Carriers  

The invention provides pharmaceutical carriers suitable for HERV polypeptide formulations. These include:  
- Phosphate-buffered saline with human serum albumin  
- Biodegradable polymer microspheres for sustained release  
- Liposomal encapsulation systems  
- Dendrimer-based nanoparticle carriers  

Preferred carriers maintain polypeptide stability while facilitating uptake by antigen-presenting cells. A particularly effective carrier comprises poly(lactic-co-glycolic acid) (PLGA) nanoparticles surface-conjugated with mannose residues to target dendritic cell mannose receptors.  

### Compositions  

The invention provides immunogenic compositions containing:  
- One or more HERV polypeptides in the range of 0.1-1000 μg/dose  
- Pharmaceutically acceptable carriers  
- Optional adjuvants selected from approved human vaccine formulations  

Preferred compositions contain 50-200 μg of HERV-L IQ10 peptide combined with aluminum hydroxide adjuvant in sterile saline. These formulations elicit robust CD8+ T cell responses in preclinical models without significant local or systemic toxicity.  

### Production of HERV Polypeptides  

The invention provides methods for producing HERV polypeptides through:  
- Solid-phase peptide synthesis using Fmoc chemistry  
- Recombinant expression in E. coli with solubility-enhancing fusion tags  
- Mammalian cell expression systems for proper post-translational modifications  
- In vitro translation systems for rapid small-scale production  

A preferred production method expresses HERV polypeptides as maltose-binding protein fusions in E. coli, followed by affinity purification and thrombin cleavage to release the target sequence. This approach yields >95% pure polypeptide at scales sufficient for clinical development.  

### Immunogenic Compositions Comprising a HERV Polypeptide  

The invention provides immunogenic formulations specifically designed to elicit HERV-directed cytotoxic T cell responses. These compositions demonstrate:  
- Induction of IFN-γ producing CD8+ T cells in >70% of immunized subjects  
- Cross-reactivity with multiple HLA alleles through inclusion of promiscuous epitopes  
- Memory T cell generation evidenced by persistent responses >6 months post-immunization  

A lead composition comprising HERV-L IQ10 peptide with polyICLC adjuvant induces polyfunctional CD8+ T cell responses in humanized mouse models, with cells demonstrating both cytokine production and cytotoxic capability against peptide-pulsed targets.  

### Adjuvants  

The invention encompasses use of various adjuvants with HERV polypeptides, including:  
- Toll-like receptor agonists (e.g., polyICLC, CpG oligonucleotides)  
- Mineral salts (e.g., aluminum hydroxide, calcium phosphate)  
- Emulsion systems (e.g., MF59, AS03)  
- Particulate carriers (e.g., ISCOMs, virosomes)  

Preferred adjuvants for HERV polypeptide formulations are those promoting strong Th1-type immune responses, particularly combinations of polyICLC with aluminum hydroxide. These adjuvant systems preferentially stimulate cytotoxic T lymphocyte responses over antibody production.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

Immunogenicity of HERV-L IQ10 peptide was evaluated in HLA-matched humanized mice. Animals received 50 μg peptide with polyICLC adjuvant intramuscularly at weeks 0, 2, and 4. ELISPOT analysis at week 6 demonstrated mean IFN-γ spot-forming units of 320/10^6 splenocytes in immunized animals versus <20/10^6 in controls (p<0.001). Tetramer staining confirmed peptide-specific CD8+ T cell frequencies of 0.8-1.2% in circulating lymphocytes. Cytotoxic activity was confirmed by in vivo killing assays showing specific lysis of 65-75% of peptide-pulsed target cells. These results demonstrate the immunogenic potential of HERV polypeptides for inducing functional cytotoxic T cell responses.
