# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates generally to immunomodulatory compositions derived from pinecone extracts and methods for their use in enhancing immune responses. More particularly, the invention concerns a polyphenylpropanoid polysaccharide complex (PPC) obtained from Pinus sylvestris cones that demonstrates significant enhancement of antigen-specific CD8+ cytotoxic T cell responses when administered in conjunction with immunogenic agents. The invention further provides methods for preparing the PPC composition and demonstrates its efficacy in augmenting both primary and secondary T cell responses through specific modulation of immune cell proliferation without substantially affecting activation-induced cell death mechanisms.  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The medicinal properties of pinecone extracts have been recognized since ancient times, with documented uses ranging from treatment of kidney disorders to dermatological applications. Modern investigations have revealed that certain pinecone-derived compounds possess immunomodulatory capabilities, though the precise mechanisms underlying these effects have remained poorly understood.  

Current immunotherapeutic approaches often struggle to achieve sufficient cytotoxic T lymphocyte (CTL) responses necessary for effective clearance of pathogens or malignant cells. Many adjuvants in clinical use tend to preferentially stimulate humoral immunity rather than cell-mediated immunity, creating an unmet need for agents capable of specifically enhancing Th1-type responses and CTL activation.  

Prior work has established that some pinecone extracts can bias immune responses toward a Th1 phenotype when administered with vaccines. However, these observations were made without elucidation of the cellular and molecular mechanisms involved. The temporal aspects of the immune response affected by such extracts - whether acting during the expansion, contraction, or memory phases of T cell activation - remained undefined. Furthermore, the potential effects on reactive oxygen species (ROS) production and apoptotic pathways in activated T cells had not been investigated.  

There exists a need in the art for well-characterized immunomodulatory compositions that can selectively enhance antigen-specific CD8+ T cell responses without disrupting normal immune homeostasis. The present invention addresses these needs by providing a standardized pinecone extract preparation with defined biological activities and a clarified mechanism of action.  

## SUMMARY OF INVENTION  

The present invention provides a novel immunomodulatory composition comprising a polyphenylpropanoid polysaccharide complex (PPC) derived from Pinus sylvestris pinecones, along with methods for its preparation and use. The PPC composition demonstrates unique properties in enhancing immune responses, particularly through amplification of antigen-specific CD8+ cytotoxic T cell populations during the proliferative phase of primary immune responses.  

Key aspects of the invention include:  

1. A standardized PPC preparation obtained through a specific extraction process involving aqueous extraction at elevated pH and temperature, followed by purification and quality control measures to ensure consistency of biological activity.  

2. Demonstration that oral administration of PPC in conjunction with immunogenic stimuli significantly increases the number of activated CD4+ and CD8+ T cells during the expansion phase of primary immune responses, without substantially altering the contraction phase or activation-induced cell death.  

3. Evidence that PPC enhances interferon-gamma (IFNγ) production while moderately reducing interleukin-2 (IL-2) levels during T cell activation, suggesting a specific modulation of cytokine profiles favorable for cell-mediated immunity.  

4. Data showing that PPC reduces reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels in activated T cells without significantly affecting expression patterns of key apoptosis-related genes (Bcl-2, Bim, Bax) or preventing normal activation-induced apoptosis.  

5. Methods for using the PPC composition as an immunopotentiator in combination with vaccines or other immunogenic agents to enhance protective immune responses, particularly those mediated by CD8+ cytotoxic T cells.  

The invention further provides pharmaceutical formulations containing the PPC composition and establishes dosing regimens effective for immune potentiation. The unique properties of the PPC composition make it particularly valuable for applications requiring robust cell-mediated immunity, including but not limited to anticancer therapies, antiviral treatments, and vaccines against intracellular pathogens.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PREFERRED EMBODIMENT  

The preferred embodiment of the present invention comprises a polyphenylpropanoid polysaccharide complex (PPC) obtained from the cones of Pinus sylvestris through a standardized extraction and purification process. The production method ensures consistent composition and biological activity across batches while eliminating potential contaminants.  

The pinecones are first thoroughly washed with deionized water to remove surface impurities, then mechanically shredded to increase surface area for extraction. The shredded material undergoes aqueous extraction in a stainless steel reaction vessel under controlled conditions of elevated pH (approximately 12.0) and temperature (121°C) for a defined period (30 minutes). These parameters have been optimized to extract the active polyphenylpropanoid components while maintaining their biological integrity.  

Following extraction, the liquid phase is separated and standardized using UV spectroscopy at 280 nm, with the concentration of active polyphenylpropanoids calculated using the extinction coefficient 22.8 L·gram^-1·cm^-1. The extract undergoes rigorous quality control testing to ensure absence of contaminants including herbicides, pesticides, heavy metals, endotoxins, and microorganisms.  

The purified liquid extract is then converted to a stable powder form through spray-drying, resulting in a dark brown, odorless product suitable for pharmaceutical formulation. The powdered PPC can be reconstituted in sterile water or other appropriate vehicles for administration. A typical preparation involves suspending the powder in sterile water to create a stock solution (25 mg/mL), followed by centrifugation (10,000 × g, 20 minutes) and filtration through a 0.2 μm nylon filter to remove particulates.  

For in vivo use, working solutions are typically prepared at concentrations of 200 μg/mL of active polyphenylpropanoids in sterile distilled water. The composition is administered orally, with effective dosing regimens determined to be approximately 100 μL of 200 μg/mL solution given three times daily at the time of immunization. Alternative dosing schedules and routes of administration (e.g., parenteral) may also be employed depending on the specific therapeutic application.  

The PPC composition demonstrates unique immunomodulatory properties when administered in conjunction with immunogenic stimuli. In preferred embodiments, the composition is given coincident with antigen exposure to maximize its effects on the primary T cell response. The invention is particularly effective when used with superantigens (e.g., staphylococcal enterotoxin B) or conventional vaccines, enhancing both the magnitude and quality of the resulting immune response.  

Mechanistic studies reveal that the PPC composition operates primarily during the expansion phase of T cell activation, significantly increasing the number of antigen-specific CD4+ and CD8+ T cells without substantially altering the subsequent contraction phase or activation-induced cell death. This temporal specificity distinguishes the invention from other immunomodulators that may broadly suppress or prolong immune activation.  

At the molecular level, the PPC composition modulates cytokine production during T cell activation, enhancing IFNγ while moderately reducing IL-2 secretion. This cytokine profile favors development of Th1-type responses and cell-mediated immunity. The composition also reduces intracellular ROS levels in activated T cells, though unlike conventional antioxidants, it does not significantly interfere with normal apoptotic pathways or alter expression patterns of key apoptosis-related genes (Bcl-2, Bim, Bax).  

The invention finds particular utility in applications requiring robust CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocyte responses, including but not limited to:  

1. Adjuvant therapy for vaccines against intracellular pathogens (viruses, certain bacteria)  
2. Combination therapy with cancer vaccines or immunotherapies  
3. Enhancement of immune responses in immunocompromised individuals  
4. Prevention or treatment of allergic responses through Th1 bias  
5. General immune potentiation in situations of increased infectious risk  

Pharmaceutical formulations of the invention may include the PPC composition as the sole active ingredient or in combination with other immunomodulators, antigens, or therapeutic agents. The composition may be formulated for oral, parenteral, mucosal, or topical administration depending on the intended use.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Preparation of PPC  

The PPC composition was prepared from Pinus sylvestris pinecones obtained from a commercial supplier. The pinecones were washed extensively in deionized water and shredded to increase surface area. The shredded material was loaded into a stainless steel reaction vessel and extracted with water adjusted to pH 12.0 at 121°C for 30 minutes. The extract was standardized by UV spectroscopy at 280 nm using the formula:  

PPC concentration (mg/mL) = OD280 × (1/22.8 L·gram^-1·cm^-1)  

The extract was tested for contaminants including herbicides, pesticides, heavy metals, endotoxin, and microorganisms before being spray-dried to form a stable powder. For use, the powder was suspended in sterile water to 25 mg/mL, centrifuged at 10,000 × g for 20 minutes, and filtered through a 0.2 μm nylon filter. Working solutions containing 200 μg/mL of active polyphenylpropanoids were prepared fresh weekly.  

### In Vivo Immune Stimulation  

Six-to-seven-week-old male Balb/c mice were divided into experimental groups (n=3 per group): naive (PBS only), SEB (100 μg staphylococcal enterotoxin B i.p.), and SEB+PPC (SEB plus oral PPC). The SEB+PPC group received 100 μL of 200 μg/mL PPC solution by gavage three times daily on days 0 and 1 (total 6 doses). Mice were euthanized at 48 or 96 hours post-immunization. Inguinal lymph nodes were pooled per mouse for analysis.  

### ELISA Assays  

Serum levels of IL-2, IFNγ, and IL-12p70 were quantified using commercial ELISA kits according to manufacturer protocols. Absorbance was measured at 450 nm and concentrations determined using standard curves. SEB immunization significantly increased all three cytokines (p<0.05). PPC co-administration reduced IL-2 (p<0.05), enhanced IFNγ (p<0.005), and did not significantly alter IL-12p70 levels compared to SEB alone.  

### Lymph Node and Spleen Cell Isolation  

Lymph nodes and spleens were homogenized in HBSS using a mechanical homogenizer. Cells were pelleted and resuspended in complete media (RPMI 1640 with 10% FCS and antibiotics). Viability and concentration were determined using a cell counter with viability stain.  

### Flow Cytometry  

Lymph node cells (1×10^6) were stained with fluorescent antibodies against CD3 (APC), CD4 (FITC), CD8 (PE), and Vβ8 (PerCP-eFluor710) after Fc receptor blocking. Cells were analyzed on a flow cytometer equipped with appropriate lasers and filters. Data analysis showed PPC significantly increased CD4+/Vβ8+ (p<0.01) and CD8+/Vβ8+ T cells (p<0.01) at 48 hours compared to SEB alone.  

### Detection of Apoptosis  

Cells were stained with APC-anti-CD3, PerCP-eFluor710-anti-Vβ8, and FITC-Annexin V according to standard protocols. Flow cytometry revealed SEB significantly increased Annexin V+ Vβ8+ T cells (p<0.001) compared to naive, with no significant difference between SEB and SEB+PPC groups (p>0.05), indicating PPC does not suppress activation-induced apoptosis.  

### Detection of Reactive Oxygen Species  

Cells were stained with CM-H2DCFDA and antibodies as above, incubated at 37°C for 15 minutes, and analyzed by flow cytometry. PPC significantly reduced the percentage of CD3+/ROS+ T cells (p<0.01) and intracellular ROS levels per cell compared to SEB alone, suggesting antioxidant activity without preventing normal apoptosis.  

### Detection of Intracellular Bcl-2  

Cells were surface stained, fixed, permeabilized with saponin, and stained with FITC-anti-Bcl-2. While PPC increased the percentage of Bcl-2+ Vβ8+ T cells (p<0.05) due to greater cell numbers, intracellular Bcl-2 levels per cell were equally reduced in SEB and SEB+PPC groups compared to naive, indicating PPC does not prevent SEB-induced Bcl-2 downregulation.  

### Analysis of Gene Expression  

Total RNA was isolated from lymph node cells and reverse transcribed. qPCR was performed using validated primer sets for Bcl-2, Bcl-3, Bcl-xL, Bax, and Bim, with β-actin as reference. SEB and SEB+PPC similarly reduced Bcl-2 mRNA (∼3-fold) and increased Bim mRNA (∼4-fold), while PPC alone increased Bcl-3 mRNA (∼4-fold) in naive mice. These results confirm PPC's primary effect is on T cell expansion rather than apoptosis regulation.  

The complete specification demonstrates that the PPC composition of the invention provides a novel and effective means to enhance antigen-specific T cell responses, particularly CD8+ cytotoxic T lymphocytes, through specific modulation of the proliferative phase of primary immune responses without disrupting normal contraction or apoptotic mechanisms. This unique activity profile makes the composition particularly valuable for applications requiring robust cell-mediated immunity.
