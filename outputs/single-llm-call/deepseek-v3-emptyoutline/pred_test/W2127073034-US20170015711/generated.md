## DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention relates to the field of immunology and vaccine development, specifically concerning the production of stable, native-like HIV-1 envelope glycoprotein (Env) trimers for use as vaccine immunogens. Despite decades of research, an effective vaccine against HIV-1 remains elusive, largely due to the virus's ability to evade the immune system through rapid mutation and structural camouflage. One promising strategy involves inducing broadly neutralizing antibodies (bNAbs) that target conserved epitopes on the viral Env spike, a trimeric complex composed of gp120 and gp41 subunits. However, producing stable, native-like Env trimers that mimic the structure of the virion-associated spike has proven challenging.  

Conventional methods for producing soluble Env proteins often result in unstable or non-native conformations that fail to elicit potent bNAbs. Previous approaches, such as introducing stabilizing disulfide bonds (SOS) and proline substitutions (e.g., I559P), have improved trimer stability but still rely on transient transfection systems, which are inefficient for large-scale production. Additionally, these methods require co-expression of the Furin protease to ensure proper cleavage of gp120 and gp41, further complicating manufacturing. There is thus a critical need for stable cell lines capable of consistently producing high-quality, native-like Env trimers for preclinical and clinical applications.  

### BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention addresses these challenges by providing stable mammalian cell lines that produce soluble, cleaved HIV-1 Env trimers with native-like structure and antigenicity. These cell lines, derived from 293T and CHO backgrounds, incorporate a Flp Recombination Target (FRT) system to ensure defined genomic integration and constitutive expression of both the Env trimer (BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140) and Furin protease. The resulting trimers are fully cleaved, resistant to proteolytic degradation, and exhibit antigenic properties indistinguishable from those produced by transient transfection.  

Key advantages of the invention include:  
1. **High-Yield Production**: Stable cell lines yield up to 12–15 mg of purified trimers per 1 × 10^9 cells, a 10- to 32-fold improvement over transient transfection.  
2. **Structural Authenticity**: The trimers retain native-like conformation, as confirmed by electron microscopy (EM), surface plasmon resonance (SPR), and differential scanning calorimetry (DSC).  
3. **Scalability**: The method is adaptable to Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) conditions, enabling large-scale production for clinical trials.  

### DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The invention encompasses the design, construction, and characterization of stable 293T and CHO cell lines expressing BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 trimers. Below is a detailed description of the components and processes involved.  

#### **Cell Line Design**  
The pAM/C BG505 vector was engineered to co-express the BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 trimer and Furin protease under the control of distinct promoters:  
- **Env Expression**: The CMV promoter drives high-level constitutive expression of the BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 gene, cloned between HindIII and NotI sites.  
- **Furin Co-Expression**: The EF1α promoter ensures moderate Furin expression to facilitate gp120-gp41 cleavage without cytotoxicity.  

The vector integrates into the FRT site of Flp-In™ cells via Flp recombinase-mediated recombination, enabling stable, single-copy insertion. Hygromycin resistance serves as a selectable marker.  

#### **Selection and Propagation**  
Stable clones were isolated via limiting dilution and screened for Env expression using fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS) and ELISA. High-producing clones (e.g., 293T clone 13 #3-5 and CHO clone B-D7) were expanded and maintained for >10 passages without loss of trimer yield or cell viability.  

#### **Trimer Purification and Characterization**  
Trimers were purified by:  
1. **Affinity Chromatography**: Env proteins were captured using the 2G12 monoclonal antibody (MAb) conjugated to Sepharose 4B.  
2. **Size Exclusion Chromatography (SEC)**: Trimers were separated from dimers and monomers, yielding >90% homogeneity.  

Biochemical analyses confirmed:  
- **Full Cleavage**: SDS-PAGE under reducing conditions revealed discrete gp120 and gp41ECTO bands.  
- **Protease Resistance**: Unlike monomeric gp120 or uncleaved gp140, the trimers showed no degradation products in Western blots probed with HIV-Ig or MAbs.  
- **Antigenicity**: SPR and ELISA demonstrated strong binding to bNAbs (e.g., PGT145) and minimal reactivity with non-neutralizing antibodies (e.g., F105).  
- **Structural Integrity**: Negative-stain EM and DSC revealed native-like morphology and thermal stability (Tm ~66–68°C).  

### EXAMPLE 1  
**Construction of the pAM/C BG505 Vector**  
The pAM/C BG505 plasmid was assembled by inserting the BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 gene (subtype A founder strain) into the HindIII/NotI sites of pcDNA5/FRT, downstream of the CMV promoter. The Furin gene was cloned under the EF1α promoter in the same vector. Large-scale plasmid preparation was performed using an EndoFree MaxiPrep kit.  

### EXAMPLE 2  
**Generation of Stable 293T Cell Lines**  
293T Flp-In™ cells were co-transfected with pAM/C BG505 and pOG44 (Flp recombinase) using Lipofectamine 2000. Hygromycin-resistant clones were isolated by limiting dilution and screened for Env expression via FITC-2G12 FACS. Clone 13 #3-5 was selected for high trimer production (2.1 µg/mL in supernatant).  

### EXAMPLE 3  
**Generation of Stable CHO Cell Lines**  
CHO Flp-In™ cells were transfected and selected similarly, yielding clone B-D7 with a trimer yield of 1.7 µg/mL. Cell viability remained >87% over 10 passages.  

### EXAMPLE 4  
**Trimer Purification from 293T Stable Lines**  
Supernatants from 293T clone 13 #3-5 were subjected to 2G12-affinity chromatography, eluted with 3 M MgCl2, and further purified by SEC. The final yield was 12 mg per 1 × 10^9 cells. BN-PAGE confirmed trimer dominance (>90%).  

### EXAMPLE 5  
**Antigenic Profiling by SPR**  
Purified trimers were flowed over CM5 chips coated with PGT145 or F105. PGT145 binding (response units: 120–150 RU) confirmed trimer integrity, while F105 reactivity was negligible (<5 RU), consistent with native-like conformation.  

### EXAMPLE 6  
**Electron Microscopy Analysis**  
Negative-stain EM of 293T-derived trimers revealed >90% native-like particles, with class averages matching published cryo-EM structures (e.g., closed, three-bladed propeller morphology).  

### EXAMPLE 7  
**Differential Scanning Calorimetry**  
DSC of CHO-derived trimers showed a single thermal transition at 66.3°C, comparable to transiently produced trimers (67.9°C), confirming structural homogeneity.  

### EXAMPLE 8  
**Scalability Assessment**  
A pilot-scale run using 10 hyperflasks yielded 20–40 mg of trimers over 2–3 weeks, demonstrating feasibility for GMP-compliant manufacturing.  

### EXAMPLE 9  
**Comparative Protease Resistance**  
SDS-PAGE/Western blotting of trimers vs. monomeric gp120 (MN strain) and uncleaved gp140 (CZA97.012) revealed no degradation products for the trimers, while gp120 showed V3-clipping (70/50 kDa fragments) and gp140 formed aggregates.  

### INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  
The invention enables scalable production of HIV-1 Env trimers for:  
- **Vaccine Development**: As immunogens in preclinical/clinical trials.  
- **Structural Studies**: For epitope mapping and rational vaccine design.  
- **Diagnostics**: As reagents for antibody detection assays.  

The stable cell lines and methods described herein are adaptable to other Env genotypes and protein-based vaccines, offering broad utility in infectious disease research.
