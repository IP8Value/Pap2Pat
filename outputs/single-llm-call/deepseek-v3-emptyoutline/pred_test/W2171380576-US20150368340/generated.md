# DESCRIPTION  

## FIELD  

The present invention relates to monoclonal antibodies that bind to heparan sulfate (HS) chains on glypican-3 (GPC3) and inhibit hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) tumor growth. More specifically, the invention provides human monoclonal antibodies, such as HS20, that target HS-modified GPC3 and disrupt hepatocyte growth factor (HGF)/Met signaling, thereby suppressing HCC cell migration, motility, and tumor progression. The invention further encompasses immunoconjugates, pharmaceutical compositions, and methods of treating HCC and other GPC3-expressing malignancies.  

## BACKGROUND  

Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) is the fifth most common and third most lethal malignancy worldwide, with limited treatment options for advanced-stage disease. Current therapies are largely ineffective due to chemoresistance, necessitating novel therapeutic strategies. Glypican-3 (GPC3), a heparan sulfate proteoglycan (HSPG), is overexpressed in over 70% of HCCs but absent in normal adult tissues, making it an ideal therapeutic target. GPC3 promotes tumorigenesis by modulating Wnt and Yap signaling, yet its role in HCC metastasis remains poorly understood.  

The HGF/Met pathway is critical for liver development and HCC progression, with 40% of HCC patients exhibiting Met activation linked to poor prognosis. HSPGs, including GPC3, facilitate HGF/Met signaling by binding HGF via HS chains, thereby enhancing tumor cell motility and survival. Existing HGF/Met inhibitors, such as monoclonal antibodies and small molecules, remain in early clinical development and lack tumor specificity.  

Prior efforts to target HSPGs in cancer have focused on HS-mimetics like PI-88 and PG545, which broadly inhibit growth factor interactions but suffer from off-target effects. Antibody-based therapies against GPC3 core protein (e.g., HN3) show promise but do not fully exploit the functional role of HS chains in tumor progression. There remains an unmet need for targeted therapies that selectively disrupt HS-mediated oncogenic signaling in HCC.  

## SUMMARY  

The invention provides human monoclonal antibodies, particularly HS20, that specifically bind HS chains on GPC3 and inhibit HCC progression through dual mechanisms: (1) blockade of HGF/Met signaling, reducing cell migration and motility, and (2) suppression of Wnt signaling, limiting tumor proliferation. HS20 exhibits high specificity for GPC3-associated HS, minimizing off-target effects.  

Key advantages include:  
- **HS Chain Specificity**: HS20 binds conserved HS epitopes on GPC3, disrupting HGF binding and Met phosphorylation without cross-reactivity to normal tissues.  
- **Dual Pathway Inhibition**: Simultaneously blocks Wnt and HGF/Met pathways, overcoming resistance in HCCs with β-catenin mutations.  
- **In Vivo Efficacy**: Reduces HCC tumor growth in xenograft models by >50% and inhibits 3D spheroid formation.  
- **Broad Applicability**: Targets multiple glypicans (e.g., GPC1, GPC5) in other malignancies.  

The invention further covers immunoconjugates (e.g., antibody-drug conjugates), pharmaceutical compositions, and methods for treating HCC and other GPC3-positive cancers.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION  

### I. Abbreviations  

- **GPC3**: Glypican-3  
- **HCC**: Hepatocellular carcinoma

- **HSPG**: Heparan sulfate proteoglycan

### II. Terms and Methods  

**Human Monoclonal Antibody**: An antibody derived from a single B-cell clone, engineered to bind a specific epitope (e.g., HS20 targets HS chains on GPC3).  

**Immunoconjugate**: A monoclonal antibody linked to a cytotoxic agent (e.g., chemotherapeutic drug or radioisotope).  

**Pharmaceutical Composition**: A formulation comprising the antibody and a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier, suitable for intravenous administration.  

### III. Introduction  

GPC3 is a validated HCC biomarker and therapeutic target due to its tumor-specific expression and role in oncogenic signaling. The invention leverages the functional importance of GPC3’s HS chains in HGF/Met activation, providing antibodies that selectively disrupt this interaction. HS20 demonstrates superior efficacy compared to core protein-targeting antibodies (e.g., HN3) by inhibiting both Wnt-independent and HGF-dependent tumorigenic pathways.  

### IV. Human Monoclonal Antibodies that Bind GPC3 or HS Chains on GPC3  

**HS20 Antibody**:  
- **Binding Specificity**: Recognizes a conserved HS structure on GPC3 (KD < 10 nM).  
- **Mechanism**: Blocks HGF binding to GPC3, reducing Met phosphorylation and downstream motility signals (Fig. 5B).  
- **Dose-Dependent Inhibition**: Suppresses HCC cell migration at concentrations ≥10 μg/mL (Fig. 2C).  

**Other Embodiments**: Antibodies with modified Fc regions (e.g., IgG1 for enhanced ADCC) or affinity-matured variants.  

### V. Immunoconjugates  

- **Payloads**: Cytotoxic agents (e.g., MMAE, DM1) or radioisotopes (e.g., 90Y) linked via cleavable linkers.  
- **Applications**: Targeted delivery to GPC3-positive tumors, minimizing systemic toxicity.  

### VI. Compositions and Methods of Use  

**Formulations**:  
- Lyophilized powder for reconstitution (20–50 mg/mL HS20 in PBS).  
- Stable liquid formulations (pH 6.0–7.5) with excipients (e.g., trehalose).  

**Dosing**: 20–25 mg/kg intravenously, twice weekly (Fig. 6D).  

**Indications**:  
- First-line or adjuvant therapy for advanced HCC.  
- Hepatoblastoma and other GPC3-expressing cancers.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  
**HS20 Inhibits HCC Cell Migration**:  
- HS20 (50 μg/mL) reduced Hep3B cell migration by 30% in wound healing assays (Fig. 2D). No effect observed in GPC3-negative SK-hep1 cells.  

### Example 2  
**HS20 Blocks HGF/Met Signaling**:  
- Pre-treatment with HS20 decreased phosphorylated Met levels in Hep3B cells by >50% (Fig. 5B).  

### Mutation of HS20 to Remove an N-Glycosylation Site does not Alter Binding Affinity and Specificity  
- HS20 variants with N-glycosylation site mutations (e.g., Asn→Ala) retained equivalent GPC3 binding (KD < 15 nM).  

### HS20 Inhibits HCC Tumor Growth In Vivo  
- Hep3B xenografts showed >50% reduction in tumor volume after 3 doses of HS20 (25 mg/kg) (Fig. 6D).  

### The HN3 Human Antibody Inhibits HCC Tumor Growth In Vivo  
- Comparative studies confirmed HS20’s superior efficacy over HN3 in inhibiting HGF-driven metastasis.  

### Example 6  
**HS20 Suppresses 3D Spheroid Formation**:  
- HS20 (50 μg/mL) inhibited HGF-induced spheroid growth by 20-fold in Hep3B cells (Fig. 6A).
