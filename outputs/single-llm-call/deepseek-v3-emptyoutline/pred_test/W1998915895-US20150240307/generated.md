# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of medical diagnostics, specifically to methods and reagents for diagnosing rheumatoid arthritis (RA) by detecting specific microRNA (miRNA) biomarkers in blood samples. More particularly, the invention provides a novel diagnostic approach utilizing plasma miR-24, miR-125a-5p, and combinations thereof as highly specific biomarkers for RA, enabling early and accurate diagnosis of the disease.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Rheumatoid arthritis is a chronic inflammatory disorder characterized by joint destruction and systemic complications. Current diagnostic methods rely on clinical symptoms and serological markers such as rheumatoid factor (RF) and anti-citrullinated protein antibodies (ACPA). However, these markers lack sufficient sensitivity and specificity, particularly in early disease stages. Approximately 30% of RA patients are seronegative for both RF and ACPA, complicating diagnosis and delaying treatment initiation.  

MicroRNAs have emerged as promising biomarkers due to their stability in biological fluids and tissue-specific expression patterns. Prior studies have identified dysregulated miRNAs in RA synovial tissue and peripheral blood cells, but these intracellular miRNAs require invasive sampling methods. While circulating miRNAs in plasma offer a non-invasive diagnostic opportunity, previous attempts to identify RA-specific plasma miRNAs have been limited by poor specificity, particularly in distinguishing RA from osteoarthritis (OA) and other inflammatory conditions.  

Existing diagnostic methods fail to provide adequate sensitivity for early RA detection or reliable differentiation from other arthritic conditions. There remains an unmet need for non-invasive, highly specific biomarkers capable of accurately diagnosing RA across diverse patient populations, including seronegative cases.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

### Problems to be Solved by the Invention  

The invention addresses several critical limitations in current RA diagnostics:  

1) Insufficient sensitivity and specificity of existing serological markers (RF and ACPA), particularly in early disease stages  
2) Inability to reliably diagnose seronegative RA cases  
3) Poor differentiation between RA and other inflammatory conditions such as OA and systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE)  
4) Lack of non-invasive biomarkers capable of reflecting disease activity  

### Means of Solving the Problems  

The invention provides solutions through the following technical approaches:  

1) Identification of specific plasma miRNA biomarkers (miR-24, miR-125a-5p) that are elevated in RA patients compared to healthy controls and patients with other inflammatory conditions  
2) Development of a diagnostic algorithm (ePRAM) combining multiple miRNA measurements to enhance diagnostic accuracy  
3) Establishment of precise cutoff values for each biomarker to optimize sensitivity and specificity  
4) Creation of standardized protocols for plasma sample processing and miRNA quantification  

### Effect of the Invention  

The invention provides significant clinical advantages:  

1) High diagnostic specificity (89-92%) for RA, particularly in differentiating from OA and SLE  
2) Improved sensitivity (64-78%) compared to existing biomarkers, including in seronegative cases  
3) Non-invasive sampling through standard blood collection procedures  
4) Correlation with disease activity, enabling monitoring of therapeutic response  
5) Potential for early diagnosis before clinical symptoms manifest  

## DESCRIPTION OF EMBODIMENTS  

### 1. Test Method of a Sample Derived From the Blood of a Test Subject for Determining the Pathology of Rheumatoid Arthritis  

The invention provides a method for diagnosing RA comprising:  

a) Obtaining a blood sample from a test subject and isolating plasma therefrom  
b) Extracting total RNA from the plasma sample while spiking with synthetic cel-miR-39 as an internal control  
c) Quantifying the concentration of at least one miRNA selected from miR-24 and miR-125a-5p using quantitative reverse transcription polymerase chain reaction (qRT-PCR)  
d) Comparing the measured miRNA concentration to predetermined cutoff values:  
   - 1.46 pM for miR-24  
   - 8.17×10-2 pM for miR-125a-5p  
e) Diagnosing RA when the miRNA concentration exceeds the cutoff value  

The method may further include calculating an estimated Probability of RA by plasma MiRNAs (ePRAM) score using the formula:  

ePRAM = exp(-x)/(1+exp(-x))  

where x is a linear combination of miR-24, miR-125a-5p, and miR-30a-5p concentrations. An ePRAM score >0.50 indicates high probability of RA.  

### 2. Diagnostic Reagent for Determining the Pathology of Rheumatoid Arthritis  

The invention provides a diagnostic reagent kit comprising:  

a) Primers specific for amplification of miR-24 and miR-125a-5p  
b) Synthetic cel-miR-39 as an internal control  
c) Reverse transcription reagents for cDNA synthesis  
d) qPCR master mix containing DNA polymerase and fluorescent reporter  
e) Reference standards for absolute quantification of target miRNAs  
f) Instructions for sample processing and data interpretation  

The kit may additionally include primers for miR-30a-5p to enable calculation of the ePRAM score. All components are optimized for consistent performance across different instrumentation platforms.  

## EXAMPLES  

### Method  

Validation studies were conducted using plasma samples from:  
- 102 RA patients (mean age 58.3 years, 82% female)  
- 104 healthy controls (mean age 56.1 years, 80% female)  
- 24 OA patients  
- 11 SLE patients  

Blood samples were collected in EDTA tubes and processed within 2 hours. Plasma was separated by centrifugation at 1400g for 7 minutes and stored at -80°C until analysis. Total RNA was extracted using TriPure Isolation Reagent with spiked cel-miR-39.  

qRT-PCR was performed using EXPRESS SYBR GreenER chemistry with miRNA-specific primers. Absolute concentrations were calculated using standard curves generated from synthetic miRNAs.  

### Results  

The diagnostic performance of individual biomarkers was:  

miR-24:  
- Sensitivity: 63.7%  
- Specificity: 89.5%  
- AUC: 0.83  

miR-125a-5p:  
- Sensitivity: 64.7%  
- Specificity: 89.5%  
- AUC: 0.86  

ePRAM (combined score):  
- Sensitivity: 78.4%  
- Specificity: 92.3%  
- AUC: 0.89  

The biomarkers showed excellent differentiation between RA and OA/SLE patients (p<0.01). In ACPA-negative RA patients, the ePRAM score maintained 73% sensitivity.  

## COMPARATIVE EXAMPLE  

Conventional biomarkers showed inferior performance compared to the invention:  

RF:  
- Sensitivity: 67%  
- Specificity: 85%  

ACPA:  
- Sensitivity: 72%  
- Specificity: 88%  

Notably, the invention's biomarkers demonstrated significantly better specificity in differentiating RA from OA (79-92% vs 60-75% for conventional markers).  

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  

The invention has broad applications in:  

1) Clinical diagnostics - enabling accurate RA diagnosis in hospital and outpatient settings  
2) Pharmaceutical development - facilitating patient stratification for clinical trials  
3) Research tools - providing insights into RA pathogenesis through miRNA studies  
4) Companion diagnostics - guiding personalized treatment decisions  

The diagnostic method can be readily implemented in clinical laboratories using standard qPCR equipment, with potential for automation and high-throughput screening. The stability of plasma miRNAs allows for centralized testing with sample shipping.  

The invention represents a significant advance in rheumatologic diagnostics by providing objective, quantitative biomarkers that address critical limitations of current diagnostic approaches. The combination of high specificity and correlation with disease activity makes these biomarkers particularly valuable for early intervention and treatment monitoring.
