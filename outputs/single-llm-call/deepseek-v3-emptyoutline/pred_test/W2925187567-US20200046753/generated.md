# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to a novel non-viral cytoplasmic expression system for sustained short hairpin RNA (shRNA) production and subsequent gene silencing. More specifically, the invention provides a hybrid mRNA/DNA system comprising T7 polymerase (T7pol) mRNA, a pT7/shRNA DNA fragment, and a T7 autogene plasmid, which enables prolonged cytoplasmic expression of shRNA without nuclear entry requirements. The invention further encompasses liposomal formulations for delivering this system to target cells, methods of preparing the components, and applications for long-term gene silencing in vitro and in vivo, particularly in cancer therapy.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

RNA interference (RNAi) has emerged as a promising therapeutic strategy for suppressing specific genes in disease treatment and functional studies. Conventional approaches rely on chemically synthesized small interfering RNAs (siRNAs), which induce transient gene silencing lasting only 2–4 days in cell culture systems. While viral vectors such as adenoviruses and lentiviruses have demonstrated prolonged shRNA expression, their clinical use is limited by safety concerns, including immunogenicity and insertional mutagenesis, as well as manufacturing challenges.  

Non-viral plasmid-based shRNA expression systems utilizing RNA polymerase III (pol III) promoters (e.g., U6 or H1) have been developed to circumvent viral limitations. However, these systems suffer from inefficient nuclear entry of plasmid DNA, particularly in post-mitotic or non-dividing cells, resulting in low shRNA expression levels. Additionally, nuclear export of shRNA transcripts can saturate cellular export machinery, leading to unintended toxicity.  

Cytoplasmic shRNA expression systems, such as the pCMV_T7pol/pT7_shRNA plasmid system, have been explored to bypass nuclear entry requirements. However, these systems still depend on initial nuclear transcription of T7pol by a cytomegalovirus (CMV) promoter, limiting their efficiency. T7-based autogene systems, which exploit the autocatalytic amplification of T7pol, have been used for high-level transgene expression but have not been adapted for sustained shRNA production.  

There remains an unmet need for a non-viral, cytoplasmic shRNA expression system capable of prolonged gene silencing without nuclear entry requirements or immunogenic side effects.  

## DISCLOSURE  

### Technical Problem  

The primary technical problem addressed by the present invention is the transient nature of siRNA-mediated gene silencing and the inefficiency of existing shRNA expression systems. Current approaches fail to provide sustained shRNA production due to:  
1. The short half-life of synthetic siRNAs.  
2. Dependence on nuclear entry for plasmid-based shRNA expression.  
3. Limited duration of T7pol expression in existing cytoplasmic systems.  
4. Immunogenicity and toxicity associated with viral vectors and synthetic delivery systems.  

### Technical Solution  

The invention provides a hybrid mRNA/DNA system comprising:  
1. **T7pol mRNA**: A 5′-capped mRNA encoding T7 polymerase, enabling immediate cytoplasmic translation without nuclear entry.  
2. **T7 autogene plasmid**: A plasmid containing T7pol gene under the control of a T7 promoter and an internal ribosomal entry site (IRES), facilitating autocatalytic amplification of T7pol.  
3. **pT7/shRNA DNA fragment**: A short DNA fragment encoding shRNA under a T7 promoter, transcribed by T7pol in the cytoplasm.  

This system is encapsulated in neutral 1,2-dioleoyl-sn-glycero-3-phosphatidylcholine (DOPC) liposomes (auto_shRNA@LS) for efficient cellular delivery. The liposomal formulation ensures stability, minimizes immunogenicity, and enhances tumor accumulation via the enhanced permeability and retention (EPR) effect.  

### Advantageous Effects of the Invention  

The invention offers the following advantages:  
1. **Sustained Gene Silencing**: The autocatalytic T7pol amplification loop enables prolonged shRNA expression (>13 days) and subsequent siRNA production.  
2. **Cytoplasmic Operation**: Eliminates nuclear entry requirements, enhancing efficiency in non-dividing cells.  
3. **Low Immunogenicity**: The DOPC liposomal formulation avoids innate immune activation, as demonstrated by negligible TNF-α and INF-α release in human PBMCs and murine models.  
4. **Tumor-Specific Delivery**: Liposomes accumulate preferentially in tumor tissues via the EPR effect.  
5. **Single-Dose Efficacy**: A single intravenous injection achieves long-term gene silencing in vivo, unlike conventional siRNAs requiring repeated administration.  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

### Example 1. Design and Preparation of pT7/T7 Polymerase System Capable of Sustained Expression of shRNA in Cytoplasm  

The T7 autogene plasmid was constructed by cloning the T7pol gene (GenBank FJ881694.1) downstream of a T7 promoter and an EMCV IRES element and upstream of a polyA sequence and T7 terminator. The plasmid was verified by sequencing.  

For initial T7pol expression, 5′-capped T7pol mRNA was synthesized in vitro using T7 RNA polymerase and anti-reverse cap analog (ARCA) to enhance translational efficiency. The pT7/shRNA DNA fragment was generated via assembly PCR, encoding a 19-nt target sequence against red fluorescent protein (RFP) mRNA, a 9-nt loop, and the reverse complement. The fragment was designed to initiate transcription with guanosine for optimal T7pol activity.  

### Example 2. Preparation of DOPC Liposome Transporter Loaded with Sustained Cytoplasmic Expression System for shRNA  

Liposomes were formulated by mixing DOPC lipids (26.5 µg) with pT7/shRNA DNA fragment (0.15 µg), T7pol mRNA (0.625 µg), and T7 autogene plasmid (1.875 µg) in t-butanol. Tween 20 was added (1:19 w/w to oligonucleotides/DOPC), and the mixture was lyophilized. Hydration with saline yielded lipoplexes (auto_shRNA@LS) with >90% complexation efficiency, as confirmed by gel retardation assay.  

### Example 3. Examination of Biophysicochemical Properties of DOPC Liposome Transporter Loaded with Sustained Cytoplasmic Expression System for shRNA  

Dynamic light scattering revealed lipoplexes with a hydrodynamic diameter of 31.4 ± 1.2 nm and a zeta potential of 1.1 mV. Gel electrophoresis confirmed complete complexation at a 1:10 oligonucleotide:DOPC weight ratio. MTT assays demonstrated negligible cytotoxicity in B16F10/RFP cells at concentrations up to 60 µg/mL.  

### Example 4. Experiment on Sustained Expression of T7 Polymerase Using Modified pT7/T7 Polymerase System  

B16F10/RFP cells transfected with T7pol mRNA and T7 autogene plasmid showed a 4.0-fold increase in T7pol expression over 9 days compared to cells transfected with pCMV_T7pol plasmid and T7 autogene (p < 0.0001). Western blotting confirmed sustained T7pol production, attributable to the autocatalytic loop triggered by cytoplasmic T7pol mRNA.  

### Example 5. Examination of Long-Term Cytoplasmic Expression of shRNAs by Auto_shRFP@LS and their Conversion to siRNAs  

TaqMan assays quantified mature RFP siRNA (siRFP) levels in transfected cells. Auto_shRFP@LS produced siRFP for >13 days, while a control system lacking T7 autogene (auto(−)_shRFP@LS) showed minimal siRNA production. The results demonstrate the critical role of T7pol amplification in sustained shRNA processing.  

### Example 6. Examination of In Vitro Gene Silencing by Auto shRNA @LS Nanoparticles  

qRT-PCR and flow cytometry revealed a 54% reduction in RFP mRNA and a 49% decrease in RFP fluorescence by day 7, with silencing persisting for 13 days. In contrast, synthetic siRFP@LS achieved only transient suppression (≤4 days).  

### Example 7. Examination of Cancer Accumulation Property of Auto_shRNA@LS Nanoparticles in Mouse Xenograft Model  

Cy5.5-labeled auto_shRFP@LS injected intravenously into B16F10 tumor-bearing mice showed preferential tumor accumulation via the EPR effect, with ex vivo fluorescence confirming high tumor uptake 24 h post-injection.  

### Example 8. Examination of Gene Silencing of Auto_shRNA@LS Nanoparticles in Mouse Xenograft Model  

A single injection of auto_shRFP@LS suppressed RFP fluorescence in tumors for 6 days, with immunohistochemistry and qRT-PCR confirming reduced RFP protein and mRNA levels. In contrast, synthetic siRFP@LS required repeated dosing for comparable effects.
