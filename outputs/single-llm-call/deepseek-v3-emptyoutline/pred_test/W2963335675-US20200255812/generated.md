# **DESCRIPTION**

## **FIELD OF THE INVENTION**  
The present invention relates to novel variants of *Rhizomucor miehei* lipase (RmL), including prolipase forms (ProRmL) and modified propeptide sequences, as well as methods for their production and use. Specifically, the invention encompasses engineered propeptide sequences that enhance stability, inhibit premature activation, and improve industrial applicability in detergent formulations, lipid hydrolysis, and other enzymatic processes. The invention also provides crystallographic data confirming structural interactions between the propeptide and mature enzyme domains, enabling rational design of optimized lipase variants.

## **BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION**  
Class 3 lipases, including *Rhizomucor miehei* lipase (RmL), are industrially important enzymes used in detergents, food processing, and biofuel production. These enzymes require a propeptide domain for proper folding and activity regulation. Prior to the present invention, structural insights into the propeptide’s role in inhibiting premature lipase activation were lacking. Conventional RmL variants suffer from instability during expression due to autoprocessing of the propeptide, leading to inconsistent yields and activity. Additionally, the absence of structural data on propeptide-enzyme interactions hindered targeted engineering. The present invention addresses these limitations by providing stabilized ProRmL variants, crystallographic models of propeptide binding, and methods to exploit these findings for industrial applications.

## **SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION**  
The invention provides:  
1. **Stabilized Prolipase Variants**: Engineered ProRmL constructs (e.g., ProRmL-del) with deletions or mutations in the propeptide-mature enzyme junction to prevent autoprocessing.  
2. **Structural Insights**: X-ray crystallography data revealing the propeptide’s inhibitory mechanism, including its binding to the lid domain and active site occlusion.  
3. **Industrial Applications**: Methods to produce and purify ProRmL variants, along with formulations for detergent and lipid-modification processes.  
4. **Inhibition Assays**: Demonstrated suppression of lipase activity by isolated propeptides, enabling controlled activation in industrial settings.

### **Example 3: Temperature Stability Assay (TSA)**  
ProRmL-del retained stability at 50°C for 1 h, while wild-type RmL aggregated.  

### **Example 4: Zymogram Analysis**  
ProRmL-del showed no hydrolysis zones, confirming propeptide-mediated inhibition.  

### **Example 5: Decylamine Agarose Chromatography**  
ProRmL-del failed to bind decylamine, unlike mature RmL, due to propeptide occlusion of hydrophobic residues.  

### **Example 6: Relative Wash Performance**  
In detergent formulations, ProRmL-del (activated post-addition) outperformed pre-activated RmL by 30% in lipid removal (GLAD assay).
