# DESCRIPTION  

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION  

The field of cell-based therapies has experienced significant advancements in recent years, yet a critical limitation remains in the ability to non-invasively monitor the distribution, persistence, and function of therapeutic cells in vivo. Current clinical approaches rely on invasive biopsy techniques, which suffer from sampling errors, patient discomfort, and inability to provide whole-body assessment of cell distribution. Positron emission tomography (PET) reporter gene (PRG) imaging offers a promising solution by enabling non-invasive tracking of genetically engineered cells.  

Existing PRG systems, particularly those based on viral thymidine kinases such as herpes simplex virus type 1 thymidine kinase (HSV1-tk), present significant drawbacks including immunogenicity that can lead to immune-mediated elimination of therapeutic cells. While human-derived nucleoside kinases have been explored as alternatives, these systems often retain substantial activity toward endogenous nucleosides, potentially disrupting cellular nucleotide metabolism and creating genotoxic risks. Furthermore, current PRG-PRP combinations demonstrate suboptimal biodistribution profiles in humans, with undesirable accumulation in non-target tissues.  

There exists an unmet need for a non-immunogenic PRG system that demonstrates: 1) high specificity for synthetic reporter probes over endogenous nucleosides, 2) reduced potential to disrupt cellular metabolism, 3) favorable biodistribution in humans, and 4) sensitivity comparable to existing viral-based systems. The development of such a system would represent a significant advancement in the field of molecular imaging and cell therapy monitoring.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a novel PET reporter gene/probe (PRG-PRP) system comprising:  
1) A mutated human thymidine kinase 2 (TK2) enzyme (TK2-N93D/L109F) as the PRG, engineered through structure-guided mutagenesis to exhibit:  
   - Reduced activity toward endogenous nucleosides thymidine and deoxycytidine  
   - Enhanced phosphorylation of L-configuration nucleoside analogs  
   - Decreased susceptibility to feedback inhibition by thymidine triphosphate (dTTP)  
2) 2-deoxy-2-18F-5-methyl-1-β-L-arabinofuranosyluracil (L-18F-FMAU) as the PRP, demonstrating favorable biodistribution characteristics  

The TK2-N93D/L109F mutant exhibits several advantageous properties:  
- The N93D mutation reduces dTTP-mediated feedback inhibition by disrupting the enzyme's closed conformation  
- The L109F mutation increases catalytic efficiency for L-configuration nucleosides  
- The combined mutations create an orthogonal enzyme with 4-fold increased preference for L-18F-FMAU over natural thymidine substrates  
- Maintains only 62% of wild-type activity toward deoxycytidine, minimizing metabolic disruption  

Preclinical studies demonstrate the system's sensitivity equals current viral-based systems (HSV1-sr39tk/18F-FHBG), while offering superior specificity and reduced immunogenicity risk. Human biodistribution studies confirm L-18F-FMAU clearance through hepatobiliary excretion with acceptable non-target accumulation.  

The invention further encompasses:  
- Methods for detecting PRG-expressing cells in vivo using PET imaging  
- Pharmaceutical compositions containing L-18F-FMAU for use with the PRG system  
- Vectors and cells engineered to express the TK2-N93D/L109F PRG  
- Methods for monitoring cell-based therapies using the PRG-PRP system  

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION  

The present invention provides a comprehensive solution to the limitations of current PRG systems through the development and characterization of a human-derived TK2 mutant paired with an optimized PET reporter probe.  

### The TK2-N93D/L109F Mutant Enzyme  

The PRG component comprises a truncated human TK2 enzyme (lacking the mitochondrial sorting signal) containing two key mutations:  

1. **N93D Mutation**:  
   - Located in a region homologous to the regulatory domain of Drosophila melanogaster deoxyribonucleoside kinase  
   - Disrupts hydrogen bonding with Glu200 in the closed conformation, favoring the open conformation  
   - Reduces dTTP-mediated feedback inhibition (only 11% inhibition at 100 μM dTTP vs 55% for wild-type)  
   - Increases preference for L-FMAU over D-thymidine (kcat ratio increases from 3.7 to 5.4)  

2. **L109F Mutation**:  
   - Positioned in the nucleoside binding pocket near the pyrimidine base  
   - Introduces steric bulk that unexpectedly improves catalysis for both D and L nucleosides  
   - Increases kcat for L-FMAU by 100% compared to wild-type  
   - When combined with N93D, creates synergistic improvement in L-FMAU specificity  

The double mutant exhibits kinetic parameters demonstrating its orthogonality to wild-type TK2:  

| Substrate | Wild-type kcat (min⁻¹) | N93D/L109F kcat (min⁻¹) | Fold Change |
|-----------|-----------------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| D-thymidine | 4.3 ± 0.4 | 2.2 ± 0.2 | ↓ 49% |
| L-FMAU | 16.0 ± 1.5 | 32.0 ± 3.0 | ↑ 100% |
| Deoxycytidine | 5.8 ± 0.6 | 3.6 ± 0.4 | ↓ 38% |

### The L-18F-FMAU Reporter Probe  

The PRP component, L-18F-FMAU, possesses several advantageous properties:  

1. **Biodistribution Characteristics**:  
   - Rapid clearance from blood pool (t1/2 < 30 min)  
   - Primary excretion via hepatobiliary route  
   - Minimal retention in thoracic cavity and brain  
   - Lower gastrointestinal accumulation compared to 18F-FHBG  

2. **Chemical Properties**:  
   - L-configuration reduces recognition by most human nucleoside kinases  
   - 5-methyl substitution provides metabolic stability  
   - 18F label (t1/2 = 109.8 min) ideal for clinical imaging  

3. **Synthetic Accessibility**:  
   - Can be produced in high radiochemical yield (>60%)  
   - Suitable specific activity (>50 GBq/μmol) for clinical use  
   - Demonstrated stability in formulation for >4 hours  

### System Performance  

The combined PRG-PRP system demonstrates exceptional performance characteristics:  

1. **In Vitro Sensitivity**:  
   - L1210 cells expressing TK2-N93D/L109F accumulate 4-fold more L-18F-FMAU than wild-type TK2 cells  
   - Uptake remains stable in presence of physiological thymidine concentrations (5 μM)  

2. **In Vivo Imaging**:  
   - Tumor xenografts show 19.9 ± 1.5 %ID/g at 3 hours post-injection  
   - Signal-to-noise ratio >10:1 in most tissues  
   - Comparable sensitivity to HSV1-sr39tk/18F-FHBG system (p=0.37)  

3. **Metabolic Safety**:  
   - Minimal impact on dNTP pool sizes in transduced cells  
   - No detectable genotoxicity in long-term culture  
   - Preserved cellular proliferation rates  

### Clinical Applications  

The invention enables multiple clinical applications:  

1. **Cell Therapy Monitoring**:  
   - Tracking of CAR-T cells in cancer immunotherapy  
   - Monitoring stem cell engraftment in regenerative medicine  
   - Assessing adoptive immune cell transfers  

2. **Gene Therapy Evaluation**:  
   - Non-invasive measurement of gene expression levels  
   - Determination of vector biodistribution  
   - Assessment of therapeutic duration  

3. **Research Applications**:  
   - Cell migration studies in preclinical models  
   - Optimization of cell delivery methods  
   - Pharmacodynamic assessment of supportive drugs  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Production and Characterization of TK2-N93D/L109F Mutant**  

The TK2-N93D/L109F mutant was generated through site-directed mutagenesis of a truncated human TK2 gene (Δ50N variant) cloned into a pET14b expression vector. Recombinant protein was expressed in E. coli BL21(DE3) cells and purified using nickel affinity chromatography followed by size-exclusion chromatography.  

Kinetic characterization revealed the mutant's unique properties:  
- kcat for L-FMAU increased to 32.0 ± 3.0 min⁻¹ (vs 16.0 ± 1.5 for wild-type)  
- Km for L-FMAU decreased to 45 ± 5 μM (vs 62 ± 7 μM for wild-type)  
- Catalytic efficiency (kcat/Km) for L-FMAU improved 2.8-fold  
- Only 11% inhibition at 100 μM dTTP vs 55% for wild-type  

### Example 2  

**L-18F-FMAU Radiosynthesis**  

L-18F-FMAU was produced via nucleophilic substitution of a 5-methyl-2,3-di-O-acetyl-1-β-L-arabinofuranosyluracil precursor with [18F]fluoride, followed by deprotection. The process yielded:  
- Radiochemical purity >98%  
- Specific activity >50 GBq/μmol  
- Total synthesis time <60 minutes  
- Consistent yields of 56 ± 12% (decay-corrected)  

### Example 3  

**In Vitro Uptake Studies**  

L1210 cells transduced with TK2-N93D/L109F showed:  
- 4.3 ± 0.5 % uptake/10⁶ cells at 1 hour  
- Linear uptake kinetics up to 2 hours  
- 47% reduction in uptake with 5 μM D-thymidine (vs 75% for TK2-N93D)  
- No significant cytotoxicity at concentrations up to 100 μM  

### Example 4  

**Mouse MicroPET/CT Imaging**  

SCID mice bearing L1210-TK2-N93D/L109F xenografts demonstrated:  
- Tumor uptake of 19.9 ± 1.5 %ID/g at 3 hours  
- Liver clearance by 2 hours post-injection  
- Bladder accumulation indicating renal excretion  
- Excellent tumor-to-muscle ratio (8.2:1)  

### Example 5  

**Comparison to HSV1-sr39tk System**  

Head-to-head comparison showed:  
- Equivalent sensitivity (24.1 ± 6.2 vs 19.9 ± 1.5 %ID/g, p=0.37)  
- Lower background in abdominal cavity for L-18F-FMAU  
- Similar image quality at equivalent time points  

### Example 6  

**Human Biodistribution Study**  

Healthy volunteers receiving L-18F-FMAU exhibited:  
- Peak liver uptake at 30 minutes (SUV 3.2 ± 0.4)  
- Myocardial uptake (SUV 2.1 ± 0.3)  
- Rapid blood clearance (t1/2 ~25 minutes)  
- Safe administration with no adverse events  

### Example 7  

**Metabolic Impact Assessment**  

TK2-N93D/L109F-expressing cells showed:  
- dTTP levels 98 ± 7% of control cells  
- dCTP levels 95 ± 6% of control  
- Normal cell cycle progression  
- Unchanged proliferation rates  

### Example 8  

**Long-term Genotoxicity Evaluation**  

After 30 population doublings, TK2-N93D/L109F cells displayed:  
- No increase in γ-H2AX foci  
- Normal karyotype by G-banding  
- Mutation frequency equivalent to controls  
- Stable transgene expression  

The complete specification demonstrates that the TK2-N93D/L109F/L-18F-FMAU system represents a significant advancement over existing PRG technologies, combining human origin, metabolic safety, and excellent imaging performance suitable for clinical translation.
