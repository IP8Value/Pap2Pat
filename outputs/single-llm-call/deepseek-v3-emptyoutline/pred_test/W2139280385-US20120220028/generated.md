# DESCRIPTION  

## TECHNICAL FIELD  

The present invention relates to the field of molecular biology and genetic engineering, specifically to novel promoter sequences derived from the major immediate-early (MIE) gene of human herpesvirus 6 (HHV-6). The invention encompasses enhancer elements, promoter constructs, gene expression systems, and methods for enhancing gene expression in eukaryotic cells, particularly in T-cell lines. The disclosed promoter sequences exhibit superior activity compared to conventional promoters such as the cytomegalovirus (CMV) MIE promoter, particularly in T cells, and are useful for driving heterologous gene expression in recombinant viral vectors, including varicella-zoster virus (VZV) vaccines.  

## BACKGROUND ART  

Promoters are essential regulatory elements in gene expression systems, enabling the transcription of downstream genes. The CMV MIE promoter is widely used due to its strong activity in various cell types. However, its performance in T cells is suboptimal, limiting its utility in T-cell-specific applications.  

HHV-6, a beta-herpesvirus, exhibits tropism for T cells and possesses a genomic structure analogous to CMV, including positional homologs of the MIE genes (U89/IE1 and U86/IE2). The R3 region in HHV-6 has been identified as an enhancer for the U95 gene, suggesting its potential role in regulating MIE gene expression. Prior studies have not characterized the HHV-6 MIE promoter or its utility in heterologous gene expression systems.  

There remains a need for promoters with enhanced activity in T cells and compatibility with viral vectors for therapeutic and vaccine applications.  

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION  

### Problems to be Solved by the Invention  

1. **Weak Promoter Activity in T Cells**: Existing promoters, such as the CMV MIE promoter, exhibit limited activity in T-cell lines, hindering efficient gene expression in these cells.  
2. **Lack of T-Cell-Specific Promoters**: No well-characterized promoters are available for high-level gene expression specifically in T cells.  
3. **Inefficient Heterologous Gene Expression in Viral Vectors**: Current promoters may not optimally drive foreign gene expression in recombinant viral vectors, such as VZV-based vaccines.  

### Solutions to the Problems  

The invention addresses these challenges by providing:  
1. An isolated HHV-6 MIE promoter sequence comprising the R3 enhancer region and the upstream regulatory region of the IE1 gene.  
2. An enhanced promoter construct incorporating the first intron of the HHV-6 IE1 gene, which significantly upregulates promoter activity.  
3. Gene expression systems and recombinant viral vectors (e.g., VZV) employing the HHV-6 MIE promoter to drive heterologous gene expression.  

### Advantageous Effects of Invention  

1. **Superior Activity in T Cells**: The HHV-6 MIE promoter exhibits stronger activity than the CMV promoter in T-cell lines (e.g., Jurkat, Molt-3, SupT1).  
2. **Enhanced Expression via Intron 1**: Inclusion of the first intron of the IE1 gene boosts promoter activity across multiple cell types.  
3. **Versatility in Viral Vectors**: The promoter functions effectively in recombinant VZV, enabling its use in polyvalent vaccines and gene therapy.  

## MODE FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION  

### DEFINITION  

For the purposes of this invention, the following terms are defined:  
- **HHV-6 MIE Promoter (HHV-6MIEp)**: A 971-bp DNA sequence (positions -983 to -13 relative to the IE1 exon 1 start site) derived from HHV-6 strain HST, comprising the R3 enhancer region.  
- **HHV-6MIEp-in1**: The HHV-6MIEp promoter including the first intron of the IE1 gene (total length: 1245 bp).  
- **NF-κB-Binding Site**: A critical regulatory element within the R3 region (positions -381 to -552) essential for promoter activity.  

## DESCRIPTION OF PREFERRED EMBODIMENTS  

### (Enhancer for Promoter)  

The R3 region within the HHV-6MIEp contains an NF-κB-binding site and an AP-1-binding site. Deletion of the NF-κB-binding site (e.g., in HHV-6MIEpΔNF-κBin1) abolishes promoter activity, confirming its functional necessity.  

### (Promoter)  

The HHV-6MIEp and its variants (e.g., deletion mutants HHV-6MIEp-d1 to HHV-6MIEp-d3) are cloned into reporter plasmids (e.g., pGL3-basic) to assess activity. The full-length promoter with intron 1 (HHV-6MIEp-in1) is preferred for maximal expression.  

### (Gene Construct)  

A gene construct comprises:  
1. The HHV-6MIEp or HHV-6MIEp-in1.  
2. A heterologous gene (e.g., luciferase, DsRed2, or therapeutic genes).  
3. A polyadenylation signal (e.g., BGH poly(A)).  

### (Method for Enhancing Expression of Gene in Cell)  

The method involves:  
1. Transfecting a cell (e.g., T cell or MRC-5) with a gene construct containing HHV-6MIEp-in1.  
2. Culturing the cell under conditions permitting gene expression.  
3. Detecting the expressed protein (e.g., via luciferase assay or fluorescence microscopy).  

### (Use)  

The promoter is used for:  
1. Driving gene expression in T-cell lines for research or therapy.  
2. Producing recombinant VZV vaccines expressing foreign antigens (e.g., mumps virus HN protein).  

## EXAMPLES  

### Example 1  

**Comparison of HHV-6MIEp and CMV MIEp Activity**  
- Luciferase assays in Jurkat, Molt-3, and SupT1 cells showed HHV-6MIEp-in1 activity was 2–3-fold higher than CMV MIEp.  
- In adherent cells (MRC-5, MeWo), HHV-6MIEp-in1 activity matched CMV MIEp.  

### Example 2  

**Role of Intron 1 in Promoter Activity**  
- Inclusion of intron 1 increased HHV-6MIEp activity by 50–70% in all tested cell lines.  
- Constructs with additional introns (1–4) showed reduced activity, indicating intron 1 specificity.  

### Example 3  

**Recombinant VZV with HHV-6MIEp-Driven DsRed2**  
- HHV-6MIEp-in1-DsRed-vOka-BAC was reconstituted into infectious virus in MRC-5 cells.  
- Western blotting confirmed DsRed2 expression, demonstrating promoter functionality in VZV.  

### Example 4  

**Deletion Analysis of HHV-6MIEp**  
- Deletion of the NF-κB-binding site (HHV-6MIEpΔNF-κBin1) reduced promoter activity by >80%.  
- The -381 to -552 region (containing R3) was critical for activity.  

## INDUSTRIAL APPLICABILITY  

The HHV-6 MIE promoter has broad applications, including:  
1. **Vaccine Development**: Driving antigen expression in recombinant viral vaccines (e.g., VZV-mumps).  
2. **Gene Therapy**: Enhancing transgene expression in T-cell therapies.  
3. **Biopharmaceutical Production**: Optimizing protein expression in T-cell cultures.
