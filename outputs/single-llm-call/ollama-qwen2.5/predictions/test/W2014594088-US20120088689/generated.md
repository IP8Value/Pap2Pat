- Versican isoforms V0 and V1 mRNA levels were correlated with various clinical parameters at time of biopsy, including age, creatinine, eGFR-MDRD, proteinuria. Correlations were also evaluated for follow-up parameters like creatinine, eGFR-MDRD, proteinuria, and histological parameters such as diagnosis, degree of TAIF, GS, and II.
- RNA isolation from kidney tissue and cell cultures was performed using the RNeasy Micro Kit and High Capacity cDNA Reverse Transcription kit. Real-time PCR was conducted with TaqMan Gene Expression Assays to quantify versican isoforms, normalized to cyclophilin A.
- Human renal proximal tubule cells (HK2) and kidney fibroblast cell line TK173 were cultured under specified conditions. Cells were treated with Ang II, PDGF-AB, or TGF beta-1 for 24 hours, followed by RNA isolation and quantitative real-time PCR to assess versican isoform expression.
- Immunohistochemistry was performed on frozen sections from six patients (three IgAN, three class IV lupus nephritis) and healthy control tissue. Sections were stained with rabbit anti-human Versican antibody and visualized using the Vectastain Elite ABC Kit, with AEC as the chromogen and hematoxylin as a counterstain.
- Three rodent models of renal disease—accelerated anti-GBM nephritis in mice, passive Heymann nephritis in rats, and adriamycin nephropathy in rats—were used to investigate versican expression. Real-time PCR was performed to analyze renal versican expression using species-specific TaqMan Gene Expression Assays.
- Western blot analysis of rat kidney tissue lysates confirmed the presence of versican protein. Tissue blocks were lysed, resolved on Bis-Tris gels, and transferred to nitrocellulose membranes. Membranes were incubated with rabbit anti-human versican antibody, followed by fluorescence-labeled secondary antibodies and imaging using an Odyssey infrared system.
