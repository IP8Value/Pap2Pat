- **Introduction**: This study explores enhancing GLP-1 analogues' stability and efficacy by substituting natural amino acids with unnatural ones. The modified peptides show improved pharmacokinetic properties and glucose-lowering effects in diabetic mice.

- **Blood Glucose Experiment in Healthy Mice**: Male C57BL/6J mice were randomized into 10 groups (n=3) based on body weight. They received intravenous treatments, and blood glucose levels were measured at various time points post-dosing to assess the acute effects of the GLP-1 analogues.

- **IPGTT Experiments in Mice**: C57BL/6J mice were fasted for 6 hours before receiving intravenous injections. Intraperitoneal glucose tolerance tests (2 g/kg) were conducted at different intervals post-dosing to evaluate the glucose-lowering effects of the GLP-1 analogues.

- **Blood Glucose Experiment in Db/db Mice**: Male db/db mice, a model for type 2 diabetes, were fasted and then randomized into five groups based on blood glucose levels, HOMA-IR, and body weight. They received intraperitoneal injections of the test items or vehicle, and blood glucose was measured at multiple time points to assess efficacy.

- **Pharmacokinetics**: Fifteen male C57BL/6J mice were treated with GLP-1 analogues via intravenous injections. Blood samples were collected at various time points, and plasma was analyzed by LC-MS/MS to determine the pharmacokinetic profile of the modified peptides.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Data were analyzed using GraphPad Prism 7.0. Statistical tests including two-way t-tests, ANOVA with Bonferroni post-test, and one-way ANOVA with Dunnett’s multiple comparison test were used to assess significance, with p-values < 0.05 considered significant.

- **Reporting Summary**: Detailed experimental design information is provided in the Nature Research Reporting Summary, ensuring transparency and reproducibility of the study's methods and results.
