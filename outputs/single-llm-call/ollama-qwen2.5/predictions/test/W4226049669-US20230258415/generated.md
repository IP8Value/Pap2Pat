- For laminar flows over LIS textures with transverse grooves, induced recirculation in cavities enhances heat transfer via dispersive convection (q conv,d). This effect is significant when the thermal conductivity of the solid and infusing liquid are similar (κ s ≈ κ i), making q conv,d essential. The ratio q conv,d /q depends on the Péclet number based on slip velocity and groove height, approximated by a logarithmic function for 10^1 < Pe_i < 10^3.

- In turbulent flows, dispersive convection still contributes to increased heat transfer, albeit less dominantly than in laminar cases. The random convective component (q conv,r) from bulk flow fluctuations plays a more significant role but changes only slightly compared to smooth surfaces. Dispersion amplifies the total heat flux considerably due to the high q conv,d /q ratio, which can be expressed as a function of groove height and system dimensions.
