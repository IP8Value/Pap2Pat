- **In Vitro Selection Process**: The study utilized a method called CeVICA (Combinatorial Enrichment and VHH Identification by CRISPR-Cas9 Assisted Capture) to identify binders for EGFP and RBD. This involved three rounds of ribosome display and in vitro selection, followed by sequencing to analyze the enrichment of specific nanobody sequences.

- **Affinity Maturation**: Error-prone PCR was used to introduce random mutations into selected nanobody DNA sequences, creating a mutagenized library. Three rounds of stringent selection were performed on this library to identify beneficial mutations that enhance binding affinity.

- **Pseudotyped SARS-CoV-2 Lentivirus Neutralization Assay**: HEK293T cells expressing ACE2 and TMPRSS2 were used to assess the neutralizing activity of nanobodies against SARS-CoV-2 pseudoviruses. The percentage of GFP-positive cells was quantified using flow cytometry to determine the effectiveness of the nanobodies.

- **Data Analysis**: Amino acid profiles were generated for pre- and post-affinity maturation libraries to identify beneficial mutations. These mutations were ranked based on their frequency increase, providing insights into potential improvements in binding affinity.
