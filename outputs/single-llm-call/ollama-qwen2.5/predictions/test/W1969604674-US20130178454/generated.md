- **Recombinant Follistatin (rFst) Production**: rFst was produced by expressing the gene in mammalian cells, followed by purification through endotoxin removal and dialysis. Its biological activity was confirmed by its ability to neutralize activin's growth inhibition in MPC11 cells, measured by thymidine incorporation.

- **Animal Studies for rFst Administration**: Eight-week-old C57BL6 mice were randomly assigned to groups receiving 0, 10, 30, or 100 μg of rFst daily via subcutaneous injection for 13 weeks. Body weights and lean/fat mass were monitored weekly, and tissue weights were measured post-euthanasia.

- **Microarray Transcriptome Analysis**: Mice were castrated and treated with testosterone or rFst. Prostate and levator ani muscle tissues were harvested, RNA extracted, and hybridized to Affymetrix chips. Data were normalized and analyzed for differential gene expression using linear models and Benjamini-Hochberg correction.

- **Time-Course Experiments of Testosterone Administration**: Castrated mice received subcutaneous testosterone or oil, and tissue weights and Odc1 expression were measured at days 1, 3, 7, and 14. This experiment aimed to track the temporal changes in prostate weight and gene expression following testosterone treatment.

- **In Vitro Studies with LNCaP Cells**: LNCaP cells were treated with testosterone or rFst, and cell cycle analysis was performed using BrdU incorporation and flow cytometry. Odc1 protein levels were assessed by Western blotting at various time points to evaluate the effects of these treatments on cellular proliferation.

- **Testosterone and DFMO Administration**: Mice were divided into groups: intact, castrated, castrated with testosterone, and castrated with testosterone plus DFMO. Subcutaneous testosterone and intraperitoneal DFMO or saline injections were administered daily for 14 days, after which tissue weights were measured to assess the effects of these treatments.
