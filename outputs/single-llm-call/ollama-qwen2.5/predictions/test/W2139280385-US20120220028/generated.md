- The study aimed to generate a recombinant VZV Oka strain expressing DsRed under the control of HHV-6 MIE promoter using bacterial artificial chromosome (BAC) technology. This involved constructing and validating plasmids, inserting cassettes into the BAC genome, and reconstituting infectious virus.
- Luciferase assays were conducted in adherent and suspended cells to compare promoter activities of HHV-6 MIE and CMV MIE promoters. The HHV-6 MIE promoter showed higher activity in some cell types, justifying its use for DsRed expression in the recombinant VZV strain.
- A LacZα-mini-attTn7 cassette was inserted into vOka-BAC genome using RecA-mediated recombination to create a site for subsequent Tn7 transposition. The insertion was confirmed by PCR and Southern blotting, enabling precise integration of the HHV-6 MIE-DsRed cassette.
- The HHV-6MIEpin1-DsRed-pFastBac plasmid was constructed by replacing gentamicin resistance and PH promoter regions with HHV-6 MIE promoter including intron 1. DsRed fragment was PCR amplified, HindIII sites added, and inserted into the plasmid to generate the final expression cassette for VZV recombination.
- The HHV-6MIEpin1-DsRed cassette was integrated into vOka-BAC-Tn genome using Tn7 transposition. E. coli colonies were selected based on antibiotic resistance, blue/white screening, and PCR verification of correct insertion. This yielded the final recombinant BAC construct for virus reconstitution.
- Infectious HHV-6MIEpin1-DsRed-rvOka was successfully reconstituted by transfecting MRC-5 cells with the recombinant BAC DNA. Typical VZV cytopathic effects and green/red fluorescence were observed, confirming expression of DsRed under the HHV-6 promoter in the viral context.
- Southern blot analysis verified the correct integration of HHV-6MIEpin1-DsRed cassette into vOka-BAC genome by detecting specific bands corresponding to ORF12, ORF13, DsRed and HHV-6 MIE probes. This confirmed the integrity of the recombinant BAC construct.
- Western blot analysis detected a specific band for DsRed protein in lysates from infected cells using anti-DsRed mAb, while VZV gB was detected with an anti-gB Ab as positive control. This demonstrated successful expression of DsRed under HHV-6 MIE promoter in the recombinant virus.
