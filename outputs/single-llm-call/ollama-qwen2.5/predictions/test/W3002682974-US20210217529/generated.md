- **Multivariate Normal Distribution for Likelihood Estimation:** A multivariate normal (MVN) distribution was used to estimate likelihoods due to its efficiency and widespread use. This approach involved fitting separate MVNs to positive and negative patient populations, estimating mean and covariance matrices from the data. The relative likelihood \(\beta_{\overset{\rightharpoonup}{x}}\) was then calculated as the ratio of these likelihoods.
  
- **Bootstrapping for Statistical Measures:** Bootstrapping was employed to obtain statistical measures of performance. For both risk models, a bootstrapped set was 20% the size of the test set and stratified for the outcome of interest. One-hundred bootstraps were performed for the GRACE score, while three were done for the Stroke risk model due to its lower event rate.

- **Trust Score Methodology:** The trust score method involved pre-processing the training set to identify examples from both classes, removing outliers, and measuring distances from a given test example. This resulted in a ratio of distances, serving as an alternate metric for quantifying prediction trustworthiness. The process ensured robustness by considering proximity to known outcomes.

- **Brier Score Calculation:** The average model error, or Brier score, was calculated using the formula \(B = \frac{1}{N}\sum\limits_{i = 1}^{N}\left( {y_{i} - f\left( \underset{i}{\overset{\rightharpoonup}{x}} \right)} \right)^{2}\). This score was normalized by the average error of a null model predicting every patient's risk as the outcome prevalence. Normalized Brier scores could exceed 1, providing a relative measure of model performance.

- **Calibration Curve Construction:** Calibration curves were constructed by binning predictions into intervals and computing the fraction of patients who died within each bin. For the GRACE model, 10 bins centered at specific points were used, while for the Stroke model, three equally spaced bins were applied due to fewer positive examples. The process was repeated for multiple bootstraps to ensure reliability and robustness in the calibration data.
