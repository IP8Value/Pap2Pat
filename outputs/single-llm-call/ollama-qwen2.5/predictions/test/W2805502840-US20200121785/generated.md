- **Growth Curve Studies**: Confluent CEF cells were infected with 100 μl of 10³ pfu SB-1 viruses and harvested at 0, 12, 24, 48, 96, and 120 hours post-infection. Titers were determined by plaque assay, and the experiment was repeated three times to ensure consistency.

- **Immunogenicity Validation - Experiment 1**: Forty one-day-old SPF RIR chicks were divided into four groups of ten birds each. Three groups received subcutaneous injections of pSB-1 UL10/11 VP2, pSB-1 UL21/22 VP2, or pSB-1 UL3/4 VP2 vaccines (3 × 10³ pfu). The control group was vaccinated with VaxxitekHVT+IBD® (5 × 10³ pfu).

- **Immunogenicity Validation - Experiment 2**: Two groups of eight one-day-old birds each were inoculated subcutaneously with 1000 pfu of pSB-1 UL3/4 VP2 or pSB-1 UL21/22 VP2. Control groups received either the parental pSB-1 virus (1000 pfu) or VaxxitekHVT+IBD®.

- **Challenge and Monitoring**: Four weeks post-vaccination, birds were challenged intra-nasally with 10⁴.³ TCID₅₀ of virulent UK661 IBDV. Clinical signs were monitored using a scoring system, and birds showing advanced symptoms (score >9) or consecutive scores of 5 were euthanized.

- **Serology**: Heat-treated serum samples were incubated with D78 IBDV strain for one hour at 37°C, then added to DF-1 monolayers. Neutralization titers were determined by the highest dilution preventing cytopathic effects, checked after four days.

- **Real-Time PCR Assay**: Confluent CEF cells infected with 500 pfu of SB-1 viruses were harvested 72 hours post-infection. RNA was purified, DNase-treated, and reverse transcribed. SYBR Green-based real-time PCR was performed to analyze gene expression, normalized to GAPDH.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Virus titers at each time point were compared using two-way ANOVA with multiple comparisons. Neutralizing antibody levels between groups and over time were analyzed using two-way ANOVA. Antibody levels within groups were assessed using one-way ANOVA. Survival rates post-challenge were compared using the Mantel-Cox test.
