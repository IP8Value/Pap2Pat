- **Cell Lines**: HNSCC cell lines were obtained from various sources and authenticated. They were cultured in specific media under controlled conditions to maintain their characteristics for experimental use.

- **2-D Fingerprint Screening**: Using C188 as a reference, over 490,000 compounds were screened for 100% similarity using the Tanimoto coefficient method, resulting in 207 potential candidates.

- **3-D Pharmacophore Sorting**: The selected 207 compounds from 2-D screening were converted into 3-D structures for further analysis to identify their pharmacological properties and potential interactions.

- **QSAR Studies**: A 3-D quantitative structure activity relationship (QSAR) study was conducted on the selected compounds to predict their biological activities and optimize their chemical structures.

- **STAT3/Py-Peptide Binding Assay**: The binding of STAT3 to phosphorylated and non-phosphorylated peptides was measured using surface plasmon resonance, assessing the impact of C188 and C188-9 on this interaction.

- **Phosphoflow Assay**: Kasumi-1 cells were treated with compounds, stimulated, permeabilized, and stained for phosphoproteins. Flow cytometry analysis determined the IC50 values for pSTAT3 and pSTAT1 activation.

- **Luminex Bead-Based Assay**: Luminex assays measured levels of pSTAT1, pSTAT3, and GAPDH in cell lysates to assess the effects of C188 and C188-9 on STAT protein phosphorylation.

- **Microscale Thermophoresis**: This technique measured binding affinities of C188-9 and EGFR Tyr(P)-1068 phosphopeptide to WT STAT3β, providing dissociation constants for these interactions.

- **Anchorage-Independent and Dependent Cell Growth**: Cells were cultured in the presence or absence of drugs in ultra-low attachment plates or regular cell culture plates, and viability was assessed using MTT assays.

- **UM-SCC-17B Xenografts**: UM-SCC-17B cells were injected into nude mice to form xenograft tumors. Tumor growth was monitored after treatment with DMSO, C188, or C188-9, and statistical analysis was performed.

- **RNA Extraction and Sequencing Using RNA-Seq**: Total RNA was extracted from cryosliced tumor tissues, processed using the Illumina TruSeq RNA sample prep kit, and sequenced to analyze gene expression changes.

- **PK Studies in Mice**: Pharmacokinetic studies were conducted to assess plasma levels of C188-9 following intraperitoneal and oral administration, as well as tumor vs. plasma levels in nude mice with xenograft tumors.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Student's t-test or paired t-test was used to compare control and C188-9 treated groups, ensuring statistical significance of the results.
