### Peptide Design and Synthesis
Peptides RI-3 and RERF were designed to target specific interactions involved in tumor progression. These peptides were synthesized using standard solid-phase peptide synthesis techniques, ensuring high purity and consistent batch-to-batch quality.

### Cell Culture
Human umbilical vein endothelial cells (HUVECs) and Sarc cell lines were cultured in complete medium supplemented with 10% fetal bovine serum (FBS), 1% penicillin-streptomycin, and 1% L-glutamine. Cells were maintained at 37°C in a humidified atmosphere containing 5% CO2.

### Peptide Characterization
The peptides RI-3 and RERF were characterized by mass spectrometry to confirm their molecular weights and by circular dichroism spectroscopy to assess their secondary structures in solution. Both peptides exhibited the expected characteristics, confirming their structural integrity.

### Cell Viability Assay
Cell viability was assessed using the MTS assay. HUVECs and Sarc cells were treated with varying concentrations of RI-3 or RERF for 48 hours. The absorbance of formazan produced by viable cells was measured at 490 nm, and cell viability was calculated as a percentage of untreated controls.

### Endothelial Cell Tube Formation
The ability of HUVECs to form vascular-like structures on matrigel was evaluated in the presence or absence of RI-3. HUVEC cultures were seeded on pre-coated matrigel and incubated for 6 hours. The number of tubes formed by cord-like structures exceeding 100 μm in length was quantified using Axiovision 4.4 software.

### Cell Adhesion Assay
GFP-tagged Sarc cells were used to assess adhesion onto an endothelial monolayer. HUVECs were seeded on matrigel-coated coverslips and allowed to form a confluent monolayer. GFP-Sarc cells were then added, and cell-associated fluorescence was measured using a plate reader after 2 hours.

### Trans-Endothelial Migration
Trans-endothelial migration of Sarc cells was monitored in real-time using the xCELLigence RTCA system. HUVEC monolayers were challenged with Sarc cells in the presence or absence of RI-3, and changes in electrical resistance due to cell migration were recorded over 24 hours.

### Tumor Growth and Vascularization
Sarc cells were injected subcutaneously into nude mice to evaluate tumor growth and vascularization. Mice were treated with RI-3 (6 mg/kg) or vehicle daily for 10 days. Tumor volumes were measured, and vascular density was assessed by counting CD31-positive vessels in tumor sections.

### Circulating Tumor Cells
Circulating tumor cells (CTCs) were isolated from murine blood samples using the QIAamp DNA Mini Kit. Quantitative RT-PCR targeting human Alu sequences was performed to enumerate CTCs, and results were normalized to a standard amplification curve generated from known numbers of spiked cells.

### Statistical Analysis
Data were analyzed using one-way ANOVA followed by post hoc Dunnett t-test for multiple comparisons. Results are expressed as means ± standard deviations. P < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.
