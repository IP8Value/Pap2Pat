- The study screened a library of 90,000 molecules to identify compounds that activate quorum sensing (QS) in V. cholerae by inducing bioluminescence in reporter strains. Compounds were tested at 20 µM concentration and those inducing >100-fold light production were selected for further analysis.

- Bioluminescence assays measured QS activation in V. cholerae and V. harveyi reporter strains. Cultures were diluted, treated with compounds dissolved in DMSO, and monitored for luminescence after 4-hour incubation at 30°C. DMSO served as the negative control.

- LuxO D47E protein was purified from E. coli using nickel affinity chromatography. The protein was expressed, harvested, lysed, and eluted with a linear imidazole gradient. Purified protein was concentrated, snap-frozen in liquid nitrogen, and stored at -80°C for ATPase assays.

- ATPase activity of LuxO D47E was measured using a coupled enzyme assay. ADP released by ATP hydrolysis reacts with PEP to form pyruvate, which is then converted to lactate, reducing NADH. The rate of NAD production at 340 nm infers the ATPase activity.

- DNA binding assays studied LuxO and Qrr promoter interactions using electrophoretic mobility shift and fluorescence anisotropy methods. These techniques assessed how compounds affect LuxO's ability to bind its target DNA sequences in the QS pathway.

- Mutagenesis generated a library of luxOD47E mutants, which were screened for resistance to compounds 11 and 12. Resistant clones producing comparable light to background levels were selected and sequenced to identify specific mutations conferring inhibitor resistance.

- Western blot analysis measured TcpA production in V. cholerae and VopD secretion in V. parahaemolyticus after treatment with compound 12. Cultures were grown under specified conditions, cells collected, and protein samples analyzed by SDS-PAGE and immunoblotting.

- Cytotoxicity assays evaluated the effect of compounds on V. parahaemolyticus-induced cell death in HeLa cells. Bacteria were added to HeLa monolayers pre-treated with DMSO or 500 µM compound 12, and LDH release was measured as an indicator of cytotoxicity over time.

- Chemical synthesis methods for compounds and analytical techniques like NMR and mass spectrometry are described in the Supporting Text. These procedures detail how candidate molecules were prepared and characterized to confirm their structures and purity.
