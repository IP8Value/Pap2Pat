- The study was designed by FG, SW, BEC, DEM, and DAB to explore intermittent baroreflex function in SHR and Wistar rats. EBR, FG, BEC, and DAB developed the computational methods for analyzing BP and HR data.
- FG and EBR created the mathematical model and algorithm to detect baroreflex intermittency. FG collected and analyzed the data, while GDF, DEM, and DAB provided necessary resources.
- FG and SW performed animal surgeries, and KCB conducted ultrasound measurements. FG drafted the manuscript, which was reviewed and edited by EBR, SW, GDF, DEM, and DAB. BEC, GDF, DEM, and DAB supervised the project.
- The Institutional Animal Care and Use Committees of U-Mich and MSU approved all experiments. Statistical analyses were conducted using GraphPad Prism 8.1.1, with significance levels indicated as *P < 0.05, **P < 0.01, ***P < 0.001, and ****P < 0.0001.
- The computational analysis was performed in MATLAB 2019b, and all codes and raw data are available via the provided DOI or GitHub repository. The time-series data were processed to identify on and off states of baroreflex function using a linear filter model.
- To estimate parameters α and τ, the cost functional J was minimized by comparing the variability in HR between the model output and the data. On and off periods were identified based on the slopes of the regression lines within fixed windows, with noise filtered out to smooth the results.
- The on fraction index was defined as the total time in the on state divided by the total time, representing the proportion of time when baroreflex function is active. Representative probability distributions and correlation statistics were computed using MATLAB functions ksdensity and polyfit.
- All authors contributed to the conception, methodology, data collection, analysis, and manuscript preparation, ensuring a comprehensive and rigorous approach to understanding intermittent baroreflex function in hypertensive rats.
