### Here’s a concise summary:

- **Rationale and Objective**: This study aims to develop an efficient gene silencing platform using self-amplifying lipoplexes (auto_shRNA@LS) for targeted delivery of shRNAs. The platform integrates T7 RNA polymerase, enabling sustained production of siRNAs from transfected cells.

- **Design of Auto_shRFP@LS**: The auto_shRFP@LS complexes are composed of plasmids encoding T7 RNA polymerase and shRNA against RFP, encapsulated in liposomes. This design ensures efficient cellular uptake and intracellular processing to produce mature siRNAs.

- **Construction of Plasmids**: Plasmids were constructed to express T7 RNA polymerase (pCMV_T7pol) and shRNA targeting RFP (pT7/shRFP). These plasmids were co-transfected into B16F10/RFP cells to evaluate their expression and silencing efficiency.

- **In Vitro Characterization**: The complexes were characterized for their physicochemical properties, including size, zeta potential, and stability. Cellular uptake was assessed using flow cytometry and confocal microscopy, demonstrating efficient internalization and nuclear localization of the lipoplexes.

- **Expression and Silencing Efficiency**: Western blotting and qRT-PCR analyses confirmed the expression of T7 RNA polymerase and the subsequent production of mature siRNAs. RFP mRNA and protein levels were significantly reduced in cells transfected with auto_shRFP@LS, indicating effective gene silencing.

- **Biodistribution Studies**: In vivo biodistribution studies using Cy5.5-labeled lipoplexes showed preferential accumulation in tumor tissues compared to other organs, highlighting the targeted delivery capability of the complexes.

- **In Vivo Gene Silencing**: Intravenous administration of auto_shRFP@LS to B16F10/RFP xenograft mice resulted in a significant reduction in RFP fluorescence signals. Ex vivo analysis confirmed the sustained silencing effect and reduced RFP expression in tumor tissues, validating the therapeutic potential of the platform.

- **Supplementary Information**: Additional data, including detailed protocols, raw images, and statistical analyses, are provided to support the findings and ensure transparency and reproducibility of the study.
