### Dengue Virus Infectivity Assays

In vitro anti-dengue activity assays involved mixing Csp_P bacterial cultures with dengue virus serotype 2 and incubating the mixture at room temperature for 45 minutes. The mixture was then filtered, serially diluted, and used to infect BHK-21 cells. Plaque assays were conducted to assess dengue virus infectivity, with percent inhibition calculated relative to a PBS+LB control.

### Sequestration of Viral Particles

To determine if Csp_P biofilm sequesters DENV, the virus was mixed with 48-hour-old Csp_P biofilm or LB broth and incubated for 45 minutes. Samples were centrifuged, supernatants collected, and RNA extracted using a RNeasy kit. RT-qPCR was performed to compare viral RNA loads in experimental and control samples.

### pH Effects on Dengue Virus

The pH of bacterial biofilms and supernatants was measured using a micro-pH electrode. The effects of pH changes on dengue virus infectivity were assessed by adjusting the pH of MEM with NaOH and HCl to values of 5.0, 7.7, 8.5, and 10.0. The adjusted MEM was mixed with dengue virus-laden blood, incubated for 45 minutes, and then used to infect C6/36 cells.

### Cell Viability Assays

Cell viability assays were conducted on the mosquito cell line C6/36 and the vertebrate cell line BHK-21 using trypan blue staining. Cells were mixed with filtered Csp_P biofilm or PBS and incubated at respective temperatures for 45 minutes. The mixture was then stained with trypan blue, allowed to stand for 5 minutes, and assessed under a microscope for viability.

### Host Cell Susceptibility

To assess the effect of Csp_P biofilm on host cell susceptibility to DENV, C6/36 cells were overlaid with filtered Csp_P biofilm or PBS for 10 minutes. The medium was then washed off, and cells were infected with dengue virus for 45 minutes. Plaque formation was assessed at 6 days post-infection to determine any changes in cell susceptibility.

### Hemolysis Assay

Human erythrocytes were washed in RPMI 1640 until the supernatant was free of hemoglobin. Erythrocytes were suspended in malaria complete medium to a 1% hematocrit and mixed with filtered Csp_P biofilm. The mixture was incubated at 37°C for up to 24 hours, centrifuged, and the supernatant was read at 405 nm to determine % lysis compared to freeze-thawed erythrocyte lysate.

### Real-Time qPCR Assays

RNA samples were treated with Turbo DNase and reverse-transcribed using M-MLV Reverse Transcriptase. Real-time PCR assays were performed using the SYBR Green PCR Master Mix Kit in a 20-µl reaction volume, with all samples tested in duplicate. The ribosomal protein S7 gene was used for normalization of cDNA templates.

### Statistical Analysis

Statistical analyses were conducted using GraphPad Prism software, including Mann-Whitney U tests, one-way ANOVA with Dunnett's post-test, and pairwise Log-Rank tests for survival analysis. Data in Figure 5 were analyzed using an ANOVA followed by a Tukey's test in R.
