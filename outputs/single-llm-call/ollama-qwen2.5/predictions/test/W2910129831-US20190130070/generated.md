- The framework described constructs disease progression models from observational data, applied here to Huntington's Disease (HD). It provides a comprehensive view of HD states across the entire progression pathway and characterizes disease transitions. Expected durations for disease states are generated, and individual patient state sequences can be inferred.

- For this study, an integrated observational HD dataset was used to inform a HD progression model. This model offers a nuanced understanding of patients' conditions and identifies those nearing motor onset or advancing stages, aiding personalized care management and clinical trial recruitment.

- The framework's flexibility allows it to be applied to other diseases with appropriate adjustments based on clinical knowledge. However, quantitative evaluation is challenging due to the lack of a proper gold standard for HD progression models.

- Validation of the developed model utilized existing HD literature and domain expert feedback. Future research will focus on validating the model using independent datasets and translating it into clinical practice. Contributions from all authors are acknowledged, with specific roles outlined in constructing the dataset, designing methods, and guiding clinical aspects.
