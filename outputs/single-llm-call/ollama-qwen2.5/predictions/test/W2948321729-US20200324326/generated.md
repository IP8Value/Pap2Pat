- **Ami-Pc Electrode Fabrication**: Carbon felt pieces (1 cm diameter) were dip-coated with a PAN, Super P carbon black, and DMF slurry, dried at 70°C. They were then treated in a water bath with hydroxylamine hydrochloride and sodium carbonate for 90 minutes, washed, and dried in a vacuum furnace.

- **Characterization of Soil Properties**: Metal concentrations were measured using U.S. EPA Method 3050 and ICP-MS. Soil pH was determined with a soil-water ratio of 1:2. Texture was analyzed by the hydrometer method, organic carbon by the Walkley–Black method, and cation exchange capacity by sodium ion saturation.

- **Soil Remediation Experiment**: Contaminated soil (3 g) was packed into a plastic tube with tissue paper to prevent particle loss. An AACE filter device was connected to a power supply. EDTA solution (8 ml, 30 mM) was infused and circulated through the soil column and filter by a peristaltic pump.

- **Material Characterization**: FTIR spectra were measured using Nicolet iS50 in ATR mode. SEM images and EDS were taken with FEI Nova NanoSEM 450 at 5 kV for imaging and 15 kV for EDX. XPS spectra were collected with PHI VersaProbe Scanning XPS Microprobe, and TEM images with FEI Titan TEM at 300 keV.

- **Long-Term Performance Evaluation**: Twenty-five soil samples (3 g each) spiked with 10,000 ppm Cu and mixed with 1.2 ml EDTA solution were prepared. For the first cycle, 6.8 ml of 30 mM EDTA was infused at 0.5 ml/min and filtrated by the AACE filter. The generated washing effluent (6 ml) was reused for subsequent cycles.

- **Plant Assay**: Pisum Sativum seeds were planted in pots with a coir-to-soil ratio of 1:9 to improve drainage. After 15 days, pea sprouts were harvested, dried at 70°C, and ground. Heavy metal concentrations were determined by U.S. EPA Method 3050 and ICP-MS analysis.
