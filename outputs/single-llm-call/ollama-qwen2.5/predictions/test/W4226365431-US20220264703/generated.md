- The paper introduces a fully integrated magnetic microheater array using ferromagnetic resonance of MNPs at GHz frequencies. This innovative design offers the highest spatial resolution, lowest dc power consumption, and best dc-to-RF energy efficiency compared to existing MNP-based thermal applicators.

- The stacked oscillator core generates >19.5 Vpp RF swing for four-stacked oscillators and >26.5 Vpp for five-stacked oscillators from 1.44-1.95 GHz, closely matching simulations. Safe operation is verified through six-day monitoring of constant oscillation amplitudes.

- Temperature sensing uses a PTAT sensor with 27 mV/°C slope and -220 mV/°C temperature-to-Vtail conversion gain. The operating range can be tuned by adjusting reference voltages, enabling precise closed-loop temperature regulation from 37-49°C with 0.53/0.29°C max/RMS error.

- A PDMS membrane demo validates localized heating performance. With MNPs, temperatures reach 41.93-47.05°C vs <37.8°C without MNPs under the same biasing. Only areas above inductors (~0.03 mm2) heat efficiently, demonstrating sub-millimeter spatial resolution.

- The design balances penetration depth, spatial resolution and heating efficiency for in-vitro/microfluidic applications at ~100 μm depth. For deeper in-vivo heating, the frequency can be decreased to hundreds of MHz or inductor size increased, trading off some spatial resolution.

- This high-resolution magnetic microheater array has broad biomedical potential for localized thermal therapies. The authors thank GlobalFoundries for fabrication and acknowledge helpful technical discussions with RISE Lab members and Dr. Liuyang Sun.
