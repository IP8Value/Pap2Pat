- The system design integrates an HOA 2.0 ion trap with a compact cryogenic package, featuring a high NA lens and helical RF resonator on the 90-K plate. This enables radial mode frequencies of 2-3 MHz and supports long ion chains in ultra-high vacuum.
- Differential pumping combined with cryopumping generates state-of-the-art UHV conditions, essential for maintaining stable long ion chains over extended periods. Zig-zag chain transitions map out pressure as a function of temperature, indicating exponential scaling due to H₂ desorption from the getter.
- Motional heating rates were measured using Doppler and sideband cooling techniques, yielding an average rate of 13 quanta/s with low variability. This translates to a noise spectral density significantly better than other cryogenic traps, ensuring high-fidelity entangling gates are achievable.
- Ramsey spectroscopy on carrier Raman rotations demonstrated a 330 ms optical coherence time, indicating excellent stability despite the closed-cycle cryostat environment. This coherence time suggests that optical phase fluctuations will not limit entangling gate fidelity in this system.
