# DESCRIPTION

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates generally to methods for network-based gene expression analysis, particularly in the context of identifying candidate biomarkers and improving microarray classification accuracy. Current approaches often fail to fully leverage the interactions among genes or gene products, treating each gene's expression individually without considering its neighbors with similar functions within a network. This limitation hinders the identification of functionally related genes that may be weakly expressed but collectively significant. There is a critical need for an approach that integrates gene expressions and network interactions to enhance biomarker discovery and classification accuracy.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The invention introduces the MIXP (Integrative eXpression Profiling) approach, which significantly improves microarray classification accuracy by serving as a feature transformation method. MIXP not only helps in identifying groups of genes that are too weakly expressed to be detected individually but also enhances the discovery of functionally related genes through network-based gene expression analysis. The core of MIXP is the iterative weighing of network nodes, which allows for more accurate and comprehensive profiling.

The MIXP method involves several key steps: selecting seed molecules, constructing a disease-specific protein-protein interaction (PPI) network, reordering the network using the Ant Colony Optimization Reordering (ACOR) algorithm, and integrating gene expressions. The ACOR algorithm is particularly effective in reordering nodes to reveal system-level features of the network, creating a fractal-like pattern that enhances the integration of expression profiles.

The database creation method involves mining large-scale protein interaction databases such as HAPPI to construct disease-specific PPI networks. Gene-expression profile mapping is achieved by integrating gene expressions onto the reordered gene list using Gaussian functions. This approach facilitates the determination of health situation propensity, such as the classification of Alzheimer's disease (AD) samples.

## DESCRIPTION OF EMBODIMENTS OF THE PRESENT INVENTION

Gene expression profiling is a critical tool in understanding biological processes. DNA Microarray technology allows for the simultaneous measurement of thousands of genes, providing a snapshot of gene activity. Sequence-based techniques, such as SuperSAGE and RNA-Seq, offer high-resolution and quantitative data on gene expression levels. These methods are essential for identifying differentially expressed genes and understanding gene regulation.

Expression profiling is motivated by the need to understand how gene expression changes in response to various conditions, such as disease states. Gene regulation involves complex interactions between transcription factors, enhancers, and other regulatory elements. Factors affecting gene expression include environmental cues, genetic variations, and cellular signals. Expression profiling experiments typically involve measuring mRNA levels using microarrays or RNA-Seq, contrasting healthy and diseased samples to identify differentially expressed genes.

The MIXP method is particularly effective in improving the classification of disease states. For example, in Alzheimer's disease (AD), the AD-specific PPI network is constructed by selecting seed molecules associated with AD and mining protein interaction databases like HAPPI. The ACOR algorithm is then applied to reorder the adjacency matrix of the PPI network, creating a fractal-like pattern that groups functionally related genes together.

The integrated expression profiles are generated by mapping average differential expression values onto the reordered gene list and integrating these values using Gaussian functions. This process enhances the detection of weakly expressed but collectively significant genes, improving microarray classification accuracy. The MIXP approach has been shown to outperform traditional methods based on ranking, clustering, or random-ordering, demonstrating its utility in knowledge-supervised feature transformation.

In conclusion, the MIXP method provides a robust and effective way to integrate gene expression data with network interactions, enhancing the identification of biomarkers and improving classification accuracy. This invention has significant implications for disease diagnosis and personalized medicine.
