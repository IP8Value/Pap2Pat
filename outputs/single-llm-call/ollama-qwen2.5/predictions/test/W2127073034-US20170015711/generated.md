- **Cell Line Development**: NPYC and KM led the development and analysis of cell lines for producing BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 trimers, working closely with MG, KR, and PJK to optimize expression and stability. This involved selecting high-producing clones and characterizing their trimer yield and quality.

- **ELISA Development**: TK developed an ELISA method to quantify trimer production in culture supernatants. The assay used MAb 2G12 for capture and biotin-labeled PGT145 or F105 for detection, allowing the team to assess both desired trimers and undesired forms of Env.

- **Electron Microscopy**: HJK, IAW, and ABW performed negative stain electron microscopy (EM) to visualize the purified BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 trimers. This provided structural insights into the trimer's conformation and helped confirm its native-like properties.

- **Thermal Stability Analysis**: JK and AC conducted differential scanning calorimetry (DSC) to evaluate the thermal stability of 293T and CHO cell-derived trimers. The analysis involved SEC-purification in PBS, followed by thermal denaturation at a scan rate of 90°C/h.

- **SPR Binding Studies**: AY and PJK performed surface plasmon resonance (SPR) experiments to assess the binding kinetics of MAbs to purified trimers. This involved capturing Env-reactive MAbs on a CM5 chip and recording trimer binding responses at 25°C.

- **Vector Design**: AM and AC designed the pAM/C vector, which was used to express the BG505 SOSIP.664 gp140 trimers in mammalian cells. This vector incorporated elements for high-level expression and stability, crucial for successful trimer production.

- **Manuscript Preparation**: NPYC, KM, AC, and JPM wrote the manuscript, integrating data from various experiments and analyses. The manuscript was refined with input from PJK, RWS, IAW, and ABW, ensuring a comprehensive and accurate representation of the study's findings.
