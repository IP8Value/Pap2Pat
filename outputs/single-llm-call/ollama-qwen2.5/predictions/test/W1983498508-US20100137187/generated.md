- Peptide Glu-Oct6-FITC efficiently entered DU-145, PANC-1, and LNCaP cells, localizing to nuclei. Its uptake was concentration- and temperature-dependent, inhibited by azide. The homologous peptide Oct6-FITC showed no nuclear localization.

- Substituting Glu with Phe, Tyr, or Asn in the N-terminal glutamate-rich sequence abolished Glu-Oct6's ability to function as a cell-penetrating peptide (CPP).

- Glu-Oct6 facilitated entry of carboxyfluorescein-PNA into DU-145 and PANC-1 cells. This suggests potential for delivering therapeutic agents.

- Anti-STAT3 PNAs, delivered by Glu-Oct6, induced significant apoptosis in DU-145 and PANC-1 cells. This highlights the peptide's therapeutic potential.

- Glutamate receptors, often overexpressed in cancer cells, may mediate Glu-Oct6 binding. This opens possibilities for cancer-specific targeting strategies.

- The study demonstrates how adding a glutamate-rich sequence enhances nuclear localization of transcription factor NLS peptides. Future research will explore this enhancement with other NLS sequences.
