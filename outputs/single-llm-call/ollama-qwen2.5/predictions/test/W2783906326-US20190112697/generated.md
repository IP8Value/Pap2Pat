# Patent Application: Si-Gradient Steel Sheet

## Background

### Field of Invention

The present invention relates to a method for producing silicon (Si)-gradient steel sheets and their applications in electrical devices. These steel sheets are designed to optimize magnetic properties, particularly at high frequencies, by creating a controlled gradient of silicon concentration across the thickness of the sheet.

### Description of Related Art

Conventional electrical steel sheets used in transformers and motors typically have uniform silicon content throughout their thickness. While this approach has been effective for low-frequency applications, it falls short in high-frequency environments due to increased eddy current losses and reduced permeability. The need for improved magnetic properties at higher frequencies has driven the development of new materials with tailored compositions.

## Summary of the Invention

The invention provides a method for producing Si-gradient steel sheets that exhibit enhanced magnetic performance, particularly at high frequencies. By creating a gradient in silicon concentration from the surface to the core of the sheet, the material achieves lower eddy current losses and improved permeability. This is achieved through controlled metallurgical processes that ensure a consistent and predictable distribution of silicon.

## Detailed Description

### Production Method

The production method involves several key steps: (1) preparing a base steel with a low silicon content, (2) applying a high-silicon layer to one or both surfaces, and (3) diffusing the silicon into the bulk material through controlled heat treatment. The diffusion process is carefully managed to create a linear or non-linear gradient of silicon concentration across the thickness of the sheet.

### Material Properties

The Si-gradient steel sheets exhibit unique properties due to their composition. At the surface, where the silicon concentration is highest, the material has higher resistivity and lower permeability, which helps reduce eddy current losses. In the core, where the silicon content is lower, the material retains high permeability, facilitating efficient magnetic flux conduction.

### Magnetic Performance

The Si-gradient steel sheets show superior magnetic performance compared to conventional uniform silicon steel sheets. At low frequencies, they exhibit similar or slightly higher iron losses but at high frequencies, their eddy current losses are significantly reduced. This makes them ideal for applications in high-frequency electrical devices such as transformers and electric motors.

### Stress Distribution

The gradient in silicon concentration also leads to a stress distribution within the material. Tensile stress is formed at the surface layer due to the higher silicon content, while compressive stress is present in the inner layers. This internal stress affects the magnetization behavior of the steel sheet, with the surface layer being preferentially magnetized during excitation.

### Applications

Si-gradient steel sheets are particularly suitable for high-frequency applications where minimizing eddy current losses is crucial. They can be used in a variety of electrical devices, including transformers, motors, and generators, leading to improved efficiency and performance.

## Claims

1. A method for producing a Si-gradient steel sheet comprising the steps of:
   - Preparing a base steel with a low silicon content.
   - Applying a high-silicon layer to one or both surfaces of the base steel.
   - Diffusing the silicon into the bulk material through controlled heat treatment to create a gradient in silicon concentration across the thickness of the sheet.

2. The method of claim 1, wherein the diffusion process is managed to create a linear gradient of silicon concentration from the surface to the core of the sheet.

3. A Si-gradient steel sheet produced by the method of claim 1, characterized by a higher silicon concentration at the surface and a lower silicon concentration in the core.

4. The Si-gradient steel sheet of claim 3, wherein the surface layer exhibits higher resistivity and lower permeability compared to the core.

5. The Si-gradient steel sheet of claim 3, suitable for use in high-frequency electrical devices due to its reduced eddy current losses and improved magnetic performance.

6. An electrical device comprising a Si-gradient steel sheet as defined in claim 3, wherein the device is selected from the group consisting of transformers, motors, and generators.
