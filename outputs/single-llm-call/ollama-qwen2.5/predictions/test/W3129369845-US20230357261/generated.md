- **Induction of NEC Model**: Neonatal mice were fed formula and exposed to intermittent hypoxia and cold stress to induce necrotizing enterocolitis (NEC). Pups were monitored for clinical signs and sacrificed at day 7 for tissue collection. Disease severity was assessed by histological scoring, cytokine levels, and intestinal permeability.
- **Ahr Activation**: Mice were treated with Ahr agonists such as indole-3-carbinol (I3C) or 6-formylindolo[3,2-b]carbazole (FICZ) to activate the aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AhR). Tissue samples were collected for gene expression analysis and immunohistochemistry.
- **Histological Analysis**: Ileal tissues were fixed in formalin, embedded in paraffin, sectioned, and stained with hematoxylin and eosin. Histological sections were scored for inflammation, epithelial damage, and villus atrophy by a blinded pathologist.
- **Cytokine Measurement**: Cytokines (IL-1β, IL-6, TNF-α) in serum and ileal tissues were measured using ELISA kits according to the manufacturer's instructions. Data were normalized to protein content and expressed as picograms per milligram of protein.
- **Intestinal Permeability Assay**: Pups were gavaged with FITC-dextran (4 kDa), and blood was collected 3 hours later. Serum fluorescence was measured using a spectrophotometer, and intestinal permeability was quantified by the amount of FITC-dextran in the serum.
- **Flow Cytometry Analysis**: Single-cell suspensions from ileal tissues were prepared and stained with antibodies against CD45, CD11c, CD11b, Ly6G, and other markers. Cells were analyzed using flow cytometry to assess immune cell populations, including neutrophils, macrophages, and dendritic cells.
- **Peritoneal Macrophage Isolation**: Peritoneal exudate cells were isolated from wild-type and Ahr knockout mice, cultured, and treated with I3C. Gene expression of AhR and Cyp1a1 was analyzed by qRT-PCR to evaluate the activation status of AhR.
- **Statistical Analysis**: Data were analyzed using GraphPad Prism 9. Statistical significance was determined by two-sided t-tests or one-way ANOVA followed by multiple comparisons. Results are presented as mean ± SEM, with p-values < 0.05 considered significant.
