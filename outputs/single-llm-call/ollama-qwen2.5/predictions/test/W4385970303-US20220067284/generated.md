In response to U.S. President-elect Barack Obama's stance on Iran’s nuclear program, Ali Larijani, Iran’s parliament speaker, emphasized that change must be strategic, not superficial. Larijani insisted that the U.S. should alter its approach fundamentally and not merely repeat objections to Iran’s nuclear ambitions. He noted that Iran does not object to the U.S. providing nuclear technology to other Gulf countries but will pursue its own goals regardless.
