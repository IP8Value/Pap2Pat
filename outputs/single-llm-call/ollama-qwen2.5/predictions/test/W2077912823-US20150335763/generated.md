- **Introduction**: This study explores a novel approach using HMGB1-specific DNA beads to ameliorate disease severity in experimental models of colitis. High levels of HMGB1 are known to increase epithelial cell permeability and impair intestinal barrier function, making it an important target in inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD).

- **Development of DNA Beads**: The strong DNA-binding property of HMGB1 was leveraged to develop DNA beads that selectively bind and sequester HMGB1 from biological fluids. These beads were designed to address the various redox forms of HMGB1 present during inflammation, ensuring effective neutralization.

- **Assessment of Redox States**: In vitro assessments showed that DNA beads dose-dependently sequestered different isoforms of HMGB1, including cytokine-inducing disulfide and chemotaxis-inducing fully reduced forms. The predominant form captured from colitis fecal material was the disulfide isoform, highlighting its role in mucosal inflammation.

- **Binding Specificity**: DNA beads specifically bound to HMGB1 and not to other proteins like histone H3, indicating their selective nature. This specificity is crucial for effective therapy without off-target effects.

- **Stability and Safety**: The DNA beads were stable and did not show toxicity in animals treated with either DNA beads or control beads. Additionally, the neutralizing effect of DNA beads was independent of type 1 IFN expression, further enhancing their therapeutic potential.

- **In Vivo Efficacy in DSS Colitis**: Oral administration of B2 DNA beads significantly improved weight loss, reduced circulating and fecal HMGB1 levels, and decreased colonic wall thickening in mice with DSS-induced colitis. Histological scores also showed significant improvement, confirming the therapeutic benefits.

- **Efficacy in Chronic Colitis (IL-10 KO Mice)**: In IL-10 knockout mice with spontaneously developed colitis, B2 DNA beads increased body weight, reduced serum HMGB1 levels, and decreased colonic inflammation. Histological evaluation revealed significant improvements in colon wall thickness and overall tissue injury.

- **Discussion**: The study demonstrates that HMGB1-specific DNA beads effectively sequester HMGB1 and improve disease pathogenesis in both acute and chronic models of colitis. This approach offers a promising therapeutic strategy for IBD, leveraging the high affinity of HMGB1 to DNA for targeted treatment.
