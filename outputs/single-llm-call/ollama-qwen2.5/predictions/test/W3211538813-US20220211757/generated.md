- **PBMC-Derived Cell Generation**: PBMCs were isolated from healthy donors and used to generate various immune cell types, including T cells (PBMC-Tc), natural killer cells (PBMC-NK), and invariant natural killer T cells (PBMC-iNKT). These cells were cultured with specific cytokines and activators to enhance their functionality and were cryopreserved for later use in experiments.

- **AlloHSC-iNKT Cell Generation**: Hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) from healthy donors were differentiated into iNKT cells using a combination of growth factors and cytokines. The resulting AlloHSC-iNKT cells were characterized by their expression of iNKT TCR and CD45, and they were also cryopreserved for subsequent experiments.

- **In Vitro Antitumor Efficacy**: In vitro assays were conducted to evaluate the antitumor efficacy of AlloHSC-iNKT cells against various cancer cell lines. The cells were co-cultured with tumor cells, and their ability to induce tumor cell death was assessed using luminescence and flow cytometry.

- **Graft-Versus-Host (GVH) Response**: Mixed lymphocyte reaction (MLR) assays were performed to study the GVH response of AlloHSC-iNKT cells. Irradiated PBMCs from multiple donors served as stimulators, while AlloHSC-iNKT cells and their derivatives acted as responders. IFN-γ production was measured to assess the immune response.

- **Host-Versus-Graft (HVH) Response**: MLR assays were also used to evaluate the HVH response of PBMCs against AlloHSC-iNKT cells. Irradiated AlloHSC-iNKT cells and their derivatives served as stimulators, while PBMCs from multiple donors acted as responders. IFN-γ production was measured to assess the immune response.

- **NK Cell-Mediated Allorejection**: The ability of PBMC-NK cells to reject allogeneic targets was studied using an MLR assay. Irradiated AlloHSC-iNKT cells and their derivatives were co-cultured with PBMC-NK cells, and live cell counts were used to quantify the extent of allorejection.

- **In Vivo Antitumor Efficacy**: NSG mice were preconditioned with irradiation and then inoculated with human tumor cells. AlloHSC-iNKT cells or PBMC-NK cells were administered intravenously, and tumor burden was monitored using bioluminescence imaging (BLI) and caliper measurements.

- **AllobCAR-iNKT Cell Efficacy**: NSG mice bearing MM-CD1d-FGFP tumors received AllobCAR-iNKT cells or conventional BCAR-T cells at different time points. Tumor load and survival were monitored over time using BLI, and the efficacy of the treatments was compared.

- **UbCAR-iNKT Cell Efficacy**: NSG mice with MM-CD1d-FGFP tumors received UbCAR-iNKT cells or conventional BCAR-T cells. Tumor burden and survival were assessed using BLI to evaluate the therapeutic potential of these cell therapies.

- **Ganciclovir (GCV) Killing Assay**: In vitro and in vivo GCV killing assays were performed to assess the sensitivity of AlloHSC-iNKT cells to GCV. Cells were cultured with varying concentrations of GCV, and live cell counts were used to determine the effect of GCV on cell viability.

- **Histological Analysis**: Tissues from experimental mice were collected, fixed, and sectioned for histological analysis. H&E staining was performed to evaluate tissue damage and infiltration by AlloHSC-iNKT cells. Images were captured and analyzed using specialized software.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Data from all experiments were analyzed using GraphPad Prism 6. Student’s t-tests, ANOVA with multiple comparisons tests, and log-rank tests were used to determine statistical significance. Results are presented as mean ± SEM, and p-values indicate the level of significance.
