- The ELIXYS synthesizer is a versatile new device capable of diverse syntheses for PET tracer production without hardware customization. It features minimal wetted fittings and tubing but uses motion to create dynamically reconfigurable fluid paths.
- To validate the system, three-reactor syntheses of d-[18F]FAC and l-[18F]FMAU were performed with yields and synthesis times comparable to other reports. Additional tracers were also successfully synthesized on this platform.
- The synthesizer supports preclinical PET tracer production. Products have been confirmed by HPLC and passed quality assurance tests for clinical use, with software updates underway for Good Manufacturing Practice compliance.
- In vivo imaging using d-[18F]FAC produced on ELIXYS showed comparable results to manually-produced tracer, validating the system's functionality. Organ uptake was normalized to remove injection biases.
- The disposable cassette approach allows easy switching between different tracers without hardware changes. This flexibility enables reaction development and routine tracer production for both preclinical and clinical applications.
