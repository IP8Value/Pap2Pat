- **Material Synthesis and Characterization**: The PNC electrode powder was synthesized using a modified glycine-citrate combustion method. X-ray diffraction (XRD), transmission electron microscopy (TEM), and X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS) were used to analyze the crystal structure, lattice parameters, and element valence states. Oxygen non-stoichiometry was measured by iodometric titration and thermogravimetric analysis.

- **Hydration Behavior**: The hydration behavior of PNC in humid air was studied using thermogravimetric analysis (TGA). Powder samples were heated to 950°C, then cooled to 500°C, and exposed to wet air. High-temperature X-ray diffraction (HT-XRD) examined phase structure changes from dry to wet conditions at 600°C.

- **Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy**: FTIR was used to investigate water and hydroxyl groups on PNC surfaces as a function of temperature. The sample was hydrated, cooled, and baseline measurements were collected under dry argon. In-situ measurements were taken every 50°C from 50 to 600°C in dry argon.

- **Temperature Programmed Desorption**: TPD was utilized to study the dehydration process in PNC. Powder samples were heated to 850°C with dry oxygen flowing through a quartz chamber, monitoring the release of water and hydroxyl groups as temperature increased.

- **Hydrogen Permeation Measurement**: Hydrogen permeation through dense PNC membranes was measured using a double-chamber reactor. The sample was sealed, and argon was used as sweep gas while a mixture of dry or wet 3%H2 was fed to the hydrogen-rich chamber. Gas chromatography monitored hydrogen concentration changes.

- **PCEC Fabrication and Electrochemical Measurements**: Half-cells with NiO-BCZYYb4411 electrodes were fabricated by tape casting and sintering. PNC electrode slurry was screen-printed onto electrolyte, forming full PCECs. Electrochemical performance was evaluated using an electrochemical workstation, including current-voltage characteristics, impedance spectra, and stability tests.

- **Faradaic Efficiency Measurement**: Faradaic efficiencies were calculated based on the ratio of experimental to theoretical hydrogen generation rates at fixed current densities. Gas chromatography monitored hydrogen concentration at open circuit and constant electrolysis conditions, considering exhaust gas volume/mass variations for accuracy.

- **Thermal Cycling and Post-Test Analysis**: Thermal cycling was conducted with controlled heating and cooling rates while recording current density at a fixed electrolysis voltage. Post-test samples were examined using scanning electron microscopy (SEM) and transmission electron microscopy (TEM) to observe layers and interfaces, ensuring structural integrity.

- **Computational Method**: DFT calculations used the Vienna ab initio Simulation Package (VASP) with PAW method and PBE-GGA functional. A 160-atom supercell was employed to minimize interactions between defects. Migration barriers for proton hopping were calculated using the CI-NEB technique, modeling non-charge-neutral defects.

- **Spin States of Co3+**: In PCO and PNC, Co3+ ions can have low-spin (S=0), intermediate-spin (S=1), or high-spin (S=2) states. At low temperatures, PCO is nonmagnetic with low-spin Co3+, but higher spin states may be activated at finite temperatures, though the precise temperature-dependent behavior remains unclear.
