- The study explores a self-assembling peptide hydrogel system for 3D MCF-7 cell culture. Hydrogels form at physiological pH by adjusting temperature or peptide concentration. Key rheological properties enable multiple pipetting deliveries and conversion to liquid form via dilution, facilitating safe and effective cell isolation through centrifugation.
- Unlike flat 2D cultures, cells in h9e hydrogel form tumor-like clusters. The system supports homogeneous encapsulation during gelation by adding peptide solution to MEM cell suspension. Hydrogels exhibit controlled formation time and strength based on temperature or concentration adjustments.
- Cell proliferation is slower in 3D compared to 2D systems, varying with different h9e peptide concentrations. Cells maintain high viability without significant apoptosis induction from the 3D culture environment itself, highlighting the system's biocompatibility and suitability for extended cell studies.
- Cisplatin diffusion through the hydrogel was tested using three methods: top surface, transwell, and pre-mixing. All methods effectively delivered cisplatin, causing dose-dependent decreases in cell viability. Pre-mixed method showed highest efficacy, demonstrating the hydrogel's permeability to drugs and nutrients for comprehensive drug testing.
- Confocal microscopy revealed distinct morphological differences between 2D and 3D cultures, with 3D cells forming rounded clusters. Cisplatin treatment induced nuclear fragmentation, cytoskeleton disruption, reduced Ki67 expression, and increased cleaved caspase-3, indicating effective apoptosis induction in the 3D hydrogel system.
- Western blot analysis confirmed that h9e hydrogel does not induce apoptosis in untreated cells, validating its suitability for long-term cell culture. Cisplatin treatment induced apoptotic markers, demonstrating the system's capability for downstream proteomic analysis and targeted drug response pathway studies.
- The h9e peptide hydrogel offers a promising 3D cell culture platform for drug testing and biomedical applications due to its controllable gelation, biocompatibility, and unique properties that enable safe and efficient cell isolation. This system supports detailed cellular and molecular analyses, enhancing the potential for advanced in vitro studies.
