**Key Points:**

- The study evaluated engineered IgG1 antibodies with silenced effector functions while maintaining WT-like biophysical properties. This is crucial for manufacturing processes, particularly in resisting acid-induced aggregation during viral inactivation.

- LALA-P329G and LALE-P329G combinations were found to effectively abolish effector function without compromising stability or biophysical developability. These mutations are prevalent in clinical use, highlighting their practical utility.

- In contrast, LALA-D265A, LALE-D265A, LALA-P331S, and LALE-P331S showed reduced CH2 Tm and increased acid-induced aggregation, indicating potential issues for manufacturing processes.

- The study also found that LALA-S267K and LALE-S267K performed well in abolishing effector function while maintaining stability. These combinations could be viable alternatives to the more commonly used P329G mutations.

- Overall, the findings provide valuable insights into the design of therapeutic antibodies with silenced effector functions, emphasizing the importance of balancing functional silencing with biophysical properties for optimal manufacturing and clinical use.
