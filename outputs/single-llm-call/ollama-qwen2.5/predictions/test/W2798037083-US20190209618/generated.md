- The study utilized 3,238 bp of the 5′-flanking sequence upstream of the human MSI2 translation start site, which was PCR amplified and cloned into pGL3-basic for luciferase reporter assays. Serial truncations were generated to identify regulatory regions within the promoter.

- Transcription factors (TFs) binding to the minimal 385 bp MSI2 promoter were predicted using MatInspector and selected based on expression in primitive HSCs from public databases. Mutagenesis of predicted TF binding sites was performed to screen for functional regulators, with mutations introduced by rolling circle site-directed mutagenesis.

- Promoter clones were transiently co-transfected into K562 cells along with a Renilla luciferase control plasmid. Luciferase activity was measured 24-36 hours post-transfection and normalized to Renilla luciferase to account for transfection efficiency, allowing quantification of promoter activity.

- Total protein extraction from K562 cells involved lysis buffer supplemented with protease inhibitors. Protein concentration was determined by Bradford assay, and samples were resolved on Bis-Tris PAGE before being transferred to PVDF membranes. Western blots were performed using antibodies against MSI2 and β-actin, followed by secondary antibody detection and imaging.

- RNA isolation from K562 cells used Trizol-LS reagent, and cDNA synthesis was followed by qPCR with specific primers for PLAG1, β-ACTIN, MSI2, and CYP1B1. PLAG1 isoforms were detected using IDT PrimeTime Mini qPCR assays. Transcript levels were quantified using the 2−ΔΔCt method.

- Open reading frame cDNAs for PLAG1 and USF2 were obtained from Mammalian Gene Collections and subcloned into MSCV-PGK/GFP vectors for overexpression studies. 3xFLAG-tagged versions of PLAG1-B and PLAG1-S isoforms were generated and cloned into the same vector, allowing for expression and detection in K562 cells.

- For co-overexpression, 3xFLAG-PLAG1-100 and USF2 were inserted into a bidirectional pSMALB vector, ensuring BFP positivity marked cells overexpressing both transgenes. shRNAs targeting PLAG1 and USF2 were designed and cloned into lentiviral vectors for knockdown experiments in K562 and primary Lin- CB cells.

- Human primitive hematopoietic cells were isolated from patient samples with informed consent and approval from the McMaster University ethics board. Flow cytometry was used to identify and analyze cell populations, supporting functional studies of MSI2 regulation by PLAG1 and USF2.
