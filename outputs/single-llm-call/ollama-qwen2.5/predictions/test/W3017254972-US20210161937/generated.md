### Here’s a concise summary of each section:

**16S rRNA Gene Sequencing:**
DNA was extracted from samples using the DNeasy PowerSoil Kit (QIAGEN). The V4 region of the 16S rRNA gene was amplified and sequenced on an Illumina MiSeq platform. Sequences were processed using QIIME2, including quality filtering, denoising, and chimera removal. Taxonomic classification was performed using the SILVA database.

**Circadian Locomotor Activity:**
Post-eclosion, flies were reared in a humidified, temperature-controlled incubator with a 12-h light-dark (LD) cycle at 25°C. Seven-day-old adults were anesthetized under light CO2 and placed individually into Drosophila Activity Monitor (DAM) tubes containing standard food. Flies were monitored using a DAM2 system for 3 days in a 12-h LD cycle, followed by 6 days of constant darkness (DD). ClockLab software was used to determine rhythmicity and period.

**Phospho-Histone H3 Immunostaining:**
Flies were anesthetized on ice, and intestines were dissected in cold PBS. Samples were fixed in PBS + 0.1% Triton X-100 containing 4% paraformaldehyde for 30 minutes at room temperature, rinsed three times in PBS + 0.1% Triton X-100, and blocked with 5% BSA in PBS + 0.1% Triton X-100 for 1 hour. Primary antibody (anti-phospho-histone H3) was added at 1:250 and incubated overnight at 4°C. After washing, secondary antibody (anti-rabbit AlexaFluor-488) and Hoechst stain were added at 1:250 and 1.5 μg/mL, respectively, and incubated overnight at 4°C.

**Blue Native PAGE and In-Gel Activity Assay:**
Mitochondria were purified from ten thoraces of 7–10-day-old male flies. BN-PAGE was performed using NativePAGE gels (Life Technologies). Mitochondria were suspended in NativePAGE sample buffer with 1% digitonin, protease inhibitors, and incubated on ice for 20 minutes. After centrifugation at 20,000 × g for 30 minutes, the supernatant was loaded onto 3–12% precast Bis–Tris NativePAGE gels. Electrophoresis was performed using NativePAGE Running buffer. Protein complexes were visualized by staining with Novex Colloidal Blue and Complex I activity was detected by incubating gels in NADH, nitrotetrazolium blue chloride, and Tris-HCl overnight at room temperature.

**TMRM/MitoSOX Intestinal Staining:**
Flies were anesthetized on ice, and intestines were dissected in cold Schneider’s Medium. For TMRM staining, intestines were incubated in 75 nM TMRM for 10 minutes at room temperature, rinsed three times with 20 nM TMRM, and mounted in Schneider’s Medium containing 1.5 μg/mL Hoechst stain. For MitoSOX staining, intestines were incubated in 50 μM MitoSOX Red for 5 minutes, washed three times with Schneider’s Medium, and mounted similarly. Samples were imaged on a Zeiss LSM800 confocal microscope within 20 minutes.

**Statistical Analysis:**
Prism7 (GraphPad) was used for statistical analysis. Significance is expressed as p values (ns p > 0.05, *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, ***p < 0.001). For two-group comparisons, an unpaired, two-tailed t-test was used if data met parametric criteria; otherwise, the Mann–Whitney U-test was applied. For more than two groups, ANOVA with Bonferroni or Tukey’s post-hoc test was performed for parametric data, and Kruskal–Wallis with Dunn’s post-hoc test for nonparametric data. Survival curves were compared using the Log-rank (Mantel–Cox) test.
