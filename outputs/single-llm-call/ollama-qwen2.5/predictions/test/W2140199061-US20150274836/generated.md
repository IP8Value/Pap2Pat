- The study developed a new engineered antibody domain called SD1 using phage display technology. This single-domain antibody recognizes an epitope at the C-terminus of mesothelin, a region not previously targeted by other mesothelin antibodies.

- SD1-hFc exhibited strong complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC) and antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) against mesothelin-expressing cancer cells in vitro. This suggests CDC likely contributes to its antitumor activity.

- In vivo, SD1-hFc caused significant growth inhibition of human tumor xenografts in nude mice as a single agent. Both CDC and ADCC may contribute to the observed antitumor effects.

- Compared to MORAb-009, the only mesothelin antibody currently in clinical trials, SD1 showed stronger activity in preclinical studies. Direct comparisons between the two antibodies are needed moving forward.

- The human VH domain of SD1 was fused to CH2 and CH3 regions of human IgG1 to produce a dimeric IgG-like molecule for improved stability and functionality. Recombinant immunotoxins based on SD1 also showed antiproliferative activity against mesothelin-positive tumor cells.
