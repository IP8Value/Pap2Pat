### Summary of Key Points

- **Cell Lines and Culture**: Human PCa cell lines C4-2B and PC-3 were cultured in RPMI medium with supplements. These cells were used for migration assays within 2 months after thawing.
  
- **Conditioned Media Preparation**: SAT and BM-Ads were collected from patients undergoing hip replacement surgery. Conditioned media (SAT-CM and BM-Ad-CM) were prepared by incubating the tissues overnight in DMEM 1% BSA.

- **Boyden Chamber Migration Assays**: Cell migration assays were conducted using diluted SAT-CM or BM-Ad-CM. Cells were pre-incubated with blocking monoclonal antibodies or pharmacological inhibitors to evaluate chemokine receptor involvement.

- **Tissue Microarrays (TMAs)**: Two TMAs were used to assess CCR3 expression in visceral, lymph node, and bone metastases from PCa patients. Staining was scored by two pathologists blinded to clinical data.

- **In Silico Meta-Analysis**: The Oncomine database was utilized to perform an in silico meta-analysis comparing gene expression between primary sites and bone metastases of PCa. Six datasets were analyzed with specific filters and thresholds.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Statistical analyses were conducted using STATA 12 and Prism v.7 software. Two-group comparisons used Mann-Whitney’s test or Student’s t-test, while multiple comparisons used ANOVA with appropriate post hoc tests. TMA analysis compared CCR3 expression distributions using Chi2 distribution comparison.
