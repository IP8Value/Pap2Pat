### Mammalian Cell Sorting
Mammalian cell sorting for the study was conducted at the Analytical Cytometry Core at City of Hope in Duarte, California. This facility provided essential support and expertise to ensure high-quality cell sorting, which is crucial for isolating specific cell populations expressing gas vesicles (GVs) or other fluorescent markers.

### Confocal Microscopy
Confocal microscopy was performed in the Beckman Institute Biological Imaging Center. This advanced imaging technique allowed for detailed visualization of tumor sections, enabling researchers to assess the expression and localization of gas vesicle proteins and fluorescent markers within the tumor microenvironment.
