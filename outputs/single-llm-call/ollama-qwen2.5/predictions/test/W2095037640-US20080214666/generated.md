- **RNA Interference by Feeding**: RNAi was induced using *E. coli* HT115 transformed with dsRNA-producing constructs or empty pPD129.36 vector (controls). Synchronized adult worms were placed on these plates, and their progeny were scored for phenotypes. This method allowed for the systematic knockdown of gene expression to study functional consequences.

- **GC Analysis**: Well-fed worm populations were washed, rinsed, and frozen. Fatty acid methyl esters were extracted and analyzed using GC on an HP6890N equipped with a DB-23 column. Each experiment was repeated at least five times, and average values with standard deviations were calculated for each compound.

- **Staging Worms to Test FA Composition**: Eggs from gravid adults were incubated overnight in M9 buffer. L1 larvae were collected and plated on agar plates. Subsequent stages (L2, L3, L4, young adults, and adults) were collected separately for GC analysis. Starved populations were also included to monitor the effect of starvation.

- **Phenotype Rescue Using FA Supplements**: FA supplements (4 mM in 1% NP40) were added to elo-5(RNAi) plates. Synchronized young adults were plated, and their progeny was scored for rescue from L1 arrest. Each experiment was replicated at least 30 times. let-418(RNAi) animals served as a control to ensure that supplements did not affect RNA interference.

- **Designing of GFP Reporter Constructs**: Genomic fragments were PCR amplified and cloned into GFP fusion vectors to create reporter constructs for elo-5, elo-6, acs-1, and pnk-1. These constructs were injected into *C. elegans* at 10–50 ng/μl to form extrachromosomal arrays. The acs-1 array was integrated into the genome.

- **Staining Chemosensory Ciliated Neurons with DiI**: Worms were soaked in a 5-μg/ml solution of DiI in M9 buffer for 1 hour, rinsed three times, and visualized using fluorescence microscopy with a Texas Red filter. This method allowed for the visualization of chemosensory ciliated neurons.

- **Correlation Analysis**: FA quantities from GC were expressed as percentages of the total. t-tests (two-tailed distribution) and correlation analysis were performed using Microsoft Excel to determine statistical significance and relationships between different FAs.

- **Visualization and Scoring of GFP Expression in Promoter::GFP Lines**: Synchronized adults were placed on control and RNAi plates. Worms from the next generation were picked, mounted on a slide, and imaged with fixed settings and exposure to compare GFP expression levels.

- **Microarray Analysis**: Young adult N2 Bristol worms were placed on control and RNAi feeding plates. RNA was extracted, and microarray analysis was performed as detailed in Protocol S1. The data were analyzed to identify differentially expressed genes in response to RNAi. Expression data are presented in Dataset S1.
