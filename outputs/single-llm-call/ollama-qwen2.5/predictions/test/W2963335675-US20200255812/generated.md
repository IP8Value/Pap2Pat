- **ProRmL Purification and Analysis**: V.R. purified the full-length Rhizomucor miehei prolipase (ProRmL) and mature RmL, contributing to the structure analysis and co-writing the paper. The purification process involved multiple chromatographic steps, followed by mass spectrometry (MS) analysis to confirm the integrity of the protein. The MS data were crucial for validating the presence of the propeptide in the full-length enzyme.
- **Variant Generation and Cloning**: R.S. and S.D. generated the ProRmL deletion mutant (ProRmL-del), cloned it, expressed it in a suitable host, and purified it. They also performed detailed characterization of the variant, including activity assays and structural analysis. Their work was essential for understanding the role of the propeptide in enzyme function.
- **Mass Spectrometry Analysis**: C.I.J. planned and analyzed the MS data, which provided insights into the post-translational modifications and protein interactions. The MS analysis confirmed the presence of the propeptide in the full-length enzyme and helped identify specific residues involved in propeptide-mature protein interactions. This information was crucial for structural studies.
- **Peptide Binding Experiments**: V.K.B. performed fluorescence polarization (FP) experiments to measure the binding affinity of a fluorescently labeled propeptide to mature RmL. The results showed that the propeptide binds with high affinity, providing evidence for its role in regulating enzyme activity. These experiments were essential for understanding the mechanism of propeptide-mature protein interactions.
- **In Vitro Lipase Inhibition**: L.B. supervised the research at Novozymes and initiated, planned, and analyzed the in vitro lipase inhibition assays. The assays demonstrated that the propeptide can inhibit the activity of mature RmL, suggesting a regulatory role for the propeptide. These findings were crucial for understanding the functional significance of the propeptide.
- **Propeptide Purification**: B.A. planned and analyzed the purification of the propeptide alone, which was essential for characterizing its properties independently of the mature enzyme. The purified propeptide was used in binding assays and structural studies to understand its interactions with the mature enzyme. This work provided a comprehensive view of the propeptide's role.
- **Variant Generation Supervision**: A.S. planned, designed, and supervised the generation of ProRmL variants at Novozymes. The variants were created to explore the functional and structural implications of specific mutations in the propeptide. A.S.'s oversight ensured that the variants were correctly generated and characterized, contributing significantly to the project.

K.S.W.'s expertise was crucial for the success of these studies.
