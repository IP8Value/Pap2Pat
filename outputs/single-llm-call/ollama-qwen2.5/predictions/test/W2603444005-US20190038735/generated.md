- The study aimed to evaluate the efficacy of PP7-AIP1S VLPs as a vaccine against S. aureus skin infections in mice. BALB/cJ mice were immunized with PP7-AIP1S or control PP7 particles, and their immune responses and protection against infection were assessed.
- Mice received two intramuscular injections of 10 µg of PP7-AIP1S or PP7 VLPs four weeks apart. Serum antibodies specific to AIP1S were measured by ELISA at two, four, and eight weeks post-vaccination. Depleted serum was used to reduce non-specific binding.
- For the infection challenge, depilated mice were subcutaneously injected with 4 × 10^7 CFU of S. aureus strain LAC six days after immunization. Abscess size, dermonecrosis area, and bacterial burden were monitored daily and measured post-sacrifice.
- Cytokine levels in abscess tissue homogenates were analyzed using a BioPlex 200 system with a custom mouse multiplex assay to assess the inflammatory response. RNA was isolated from infected skin samples for qPCR analysis of S. aureus gene expression, focusing on RNAIII and 16S rRNA.
- Western blotting was performed to quantify Hla protein levels in abscess homogenates. Data were analyzed using GraphPad Prism, with statistical significance determined by one-way ANOVA or Kruskal-Wallis tests followed by post-hoc multiple comparison analyses (p < 0.05).
