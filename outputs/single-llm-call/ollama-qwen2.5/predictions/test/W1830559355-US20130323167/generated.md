- **Introduction**: This study evaluates the impact of tumor necrosis factor-alpha (TNF-α) on enhancing the delivery and therapeutic efficacy of liposomal formulations in cancer treatment. TNF-α is known for its ability to modulate tumor vasculature, potentially improving drug penetration and distribution.

- **Materials and Methods - Antibody Radioiodination**: Purified antibodies were labeled with 125I or 131I using an iodogen-coated vial method. The reaction was incubated at room temperature for 10 minutes, followed by purification on a Sephadex G-25 column to remove unincorporated radioiodine. Radiochemical purity was confirmed by thin-layer chromatography.

- **Liposome Preparation**: Liposomes were prepared using a mixture of HEPC:Chol:DSPE-PEG2000 at a 50:45:5 molar ratio, solubilized in chloroform, and dried under vacuum. The film was hydrated with arginine solution and sonicated to form large multilamellar vesicles (MLVs), which were then extruded to create single unilamellar vesicles (SUVs) of approximately 100 nm.

- **Bolton-Hunter Reagent Radioiodination**: Bolton-Hunter reagent was labeled with sodium 125I or 131I using the chloramine-T method. The reaction mixture was incubated for 15 seconds, and radiolabeled BH was extracted with toluene. The organic phase was evaporated under a nitrogen stream before being added to liposome suspensions.

- **Encapsulation of Iodinated Reagents**: For chemical entrapment, arginine-containing liposomes were incubated with 125I-BH for 30 minutes at 37°C. Labeling efficiency was determined by counting the liposome suspension before and after chromatography on a PD-10 column, with typical radiochemical yields of 50% to 70%.

- **Biodistribution Assay**: CT26-bearing BALB/c mice were injected with 125I-liposomes or 125I-IgG1 via the tail vein. Organs and blood were collected at 48 hours post-injection, weighed, and their radioactivity measured using a gamma counter. Percent injected dose per gram of tissue was calculated, correcting for decay.

- **SPECT-CT Imaging**: Mice bearing subcutaneous CT26 or orthotopic GL261 brain tumors were imaged after intravenous injection of 125I-IgG1 or 125I-SSLs. SPECT and CT data were acquired, reconstructed, and coregistered using specialized software for visualization.

- **Statistical Analysis**: The statistical significance of survival differences between experimental groups was determined using the Long-rank test. This analysis helps in understanding the therapeutic impact of TNF-α on tumor treatment outcomes.
