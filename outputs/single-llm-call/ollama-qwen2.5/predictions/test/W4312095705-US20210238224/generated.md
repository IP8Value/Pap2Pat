- **Introduction**: Renal fibrosis is a common outcome of chronic kidney diseases and involves excessive accumulation of extracellular matrix proteins. This study explores the therapeutic potential of PROTAC (Proteolysis Targeting Chimera) technology in combating renal interstitial fibrosis, focusing on the degradation of specific target proteins like Smad3 and HIF-2α.

- **Mechanism of Action**: PROTACs are designed to induce the ubiquitination and subsequent proteasomal degradation of target proteins. By selectively degrading Smad3 and HIF-2α, PROTACs aim to inhibit fibrotic pathways and reduce collagen deposition in renal tissues.

- **In Vitro Experiments**: NRK-49F and HK2 cell lines were used to evaluate the efficacy of different PROTAC formulations. Cells were treated with various concentrations of PROTACs to assess target protein degradation, cellular viability, and fibrotic marker expression using Western blotting and qPCR.

- **In Vivo Toxicity Evaluation**: ICR mice were administered different doses of PROTAC via subcutaneous injection to evaluate acute and chronic toxicity. Blood samples were collected at multiple time points for pharmacokinetic analysis, and tissue concentrations were measured to ensure safety and optimal dosing.

- **Animal Models**: Two primary models were used: unilateral ureteral obstruction (UUO) and 5/6 nephrectomy (5/6Nx). UUO was induced in C57BL/6J mice to simulate acute renal fibrosis, while 5/6Nx was performed to model chronic renal failure. PROTAC treatment was administered to evaluate its efficacy in reducing fibrotic markers and improving renal function.

- **Histological Analysis**: Renal tissues were analyzed using Masson's trichrome staining to quantify collagen deposition. Immunohistochemistry was performed to assess the presence of macrophages (F4/80) and peritubular capillaries (CD34). These analyses provided insights into the extent of fibrosis and vascular changes.

- **Biochemical and Hematological Assessments**: Serum levels of VEGF, EPO, ferritin, hepcidin, iron, and cystatin C were measured using ELISA kits. Hematological parameters were evaluated using automated analyzers to assess the impact of PROTAC treatment on systemic biomarkers.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Data were analyzed using GraphPad Prism 8.0. Student's t-test was used for comparisons between two groups, while one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post-test was applied for multiple group comparisons. P-values < 0.05 were considered statistically significant.

- **Conclusion**: The study demonstrates that PROTACs effectively degrade Smad3 and HIF-2α in vitro and in vivo, leading to reduced renal fibrosis and improved kidney function. These findings highlight the therapeutic potential of PROTAC technology in treating chronic kidney diseases characterized by fibrotic progression.
