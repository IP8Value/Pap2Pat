- **Human iPSC and Their Culture**: Human iPSC lines were maintained on Laminin 511-E8 fragment-coated dishes in StemFit AK02N medium. Differentiation into hepatocytes involved a series of media changes, including activin A and Wnt-3a for definitive endoderm, followed by specific cocktails for hepatic endoderm and mature hepatocytes. Ethical approval was obtained from Yokohama City University and the University of Tokyo.

- **Detection of Residual Undifferentiated iPSC**: To detect residual undifferentiated cells in differentiated cultures, cells were seeded on lamin511-E8-coated dishes with Rock inhibitor Y-27632. After seven days, cells were immunostained for SOX2 and TRA-1-60, and colonies were counted manually.

- **Immunostaining**: Cells were fixed with 4% paraformaldehyde, permeabilized with Triton X-100, and blocked with FBS. Primary antibodies against SOX2 and TRA-1-60 were applied, followed by secondary antibody staining. Fluorescence microscopy was used to visualize the stained cells.

- **Microscopy and Colony Counting**: Whole-well images after immunostaining were captured using a fluorescence microscope (BZ-X710, KEYENCE). Colonies of SOX2-positive cells were manually counted and reported as numbers per square cm.

- **Undifferentiated iPSC Spike-In**: Undifferentiated iPSCs were spiked into hepatic endoderm cells at various proportions ranging from 0.0025% to 5%. This method was used to evaluate the sensitivity of detection methods for residual undifferentiated cells.

- **Human iPSC-EC, STM, and NSC Differentiation**: Endothelial cells (EC) and mesenchymal cells (STM) were derived from iPSCs using previously described protocols. Neural stem cells (NSC) were also differentiated and characterized by qPCR and flow cytometry for specific markers.

- **Human iPSC Trilineage Differentiation**: Three germ layer derivatives were generated using the STEMdiff Trilineage Differentiation Kit. Gene expression was analyzed by qPCR, and immunostaining confirmed the presence of specific markers for each lineage.

- **Gene Expression Analysis**: Published microarray data and single-cell RNA sequencing data were reanalyzed to study gene expression during mouse hepatocyte development. qRT-PCR analyses were performed using an RNeasy Mini Kit and Universal ProbeLibrary probes.

- **Droplet Digital PCR (ddPCR)**: cDNA was prepared from iPSCs, and ddPCR reactions were set up with specific primers and UPL probes. Thermal cycling conditions were optimized, and the number of gene copies per cell was calculated based on RNA concentration.

- **Statistics**: Data are presented as means ± SD. Statistical significance was assessed using Student’s t-test for gene expression differences and Mann–Whitney U test for albumin production. P values <0.05 were considered significant.
