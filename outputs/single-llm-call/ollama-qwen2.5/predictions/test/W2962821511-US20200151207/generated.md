- RNN-RSM is a neural temporal topic model that captures temporal relationships in historical documents through time-feedback connections via deterministic RNN within the generative and neural network framework. It models complex conditional probability distributions and heteroscedasticity in document collections over time, outperforming DTM in various aspects.
- The architecture of RNN-RSM includes two hidden layers: stochastic binary h to capture topical information and deterministic u to convey temporal information via BPTT. This allows for the dynamics of topic correlations, enabling new topics and words to appear or disappear over time.
- Experiments show that RNN-RSM demonstrates better generalization in perplexity and timestamp prediction, higher coherence scores indicating meaningful topics, and superior performance in capturing topic evolution through popularity and drift metrics compared to DTM.
- To illustrate the model's capabilities, SPAN was introduced as a metric for keyword trends. RNN-RSM captures longer SPANs for popular keywords, indicating its ability to detect trending words over time. This is particularly evident in recent topics like Word Embedding and Neural Language that emerged locally in NLP research.
- Future work will focus on investigating dynamics in variable numbers of topics over time, examining the impact of skewed paper distributions across years, and exploring applications in learning dynamic word embeddings to capture language evolution instead of document topics.
