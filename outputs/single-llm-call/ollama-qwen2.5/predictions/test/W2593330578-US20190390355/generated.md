- The study demonstrated that boron impregnation significantly enhances anode protection against oxidation. A 15-minute impregnation in a warm 2% boron solution provided substantial surface protection for small samples, reducing the oxidation rate from 4%/h to 0.2%/h.
- CT scan results revealed that while the inside of impregnated samples reacted with air at a slower rate, their surfaces maintained their initial shape. This indicates effective protection and reduced dust generation compared to unprotected samples.
- Despite the promising laboratory results, implementing this technique on an industrial scale poses challenges. Full impregnation of large anode blocks within a reasonable time is difficult, requiring further research to develop practical methods for in-depth impregnation.
