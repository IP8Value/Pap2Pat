- The study investigates how LTX affects tumor growth in colorectal cancer models, focusing on metabolic changes and gene expression patterns. It uses a combination of RNA-Seq, ChIP-Seq, metabolomics, and proteomics to analyze the impact of LTX treatment.
- Xenograft studies were conducted using athymic mice implanted with HT55 or HCT116 cells. Tumor sizes were measured every other day, and the animals were treated with LTX (25 mg/kg) for 7 days when tumors reached 100 mm³.
- Statistical analyses, including Student’s t-tests and Kaplan-Meier plots, were used to evaluate the significance of the results. The study was approved by the IACUC at Northwestern University, ensuring compliance with ethical guidelines.
- Next-generation sequencing data from RNA-Seq and ChIP-Seq experiments have been deposited in the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) under accession number GSE200120. All other data are available within the main text or supplementary materials.
