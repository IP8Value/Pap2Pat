### Summary of Key Points:

- **DARPin Efficacy in Vascular Leakage Model**: Intravitreal administration of DARPin #4 effectively inhibited vascular leakage induced by VEGF-A in rabbits. The treatment showed no adverse effects on ocular clarity or animal behavior, and body weight remained stable throughout the study.

- **Topical Application for Corneal Suture**: Topical application of DARPin in PBS significantly reduced corneal neovascularization in New Zealand white rabbits compared to PBS alone. The treatment was well-tolerated, with no significant changes in Draize scores or body weight.

- **Rat Choroidal Neovascularization Model**: Topical administration of DARPin #6 inhibited choroidal neovascularization (CNV) in Brown Norway rats as effectively as a single intravitreal injection of Triamcinolon. Ocular observations and angiography confirmed the efficacy, with no adverse effects noted.

- **Intravitreal Penetration Study**: Alexa-555 labeled DARPins demonstrated effective penetration into the vitreous cavity after intravitreal injection in mice. The labeled proteins were detectable at various time points, indicating good ocular distribution and stability.

- **Receptor Phosphorylation Assay**: DARPin #4 effectively inhibited VEGF-A-induced phosphorylation of VEGF-R2 in HUE cells, confirming its mechanism of action. This assay provided biochemical evidence supporting the therapeutic potential of DARPins in inhibiting VEGF signaling pathways.

- **Spheroid Sprouting Assay**: In a 3D spheroid sprouting assay, DARPin #4 significantly inhibited VEGF-induced endothelial cell sprouting compared to controls. This in vitro model demonstrated the ability of DARPins to block angiogenic processes driven by VEGF-A.

- **Supplementary Material**: Additional data and figures are provided as electronic supplementary material, including detailed methods, labeling efficiency of Alexa-555 labeled DARPins, and time course analysis of intravitreal penetration. This material supports the main findings and provides further validation of the experimental results.
