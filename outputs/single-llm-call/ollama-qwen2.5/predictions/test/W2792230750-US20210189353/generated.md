- The study aimed to enhance Amadoriase I thermal stability through disulfide bond engineering. Molecular dynamics simulations identified SS03 (A64C/Y295C) and SS17 (Q8C/V272C) mutants for experimental validation. Both mutants showed improved thermostability, with SS03 demonstrating the highest T50 value of 75°C compared to WT's 55°C.
- Mutant enzyme activity was assessed using a continuous assay detecting glucosone formation from fructosyl-lysine at 322 nm. All mutants retained similar specific activities to the wild-type enzyme, indicating that the introduced disulfide bonds did not significantly impact catalytic efficiency.
- Steady-state kinetics measurements for WT and SS-mutants were performed over a range of fructosyl-lysine concentrations (0.05–2 mM). Kinetic parameters (Km, Vmax) were determined using non-linear least-squares fitting to the Michaelis-Menten equation, revealing no significant differences between mutants and WT.
- Thermal stability tests involved incubating enzymes at temperatures from 25°C to 100°C for 10 minutes. Residual activity was measured post-incubation. T50 values, representing the temperature at which half-maximal activity is retained, were calculated using a Boltzmann sigmoidal fit.
- Optimal pH conditions for WT and SS-mutants were determined by measuring enzyme activity across a pH range from 6.0 to 11.0 using various buffers. All mutants showed similar pH optima as the wild-type, with optimal activity observed around pH 9.0.
- Crystals of SS03 and SS17 mutants were obtained using vapor diffusion methods and diffracted to resolutions of 2.19 Å and 2.85 Å, respectively. Structures were solved by molecular replacement and refined to R/Rfree values of 13.92/18.07% (SS03) and 17.18/24.40% (SS17), confirming the presence of designed disulfide bonds.
- The crystal structures of SS03 and SS17 were deposited in the RCSB PDB with accession codes 5OC3 and 5OC2, respectively. These structures provide insights into the structural basis for enhanced thermal stability and validate the computational predictions made during mutant design.
- Supplementary material includes additional data on enzyme activity, kinetic parameters, thermal stability, and crystallographic refinement statistics, supporting the main findings of improved thermostability without compromising catalytic efficiency or pH optima in the engineered Amadoriase I mutants.
