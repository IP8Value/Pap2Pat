### Lyophilization Process
For lyophilization, samples were diluted 1:20 in PBS containing 5% trehalose to achieve a final concentration higher than 5 x 10^10 particles/mL. The vials were cooled to -45°C for 2 hours before the chamber pressure was reduced to 0.1 mbar. Sublimation and drying steps were carefully controlled to ensure sample integrity.

### rEV PEGylation
rEV (2 x 10^10) were incubated with 0.016 mg/mL DMPE-PEG 5k at 40°C for 2 hours with gentle mixing. This concentration was optimized using a DMPE-PEG-biotin construct and streptavidin-coated magnetic beads, ensuring efficient PEGylation as confirmed by Western blot analysis targeting EGFP.

### Lipid Analysis
Lipids were extracted from rEV and mock EV samples (5 x 10^10) using a modified Bligh-Dyer protocol. Phospholipids were analyzed by ESI-MS/MS on a 4000 QTRAP mass spectrometer, with collision energies optimized for different lipid species. Total cholesterol concentrations were measured using the Amplex Red Cholesterol Assay Kit.

### RT-qPCR Gene Expression Analysis
Total RNA was isolated from vesicles using the miRNeasy Micro kit and reverse transcribed into cDNA. EGFP mRNA expression in rEV was analyzed via RT-qPCR using SYBR Green I Master mix on an LC480 system. Two primer pairs were used to ensure accurate quantification of EGFP transcripts.

### Immune Precipitation
MagnaBind goat anti-mouse IgG or anti-rabbit IgG magnetic beads were incubated with specific antibodies (anti-CD81, anti-CD63, anti-PEG) for 2 hours at 4°C. Beads were then washed and incubated with rEV or rEV-PEG samples. After washing, the supernatant was pooled and pelleted, and pellets were lysed for further analysis.

### LC-MS/MS Data Analysis
LC-MS/MS data were analyzed using MaxQuant (v1.5.4.1) and Andromeda search engine. Spectra were searched against the human Swiss-Prot database with specified modifications and cleavage rules. Proteins were quantified by MaxLFQ, and further analysis was performed in Perseus (v1.5.4.1). Results are presented as log2 fold changes and statistical significance.

### Immunoassays
Immunoassays were conducted using specific antibodies to detect protein markers on rEV and mock EV samples. Western blotting was used to confirm the presence of EGFP in rEV, while ELISA was employed for quantifying specific proteins. These assays provided crucial data on the composition and functionality of the engineered vesicles.

### Flow Cytometry
Flow cytometry was used to analyze the surface markers and overall characteristics of rEV and mock EV samples. Cells or vesicles were stained with fluorescently labeled antibodies, and data were acquired using a flow cytometer.
