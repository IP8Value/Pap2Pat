- **TRPC6 and APP Interaction**: TRPC6 specifically interacts with amyloid precursor protein (APP) to inhibit its cleavage by γ-secretase, reducing Aβ production. This interaction was identified through co-immunoprecipitation and confocal microscopy studies, demonstrating that TRPC6 forms a complex with APP in both cellular and mouse brain samples.

- **Mechanism of Inhibition**: The mechanism by which TRPC6 inhibits γ-secretase cleavage involves the transmembrane domain 2 (TM2) of TRPC6. Mutations in this domain abolish the interaction between TRPC6 and APP, leading to increased Aβ production, suggesting that TM2 is crucial for the inhibitory effect.

- **Fractionation Analysis**: Fractionation analysis revealed that TRPC6 co-localizes with APP in specific membrane fractions, including early endosomes and the Golgi apparatus. This co-localization supports the idea that TRPC6 interacts with APP at these sites to modulate its processing by γ-secretase.

- **Behavioral Experiments**: Behavioral experiments using APP/PS1 mice injected with TAT-TM2 peptides showed a significant reduction in Aβ levels compared to control mice. These findings suggest that the TM2 domain of TRPC6 can be targeted therapeutically to reduce Aβ production and potentially alleviate Alzheimer's disease symptoms.

- **Statistical Analysis**: All data were analyzed using two-tailed Student's t-test for comparisons between two groups and one-way ANOVA with Newman–Keuls post hoc test for multiple groups. The sample sizes were chosen based on previous literature, ensuring statistical power and reproducibility of the results. Data are presented as mean ± s.e.m.

- **Plasmid Construction**: Plasmids encoding various fragments of mouse TRPC6 fused with HA tags were constructed to study specific domains involved in APP interaction. These constructs were used in co-expression experiments to identify the TM2 domain as critical for the interaction and functional effects on Aβ production.

- **Peptide Injection**: TAT fusion peptides, including TAT-TM2, were synthesized and injected into male littermate APP/PS1 mice. The peptides were effective in reducing Aβ levels, demonstrating the potential of targeting TRPC6's TM2 domain for therapeutic intervention in Alzheimer's disease.

- **FAD-Linked Variants**: FAD-linked variants of APP (I716F and V717F) were mutated and cloned into expression vectors. These variants were used to study the impact of specific mutations on Aβ production, providing insights into how TRPC6 interacts with different forms of APP.

- **Additional Information**: The study provides a comprehensive understanding of the role of TRPC6 in regulating Aβ production by interacting with APP. This interaction could be a novel target for developing therapeutic strategies to combat Alzheimer's disease, emphasizing the importance of further research in this area.
