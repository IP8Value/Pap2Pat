- The study investigated the impact of doping SBA-15 with varying amounts of zirconia (5, 10, and 15 wt%) on the performance of Pd-Pt catalysts for reducing methane slip from lean burn natural gas engines. XRD analysis confirmed the presence of individual PdO and Pt particles without alloy formation.
- Among the catalysts tested, 2Pd/4Pt/15Zr/SBA-15 exhibited the best activity, stability, and sulfur resistance with a T50 of 481°C after 40 hours of aging. This superior performance was attributed to higher reducible Pt species content and enhanced sulfur trapping capacity.
- The catalyst synthesis involved sequential impregnation of Pd and Pt precursors onto zirconia-doped SBA-15, followed by drying and calcination steps. Characterization techniques included XRD, N2 adsorption, SEM-EDX, FAA, XPS, and H2-TPR to determine composition, textural properties, and reducibility.
- Catalyst performance was evaluated using a fixed-bed flow reactor system simulating lean burn NG engine exhaust conditions. Fresh and aged catalysts were tested with temperature gradient runs (light-off) from 75°C to 600°C at 3°C/min, demonstrating the effectiveness of 2Pd/4Pt/15Zr/SBA-15 in methane reduction.
- The results indicate that 2Pd/4Pt/15Zr/SBA-15 is a promising candidate for practical application in lean burn natural gas engines due to its enhanced activity, stability, and sulfur resistance. This catalyst could significantly reduce methane slip, contributing to improved environmental performance and efficiency of NG engines.
