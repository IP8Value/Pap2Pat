- **Introduction to AR-12 and Analogues**: AR-12 (OSU-03012) is a promising small molecule that targets the phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase/Akt/mammalian target of rapamycin signaling pathway, showing antitumor activity. Its analogues have been developed to enhance efficacy and reduce toxicity. This study evaluates AR-12 and its analogues in various cellular models.

- **Cell Viability Assay**: Cell viability was assessed using the TACS® XTT cell proliferation assay. Cells were treated with varying concentrations of AR-12 and its analogues for 72 hours, and absorbance was measured at 490 nm. Data were expressed as a percentage of viability compared to DMSO-treated controls.

- **Real-Time Quaking Induced Conversion (RT-QuIC) Assay**: The RT-QuIC assay was used to detect prion seeding activity. Recombinant mouse PrP was prepared and purified, and reactions were set up in 96-well plates with Thioflavin T. Samples were scored positive if at least 50% of replicates reached a fluorescence threshold.

- **Confocal Microscopy**: ScN2a cells were fixed and permeabilized, then incubated with anti-PrP mAb 4H11 and anti-lamp1 antibodies to visualize PrPSc. Immunofluorescence intensity was quantified using ImageJ software, and the average intensity per cell was calculated.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Statistical analysis was performed using GraphPad Instat. Unpaired two-tailed t-tests were used for pairwise comparisons, while one-way ANOVA with Tukey post-test was used for multiple comparisons. Significance levels were set at *p < 0.05, **p < 0.01, and ***p < 0.001.
