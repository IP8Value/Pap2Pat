- The synthesis and characterization of novel nNOS-selective inhibitors are described, including their K_i values against various NOS isoforms. Compounds were assayed for in vitro inhibition using purified enzymes from different species. Selectivity ratios and off-target binding profiles are provided.

- X-ray crystal structures were solved for inhibitor complexes with the heme domains of human iNOS, rat nNOS, and human eNOS. The binding modes and interactions reveal key insights into isoform-selective inhibition mechanisms.

- In vitro metabolic stability was evaluated using liver microsomes. Half-lives and intrinsic clearance values are reported for lead compounds. Structure-property relationships were analyzed to guide optimization efforts.

- BBB permeability was predicted using in silico models and experimentally determined in MDCK cell monolayers. Log D, log P, TPSA calculations and PAMPA assays inform on the physicochemical properties impacting brain penetration.

- A comprehensive off-target binding screen of 45 receptors was performed for lead compounds. The results are classified into concerning, moderate, weak or insignificant levels of interaction to assess selectivity against other targets.
