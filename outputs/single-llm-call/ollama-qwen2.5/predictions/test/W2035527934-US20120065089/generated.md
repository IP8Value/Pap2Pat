- This study developed a novel diagnostic system using WFA lectin and MY.1E12 antibody to detect sialylated MUC1 carrying WFA-reactive glycans in bile samples for cholangiocarcinoma (CC). The new ELISA showed high sensitivity (90%) and AUC (0.86) compared to conventional methods like biliary cytology.

- Lectin microarray analysis of tissue sections identified WFA as the most promising probe to differentiate ICC from normal BDE with a significant P value (<0.0001). Histochemical staining confirmed strong expression of WFA-reactive glycans in cancerous lesions but not in benign tissues.

- The WFA-MY.1E12 ELISA system performed well, correctly identifying 27/30 CC patients (90% sensitivity) and excluding 29/38 with benign duct disease (76% specificity). This outperformed the conventional MY.1E12 antibody sandwich assay.

- WFA staining also distinguished ICC from HCC elements in combined tumors with 100% accuracy, which has significant clinical value given the different treatments for these cancers. However, further validation with more specimens is needed to confirm its usefulness.

- The authors aim to develop this into a robust diagnostic system applicable to serum samples. Structural analysis of WFA-associated MUC1 glycans could provide clues to design even more specific probes than WFA lectin used here. Overall, the study presents a promising new method for early CC diagnosis using bile specimens.
