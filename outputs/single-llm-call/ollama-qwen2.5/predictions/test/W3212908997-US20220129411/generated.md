To achieve the standard form for Pauli operators \(O\) and \(O'\), we can apply local Clifford gates to each qubit. These transformations preserve anti-commutativity but simplify the structure of the Pauli operators. For instance, if both \(O\) and \(O'\) act as \(X\) on a qubit, applying a Hadamard gate changes one to \(Z\). This process is repeated for all qubits until \(O\) and \(O'\) match one of the five cases. The resulting circuit has a CNOT cost of at most \(3n/2 + O(1)\), ensuring efficient disentanglement.
