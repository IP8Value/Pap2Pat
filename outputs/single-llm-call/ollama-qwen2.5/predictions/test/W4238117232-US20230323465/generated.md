- This study developed a molecular test for evaluating preoperative thyroid FNAs that considers NIFTP lesions by characterizing isoforms of previously profiled gene candidates. The 5-isoform panel of CDH3, FNDC4, HMGA2, KLK7, and PLAG1 showed potential in differentiating thyroid tumor subtypes and was not expressed in PBMCs.
- TPO1 was chosen as a load control reference gene due to its constant expression across differentiated thyroid tumors and undetectability in PBMC samples. The panel demonstrated an 86% ability to distinguish benign from malignant tumors, with high sensitivity and specificity.
- Limitations include the limited number of intraoperative FNAs and the lack of FNA samples from rare FC and HC cases. Further validation trials are needed to develop this panel as a diagnostic assay for guiding preoperative surgical decisions in thyroid tumors.
