- Sequences for CRISPR/Cas9-mediated genome editing were designed based on T. reesei’s genetic information. Guide RNAs targeting specific genes of interest were cloned into plasmids and transformed into protoplasts of RUT-C30, generating strains Br_TrR02, Br_TrR02B, and Br_TrR03 with desired mutations.
- The mutant strains were characterized by PCR and sequencing to confirm the presence of intended genetic modifications. Growth rates and morphology on solid media were compared to the parental strain to assess any phenotypic changes.
- Fermentation experiments in shake flasks using different carbon sources (glucose, lactose, cellulose) were conducted to evaluate the performance of mutant strains. Samples were collected at regular intervals for biomass and enzyme production analyses.
- Bioreactor fermentations were performed with optimized conditions for high enzyme production. Parameters such as pH, temperature, aeration rate, and feeding strategy were carefully controlled. Enzyme titers and productivity were monitored throughout the fermentation process.
- Enzymatic activities (FPase, β-glucosidase, β-xylosidase, cellobiohydrolase, endoglucanase, xylanase) of collected samples were measured using standard assays. Protein concentrations were quantified to normalize enzymatic activity data. Statistical analyses were performed to compare the performance of mutant strains.
- Saccharification assays with pretreated sugarcane straw were conducted to evaluate the hydrolysis efficiency of enzyme cocktails produced by the mutant strains. Sugar yields were quantified and compared to a commercial cellulase preparation. Results indicated improved saccharification performance by Br_TrR03.
- The use of molasses and yeast-based media in fermentations was explored as an economically viable alternative to traditional synthetic media. Bioreactor experiments with these media showed comparable or better enzyme production levels, highlighting the potential for industrial scale-up.
- Supplementary information includes detailed methods for plasmid construction, transformation protocols, fermentation conditions, and analytical procedures. Additional data on gene expression, protein profiles, and statistical analyses are provided to support the findings reported in the main text.
