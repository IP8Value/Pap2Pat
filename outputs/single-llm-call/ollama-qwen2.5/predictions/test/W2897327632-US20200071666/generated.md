- **Keratinocyte Culture Methods**: Keratinocytes can be cultured using different methods such as 3T3 co-culture, LN-421, and LN-511. These methods vary in their ability to maintain keratinocyte characteristics and proliferation. The choice of method affects gene expression, protein markers, and karyotype stability.

- **Karyotyping Analysis**: Karyotype analysis was performed on keratinocytes cultured using 3T3 co-culture, LN-421, and LN-511. Cells were treated with colcemid, lysed, fixed, and metaphase spreads prepared. G-banding and Wright staining were used to analyze at least 20 metaphases for chromosomal abnormalities.

- **Transplantation Procedure**: Human epidermal grafts were prepared on fibrin mats coated with LN-511 or LN-421, or seeded with lethally irradiated 3T3-J2 fibroblasts. Grafts were placed under a rectangular skin flap in nude mice, secured with Mepilex foam and inert Silon-TSR dressing, and sutured to protect the graft.

- **RNA-Seq Data Analysis**: RNA-seq data from keratinocytes cultured using 3T3 co-culture, LN-421, and LN-511 were aligned to hg38, quantified using RSEM, and analyzed with DESeq2. Differential expression analysis identified significantly different genes between the methods, and GSEA was used to assess functional enrichment of gene sets.

- **Transcriptional Profiling**: Transcripts per million (TPM) values were pre-filtered, logged, and adjusted for patient effects. Heatmaps visualized the expression levels of laminins, integrins, keratins, basal, and differentiation marker genes in the three culturing methods. Boxplots represented laminin expression in whole skin samples.

- **Cultured Epidermal Skin Equivalent**: Fibrin mats were prepared using TISSEEL kit, mixed with aprotinin to prevent fibrinolysis, and used as a substrate for keratinocyte culture. HEKs between passages 1 and 3 were seeded at 10,000 cells per cm² and grown to confluence before transplantation onto nude mice.

- **Statistical Analyses**: Data from experimental results were presented as means ± s.e.m. One-way ANOVA with Tukey’s post hoc test was used to assess differences in relative mRNA and surface marker protein expression at different time points. Significance was set at p < 0.05, and all analyses were performed using Prism Software 7.0.

- **Electronic Supplementary Material**: The RNA-seq data have been deposited in NCBI’s Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO accession number GSE109645). Additional results and data are provided in supplementary figures and data files, including differential expression analysis, functional enrichment, and karyotyping results.
