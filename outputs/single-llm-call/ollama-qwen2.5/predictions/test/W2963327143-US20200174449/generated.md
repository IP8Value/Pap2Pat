### Here's a concise summary:

- **Inverse Design and Additive Manufacturing**: We present a platform combining an inverse electromagnetic design algorithm with additive manufacturing for novel millimeter-wave metadevices. This methodology can be applied to any photonic device where the desired behavior is defined by input and output field distributions.

- **Millimeter-Wave Metadevices**: The devices are designed and fabricated in the millimeter-wave region, but the approach is scalable to other wavelengths from visible to microwave frequencies, provided suitable low-loss dielectric materials are available.

- **Versatile Design and Prototyping**: This platform addresses the need for rapid, versatile design and prototyping of compact, low-cost, low-loss, and broadband components that can be easily integrated into complex electromagnetic systems.

- **Experimental Validation**: We designed and fabricated polarization beam-splitters and metalenses. The devices show good agreement between simulations and experiments, demonstrating broad operation bandwidth and efficient performance across a range of frequencies.

- **Data Availability**: No datasets were generated or analyzed during the current study, and electronic supplementary material is available for further details on device designs and experimental results.
