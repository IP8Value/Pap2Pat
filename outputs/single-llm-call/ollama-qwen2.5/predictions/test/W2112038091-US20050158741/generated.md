- The study identified toyocamycin and FUR as inhibitors of ribozyme self-cleavage in mammalian cells. These compounds can be used to regulate gene expression by preventing mRNA degradation mediated by active ribozymes.

- Combined administration of toyocamycin and FUR resulted in improved induction of gene expression compared to using either compound alone, potentially enabling more experimental applications with reduced toxicity.

- The ability of 5-fluorouracil (5FU), a common chemotherapeutic agent, to act as a ribozyme inhibitor suggests potential uses for RNA-based systems to monitor drug uptake and optimize administration routes in cancer treatment.
