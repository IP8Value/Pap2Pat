### Human and Mouse Datasets

Human and mouse datasets presented in Figures 5 and 6 were generated from previously published studies. These datasets are accessible through the investigators involved in the respective studies. The human subject guidelines are detailed in Gernez et al. and Levine et al., ensuring that patient records were anonymized and de-identified before use.

### Mouse PerC and Spleen Datasets

Mouse peritoneal cavity (PerC) and spleen datasets for Figure 7 were generated using adult C57BL/6 mice. The study was approved by the Stanford Animal Care and Use Committee, adhering to Administrative Panel on Laboratory Animal Care guidelines. Cells were harvested, filtered, and stained with a 12-parameter reagent panel.

### Instrument Details

The human and mouse samples were collected using instruments detailed in Gernez et al., Levine et al., and the referenced studies. PerC and spleen cells were analyzed on BD LSRII instruments equipped with four lasers (405 nm, 488 nm, 532 nm, and 640 nm) and 19 PMTs, collecting data for 0.2 to 2 million cells.

### Data Analysis Workflow

The workflow for analyzing the datasets involves two main steps: preprocessing and cluster matching. Preprocessing includes compensating (for fluorescence flow cytometry), transforming with Logicle transformation, and clustering with density-based merging (DBM). The QF-based cluster matching algorithm is then used to align cell populations of interest, such as eosinophils.
