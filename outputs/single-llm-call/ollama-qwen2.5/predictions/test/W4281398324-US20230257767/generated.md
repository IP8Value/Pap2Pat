- The study aimed to evaluate the potential of using the HSPA5 promoter (Hspa5p) for enhancing recombinant protein production in Chinese hamster ovary (CHO) cells. Luciferase and monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) were used as model proteins, with stable cell pools and clones generated through genetic engineering techniques.
- Transient transfections of CHO-K1 cells with constructs containing the Hspa5p driving luciferase expression demonstrated that this promoter was more effective in promoting gene expression compared to commonly used viral promoters like CMV. This indicated a promising potential for biopharmaceutical production.
- Stable pools and clones expressing luciferase under the control of Hspa5p showed higher luminescence activity over time, suggesting sustained and robust expression levels. This finding supported the use of Hspa5p in long-term cultures required for industrial applications.
- In fed-batch cultures, stable cell lines expressing mAbs under Hspa5p exhibited enhanced productivity compared to those using other promoters. The Hspa5p-driven cells maintained higher viability and specific productivity throughout the culture period, indicating improved cellular performance.
- Transcriptional analysis revealed that Hspa5p-driven expression of mAbs was associated with upregulation of genes involved in protein folding and secretion pathways. This suggests that the promoter may enhance overall cellular machinery for recombinant protein production, contributing to higher yields.
- In silico analysis of the hspa5 upstream region identified key regulatory elements such as TATA-box, CCAAT-box, and CpG islands, which likely contribute to its strong promoter activity. Comparative sequence analysis across species showed high conservation, suggesting evolutionary significance and broad applicability.
- Addition of ER stress inducers like DTT, tunicamycin, and thapsigargin at sublethal concentrations during fed-batch cultures further enhanced mAb production in Hspa5p-driven cells. This indicated that mild ER stress could synergize with the promoter to boost protein expression, offering a novel strategy for optimizing bioprocesses.
- Overall, the study demonstrated that Hspa5p is a potent and versatile promoter for enhancing recombinant protein production in CHO cells, providing a valuable tool for improving the efficiency of biopharmaceutical manufacturing processes. The findings open new avenues for optimizing cell line development and bioreactor operations.
