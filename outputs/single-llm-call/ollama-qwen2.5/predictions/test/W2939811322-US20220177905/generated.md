- **Construction of CCN Copycat Element**: The ccN CopyCat plasmid was constructed using homology arms targeting the yellow locus and included gRNA-y1, gRNA-N+, and a 3XP3-DsRed eye marker. It was transformed into One Shot® TOP10 cells, purified, and injected into wa NAx16 rb− embryos with pHsp70-Cas9 for transient Cas9 expression. Male transformants were identified by yellow− and DsRed phenotypes.

- **Genomic DNA Preparation**: Genomic DNA from single adult flies was prepared using a lysis buffer (10 mM Tris pH 8.2, 1 mM EDTA, 25 mM NaCl, 0.3 mg/ml Proteinase K), incubated at 37°C for 30 minutes, and heated at 95°C for 2 minutes. The samples were diluted with ddH2O and stored at −20°C.

- **Drosophila Genetics**: y<ccN> wa NAx16/FM7 females were crossed to vasaCas9 homozygous males to generate F1 master females. Crosses were performed at 25°C on standard Drosophila food. The y− w+ NAxE2 line with a gRNA-N+-sensitive Abruptex allele was used for experiments, and a cleavage-insensitive N+ allele was recombined with y<ccN> wa to generate the donor chromosome.

- **Sequence Analysis**: Mutations in the yellow locus were sequenced by PCR amplifying an ~500-bp fragment with primers 417 and 356, then sequencing with primer 418. Donor vs. receiver chromosomes were distinguished by amplifying a Notch intron 5 fragment with NintS3 and NintAS3 primers and sequencing with NintS3bis. Mutations in the NAx region were sequenced using an ~1-Kb PCR product amplified with NAxS and NAxAS2 primers.

- **Active Genetic Safety Measures**: All active genetics experiments were conducted according to UCSD Institutional Biosafety Committee-approved protocols, with full gene-drive experiments in a high-security ACL2 facility and split drive experiments in an ACL1 insectary. Plastic vials were autoclaved before disposal, following guidelines for laboratory confinement of gene-drive systems.

- **Wing Dissection and Mounting**: Drosophila wings were dissected in isopropanol and mounted in 100% Canada balsam for analysis.

- **Antibody Staining of Drosophila Embryos**: Embryos were fixed and stained with a rat anti-Elav antibody (DSHB# 7E8A10, 1/20 dilution) using standard procedures. Samples were mounted in Slowfade diamond anti-fade mountant and imaged on a Leica SP5 confocal microscope. Each data point in Fig. 2c represents the analysis of 30-50 embryos.

- **Ethical Conduct of Research**: All research complied with relevant ethical regulations for animal testing and conformed to UCSD institutionally approved biological use authorization protocol (BUA #311).
