- **Introduction**: The study aimed to evaluate the therapeutic effects of siRNAs targeting TGF-β1 and COX-2 in human hypertrophic scar tissue using a nude mouse model. Histidine-Lysine polymers (HKP) were used to deliver the siRNAs, forming nanoparticles that could be injected into the scar tissue.

- **Materials**: The primary materials included human hypertrophic scar tissue, nude mice, HKP peptides, TGF-β1 and COX-2 siRNAs, Trizol reagent, primers for qPCR, antibodies for Western blotting, and various kits for hydroxyproline detection and apoptosis analysis.

- **Methods**: Human hypertrophic scar tissue was implanted into nude mice. Four weeks post-implantation, HKP (TGF-β1/COX-2 siRNAs) nanoparticles were injected into the scars. The treatment was administered three times over 15 days, with each injection divided into five areas for even distribution.

- **Cell Culture Experiments**: PCRs and Western blots were performed to assess gene and protein expression levels of TGF-β1, COX-2, α-SMA, Col1a1, and Col3a1 in treated cells. Apoptosis was detected using the Annexin V-FITC/PI method, and cell cycle analysis was conducted via flow cytometry.

- **In Vivo Experiments**: Scar size was measured before and after treatment. RNA was extracted from scar tissue at various time points to analyze gene expression changes. Apoptotic cells were detected using an in situ Cell Death Detection Kit. SEM and TEM analyses were performed to visualize nanoparticle structure and cellular effects.

- **Results**: Treatment with HKP (TGF-β1/COX-2 siRNAs) significantly reduced scar size and decreased the expression of TGF-β1, COX-2, α-SMA, Col1a1, and Col3a1. Increased apoptosis was observed in treated scar tissues, indicating effective gene silencing and therapeutic response.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Data were analyzed using IBM SPSS Statistics ver. 20. Mean ± SD was used for cell culture results, and mean ± SE for in vivo results. Student's t-test determined significance between groups, with p-values < 0.05 considered statistically significant.
