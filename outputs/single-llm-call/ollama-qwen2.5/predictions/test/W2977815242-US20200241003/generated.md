### Viral Transduction of APCs for FRET Analysis
Virally transduced antigen-presenting cells (APCs) were sorted to remove untransduced targets and those carrying non-productive minigene-reporter transgenes. Sorting was performed on BD FACSAria Fusion or BD FACSAria II, ensuring single-cell purity by gating for PI-negative, YFP-positive cells with a resting FRET signature.

### CTL/APC Co-Cultures
Activated and expanded CTLs were used in FRET-shift assays 7 days post-stimulation. Co-cultures were set up at a 1:1 effector:target ratio in appropriate tubes or plates, maintained for 4 hours at 37°C in a 5% CO2 atmosphere.

### Flow Cytometry and FACS
Cells from co-cultures were washed, resuspended in PBS with 5% FBS and PI, filtered through 40 μm nylon mesh, and kept on ice. Analysis was performed on BD LSR II Fortessa, and sorting on BD FACSAria Fusion to isolate cells based on FRET-shift.

### Quantitative PCR
Genomic DNA from collected cells was analyzed using a custom duplex TaqMan assay to enumerate Ova minigene and scrambled minigene copies. A 5-point standard curve was constructed, and unmixed control cells were used to correct for differences in average insert copy numbers.

### Sequencing Library Preparation
Sequencing libraries were generated by amplifying minigenes from isolated gDNA using Phusion polymerase. Primers annealed to regions flanking the minigene site. Libraries were prepared either by single-round PCR or a two-round PCR protocol, pooled, and gel-purified before sequencing on the Illumina MiSeq platform.

### Data Analysis
Gel and CFU analyses were conducted using ImageQuant TL v8.1. Flow-cytometry data was analyzed with FlowJo V10.0.8r1. Quantitative PCR results were processed using SDS2.4. Sequence data handling involved Geneious v8.1.2 and Bioconductor package seqinr, while other analyses used R v3.1.2 and RStudio.
