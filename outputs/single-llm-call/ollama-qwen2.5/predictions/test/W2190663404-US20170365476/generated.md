- Nanosecond laser annealing can crystallize and remove pressure-induced phases from hyperdoped black silicon while maintaining light-trapping morphology and high sub-bandgap optical absorptance. It also reactivates sub-bandgap optical absorptance after thermal deactivation, without interfering with rectifying homojunctions.
- Combining thermal and ns laser annealing produces highly crystalline, optically absorptive, electrically rectifying hyperdoped black silicon. This approach leverages equilibrium and non-equilibrium processing to control material properties.
- Challenges remain in fabricating sub-bandgap optoelectronic devices using hyperdoped black silicon, including controlling dopant concentration, layer thickness, and carrier transport. Methods to independently tune these parameters are needed for efficient device fabrication.
