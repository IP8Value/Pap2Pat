- A frequency tunable piezoelectric vibration energy harvester using IPMC was designed and tested. Experiments showed effective resonant frequency shifts with minimal power spent on tuning compared to PVEH output, yielding substantial net power gains.

- The impact of the distance between the IPMC tip and the harvester body was studied. Results indicated that closer points of loading led to higher resonant frequencies, with a slight drop in PVEH output but no significant effect on total charge generated over varying frequencies.

- Future work will focus on optimizing actuation force and topology. The IPMC can be tailored for variable loading by adjusting anion/cation concentration, hydration level, and Nafion film thickness during fabrication, making it highly suitable for applications requiring dynamic stiffness changes.

- Integration of a low-power microcontroller is in progress to enable autonomous operation. The power for the IPMC can be sourced from the PVEH output itself. Additionally, exploring nanoscale devices integrating IPMC with piezoelectric nano-fibers could have potential biomedical applications, leveraging advancements in IPMC design such as silver nanopowder usage.
