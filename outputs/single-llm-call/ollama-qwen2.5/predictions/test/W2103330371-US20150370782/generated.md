- The paper presents a method to improve medical relation extraction by leveraging large amounts of unlabeled data through manifold models. It compares this approach against state-of-the-art methods like SVMs and tree kernels on a dataset of 7 medical relations.
- Experiments show that integrating unlabeled data via the proposed manifold model significantly improves F1 scores, achieving up to 15% improvement over baselines. More unlabeled data leads to better performance.
- The authors also construct a new knowledge base (KB) by running their relation detectors on a large medical corpus. This KB outperforms UMLS in an answer generation task on Doctor Dilemma questions, indicating much better coverage of medical relations.
- The system is computationally efficient, taking 8 hours to process 11GB of text using 16 machines. It demonstrates the potential for automatically constructing comprehensive medical relation KBs to complement existing manually curated resources like UMLS.
- Overall, this work shows promising results in leveraging unlabeled data and manifold learning for medical relation extraction, while also highlighting challenges in effectively utilizing large amounts of unlabeled examples.
