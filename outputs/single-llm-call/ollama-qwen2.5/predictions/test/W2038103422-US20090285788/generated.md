- **Library Construction**: A library of shRNA sequences was constructed by integrating a hairpin-loop structure into the pSuper vector. This involved multiple enzymatic steps including Klenow extension, ligation with a hairpin-loop linker, and digestion with specific enzymes to create the desired DNA cassette.

- **Random Mutagenesis**: Random mutagenesis of the 3p sequence was performed using a PCR-MATE EP 391 DNA synthesizer. Phosphoramidites were spiked into each bottle to achieve a 2% mutation rate across the 87-mer oligo, ensuring diverse mutants.

- **Cell Culture and Transduction**: FL5.12 pro-B cells were cultured in RPMI 1640 media supplemented with IL3. Retroviral supernatant was prepared by transfecting 293T cells with pSiren library and a packaging plasmid, followed by infecting FL5.12 cells using centrifugation and polybrene.

- **Sequence Retrieval**: Genomic DNA from enriched GFP-positive cells was extracted and the shRNA-encoding cassette amplified using specific primers. The hairpin-loop structure was sequenced by amplifying each half of the stem separately, ensuring accurate sequence retrieval.

- **Apoptosis Induction and Caspase 3 Assay**: Apoptosis in FL5.12 cells was induced by IL3 withdrawal, and recovery was assessed by adding back IL3. Cell viability was measured using propidium iodide staining and flow cytometry. Caspase 3 activity was quantified using a colorimetric substrate assay.

- **Statistical Analysis**: Data from multiple independent experiments were analyzed using Student's t-test to compare means, with error bars representing standard deviations. Each data point in the bar graphs was derived from 3 to 6 independent experiments, ensuring robust statistical significance.
