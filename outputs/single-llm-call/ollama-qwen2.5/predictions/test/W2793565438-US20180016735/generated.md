- LC panels were fabricated using a cold aqueous alkaline solvent process, employing low-cost raw materials like cotton, osage orange wood sawdust (OOW), and agave fibers (AF) without prior chemical pretreatment. The final product is lightweight yet possesses strong mechanical properties.
- Various factors significantly influenced the mechanical properties of LCs, including reaction temperature, matrix concentration, solvent volumes, and types. Optimal conditions were identified to produce panels with favorable mechanical values compared to common plastics and commercial packaging materials.
- FTIR analysis revealed that the cold alkaline solution extracted hemicellulose during processing, while thermal degradation profiles showed distinct decomposition patterns for different ingredients and the final composite.
- Despite promising mechanical properties, LCs exhibited poor dimensional stability when exposed to water. Coatings may be necessary to improve this aspect, but further research is needed to enhance overall performance and explore potential applications as sustainable alternatives to conventional materials.
