- The provided document outlines a comprehensive experimental approach to enhance homology-directed repair (HDR) efficiency using CRISPR/Cas9 and dual guide RNAs (dgRNAs). It details the preparation of cell lines, transfection methods, lentivirus production and transduction, flow cytometry analysis, genomic DNA isolation, and sequencing.
- The study uses HEK293, HEK293T, HEK293FT, and HeLa cells, which are stably transfected with Cas9. These cells are then transfected with sgRNA plasmids and PCR HR donors to induce HDR events, followed by selection and analysis.
- Lentivirus production involves transfecting HEK293FT cells with transfer plasmids, psPAX2, and pMD 2.G using polyethylenimine (PEI). The lentivirus supernatant is harvested 48 hours post-transfection and used to transduce target cell lines, followed by selection with puromycin or blasticidin.
- For gene expression analysis, total RNA is isolated from cells 48 hours after drug treatment. cDNA synthesis is performed using the Advantage RT-for-PCR kit, and qPCR is carried out using SYBR Fast qPCR Mix to quantify mRNA levels relative to GAPDH.
- Confocal fluorescence imaging is used to visualize EGFP or mCherry expression in transfected cells. Cells are fixed with 4% formaldehyde, stained with DAPI, and imaged using a Zeiss LSM 800 confocal microscope. The data are analyzed using ImageJ software.
- Flow cytometry analysis is performed to determine the percentage of EGFP-positive or mCherry-positive cells. Transfected cells are trypsinized, resuspended in PBS, and analyzed using a BD Fortessa or BD FACSAria flow cytometer within one hour of preparation.
- Genomic DNA isolation involves lysing transfected cells and extracting gDNA using the DNeasy Tissue Kit. PCR is performed to amplify HDR-positive events, and the products are sequenced by Sanger sequencing or cloned into the pMD-19 T vector for further analysis.
- Cell cycle analysis is conducted by fixing cells in 70% ethanol, staining with Propidium Iodide (PI), and analyzing using flow cytometry. This helps assess the impact of CRISPR/dgRNAs on cell cycle distribution.
- Cell viability is measured using a CCK-8 assay. Transfected cells are seeded in a 96-well plate, incubated with CCK-8 reagent, and absorbance is read at 450 nm to determine cell viability.
- All relevant plasmids and sequencing data will be publicly deposited upon publication. The study does not use custom codes, and most experiments were repeated at least three times to ensure reproducibility. Investigators were not blinded for data collection or analysis.
