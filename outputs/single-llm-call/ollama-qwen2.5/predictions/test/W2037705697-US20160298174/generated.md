- In this study, researchers modified a KlenTaq DNA polymerase to improve its mismatch selectivity. They substituted polar amino acid residues interacting with the primer strand backbone and screened for enhanced performance.

- Mutants with increased mismatch selectivity were identified for each examined position. However, combining advantageous mutations through gene shuffling did not further improve properties, as it resulted in decreased protein stability and activity.

- The R660V mutant showed promising results, demonstrating high mismatch selectivity suitable for SNP detection, even from whole blood without prior DNA purification. It also performed well in methylation-specific PCR to detect epigenetic changes.

- Error rate analysis revealed that KlenTaq R660V had a slightly improved fidelity compared to the wild-type enzyme, mainly due to reduced transitions while transversions remained unaffected.

- In HLA typing by sequence-specific primed PCR (PCR-SSP), KlenTaq R660V outperformed AmpliTaq Gold in specificity for detecting specific HLA alleles, while maintaining equivalent sensitivity. This highlights its potential for clinical diagnostics and genotyping applications.
