# DESCRIPTION

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Bipolar disorder (BD) is a major psychiatric condition characterized by episodes of depression and mania or hypomania, interspersed by periods of euthymia. Typically manifesting in late adolescence or early adulthood, BD is a significant health issue requiring continuous monitoring and often lifelong treatment. The economic burden of BD is substantial, with a recent Swedish study estimating the average annual cost at 28,011 euros per patient and a 2009 US study estimating the direct and indirect costs at 151 billion dollars. Despite the importance of genetic factors in BD susceptibility, the exact pathogenesis remains poorly understood. Emerging evidence suggests that mitochondrial dysfunction plays a critical role in BD, supported by findings from microarray studies, biochemical studies, neuroimaging studies, and post-mortem brain studies. Metabolomics, the profiling of small-molecule metabolites, offers a unique approach to characterizing specific metabolic phenotypes associated with BD. Capillary electrophoresis time-of-flight mass spectrometry (CE-TOFMS) is a state-of-the-art metabolomics technique that can simultaneously quantify virtually all charged low-molecular-weight compounds in a sample. This invention leverages CE-TOFMS to identify specific metabolites in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) that serve as biomarkers for BD, thereby facilitating early detection and improved management of the disorder.

## Non-Patent Literature

1. **Bipolar Disorder Overview**:
   - Bipolar disorder is a chronic mental health condition characterized by extreme mood swings, including emotional highs (mania or hypomania) and lows (depression).
   - The disorder affects approximately 2.8% of the global population and is a leading cause of disability worldwide.

2. **Economic Burden**:
   - A 2009 US study estimated the total economic burden of BD at 151 billion dollars, encompassing both direct medical costs and indirect costs such as lost productivity.
   - A Swedish study reported an average annual cost of 28,011 euros per patient, highlighting the significant financial impact of BD on healthcare systems.

3. **Genetic Factors**:
   - Twin and family studies have consistently shown a strong genetic component in BD, with heritability estimates ranging from 60% to 85%.
   - However, the precise genetic mechanisms and specific genes involved remain elusive.

4. **Mitochondrial Dysfunction**:
   - Accumulating evidence suggests that mitochondrial dysfunction is a key factor in the pathogenesis of BD.
   - Studies have reported abnormalities in calcium regulation, energy metabolism, oxidative phosphorylation, and cellular resilience in BD patients.
   - Post-mortem brain studies have identified altered levels of metabolites such as myo-inositol, creatine, glutamate, lactate, and phosphocholine, indicative of mitochondrial dysfunction.

5. **Metabolomics**:
   - Metabolomics is the systematic study of small-molecule metabolites in biological systems.
   - It provides a comprehensive snapshot of the metabolic state of an organism, integrating information from changes at the gene, transcript, and protein levels.
   - CE-TOFMS is a powerful metabolomics tool that offers high resolution and versatility, making it suitable for identifying biomarkers in complex biological fluids like CSF.

6. **CSF as a Biomarker Source**:
   - CSF is a valuable substrate for studying brain disorders due to its close proximity to brain tissue and its ability to reflect the metabolic status and biochemistry of the brain.
   - Previous studies have used CSF to identify biomarkers for various neurological and psychiatric conditions, including Alzheimer's disease and Parkinson's disease.

7. **Relevant Studies**:
   - Lan et al. (2010) conducted a metabolomics analysis of post-mortem brain samples from BD patients and controls, identifying increased levels of myo-inositol, creatine, glutamate, lactate, and phosphocholine in BD patients.
   - Two studies using CE-TOFMS in plasma samples from autism spectrum disorders and schizophrenia patients have reported robust changes in specific metabolites, suggesting the potential of this technique for identifying biomarkers in psychiatric disorders.

## SUMMARY OF THE PRESENT INVENTION

The present invention relates to a method for identifying and quantifying specific metabolites in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) that serve as biomarkers for bipolar disorder (BD). The method involves performing metabolomics assays using capillary electrophoresis time-of-flight mass spectrometry (CE-TOFMS) on CSF samples from BD patients and healthy controls. The invention identifies isocitrate as a significant metabolite whose levels are elevated in the CSF of BD patients compared to controls. Additionally, the invention includes methods for analyzing the expression of genes and proteins related to isocitrate metabolism, specifically the aconitase (ACO) and isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) genes, in post-mortem brain samples. The invention further encompasses genetic association analyses to determine the role of genetic variations in ACO and IDH genes in BD susceptibility. The identified biomarkers and associated methods provide a novel approach for the early detection, diagnosis, and management of BD, potentially leading to improved patient outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PRESENT INVENTION

### Metabolomics Analysis Using CE-TOFMS

The invention utilizes capillary electrophoresis time-of-flight mass spectrometry (CE-TOFMS) to perform metabolomics analysis on cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) samples. CE-TOFMS is a highly sensitive and accurate technique that can simultaneously quantify a wide range of low-molecular-weight compounds in a sample. The method involves the following steps:

1. **Sample Collection**:
   - CSF samples are collected from BD patients and healthy controls during a euthymic period, ensuring that patients are not experiencing a depressive or manic episode.
   - Lumbar puncture is performed between 0900 and 1000 hours after an overnight fast to minimize variability in metabolic profiles.
   - The collected CSF is immediately processed and stored at −80°C to preserve the integrity of the metabolites.

2. **Sample Preparation**:
   - CSF samples are thawed and prepared according to standardized protocols to ensure consistency across samples.
   - The samples are filtered and diluted as necessary to optimize the CE-TOFMS analysis.

3. **CE-TOFMS Analysis**:
   - The prepared CSF samples are analyzed using CE-TOFMS, which separates and detects the metabolites based on their charge-to-mass ratio.
   - The data are processed to identify and quantify the metabolites present in the samples.

4. **Data Analysis**:
   - The metabolomics data are analyzed using statistical methods to identify metabolites that show significant differences between BD patients and healthy controls.
   - Unpaired t-tests and Wilcoxon rank-sum tests are used to compare the levels of individual metabolites between the two groups.
   - Multivariate logistic regression analysis is performed to identify metabolites that are independently associated with BD.

### Identification of Isocitrate as a Biomarker

The invention identifies isocitrate as a significant metabolite whose levels are elevated in the CSF of BD patients compared to healthy controls. Isocitrate is a key intermediate in the citric acid cycle, which plays a central role in energy metabolism and mitochondrial function. The increased levels of isocitrate in BD patients suggest a disruption in the citric acid cycle and potential mitochondrial dysfunction.

### Gene and Protein Expression Analysis

#### Expression of ACO and IDH Genes in the Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex

The invention includes methods for analyzing the expression of aconitase (ACO) and isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) genes in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) of post-mortem brain samples from BD patients and healthy controls. The DLPFC is a critical brain region involved in cognitive and emotional processing, and alterations in its function are implicated in BD.

1. **RNA Extraction and Quantification**:
   - Total RNA is extracted from the DLPFC samples using standard protocols.
   - The mRNA levels of ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, IDH2, IDH3A, and IDH3B are quantified using real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR).

2. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The mRNA levels are compared between BD patients and healthy controls using unpaired t-tests and analysis of covariance (ANCOVA) to account for potential confounding factors such as pH and post-mortem interval.
   - The results show that the mRNA levels of IDH3A and IDH3B are significantly lower in the DLPFC of BD patients compared to controls, while the mRNA levels of ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, and IDH2 are not significantly different.

#### Protein Expression of IDH3A and IDH3B in the Cerebellum and Parietal Cortex

The invention also includes methods for analyzing the protein expression of IDH3A and IDH3B in the cerebellum and parietal cortex (Brodmann's area 7) of post-mortem brain samples from BD patients, major depressive disorder (MDD) patients, schizophrenia patients, and healthy controls.

1. **Protein Extraction and Quantification**:
   - Total protein is extracted from the cerebellum and parietal cortex samples using standard protocols.
   - The protein levels of IDH3A and IDH3B are quantified using Western blotting.

2. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The protein levels are compared between the different groups using one-way ANOVA or ANCOVA.
   - The results show that the protein levels of IDH3A are significantly lower in the cerebellum of BD, MDD, and schizophrenia patients compared to controls, while the protein levels of IDH3B are not significantly different in any of the brain regions.

### Genetic Association Analyses

The invention includes methods for performing genetic association analyses to determine the role of genetic variations in ACO and IDH genes in BD susceptibility. The analyses are conducted in two independent sample sets of BD patients and healthy controls.

1. **Genotyping**:
   - DNA is extracted from blood samples of BD patients and healthy controls.
   - Single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in the ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, IDH2, IDH3A, and IDH3B genes are genotyped using high-throughput genotyping platforms.

2. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The genotyping data are analyzed using logistic regression models to identify SNPs that are associated with BD.
   - Epistasis tests are conducted to identify interactions between SNPs in different genes.
   - The results show that several SNPs within the ACO1 and IDH2 genes attain nominal significance, but none remain significant after multiple testing correction.

### Effects of Medication on Metabolites and Gene Expression

The invention includes methods for examining the effects of chronic treatment with mood stabilizers, such as lithium (Li) and valproic acid (VPA), on CSF metabolites and gene expression in the rat brain.

1. **Animal Model**:
   - Rats are chronically treated with Li or VPA for 4 weeks to mimic the long-term medication regimens used in BD patients.
   - CSF samples are collected from the rats, and the levels of 116 major metabolites are measured using CE-TOFMS.

2. **Metabolomics Analysis**:
   - The CSF metabolomics data are analyzed to identify metabolites that are altered by chronic treatment with Li or VPA.
   - The results show that chronic treatment with Li significantly increases CSF levels of succinic acid and argininosuccinic acid, while chronic treatment with VPA significantly alters CSF levels of threonine, glutamine, arginine, tryptophan, and argininosuccinic acid. However, CSF levels of isocitrate are not altered by chronic treatment with Li or VPA.

3. **Gene Expression Analysis**:
   - The mRNA levels of Aco1, Aco2, Idh3a, and Idh3b are quantified in the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus of the treated rats using qPCR.
   - The results show that chronic treatment with Li or VPA does not alter the expression of these genes in the rat brain.

The present invention provides a comprehensive method for identifying and quantifying specific metabolites in cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) that serve as biomarkers for bipolar disorder (BD). The invention identifies isocitrate as a significant metabolite whose levels are elevated in the CSF of BD patients, suggesting a disruption in the citric acid cycle and potential mitochondrial dysfunction. The invention also includes methods for analyzing the expression of genes and proteins related to isocitrate metabolism, specifically the aconitase (ACO) and isocitrate dehydrogenase (IDH) genes, in post-mortem brain samples. The identified biomarkers and associated methods offer a novel approach for the early detection, diagnosis, and management of BD, potentially leading to improved patient outcomes and reduced healthcare costs.

## EXAMPLES

### Example 1

**Objective**: To perform metabolomics analysis of CSF samples from BD patients and healthy controls using CE-TOFMS.

**Method**:
1. **Sample Collection**:
   - CSF samples were collected from 54 male BD patients and 40 male healthy controls during a euthymic period.
   - Lumbar puncture was performed between 0900 and 1000 hours after an overnight fast.
   - The collected CSF was immediately processed and stored at −80°C.

2. **Sample Preparation**:
   - CSF samples were thawed and filtered to remove particulate matter.
   - The samples were diluted 1:10 with a buffer solution containing internal standards.

3. **CE-TOFMS Analysis**:
   - The prepared CSF samples were analyzed using CE-TOFMS.
   - The data were processed to identify and quantify the metabolites present in the samples.

4. **Data Analysis**:
   - The metabolomics data were analyzed using unpaired t-tests and Wilcoxon rank-sum tests to compare the levels of individual metabolites between BD patients and healthy controls.
   - Multivariate logistic regression analysis was performed to identify metabolites that are independently associated with BD.

**Results**:
- Thirteen metabolites, including uric acid, fructose 6-phosphate, ribose 5-phosphate, CoA, 2-oxoisovaleric acid, lactic acid (lactate), pyruvic acid (pyruvate), citric acid (citrate), isocitric acid (isocitrate), cis-aconitic acid (cis-aconitate), urea, alanine, and tryptophan, were significantly altered in BD patients compared to controls.
- Isocitrate was identified as the most significant metabolite, with increased levels in the CSF of BD patients (odds ratio, 4.402; 95% confidence interval, 2.249–7.266).

### Example 2

**Objective**: To analyze the expression of ACO and IDH genes in the dorsolateral prefrontal cortex (DLPFC) of post-mortem brain samples from BD patients and healthy controls.

**Method**:
1. **Sample Collection**:
   - Post-mortem brain samples from the DLPFC were obtained from 35 BD patients and 34 healthy controls.
   - The samples were stored at −80°C.

2. **RNA Extraction and Quantification**:
   - Total RNA was extracted from the DLPFC samples using the TRIzol reagent.
   - The mRNA levels of ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, IDH2, IDH3A, and IDH3B were quantified using real-time quantitative PCR (qPCR).

3. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The mRNA levels were compared between BD patients and healthy controls using unpaired t-tests and ANCOVA to account for pH and post-mortem interval.

**Results**:
- The mRNA levels of IDH3A and IDH3B were significantly lower in the DLPFC of BD patients compared to controls (P=0.007 for both).
- The mRNA levels of ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, and IDH2 were not significantly different between the two groups.

### Example 3

**Objective**: To analyze the protein expression of IDH3A and IDH3B in the cerebellum and parietal cortex (Brodmann's area 7) of post-mortem brain samples from BD patients, major depressive disorder (MDD) patients, schizophrenia patients, and healthy controls.

**Method**:
1. **Sample Collection**:
   - Post-mortem brain samples from the cerebellum and parietal cortex were obtained from 15 BD patients, 15 MDD patients, 15 schizophrenia patients, and 15 healthy controls.
   - The samples were stored at −80°C.

2. **Protein Extraction and Quantification**:
   - Total protein was extracted from the cerebellum and parietal cortex samples using the RIPA buffer.
   - The protein levels of IDH3A and IDH3B were quantified using Western blotting.

3. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The protein levels were compared between the different groups using one-way ANOVA or ANCOVA.

**Results**:
- The protein levels of IDH3A were significantly lower in the cerebellum of BD, MDD, and schizophrenia patients compared to controls (P=0.017, P=0.006, and P=0.001, respectively).
- The protein levels of IDH3B were not significantly different in any of the brain regions.

### Example 4

**Objective**: To perform genetic association analyses to determine the role of genetic variations in ACO and IDH genes in BD susceptibility.

**Method**:
1. **Sample Collection**:
   - DNA was extracted from blood samples of 1415 BD patients and 1271 healthy controls (sample set 1) and 836 BD patients and 2093 healthy controls (sample set 2).

2. **Genotyping**:
   - SNPs in the ACO1, ACO2, IDH1, IDH2, IDH3A, and IDH3B genes were genotyped using the Illumina Infinium Global Screening Array.

3. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The genotyping data were analyzed using logistic regression models to identify SNPs that are associated with BD.
   - Epistasis tests were conducted to identify interactions between SNPs in different genes.

**Results**:
- Several SNPs within the ACO1 and IDH2 genes attained nominal significance, but none remained significant after multiple testing correction.
- Compelling interactions were detected between markers in the ACO1 and IDH2 genes in both sample sets (P=0.007 for rs10970986 and rs2970357 in sample set 1; P=0.003 for rs13302577 and rs2970359 in sample set 2).

### Example 5

**Objective**: To examine the effects of chronic treatment with lithium (Li) on CSF metabolites and gene expression in the rat brain.

**Method**:
1. **Animal Model**:
   - Rats were chronically treated with Li (30 mg/kg/day) for 4 weeks.
   - CSF samples were collected from the rats, and the levels of 116 major metabolites were measured using CE-TOFMS.

2. **Metabolomics Analysis**:
   - The CSF metabolomics data were analyzed to identify metabolites that are altered by chronic treatment with Li.
   - The mRNA levels of Aco1, Aco2, Idh3a, and Idh3b were quantified in the prefrontal cortex and hippocampus of the treated rats using qPCR.

3. **Statistical Analysis**:
   - The CSF metabolomics data were analyzed using unpaired t-tests and ANOVA.
   - The gene expression data were analyzed using qPCR and ANOVA.

**Results**:
- Chronic treatment with Li significantly increased CSF levels of succinic acid and argininosuccinic acid.
- Chronic treatment with Li did not alter the mRNA levels of Aco1, Aco2, Idh3a, or Idh3b in the prefrontal cortex or hippocampus.
