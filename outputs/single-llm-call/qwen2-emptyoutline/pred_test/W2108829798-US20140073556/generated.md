# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD OF INVENTION

The present invention relates to a novel synthetic peptide, termed Ilantide, and its use as an interleukin-1 (IL-1) receptor antagonist. Ilantide is a 10-amino acid peptide derived from the N-terminal domain of human IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra) and is designed to inhibit the binding of IL-1 to its receptor, IL-1 receptor type I (IL-1RI). The invention encompasses the peptide itself, its multimeric forms, methods of synthesis, pharmaceutical formulations, and methods of use for treating inflammatory conditions, including neuroinflammation.

## BACKGROUND OF INVENTION

Interleukin-1 (IL-1) is a key mediator of the acute-phase inflammatory response and has been implicated in the pathogenesis of chronic inflammatory diseases such as amyotrophic lateral sclerosis, diabetes, and rheumatoid arthritis. IL-1 also plays a significant role in neuroinflammation, contributing to the progression of neurodegenerative conditions like Alzheimer's disease (AD). IL-1 signals through the IL-1 receptor type I (IL-1RI), which, upon binding to IL-1α or IL-1β, recruits the IL-1 receptor accessory protein (IL-1RAcP) to initiate signaling pathways that lead to the activation of nuclear factor κB (NF-κB) and mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK), resulting in the production of various inflammatory mediators.

Current IL-1 antagonists, such as IL-1 receptor antagonist (IL-1Ra), IL-1-neutralizing antibodies, and the IL-1 trap, are effective in treating certain inflammatory conditions but have limitations, particularly in crossing the blood-brain barrier (BBB) for neuroinflammation. Anakinra (Ana), a recombinant form of IL-1Ra, is used for treating rheumatoid arthritis and other inflammatory conditions but has limited efficacy in neuroinflammation due to poor BBB penetration.

The present invention addresses these limitations by providing a novel synthetic peptide, Ilantide, which effectively inhibits IL-1 signaling and crosses the BBB, making it a promising therapeutic agent for both peripheral and central inflammatory conditions.

## SUMMARY OF INVENTION

The present invention provides a novel synthetic peptide, Ilantide, and its multimeric forms, which are effective inhibitors of IL-1 signaling. Ilantide is a 10-amino acid peptide derived from the N-terminal domain of human IL-1Ra and is designed to bind to IL-1RI, thereby inhibiting the binding of IL-1 and subsequent signaling. The invention also encompasses methods for synthesizing Ilantide, pharmaceutical formulations containing Ilantide, and methods of using Ilantide to treat inflammatory conditions, including neuroinflammation.

The key features of the invention include:
1. **Peptide Sequence**: Ilantide has the amino acid sequence SGRKSSKMQA.
2. **Binding Affinity**: Ilantide binds to IL-1RI with an affinity comparable to IL-1Ra and higher than IL-1β.
3. **Inhibition of NF-κB Activation**: Ilantide inhibits the IL-1-induced activation of NF-κB.
4. **Inhibition of TNF-α Secretion**: Ilantide inhibits the IL-1-induced secretion of tumor necrosis factor α (TNF-α) by macrophages.
5. **Neuroprotection**: Ilantide promotes the survival and neuritogenesis of primary neurons.
6. **Pancreatic Islet Protection**: Ilantide protects pancreatic islets from IL-1β-induced apoptosis.
7. **Blood-Brain Barrier Penetration**: Ilantide crosses the BBB, making it effective for treating neuroinflammation.
8. **Anti-Inflammatory Effects**: Ilantide reduces inflammation in models of collagen-induced arthritis (CIA), lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced behavioral changes, and experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE).
9. **Cognitive Improvement**: Ilantide ameliorates social memory deficits induced by amyloid-β (Aβ).

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

### Inflammation

Inflammation is a complex biological response to harmful stimuli, such as pathogens, damaged cells, or irritants. It is a protective mechanism that involves immune cells, blood vessels, and molecular mediators. Interleukin-1 (IL-1) is a crucial cytokine in the inflammatory response, mediating various physiological and pathological processes. IL-1 signaling is initiated through the binding of IL-1 to its receptor, IL-1RI, which then recruits the IL-1 receptor accessory protein (IL-1RAcP) to form a functional signaling complex. This complex activates downstream signaling pathways, including NF-κB and MAPK, leading to the production of proinflammatory mediators.

### Interleukins

Interleukins are a group of cytokines that play a central role in the immune response. IL-1, specifically IL-1α and IL-1β, are key mediators of inflammation. IL-1α is primarily produced by macrophages and epithelial cells, while IL-1β is produced by macrophages and monocytes. Both isoforms bind to IL-1RI, initiating a cascade of signaling events that result in the activation of NF-κB and the production of inflammatory mediators such as TNF-α, IL-6, and chemokines.

### Peptide Fragments of the Present Invention

The present invention provides a novel synthetic peptide, Ilantide, which is a 10-amino acid peptide derived from the N-terminal domain of human IL-1Ra. The amino acid sequence of Ilantide is SGRKSSKMQA. This peptide is designed to mimic the binding site of IL-1Ra for IL-1RI, thereby inhibiting the binding of IL-1 and subsequent signaling.

### Synthetic Preparation

Ilantide and its multimeric forms can be synthesized using solid-phase peptide synthesis techniques. The peptide is synthesized using the fluorenylmethyloxycarbonyl (Fmoc) protection strategy on TentaGel resin. The peptides are further purified by gel filtration using Sephadex G-10. The purity of the peptides is confirmed by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC).

### Compound of the Present Invention

The compound of the present invention is Ilantide, a 10-amino acid peptide with the sequence SGRKSSKMQA. Ilantide binds to IL-1RI with an affinity comparable to IL-1Ra and higher than IL-1β. This binding inhibits the IL-1-induced activation of NF-κB and the secretion of TNF-α by macrophages.

### Multimeric Compound

Multimeric forms of Ilantide, such as Ilantide-t, which consists of four monomers coupled to a lysine backbone, are also encompassed by the present invention. These multimeric forms enhance the binding affinity and biological activity of Ilantide.

### Methods

#### Binding Analysis

The binding of Ilantide to IL-1RI is analyzed using surface plasmon resonance (SPR) technology. Ilantide, IL-1β, or IL-1Ra is immobilized on a CM4 sensor chip, and the recombinant ectodomain of IL-1RI is injected. The binding kinetics are analyzed using nonlinear curve-fitting software.

#### NF-κB Activation Assay

The inhibition of NF-κB activation by Ilantide is assessed using HEK-Blue IL-1β cells, which express an IL-1β-sensitive reporter gene. The cells are treated with IL-1β in the presence or absence of Ilantide, and the activation of NF-κB is measured by the production of secreted embryonic alkaline phosphatase (SEAP).

#### TNF-α Secretion Assay

The inhibition of TNF-α secretion by Ilantide is evaluated using a murine alveolar macrophage cell line, AMJ2-C8. The cells are pretreated with Ilantide and then stimulated with IL-1β. The conditioned medium is collected and used to treat L929 cells, which are sensitive to TNF-α in the presence of actinomycin D. The amount of TNF-α is determined by ELISA.

#### Neuronal Survival and Neuritogenesis

The effects of Ilantide on neuronal survival and neuritogenesis are assessed using cerebellar granule neurons (CGNs) from 7-day-old rats. The neurons are treated with Ilantide, IL-1Ra, or IL-1β, and cell survival and neurite outgrowth are measured.

#### Pancreatic Islet Protection

The protective effect of Ilantide on pancreatic islets is evaluated by treating neonatal rat islets with IL-1β in the presence or absence of Ilantide. The islets are cultured, and the extent of apoptosis and nitric oxide production is measured.

### Pharmaceutical Formulation

Pharmaceutical formulations of Ilantide can be prepared in various forms, including solutions, suspensions, and lyophilized powders. The formulations may include pharmaceutically acceptable excipients, such as buffers, stabilizers, and preservatives. The formulations are designed for subcutaneous, intravenous, or intranasal administration.

### Dosage

The dosage of Ilantide for therapeutic use depends on the specific condition being treated and the route of administration. For example, in the treatment of collagen-induced arthritis (CIA), a dose of 10 mg/kg administered subcutaneously is effective. For neuroinflammation, a similar dose is used, with the advantage of Ilantide's ability to cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB).

## EXAMPLES

### Example 1

**Binding Analysis of Ilantide to IL-1RI**

Ilantide, IL-1β, and IL-1Ra were immobilized on a CM4 sensor chip using a Biacore 2000 instrument. The recombinant ectodomain of IL-1RI was injected, and the binding kinetics were analyzed. Ilantide bound to IL-1RI with an affinity comparable to IL-1Ra and higher than IL-1β.

### Example 2

**Inhibition of NF-κB Activation by Ilantide**

HEK-Blue IL-1β cells were treated with 1.2 pM IL-1β in the presence or absence of various concentrations of Ilantide. The activation of NF-κB was measured by the production of SEAP. Ilantide dose-dependently inhibited NF-κB activation induced by IL-1β.

### Example 3

**Inhibition of TNF-α Secretion by Ilantide**

AMJ2-C8 macrophages were pretreated with Ilantide and then stimulated with 1.2 pM IL-1β. The conditioned medium was collected and used to treat L929 cells in the presence of actinomycin D. The amount of TNF-α was determined by ELISA. Ilantide significantly inhibited IL-1β-induced TNF-α secretion.

### Example 4

**Promotion of Neuronal Survival by Ilantide**

Cerebellar granule neurons (CGNs) from 7-day-old rats were treated with Ilantide, IL-1Ra, or IL-1β. Cell survival was measured by a cell death detection ELISA. Ilantide and IL-1Ra concentration-dependently promoted CGN survival.

### Example 5

**Stimulation of Neuritogenesis by Ilantide**

CGNs were grown on fibroblast cells in the presence of Ilantide, IL-1Ra, or IL-1β. Neurite outgrowth was measured. Ilantide and IL-1Ra significantly induced neurite outgrowth in a dose-dependent manner.

### Example 6

**Protection of Pancreatic Islets by Ilantide**

Neonatal rat islets were treated with 150 pg/ml IL-1β in the presence or absence of Ilantide. The islets were cultured, and the extent of apoptosis and nitric oxide production was measured. Ilantide significantly reduced IL-1β-induced apoptosis and nitric oxide production.

### Example 7

**Pharmacokinetics of Ilantide**

Biotinylated Ilantide-t was administered subcutaneously to adult male Wistar rats at a dose of 10 mg/kg. Blood samples and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) were collected at various time points, and the concentrations of Ilantide-t were measured by ELISA. Ilantide-t was detected in plasma 20 minutes after administration and remained detectable for up to 30 hours. The peptide concentration in CSF was 125 ng/ml 30 minutes after systemic administration.

### Example 8

**Reduction of Inflammation in CIA Model**

Collagen-induced arthritis (CIA) was induced in rats, and the animals were treated with Ilantide (10 mg/kg) or vehicle starting on day 8 post-immunization. The clinical signs of arthritis were evaluated daily. Ilantide significantly attenuated the severity of CIA compared to the vehicle-treated group.

### Example 9

**Amelioration of LPS-Induced Behavioral Changes**

Rats were intraperitoneally injected with LPS (250 μg/kg) and immediately treated with Ilantide (10 mg/kg) or vehicle. The duration of social interaction was measured at various time points. Ilantide significantly ameliorated LPS-induced social depression and increased the plasma levels of the anti-inflammatory cytokine IL-10.

### Example 10

**Delay of EAE Development**

Experimental autoimmune encephalomyelitis (EAE) was induced in rats by injecting myelin basic protein (MBP) in complete Freund's adjuvant (CFA). The animals were treated with Ilantide (10 mg/kg) or vehicle starting on day 10 post-immunization. The clinical signs of EAE were evaluated daily. Ilantide significantly delayed the development of EAE compared to the vehicle-treated group.

### Example 11

**Rescue of Aβ-Induced Social Memory Deficits**

Rats were intracerebroventricularly injected with aggregated Aβ25–35 (15 μg) and treated with Ilantide (10 mg/kg) or vehicle. The social recognition test was conducted to evaluate social memory. Ilantide significantly ameliorated Aβ-induced social memory deficits.

### Example 12

**Comparison with Anakinra**

The effects of Ilantide were compared with those of Anakinra (Ana) in various in vitro and in vivo models. Ilantide was found to be more effective in inhibiting IL-1-induced apoptosis in pancreatic islets and in reducing inflammation in CIA and EAE models, despite being administered at a lower dose.
