# DESCRIPTION

## MR-TOF with Frequent Pulsing

The invention pertains to a novel method and apparatus for enhancing the throughput and sensitivity of Multi-Reflecting Time-of-Flight Mass Spectrometry (MR-TOFMS) through the use of frequent pulsing. Traditional MR-TOFMS systems suffer from low duty cycles due to the long flight times required for high-resolution analysis. This limitation is addressed by implementing a method of frequent pulsing of the orthogonal accelerator (OA) with unique time intervals, known as Encoded Frequent Pulsing (EFP). The EFP method significantly improves the duty cycle of the MR-TOFMS, thereby increasing the sensitivity and dynamic range of the instrument.

In the EFP method, the orthogonal accelerator is pulsed at a high frequency, typically in the range of 100 kHz, with unique time intervals between pulses. This ensures that the ion packets do not systematically overlap, allowing for the efficient collection and decoding of the resulting multiplexed spectra. The decoding algorithm utilizes the known pulse intervals to demultiplex the spectra, effectively reconstructing the individual mass spectra. This method overcomes the limitations of traditional single-pulse methods, which are constrained by the long flight times and low duty cycles inherent in high-resolution MR-TOFMS.

The EFP method is particularly advantageous for analyzing complex mixtures, where the detection of trace analytes is crucial. By improving the duty cycle and dynamic range, the EFP method enables the detection and quantification of minor species within a rich matrix, making it highly suitable for applications in proteomics, metabolomics, and environmental analysis.

## Comprehensive MS-MS (C-MS-MS)

The invention further includes a method for comprehensive tandem mass spectrometry (C-MS-MS) using a combination of a quadrupole mass filter and a MR-TOFMS. The C-MS-MS method aims to provide all-mass MS-MS analysis with improved sensitivity and dynamic range. In this configuration, the quadrupole mass filter is smoothly scanned with a wide mass window (typically 10-30 amu) to reduce scan losses and improve the duty cycle of the parent ion separation stage. The wide mass window allows for the simultaneous analysis of multiple parent ions, which are then fragmented and analyzed by the MR-TOFMS.

The EFP method is employed to track the time profiles of the parent and fragment ions, enabling the accurate reconstruction of fragment spectra and the assignment of parent ions with high precision. This method provides a significant improvement in sensitivity and dynamic range compared to traditional MS-MS methods, which typically use narrow mass windows and suffer from scan losses. The C-MS-MS method is particularly useful for data-independent acquisition (DIA) in proteomics and metabolomics, where the comprehensive analysis of complex mixtures is essential.

## Parallel Mass Separators

The invention also encompasses the use of parallel mass separators to further enhance the throughput and sensitivity of MS-MS analysis. One embodiment involves the use of an array of linear ion traps for lossless parent ion separation. The trap array is designed to handle high ion fluxes, typically up to 10^9 ions/sec, and provides fast and lossless separation of parent ions. Each trap in the array is capable of mass-selective ejection at a moderate resolution (30-50), allowing for the simultaneous analysis of multiple parent ions.

The trap array is coupled with a MR-TOFMS, which employs the EFP method to improve the duty cycle and dynamic range. The combination of the trap array and MR-TOFMS provides a highly efficient and sensitive platform for all-mass MS-MS analysis. The trap array method is particularly advantageous for applications requiring high throughput and sensitivity, such as clinical diagnostics and proteomics.

Another embodiment involves the use of multiple parallel MR-TOFMS analyzers to further enhance the throughput. The MR-TOFMS array can be constructed by making parallel slots in plane electrodes or radial slots in cylindrical electrodes, allowing the analyzers to share the same vacuum chamber and high-voltage power supplies. This configuration reduces the cost and complexity of the system while significantly increasing the charge throughput and dynamic range. The MR-TOFMS array is particularly suitable for high-throughput applications, such as clinical studies and proteomics.

## Resistive Ion Guides

The invention includes the use of resistive ion guides to improve the performance of the MR-TOFMS. Resistive ion guides are designed to minimize space charge effects and improve the transmission of ions through the mass spectrometer. Unlike traditional ion guides, which rely on radio-frequency (RF) fields, resistive ion guides use a combination of resistive elements and electrostatic fields to guide and focus the ions. This design reduces the space charge repulsion between ions, leading to improved ion transmission and reduced spectral overlaps.

The resistive ion guides are particularly useful in high-flux applications, where the ion density can be very high. By minimizing space charge effects, the resistive ion guides ensure that the ions are efficiently transmitted to the MR-TOFMS, thereby improving the sensitivity and dynamic range of the instrument. The use of resistive ion guides is particularly advantageous in the context of the EFP method, where the high-frequency pulsing of the orthogonal accelerator can lead to significant space charge effects.

## TOF Detectors

The invention also includes the use of advanced Time-of-Flight (TOF) detectors to improve the performance of the MR-TOFMS. The TOF detectors are designed to handle high ion fluxes and provide high-resolution mass spectra with excellent dynamic range. The detectors are capable of accurately measuring the arrival times of ions with sub-nanosecond precision, which is crucial for the EFP method and the analysis of complex mixtures.

One embodiment of the TOF detector uses a microchannel plate (MCP) coupled with a fast digitizer. The MCP amplifies the ion signal, and the fast digitizer records the arrival times of the ions with high precision. The use of a fast digitizer allows for the efficient collection and processing of the multiplexed spectra generated by the EFP method. Another embodiment involves the use of a time-to-digital converter (TDC) with high resolution and dynamic range. The TDC is capable of accurately measuring the arrival times of ions over a wide range of intensities, making it suitable for the analysis of complex mixtures with varying ion densities.

The advanced TOF detectors are essential for the successful implementation of the EFP method and the C-MS-MS method, as they ensure that the multiplexed spectra are accurately recorded and decoded. The use of these detectors significantly improves the sensitivity and dynamic range of the MR-TOFMS, making it highly suitable for a wide range of applications in analytical chemistry.

## Data System

The invention includes a comprehensive data system for the acquisition, processing, and analysis of the multiplexed spectra generated by the EFP method and the C-MS-MS method. The data system is designed to handle the high data rates and large data volumes associated with the EFP method and the C-MS-MS method. The data system includes a high-speed data acquisition module, a data processing module, and a data analysis module.

The high-speed data acquisition module is responsible for collecting the raw data from the TOF detectors. The module is capable of handling data rates of up to 100 MHz and storing the data in a high-capacity memory. The data processing module is responsible for demultiplexing the multiplexed spectra using the known pulse intervals. The module employs advanced algorithms to accurately reconstruct the individual mass spectra and assign the parent ions with high precision. The data analysis module is responsible for interpreting the mass spectra and extracting the relevant information, such as the identities and quantities of the detected analytes.

The data system is designed to be user-friendly and efficient, allowing for the rapid and accurate analysis of complex mixtures. The system is particularly useful for applications in proteomics, metabolomics, and environmental analysis, where the analysis of large and complex datasets is essential.

## Conclusion

The invention provides a novel method and apparatus for enhancing the throughput and sensitivity of MR-TOFMS through the use of frequent pulsing, comprehensive MS-MS, parallel mass separators, resistive ion guides, advanced TOF detectors, and a comprehensive data system. The EFP method significantly improves the duty cycle and dynamic range of the MR-TOFMS, making it highly suitable for the analysis of complex mixtures. The C-MS-MS method provides all-mass MS-MS analysis with improved sensitivity and dynamic range, while the use of parallel mass separators, resistive ion guides, and advanced TOF detectors further enhances the performance of the MR-TOFMS. The comprehensive data system ensures that the multiplexed spectra are accurately recorded, processed, and analyzed, making the invention highly suitable for a wide range of applications in analytical chemistry.
