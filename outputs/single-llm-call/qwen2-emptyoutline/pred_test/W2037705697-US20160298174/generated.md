# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD OF THE PRESENT INVENTION

The present invention relates to the field of molecular biology and biotechnology, specifically to the development and use of highly selective DNA polymerase variants. These variants are particularly useful in allele-specific amplification (ASA), human leukocyte antigen (HLA) typing, and methylation-specific PCR (MSP). The invention provides a novel KlenTaq DNA polymerase mutant, designated R660V, which exhibits enhanced mismatch extension selectivity, making it highly suitable for various diagnostic and research applications.

## BACKGROUND OF THE PRESENT INVENTION

Single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) are the most abundant type of genetic variation found between members of a species. SNPs can lead to structural and functional changes in proteins, enzymes, or receptors, and are often used as markers for determining parentage, anthropology, and forensic tasks. They are also crucial for pharmacogenomics, as they can affect predispositions for diseases or responses to drugs, chemicals, and vaccines. The human genome project and subsequent initiatives, such as the HapMap and 1000 Genomes projects, have identified millions of SNPs, creating a foundation for personalized medicine.

Various techniques are used for SNP analysis, including selective primer extension, pyrosequencing, and allele-specific amplification (ASA). ASA, in particular, is a cost-effective method that does not require preliminary amplification of the target genetic material. It combines amplification and detection in a single reaction, based on the discrimination of matched and mismatched primer/template complexes. The efficiency of ASA depends on the selectivity of the DNA polymerase used, with highly selective enzymes being essential for accurate SNP detection.

In transfusion medicine, SNP detection is also of critical importance, especially for human leukocyte antigen (HLA) typing. HLA genes exhibit extensive genetic diversity, and PCR-based methods have become the standard for HLA typing. Sequence-specific primed PCR (PCR-SSP) is widely used for HLA class I and class II typing, where the presence or absence of PCR products determines the HLA type. High selectivity of the DNA polymerase is crucial for obtaining high-quality HLA typing results.

Methylation-specific PCR (MSP) is another technique that benefits from highly selective DNA polymerases. MSP is used to analyze DNA methylation patterns, which are important epigenetic marks involved in gene regulation and disease onset. Bisulfite treatment of genomic DNA converts unmethylated cytosines to uracils, while methylated cytosines remain unchanged. MSP uses primer pairs designed to amplify either methylated or unmethylated DNA, and the selectivity of the DNA polymerase is crucial for accurate methylation analysis.

Several DNA polymerase variants with increased DNA replication fidelity have been developed, but there remains a need for enzymes with enhanced mismatch extension selectivity. The present invention addresses this need by providing a KlenTaq DNA polymerase mutant, R660V, which exhibits significantly increased mismatch extension selectivity, making it highly suitable for ASA, HLA typing, and MSP.
