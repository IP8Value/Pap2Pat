# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to the field of therapeutic antibodies, specifically to engineered IgG1 antibodies with reduced effector functions while maintaining favorable biophysical properties. These antibodies are designed to minimize adverse effects such as inflammation, tissue damage, and cytotoxicity, making them suitable for a wide range of therapeutic applications, including cancer, autoimmune disorders, and infectious diseases.

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Therapeutic antibodies have revolutionized the treatment of various diseases due to their high specificity, long serum half-life, and ability to trigger immune responses. However, the effector functions of these antibodies, such as antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity (ADCC) and complement-dependent cytotoxicity (CDC), can also cause unintended adverse effects. To address this, there has been significant interest in developing antibodies with reduced effector functions, a process known as "effector function modulation" or "silencing."

Effector function modulation is typically achieved through modifications in the Fc region of the antibody, alterations in glycosylation patterns, and engineering of the antibody structure. These modifications can significantly impact the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of antibodies, including their serum half-life, clearance, and tissue distribution. Reducing effector function in IgG1 antibodies has the potential to enhance their safety and efficacy in the clinical setting.

Human immune cells express four activating Fcγ receptors (FcγRs): FcγRI (CD64), FcγRIIa (CD32a), FcγRIIc (CD32c), and FcγRIIIa (CD16a). These receptors mediate ADCC and antibody-dependent cell-mediated phagocytosis (ADCP). The neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) is responsible for IgG recycling, contributing to a long half-life. The Fc region also engages the complement system, leading to cell lysis through the activation of the C1 complex.

IgG subclasses differ in their binding affinity to FcγRs and complement. IgG2 and IgG4 have been used to reduce Fc effector function, but they exhibit increased sensitivity to acid-induced aggregation and lower melting temperatures, which can compromise their stability and manufacturability. Therefore, there is a need for IgG1 antibodies with reduced effector functions that maintain favorable biophysical properties.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides engineered IgG1 antibodies with reduced effector functions while retaining favorable biophysical properties. Specifically, the invention encompasses IgG1 antibodies with mutations in the lower hinge and CH2 regions that eliminate binding to activating FcγRs and C1q, while preserving binding to FcRn and Protein A (ProA).

The invention includes the following key features:
1. **Mutations in the Lower Hinge Region**: The lower hinge region of the IgG1 antibody is modified with mutations such as L234A/L235A (LALA), L234F/L235E (LFLE), and L234A/L235E (LALE). These mutations reduce binding to FcγRs and C1q.
2. **Mutations in the CH2 Region**: The CH2 region is further modified with mutations such as D265A, S267K, P329G, and P329A. These mutations, when combined with the lower hinge mutations, completely eliminate binding to FcγRs and C1q while retaining binding to FcRn and ProA.
3. **Biophysical Properties**: The engineered antibodies exhibit biophysical properties similar to wild-type (WT) IgG1, including resistance to acid-induced aggregation and stable melting temperatures.

The invention also provides methods for producing and characterizing these engineered antibodies, including polyspecificity reagent (PSR) binding assays, affinity-capture self-interaction nanoparticle spectroscopy (AC-SINS), hydrophobic interaction chromatography (HIC), size exclusion chromatography (SEC), pH 3.5 stress SEC, and thermal melting (Tm) measurements by differential scanning fluorescence (DSF).

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

### Engineering of IgG1 Antibodies

The present invention involves the engineering of IgG1 antibodies to reduce effector functions while maintaining favorable biophysical properties. The key modifications are introduced in the lower hinge and CH2 regions of the antibody.

#### Lower Hinge Mutations

The lower hinge region of the IgG1 antibody is modified with the following mutations:
- **L234A/L235A (LALA)**: This mutation reduces binding to FcγRs and C1q.
- **L234F/L235E (LFLE)**: This mutation also reduces binding to FcγRs and C1q.
- **L234A/L235E (LALE)**: This mutation further reduces binding to FcγRs and C1q.

These mutations are derived from literature, clinical, and marketed molecules and have been shown to effectively reduce effector functions.

#### CH2 Mutations

The CH2 region of the IgG1 antibody is further modified with the following mutations:
- **D265A**: This mutation eliminates binding to C1q and reduces binding to FcγRs.
- **S267K**: This mutation enhances stability and reduces binding to FcγRs.
- **P329G**: This mutation eliminates binding to FcγRs and C1q.
- **P329A**: This mutation also eliminates binding to FcγRs and C1q.

When combined with the lower hinge mutations, these CH2 mutations completely eliminate binding to FcγRs and C1q while retaining binding to FcRn and ProA.

### Production and Characterization of Engineered Antibodies

The engineered antibodies are produced using standard recombinant DNA technology. The modified genes encoding the heavy and light chains of the antibodies are cloned into expression vectors and transfected into host cells, such as HEK293 cells. The antibodies are then purified using Protein A affinity chromatography.

#### Polyspecificity Reagent (PSR) Binding Assay

The PSR binding assay is used to assess the binding of the engineered antibodies to a panel of soluble membrane proteins (SMPs) and soluble cytosolic proteins (SCPs). The assay is performed using flow cytometry, and the results are expressed as a normalized binding score.

#### Affinity-Capture Self-Interaction Nanoparticle Spectroscopy (AC-SINS)

AC-SINS is used to assess the self-interaction propensity of the engineered antibodies. Gold nanoparticles coated with anti-human IgG Fc are incubated with the antibodies, and the wavelength shift is measured to infer self-interaction.

#### Hydrophobic Interaction Chromatography (HIC)

HIC is used to characterize the hydrophobicity of the engineered antibodies. The antibodies are analyzed using a Sepax Proteomix HIC butyl-NP5 column, and the results are expressed as retention time.

#### Size Exclusion Chromatography (SEC)

SEC is used to characterize the purity and quality of the engineered antibodies. The antibodies are analyzed using a TSKgel SuperSW mAb HTP column, and the results are expressed as monomer content.

#### pH 3.5 Stress SEC

The pH 3.5 stress SEC assay is used to assess the stability of the engineered antibodies under acidic conditions. The antibodies are incubated at pH 3.5 for 1 hour, and the results are expressed as the change in monomer content.

#### Thermal Melting (Tm) Measurements by Differential Scanning Fluorescence (DSF)

DSF is used to measure the melting temperature (Tm) of the engineered antibodies. The Tm is determined by monitoring the fluorescence of SYPRO orange as the temperature is increased.

### Binding Assays

#### Binding to FcγRs and C1q

The binding of the engineered antibodies to FcγRs and C1q is assessed using biolayer interferometry (BLI) and surface plasmon resonance (SPR) techniques. The antibodies are captured on sensor chips, and the binding kinetics are measured by injecting solutions of FcγRs and C1q.

#### Binding to FcRn and ProA

The binding of the engineered antibodies to FcRn and ProA is also assessed using BLI and SPR techniques. The antibodies are captured on sensor chips, and the binding kinetics are measured by injecting solutions of FcRn and ProA.

### Examples

#### Example 1: Antibodies

The present invention encompasses engineered IgG1 antibodies with the following mutations:
- **LALA-P329G**: L234A/L235A in the lower hinge region and P329G in the CH2 region.
- **LALE-P329G**: L234A/L235E in the lower hinge region and P329G in the CH2 region.
- **LALA-S267K**: L234A/L235A in the lower hinge region and S267K in the CH2 region.
- **LALE-S267K**: L234A/L235E in the lower hinge region and S267K in the CH2 region.

These antibodies exhibit reduced effector functions while maintaining favorable biophysical properties.

#### Example 2: THP-1 Cell Binding and C1q Binding to Antibodies

The binding of the engineered antibodies to THP-1 cells and C1q was assessed using flow cytometry and FACS-based assays. The results showed that the engineered antibodies with LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G, LALA-S267K, and LALE-S267K mutations completely eliminated binding to FcγRs and C1q under the conditions of the assays.

#### Example 3: FcγR Binding by Antibodies

The binding of the engineered antibodies to FcγRs was further assessed using BLI and SPR techniques. The results confirmed that the engineered antibodies with LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G, LALA-S267K, and LALE-S267K mutations completely eliminated binding to FcγRs.

#### Example 4: Developability Assays

The biophysical properties of the engineered antibodies were assessed using PSR binding assays, AC-SINS, HIC, SEC, pH 3.5 stress SEC, and DSF. The results showed that the engineered antibodies with LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G, LALA-S267K, and LALE-S267K mutations exhibited biophysical properties similar to WT IgG1, including resistance to acid-induced aggregation and stable melting temperatures.

#### Example 5: ADCC Activity Assay

The ADCC activity of the engineered antibodies was assessed using a standard ADCC assay. The results showed that the engineered antibodies with LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G, LALA-S267K, and LALE-S267K mutations had significantly reduced ADCC activity compared to WT IgG1.

#### Example 6: Complement Activity Assay

The complement activity of the engineered antibodies was assessed using a standard complement activity assay. The results showed that the engineered antibodies with LALA-P329G, LALE-P329G, LALA-S267K, and LALE-S267K mutations had significantly reduced complement activity compared to WT IgG1.
