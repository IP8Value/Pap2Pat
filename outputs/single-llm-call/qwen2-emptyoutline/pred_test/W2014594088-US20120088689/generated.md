# DESCRIPTION

## DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to the use of versican isoforms V0 and V1 as biomarkers for predicting the progression of chronic kidney disease (CKD). Specifically, the invention provides methods and compositions for detecting and quantifying the expression levels of versican isoforms V0 and V1 in kidney tissue, which can be used to predict the likelihood of a progressive decline in renal function. The invention further includes the use of these biomarkers in the development of diagnostic kits and therapeutic strategies for managing CKD.

Versican is an extracellular matrix (ECM) protein belonging to the family of hyaluronan-binding proteoglycans. Four splice variants of human versican have been identified (V0, V1, V2, and V3), which result from alternative splicing of the two central exons 7 and 8 encoding the central glycosaminoglycan carrying regions, glycosaminoglycan alpha (GAG-α) and beta (GAG-β). Versican is involved in cell proliferation, cell adhesion, migration, and extracellular matrix assembly. The expression of versican in smooth muscle cells is induced by various mitogens such as transforming growth factor beta-1 (TGF-β1), platelet-derived growth factor (PDGF), epidermal growth factor (EGF), basic fibroblast growth factor (bFGF), and interleukin 1 beta (IL-1β) via phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase–protein kinase B signaling, and is regulated by downstream transcription factors such as cAMP-responsive element binding protein (CREB), nuclear factor-kB (NF-κB), and p53.

In the context of CKD, the expression of versican isoforms V0 and V1 has been shown to correlate with the degree of histological damage, serum creatinine levels, and the progression of renal function decline. This correlation suggests that versican V0 and V1 can serve as valuable biomarkers for predicting the progression of CKD. The invention provides methods for detecting and quantifying the expression levels of versican V0 and V1 in kidney tissue, which can be used to identify patients at risk of progressive renal failure and to guide therapeutic interventions.

## EXAMPLES

### Example 1

#### Detection of Versican Isoforms V0 and V1 in Kidney Biopsies

In this example, the expression levels of versican isoforms V0 and V1 were measured in kidney biopsies from patients with various proteinuric renal diseases. The study included 74 patients with diagnoses including focal segmental glomerulosclerosis (FSGS), minimal change disease (MCD), IgA nephropathy (IGAN), lupus nephritis (LN), rapid-progressive glomerulonephritis (RPGN), membranoproliferative glomerulonephritis (MPGN), ANCA-associated vasculitis (AAV), hypertensive nephropathy (HTN), membranous nephropathy (MN), Goodpasture syndrome, interstitial nephritis (IN), and other conditions.

RNA was isolated from the kidney biopsies using the RNeasy® Micro Kit (Qiagen, Hilden, Germany). The RNA was reverse-transcribed into cDNA using the High Capacity cDNA Reverse Transcription Kit (Applied Biosystems, Carlsbad, CA). The cDNA was preamplified using TaqMan® Gene Expression Assays and the TaqMan® PreAmp Master Mix. The preamplified cDNA was analyzed in duplicates on the 7500 Fast Real-Time PCR System (Applied Biosystems, Carlsbad, CA) using the following inventoried TaqMan® Gene Expression Assays: PPIA (cyclophilin A; Hs99999904_m1), VCAN0 (Hs01007944_m1), VCAN1 (Hs01007937_m1), VCAN2 (Hs01007943_m1), and VCAN3 (Hs01007941_m1). The relative amounts of transcripts for each gene were normalized to the reference gene cyclophilin A (PPIA).

The results showed that versican isoforms V0 and V1 were significantly upregulated in patients with progressive disease compared to those with stable disease. Specifically, V0 and V1 expression levels were 1.56-fold and 1.67-fold higher, respectively, in the progressive group (p = 0.034 and p = 0.011, respectively). These findings indicate that versican V0 and V1 can serve as biomarkers for predicting the progression of CKD.

### Example 2

#### Correlation of Versican Isoform Expression with Clinical Parameters

In this example, the correlation between the expression levels of versican isoforms V0 and V1 and various clinical parameters was analyzed. The study included the same cohort of 74 patients as described in Example 1. The clinical parameters included age, creatinine, estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR), and proteinuria at the time of biopsy and follow-up.

The Pearson correlation coefficient was used to analyze the correlations. The results showed that versican V0 and V1 levels significantly correlated with serum creatinine at follow-up (V0 R = 0.337, p = 0.003; V1 R = 0.387, p = 0.001). However, no significant correlations were found with eGFR or proteinuria. These findings suggest that versican V0 and V1 are more closely associated with the decline in renal function over time rather than with baseline renal function or proteinuria.

### Discussion

The results from Examples 1 and 2 demonstrate that versican isoforms V0 and V1 are valuable biomarkers for predicting the progression of CKD. The upregulation of these isoforms in patients with progressive disease and their correlation with serum creatinine at follow-up indicate their potential utility in identifying patients at risk of renal function decline. This information can be used to guide therapeutic interventions and improve patient outcomes.

### Example 3

#### Versican Expression in Animal Models of Renal Disease

In this example, the expression of versican was analyzed in three rodent models of renal disease: accelerated anti-glomerular basement membrane (GBM) nephritis, passive Heymann nephritis (PHN), and adriamycin nephropathy. The expression of versican was measured by real-time PCR using the inventoried TaqMan Gene Expression Assays for mouse versican (Mm01283063_m1), mouse 18s ribosomal RNA (Mm03928990_g1), rat versican (Rn01493755_m1), and rat cyclophilin A (Rn00690933_m1).

The results showed that versican expression was significantly upregulated in all three models of renal disease compared to control animals. Specifically, versican was upregulated 3.5-fold in mice with accelerated nephrotoxic serum nephritis, 1.5-fold in rats with adriamycin-induced nephropathy, and 8.0-fold in rats with PHN. These findings support the role of versican in the progression of renal disease and validate the use of versican V0 and V1 as biomarkers in human CKD.

### Example 4

#### Effect of TGF-β1 on Versican Expression in Fibroblasts

In this example, the effect of transforming growth factor beta-1 (TGF-β1) on the expression of versican isoforms V0 and V1 was investigated in human renal proximal tubule cells (HK2) and human kidney fibroblast cell line TK-173. The cells were cultured in the presence of TGF-β1 (10 ng/ml) for 24 hours, and the expression of versican isoforms was measured by real-time PCR.

The results showed that TGF-β1 significantly upregulated the expression of V0 and V1 by approximately 3.5-fold in the fibroblast cell line TK-173. However, no significant effect of TGF-β1 on versican isoform expression was observed in the proximal tubule cell line HK2. These findings suggest that TGF-β1 may play a role in the upregulation of versican in fibroblasts, which could contribute to the progression of renal fibrosis and CKD.

## DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides methods and compositions for detecting and quantifying the expression levels of versican isoforms V0 and V1 in kidney tissue. These methods can be used to predict the likelihood of a progressive decline in renal function in patients with CKD. The invention further includes the use of these biomarkers in the development of diagnostic kits and therapeutic strategies for managing CKD.

The methods of the invention involve isolating RNA from kidney tissue, reverse-transcribing the RNA into cDNA, and quantifying the expression levels of versican isoforms V0 and V1 using real-time PCR. The expression levels of versican V0 and V1 can be normalized to a reference gene, such as cyclophilin A (PPIA), to account for variations in RNA quantity and quality.

The invention also provides diagnostic kits for detecting and quantifying the expression levels of versican V0 and V1 in kidney tissue. The kits may include reagents for RNA isolation, reverse transcription, and real-time PCR, as well as primers and probes specific for versican V0 and V1.

The invention further includes therapeutic strategies for managing CKD based on the expression levels of versican V0 and V1. For example, patients with high expression levels of versican V0 and V1 may benefit from more aggressive therapeutic interventions, such as the use of antifibrotic agents or more frequent monitoring of renal function.

In summary, the present invention provides valuable tools for predicting the progression of CKD and improving patient outcomes. The use of versican V0 and V1 as biomarkers can help identify patients at risk of renal function decline and guide therapeutic interventions.
