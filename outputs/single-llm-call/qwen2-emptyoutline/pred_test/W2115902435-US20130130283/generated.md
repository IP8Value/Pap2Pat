# DESCRIPTION

## EXAMPLE 1

### Secretion ELISA for Detecting the Effector Protein SptP when Bound to its Cognate Chaperone SicP

The present invention relates to a method for detecting the effector protein SptP when bound to its cognate chaperone SicP using a secretion enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA). The method involves the use of a specific antibody that recognizes the SptP-SicP complex and a detection system that quantifies the amount of the complex present in a sample. This method is particularly useful for studying the interactions between effector proteins and their chaperones in the context of type III secretion systems (T3SS) in bacterial pathogens such as Salmonella enterica serovar typhimurium (S. typhimurium).

The invention provides a detailed protocol for performing the secretion ELISA, including the preparation of samples, the coating of microtiter plates with the specific antibody, the addition of the sample containing the SptP-SicP complex, and the detection of the complex using a secondary antibody conjugated to an enzyme. The enzyme catalyzes a reaction that produces a color change, which can be quantified using a spectrophotometer. The method is sensitive and specific, allowing for the detection of even small amounts of the SptP-SicP complex in a sample.

The invention also includes a description of the reagents and materials required for the assay, as well as the conditions under which the assay should be performed to ensure optimal results. The method can be used in both research and diagnostic settings to study the regulation of effector protein secretion and the role of chaperones in this process.

## EXAMPLE 2

### Structure ELISA to Detect the Attachment of PrgI to the PgH-Containing Base Element

The present invention further relates to a method for detecting the attachment of the needle protein PrgI to the base element of the type III secretion system (T3SS) using a structure enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA). The base element, primarily composed of the proteins PrgH and PrgK, is a crucial component of the T3SS that anchors the needle complex to the bacterial envelope. The method involves the use of a specific antibody that recognizes the PrgI-PgH complex and a detection system that quantifies the amount of the complex present in a sample.

The invention provides a detailed protocol for performing the structure ELISA, including the preparation of samples, the coating of microtiter plates with the specific antibody, the addition of the sample containing the PrgI-PgH complex, and the detection of the complex using a secondary antibody conjugated to an enzyme. The enzyme catalyzes a reaction that produces a color change, which can be quantified using a spectrophotometer. The method is sensitive and specific, allowing for the detection of even small amounts of the PrgI-PgH complex in a sample.

The invention also includes a description of the reagents and materials required for the assay, as well as the conditions under which the assay should be performed to ensure optimal results. The method can be used in both research and diagnostic settings to study the assembly and stability of the T3SS needle complex, particularly the interaction between the needle protein PrgI and the base element proteins PrgH and PrgK.

## EXAMPLE 3

### Immunodetection of the Translocators SipB and SipC (Western Blot)

The present invention further encompasses a method for immunodetecting the translocator proteins SipB and SipC using a western blot technique. The translocator proteins SipB and SipC are essential components of the type III secretion system (T3SS) in Salmonella enterica serovar typhimurium (S. typhimurium) and play a critical role in the translocation of effector proteins into host cells. The method involves the use of specific antibodies that recognize SipB and SipC and a detection system that visualizes the presence of these proteins in a sample.

The invention provides a detailed protocol for performing the western blot, including the preparation of protein samples, the separation of proteins by sodium dodecyl sulfate-polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE), the transfer of proteins to a nitrocellulose membrane, the blocking of non-specific binding sites, the incubation with primary antibodies specific for SipB and SipC, and the detection of the proteins using a secondary antibody conjugated to a chemiluminescent or colorimetric substrate. The method is sensitive and specific, allowing for the detection of even low levels of SipB and SipC in a sample.

The invention also includes a description of the reagents and materials required for the western blot, as well as the conditions under which the assay should be performed to ensure optimal results. The method can be used in both research and diagnostic settings to study the expression and secretion of translocator proteins in the context of T3SS function and bacterial pathogenesis.
