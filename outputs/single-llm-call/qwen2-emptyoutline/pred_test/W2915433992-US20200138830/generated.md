# DESCRIPTION

## BACKGROUND

Type 1 diabetes (T1D) is a chronic autoimmune disorder characterized by the destruction of pancreatic β-cells, leading to a deficiency in insulin production. Despite significant advancements in interventive therapies, preserving insulin production over the long term remains a significant challenge. Current approaches often focus on controlling autoimmunity and increasing β-cell mass, highlighting the need for combination treatments. GABA receptors (GABA-Rs), particularly GABAA receptors (GABAA-Rs), have emerged as promising targets for next-generation T1D therapies due to their dual effects on islet cells and immune cells.

GABAA-Rs are ligand-gated ion channels that mediate fast inhibitory neurotransmission in the central nervous system (CNS). However, they are also expressed in peripheral tissues, including pancreatic β-cells and immune cells. Activation of GABAA-Rs in β-cells promotes survival, replication, and mass, while in immune cells, it inhibits inflammatory responses. This dual functionality makes GABAA-Rs a compelling target for developing novel therapeutic strategies for T1D.

GABAA receptor positive allosteric modulators (PAMs) are compounds that enhance the action of GABA when it binds to GABAA-Rs. Unlike direct agonists, PAMs do not bind to the GABA-binding site but instead interact with other sites on the receptor, increasing chloride conductance. This property allows PAMs to potentiate the effects of endogenous GABA, making them particularly useful in contexts where GABA levels are suboptimal. One such PAM, alprazolam, has been extensively studied for its potential in promoting β-cell survival and replication and inhibiting inflammatory T cell responses.

### Definitions

- **GABAA receptor positive allosteric modulators (PAMs)**: Compounds that enhance the action of GABA when it binds to GABAA receptors, without binding to the GABA-binding site.
- **GABA receptor activating ligands**: Compounds that directly bind to and activate GABA receptors.
- **Therapeutically effective amount**: The amount of a compound sufficient to achieve a desired therapeutic effect in a subject.
- **Subject**: An individual, including humans and animals, who is the recipient of a treatment or therapy.
- **Islet transplantation**: The transfer of pancreatic islets from a donor to a recipient to restore or improve insulin production.
- **T1D**: Type 1 diabetes, an autoimmune disorder characterized by the destruction of pancreatic β-cells.
- **β-cell**: A type of cell found in the pancreas that produces and secretes insulin.

### DETAILED DESCRIPTION

The present invention provides methods and compositions for the treatment and prevention of type 1 diabetes (T1D) using GABAA receptor positive allosteric modulators (PAMs) and, optionally, GABA receptor activating ligands. The invention leverages the dual functionality of GABAA-Rs in promoting β-cell survival and replication and inhibiting inflammatory T cell responses.

#### GABAA Receptor Positive Allosteric Modulators (PAMs)

GABAA-R PAMs are compounds that enhance the action of GABA when it binds to GABAA receptors. These modulators do not bind to the GABA-binding site but instead interact with other sites on the receptor, increasing chloride conductance. This property allows PAMs to potentiate the effects of endogenous GABA, making them particularly useful in contexts where GABA levels are suboptimal. Examples of GABAA-R PAMs include, but are not limited to, alprazolam, diazepam, and midazolam.

#### GABA Receptor Activating Ligands

GABA receptor activating ligands are compounds that directly bind to and activate GABA receptors. These ligands can be used to enhance the therapeutic effects of GABAA-R PAMs. Examples of GABA receptor activating ligands include, but are not limited to, GABA, muscimol, and THIP (gaboxadol).

#### Combined Formulations

The invention encompasses combined formulations of GABAA-R PAMs and GABA receptor activating ligands. These formulations can be administered simultaneously or sequentially to achieve synergistic therapeutic effects. The combined use of a GABAA-R PAM and a GABA receptor activating ligand can reduce the required dosage of each compound, thereby minimizing potential side effects and enhancing patient compliance.

### EXAMPLES

#### Example 1: Reducing Islet Cell Apoptosis Following Islet Transplantation

**Objective**: To evaluate the effect of a GABAA-R PAM (alprazolam) on islet cell apoptosis following islet transplantation.

**Methods**:
- NOD/scid mice were rendered diabetic by injection of streptozotocin (STZ).
- Diabetic mice were implanted with human islets under their kidney capsule.
- Mice were randomized into treatment groups: (1) alprazolam (0.25 mg/kg/day, IP), (2) vehicle (5% DMSO in PBS, IP), (3) oral GABA (6 mg/ml, continuously through their drinking water), or (4) combined alprazolam (0.25 mg/kg/day, IP) and oral GABA.
- After two days of treatment, the engrafted kidneys were harvested, and the percentages of apoptotic islet cells were determined by TUNEL staining.

**Results**:
- Treatment with alprazolam significantly reduced the frequency of apoptotic islet cells compared to the vehicle group.
- Combined treatment with alprazolam and GABA further decreased the percentages of apoptotic islet cells, suggesting a synergistic effect.

#### Example 2: Promoting β-Cell Replication in a Human Islet Xenograft Model

**Objective**: To assess the effect of a GABAA-R PAM (alprazolam) on β-cell replication in a human islet xenograft model.

**Methods**:
- STZ-rendered diabetic NOD/scid mice received human islets under their kidney capsule.
- Mice were randomized to receive: (1) alprazolam (0.25 or 0.75 mg/kg/day, IP), (2) vehicle (5% DMSO in PBS, IP), (3) oral GABA (6 mg/ml, continuously through their drinking water), or (4) combined alprazolam (0.25 or 0.75 mg/kg/day, IP) and GABA.
- All groups received BrdU through their drinking water.
- After ten days of treatment, the kidney tissue sections were subjected to immunofluorescent analysis to determine the percentages of BrdU+insulin+ and Ki67+insulin+ β-cells.

**Results**:
- Administration of alprazolam significantly increased the frequency of β-cell replication, as measured by Ki67 and BrdU staining.
- Combined treatment with alprazolam and GABA further enhanced β-cell replication, suggesting a synergistic effect.

#### Example 3: Inhibiting Inflammatory T Cell Responses In Vitro

**Objective**: To evaluate the effect of a GABAA-R PAM (alprazolam) on inflammatory T cell responses in vitro.

**Methods**:
- C57BL/6 mice were immunized with hen egg lysozyme (HEL) and nine days later, their lymph node mononuclear cells were isolated.
- Mononuclear cells were tested in vitro for T cell recall responses to HEL in the presence or absence of different concentrations of alprazolam and/or GABA.
- Thymidine uptake was measured to determine T cell proliferation.

**Results**:
- GABA alone significantly inhibited HEL-specific T cell proliferation.
- Alprazolam alone did not significantly change T cell proliferation.
- Combined treatment with alprazolam and GABA more effectively inhibited T cell proliferation compared to GABA alone, suggesting a synergistic effect.
