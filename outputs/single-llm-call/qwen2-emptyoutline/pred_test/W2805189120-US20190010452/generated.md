# DESCRIPTION

## TECHNICAL FIELD

The present invention relates to a method for producing cellular structures including hypothalamic tissue, particularly focusing on the differentiation of human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) into arginine vasopressin (AVP) neurons. The invention further encompasses the simultaneous maturation of the hypothalamus and pituitary gland, providing a valuable tool for the study of neuroendocrine disorders and the development of regenerative medicine.

## BACKGROUND ART

The neuroendocrine system, composed of the hypothalamus and pituitary gland, plays essential roles in regulating various physiological processes such as body temperature, reproductive behavior, and food and water intake. Arginine vasopressin (AVP) is synthesized in the magnocellular neurons of the paraventricular nucleus (PVN) and the supraoptic nucleus (SON) of the hypothalamus and is released into the systemic circulation from the posterior pituitary to act on V2 receptors in the kidney to promote the reabsorption of free water. Deficient AVP release leads to a disorder called central diabetes insipidus (CDI), characterized by polyuria and polydipsia. Current treatments for CDI, such as desmopressin, an analogue of vasopressin, have limitations, including the risk of hyponatremia and the potential for life-threatening dehydration in patients with dysfunctional osmoreceptors.

To overcome these challenges, regenerative medicine approaches are warranted. An in vitro model of the human hypothalamus would facilitate the study of its development, investigation of hypothalamic disease pathophysiology, and pharmaceutical screening for new drugs against hypothalamic diseases. Previous attempts to differentiate hypothalamic neurons from human pluripotent stem cells have been limited by low efficiency and the inability to demonstrate AVP secretion. Therefore, there is a need for an improved method to produce functional AVP neurons from hESCs.

## SUMMARY OF INVENTION

### Technical Problem

The technical problem addressed by the present invention is the need for a reliable and efficient method to differentiate human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) into functional arginine vasopressin (AVP) neurons. Additionally, the invention aims to provide a method for the simultaneous maturation of the hypothalamus and pituitary gland, which can be used for the study of neuroendocrine disorders and the development of regenerative medicine.

### Solution to Problem

The present invention provides a method for producing cellular structures including hypothalamic tissue, particularly focusing on the differentiation of human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) into arginine vasopressin (AVP) neurons. The method involves optimizing each step of hypothalamic development with reference to murine embryology, focusing on the generation of rostral and dorsal hypothalamic progenitors.

The method includes the following steps:
1. **Differentiation into Rax+ Hypothalamic Progenitors**: Human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) are cultured in a serum-free floating culture of embryoid body-like aggregates with quick reaggregation (SFEBq) using a growth factor-free chemically defined medium (gfCDM) supplemented with a small amount of knock-out serum replacement (KSR). The culture is treated with bone morphogenetic protein 4 (BMP4) and smoothened agonist (SAG) to induce the formation of Rax+ hypothalamic progenitors.
2. **Specific Induction of Dorsal or Ventral Hypothalamic Progenitors**: The concentration and timing of BMP4 and SAG application are optimized to specifically induce dorsal or ventral hypothalamic progenitors. Dorsal hypothalamic progenitors are characterized by the expression of Rax and Pax6, while ventral hypothalamic progenitors express Rax and Nkx2.1.
3. **Induction of AVP Precursor Cells**: The dorsal hypothalamic progenitors are further cultured in a gas-liquid interphase culture system using a Transwell porous filter in a medium supplemented with glucose, N2, B27, and ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF) to induce the expression of AVP precursor markers such as OTP and BRN2.
4. **Generation of AVP-Secreting Neurons**: The AVP precursor cells are dissociated and cultured in a two-dimensional (2D) culture system on Matrigel, poly-D-lysine, and laminin-coated coverslips. The culture is treated with Akt-inhibitor to promote the differentiation of AVP-secreting neurons. The neurons are capable of secreting AVP and reacting to depolarizing stimuli such as potassium chloride (KCl).

## DESCRIPTION OF EMBODIMENTS

### 1. Method for Producing Cellular Structure Including Hypothalamic Tissue

The method for producing cellular structures including hypothalamic tissue involves the following detailed steps:

#### Differentiation into Rax+ Hypothalamic Progenitors

Human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) are cultured in a serum-free floating culture of embryoid body-like aggregates with quick reaggregation (SFEBq) using a growth factor-free chemically defined medium (gfCDM) supplemented with a small amount of knock-out serum replacement (KSR). The culture is treated with bone morphogenetic protein 4 (BMP4) and smoothened agonist (SAG) to induce the formation of Rax+ hypothalamic progenitors. The Rax gene is specifically expressed in rostral hypothalamic progenitors, and the RAX::Venus reporter hESC line is used to monitor hypothalamic differentiation in real time.

#### Specific Induction of Dorsal or Ventral Hypothalamic Progenitors

The concentration and timing of BMP4 and SAG application are optimized to specifically induce dorsal or ventral hypothalamic progenitors. Dorsal hypothalamic progenitors are characterized by the expression of Rax and Pax6, while ventral hypothalamic progenitors express Rax and Nkx2.1. The optimal conditions for the induction of dorsal hypothalamic progenitors involve the application of BMP4 at a concentration of 0.5-2.0 nM and SAG at a concentration of 0.5-1.0 μM. The timing of application is crucial, with BMP4 and SAG being applied from day 6 or day 9.

#### Induction of AVP Precursor Cells

The dorsal hypothalamic progenitors are further cultured in a gas-liquid interphase culture system using a Transwell porous filter in a medium supplemented with glucose, N2, B27, and ciliary neurotrophic factor (CNTF) to induce the expression of AVP precursor markers such as OTP and BRN2. The Transwell culture system improves the induction of OTP+ and BRN2+ hypothalamic precursor cells compared to continuous floating culture.

#### Generation of AVP-Secreting Neurons

The AVP precursor cells are dissociated and cultured in a two-dimensional (2D) culture system on Matrigel, poly-D-lysine, and laminin-coated coverslips. The culture is treated with Akt-inhibitor to promote the differentiation of AVP-secreting neurons. The neurons are capable of secreting AVP and reacting to depolarizing stimuli such as potassium chloride (KCl). The AVP secretion is measured using radioimmunoassays, and the neurons are positive for neuronal markers such as TUJ1.

### 2. Simultaneous Maturation of Hypothalamus and Pituitary

The method for the simultaneous maturation of the hypothalamus and pituitary involves the following steps:

1. **Differentiation of Hypothalamic Progenitors**: The hESCs are first differentiated into Rax+ hypothalamic progenitors using the optimized conditions described above.
2. **Simultaneous Differentiation of Pituitary Progenitors**: The hypothalamic progenitors are co-cultured with pituitary progenitors derived from the same hESCs. The pituitary progenitors are induced using a separate protocol involving the application of BMP4 and fibroblast growth factor (FGF) to promote the formation of Rathke's pouch and subsequent differentiation into pituitary cells.
3. **Maturation of Hypothalamic and Pituitary Tissues**: The co-cultured hypothalamic and pituitary progenitors are further matured in a 3D culture system to allow for the development of functional connections between the hypothalamus and pituitary. The mature tissues are capable of producing and secreting hormones such as AVP and adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH).

The present invention provides a robust and efficient method for the production of cellular structures including hypothalamic tissue, particularly focusing on the differentiation of human embryonic stem cells (hESCs) into functional arginine vasopressin (AVP) neurons. The method also enables the simultaneous maturation of the hypothalamus and pituitary gland, offering a valuable tool for the study of neuroendocrine disorders and the development of regenerative medicine.

### 1. Appearance of Hypothalamic AVP Neuron

The appearance of hypothalamic AVP neurons is characterized by the expression of AVP and the presence of large somas (approximately 40 × 20 μm), similar to in vivo magnocellular neurons. The neurons are positive for neuronal markers such as TUJ1 and negative for glial markers such as GFAP, IBA1, and OLIG2. The AVP neurons are capable of secreting AVP and reacting to depolarizing stimuli such as potassium chloride (KCl).

### 2. Simultaneous Maturation of Hypothalamus and Pituitary

The simultaneous maturation of the hypothalamus and pituitary involves the co-culture of hypothalamic and pituitary progenitors derived from the same hESCs. The hypothalamic progenitors are induced using the optimized conditions described above, while the pituitary progenitors are induced using a separate protocol involving the application of BMP4 and fibroblast growth factor (FGF). The co-cultured progenitors are further matured in a 3D culture system to allow for the development of functional connections between the hypothalamus and pituitary. The mature tissues are capable of producing and secreting hormones such as AVP and adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH).
