# DESCRIPTION

## TECHNICAL FIELD

The present invention relates to the field of neurodegenerative diseases, particularly Alzheimer's disease (AD), and more specifically to methods and compositions for reducing the production of amyloid-beta (Aβ) peptides. The invention provides a novel approach to modulating γ-secretase activity by utilizing the transient receptor potential canonical 6 (TRPC6) protein or its functional derivatives to specifically inhibit the cleavage of amyloid precursor protein (APP) by γ-secretase, thereby reducing Aβ production.

## BACKGROUND

Alzheimer's disease (AD) is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder characterized by cognitive decline, memory loss, and eventual dementia. One of the hallmarks of AD is the accumulation of amyloid-beta (Aβ) peptides in the brain, which are derived from the amyloid precursor protein (APP) through sequential cleavages by β-secretase and γ-secretase. The accumulation of Aβ peptides, particularly Aβ40 and Aβ42, is believed to play a central role in the pathogenesis of AD.

Efforts to develop therapeutic strategies for AD have focused on reducing Aβ production or enhancing its clearance. However, most approaches that target γ-secretase, the enzyme responsible for the final cleavage of APP to produce Aβ, have failed in clinical trials due to off-target effects and toxicity. There is a critical need for more specific and safer methods to modulate Aβ production.

Recent studies have identified the transient receptor potential canonical 6 (TRPC6) protein as a potential modulator of Aβ production. TRPC6 is a nonselective cation channel that has been implicated in various physiological and pathological processes. Interestingly, TRPC6 has been shown to interact with APP and specifically inhibit its cleavage by γ-secretase, leading to a reduction in Aβ levels. This interaction occurs independently of TRPC6's channel activity, suggesting a novel mechanism for modulating Aβ production.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides methods and compositions for reducing the production of amyloid-beta (Aβ) peptides by modulating the interaction between the transient receptor potential canonical 6 (TRPC6) protein and amyloid precursor protein (APP). Specifically, the invention utilizes the TRPC6 protein or its functional derivatives, such as the TAT-TM2 peptide, to inhibit the cleavage of APP by γ-secretase, thereby reducing Aβ levels.

In one aspect, the invention provides a method for reducing Aβ production in a cell, comprising contacting the cell with an effective amount of a TRPC6 protein or a functional derivative thereof. The TRPC6 protein or functional derivative is capable of interacting with APP and inhibiting its cleavage by γ-secretase.

In another aspect, the invention provides a pharmaceutical composition comprising a TRPC6 protein or a functional derivative thereof and a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier. The composition is useful for treating or preventing Alzheimer's disease (AD) or other conditions associated with Aβ accumulation.

In yet another aspect, the invention provides a method for treating or preventing Alzheimer's disease (AD) in a subject, comprising administering to the subject an effective amount of a TRPC6 protein or a functional derivative thereof. The administration of the TRPC6 protein or functional derivative reduces Aβ production and ameliorates symptoms associated with AD.

In a further aspect, the invention provides a method for identifying a compound that modulates the interaction between TRPC6 and APP, comprising: (a) contacting a cell expressing TRPC6 and APP with a test compound; (b) measuring the interaction between TRPC6 and APP in the presence and absence of the test compound; and (c) comparing the interaction in the presence and absence of the test compound, wherein a change in the interaction indicates that the test compound modulates the interaction between TRPC6 and APP.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

### Introduction

The present invention is based on the discovery that the transient receptor potential canonical 6 (TRPC6) protein can specifically interact with amyloid precursor protein (APP) and inhibit its cleavage by γ-secretase, leading to a reduction in amyloid-beta (Aβ) peptide production. This interaction occurs independently of TRPC6's channel activity, suggesting a novel mechanism for modulating Aβ production. The invention provides methods and compositions for reducing Aβ production by utilizing TRPC6 or its functional derivatives, such as the TAT-TM2 peptide, which mimics the interaction domain of TRPC6.

### TRPC6 Protein and Functional Derivatives

#### TRPC6 Protein

TRPC6 is a nonselective cation channel protein that belongs to the transient receptor potential (TRP) family. TRPC6 is expressed in various tissues, including the brain, and has been implicated in multiple physiological and pathological processes. The protein consists of six transmembrane domains, with cytoplasmic N- and C-terminal regions. The second transmembrane domain (TM2) is crucial for the interaction with APP and the inhibition of Aβ production.

#### Functional Derivatives

Functional derivatives of TRPC6 include peptides or small molecules that mimic the interaction domain of TRPC6 and can inhibit the cleavage of APP by γ-secretase. One such derivative is the TAT-TM2 peptide, which contains the 487-508 amino acid sequence of the TM2 domain of TRPC6. The TAT-TM2 peptide is cell-permeable and can effectively reduce Aβ production in both in vitro and in vivo models.

### Mechanism of Action

#### Interaction with APP

TRPC6 specifically interacts with the C-terminal fragment of APP (C99) and prevents its interaction with presenilin 1 (PS1), a component of the γ-secretase complex. This interaction disrupts the cleavage of C99 by γ-secretase, leading to a reduction in Aβ production. The interaction between TRPC6 and APP is mediated by the TM2 domain of TRPC6.

#### Independence from Channel Activity

The inhibition of Aβ production by TRPC6 occurs independently of its channel activity. Mutations in the TM2 domain that abolish the channel activity of TRPC6 do not affect its ability to reduce Aβ production. This suggests that the interaction between TRPC6 and APP is a novel cellular function of TRPC6 that is distinct from its role as a cation channel.

### Methods for Reducing Aβ Production

#### In Vitro Methods

1. **Cell Culture and Transfection**: HEK293APP stable cells or primary cortical neurons can be transfected with plasmids encoding TRPC6 or functional derivatives such as TAT-TM2. The cells are then cultured, and Aβ levels in the culture medium and cell lysates are measured using enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA).

2. **In Vitro Purified C99 Cleavage Assay**: Purified C99 recombinant protein is incubated with γ-secretase fractions in the presence or absence of TRPC6 or functional derivatives. Aβ levels are measured using ELISA to assess the inhibition of C99 cleavage.

#### In Vivo Methods

1. **Transgenic Mice**: TRPC6 transgenic mice, in which TRPC6 is overexpressed in the forebrain excitatory neurons, can be crossed with APP/PS1 mice, a common model for AD. The offspring are analyzed for Aβ levels, amyloid plaque burden, and behavioral performance using techniques such as ELISA, immunohistochemistry, and Morris water maze tests.

2. **Peptide Injection**: TAT-TM2 peptide can be injected intraperitoneally into APP/PS1 mice. Aβ levels in the brain are measured using ELISA, and the effects on amyloid plaque burden and behavioral performance are assessed.

### Pharmaceutical Compositions

The invention provides pharmaceutical compositions comprising a TRPC6 protein or a functional derivative thereof and a pharmaceutically acceptable carrier. The compositions can be formulated for various routes of administration, including oral, intravenous, intramuscular, subcutaneous, and intranasal. The compositions can also include additional therapeutic agents, such as antioxidants, anti-inflammatory agents, and other neuroprotective compounds.

### Therapeutic Applications

#### Treatment of Alzheimer's Disease

The methods and compositions of the invention are useful for treating or preventing Alzheimer's disease (AD). Administration of TRPC6 or functional derivatives can reduce Aβ production, decrease amyloid plaque burden, and improve cognitive function in subjects with AD.

#### Prevention of Aβ Accumulation

The methods and compositions of the invention can also be used to prevent Aβ accumulation in individuals at risk of developing AD. Early intervention with TRPC6 or functional derivatives may delay the onset of AD and reduce the severity of symptoms.

### Screening Methods

The invention provides methods for identifying compounds that modulate the interaction between TRPC6 and APP. These methods involve contacting cells expressing TRPC6 and APP with test compounds, measuring the interaction between TRPC6 and APP in the presence and absence of the test compounds, and comparing the results. Compounds that enhance or inhibit the interaction between TRPC6 and APP can be identified and further developed as therapeutic agents.

### EXAMPLE 1

**Reduction of Aβ Production in HEK293APP Cells**

HEK293APP stable cells were transfected with a plasmid encoding TRPC6 or a control plasmid. The cells were cultured for 48 hours, and Aβ levels in the culture medium and cell lysates were measured using ELISA. The results showed that TRPC6 significantly reduced Aβ40 and Aβ42 levels compared to the control.

### EXAMPLE 2

**Inhibition of C99 Cleavage by TAT-TM2 Peptide**

Purified C99 recombinant protein was incubated with γ-secretase fractions in the presence or absence of TAT-TM2 peptide. Aβ levels were measured using ELISA. The results showed that TAT-TM2 peptide dose-dependently inhibited the cleavage of C99 by γ-secretase, leading to a reduction in Aβ production.

### EXAMPLE 3

**Reduction of Aβ Levels in APP/PS1/TRPC6 Mice**

TRPC6 transgenic mice were crossed with APP/PS1 mice. The offspring were analyzed for Aβ levels, amyloid plaque burden, and behavioral performance. The results showed that TRPC6 overexpression in the forebrain neurons reduced Aβ levels, decreased amyloid plaque burden, and improved spatial learning and memory in APP/PS1/TRPC6 mice compared to APP/PS1 mice.

### EXAMPLE 4

**Peptide Injection in APP/PS1 Mice**

TAT-TM2 peptide was injected intraperitoneally into APP/PS1 mice. Aβ levels in the brain were measured using ELISA, and the effects on amyloid plaque burden and behavioral performance were assessed. The results showed that TAT-TM2 peptide reduced Aβ levels, decreased amyloid plaque burden, and improved cognitive function in APP/PS1 mice.

### EXAMPLE 5

**Effect of TRPC6 on γ-Secretase Activity**

HEK293 cells were transfected with a plasmid encoding TRPC6 or a control plasmid. The cells were lysed, and γ-secretase activity was measured using a fluorogenic substrate. The results showed that TRPC6 did not affect the overall activity of γ-secretase, but specifically inhibited the cleavage of C99 by γ-secretase.

### EXAMPLE 6

**Interaction Between TRPC6 and APP**

HEK293 cells were co-transfected with plasmids encoding TRPC6 and APP. The cells were lysed, and the interaction between TRPC6 and APP was detected by co-immunoprecipitation. The results showed that TRPC6 specifically interacted with APP and prevented its interaction with PS1.

### EXAMPLE 7

**Effect of TRPC6 on Synaptic Density**

HEK293 cells were transfected with a plasmid encoding TRPC6 or a control plasmid. The cells were cultured, and synaptic density was measured using Golgi staining. The results showed that TRPC6 increased synaptic density, suggesting a protective effect on synapses.

### EXAMPLE 8

**Behavioral Performance in APP/PS1/TRPC6 Mice**

APP/PS1/TRPC6 mice and APP/PS1 mice were subjected to Morris water maze tests to assess spatial learning and memory. The results showed that APP/PS1/TRPC6 mice performed better in the training and probe sessions, indicating improved cognitive function.

### EXAMPLE 9

**Screening for Compounds that Modulate TRPC6-APP Interaction**

HEK293 cells expressing TRPC6 and APP were contacted with a library of test compounds. The interaction between TRPC6 and APP was measured using co-immunoprecipitation. Compounds that enhanced or inhibited the interaction were identified and further characterized for their potential as therapeutic agents.

### Conclusion

The present invention provides a novel approach to reducing Aβ production by modulating the interaction between TRPC6 and APP. The methods and compositions of the invention offer a promising strategy for treating or preventing Alzheimer's disease and other conditions associated with Aβ accumulation. The invention also provides tools for identifying and developing new therapeutic agents that target the TRPC6-APP interaction.
