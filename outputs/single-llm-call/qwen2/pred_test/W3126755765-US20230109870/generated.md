# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD

The present invention relates to a method for the early identification of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) molecular defects that are amenable to exon-skipping therapies. Specifically, the invention provides a multiplex quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR) assay to identify hemi(homo)zygotic deletions of specific exons of the DMD gene, which are flanking exons of exon-skipping targets. This method is particularly useful for newborn screening (NBS) programs to ensure early diagnosis and treatment of DMD patients who can benefit from exon-skipping therapies.

## BACKGROUND

Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) is a severe and progressive muscle degenerative disorder primarily affecting males. It is caused by mutations in the DMD gene, which encodes the protein dystrophin. Dystrophin plays a crucial role in maintaining the structural integrity of muscle fibers. Pathogenic variants in the DMD gene, particularly out-of-frame deletions, lead to a complete lack of dystrophin production, resulting in muscle weakness and degeneration. Becker Muscular Dystrophy (BMD), a milder form of the disease, is characterized by in-frame deletions that allow for partial dystrophin production.

Recent advancements in DMD treatment include exon-skipping therapies, which aim to restore the reading frame of the DMD gene by skipping specific exons. Drugs such as eteplirsen (Exondys) and golodirsen (Vyondys 53) have been approved for treating DMD patients with specific deletions amenable to exon 51 and exon 53 skipping, respectively. However, these therapies are only effective for a subset of DMD patients, and early diagnosis is crucial for optimal treatment outcomes.

Current newborn screening (NBS) programs for DMD often rely on the quantification of creatine kinase (CK) or its muscular isoform (CKMM) from dried blood spots (DBS). While these markers are indicative of muscle damage, they do not directly reflect the underlying genetic defect and can result in false negatives and false positives. Therefore, there is a need for a more accurate and specific method to identify DMD patients who can benefit from exon-skipping therapies at an early stage.

## SUMMARY

The present invention provides a method for the early identification of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) molecular defects that are amenable to exon-skipping therapies. The method involves a multiplex quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR) assay to detect hemi(homo)zygotic deletions of specific exons of the DMD gene, which are flanking exons of exon-skipping targets. The assay is designed to identify deletions of exons 44, 46, 50, 52, and 54 of the DMD gene, which are the flanking exons of exons 45, 51, and 53, respectively.

The method includes the following steps:
1. Extracting DNA from dried blood spots (DBS).
2. Performing a multiplex qPCR assay using specific primers and probes to amplify and detect the target exons.
3. Calculating a normalized fluorescence ratio (NFR) to interpret the amplification results.
4. Confirming positive results using a second-tier molecular test, such as Multiplex Ligation-Dependent Probe Amplification (MLPA).

The invention also encompasses kits and reagents for performing the qPCR assay, as well as methods for using the assay in newborn screening programs to identify DMD patients who can benefit from exon-skipping therapies.

## DESCRIPTION OF EMBODIMENTS

### Overview

The present invention provides a method for the early identification of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy (DMD) molecular defects that are amenable to exon-skipping therapies. The method utilizes a multiplex quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR) assay to detect hemi(homo)zygotic deletions of specific exons of the DMD gene. These exons are flanking exons of exon-skipping targets, specifically exons 44, 46, 50, 52, and 54, which are the flanking exons of exons 45, 51, and 53, respectively.

### Sample Collection and DNA Extraction

The method begins with the collection of dried blood spots (DBS) from newborns. DBS are a convenient and minimally invasive source of DNA for newborn screening. DNA is extracted from the DBS using standard protocols. In one embodiment, DNA is extracted from a 3.1-mm DBS using a protocol described previously. The extracted DNA is not quantitated, and 1 µL of the DNA is used for the qPCR assay.

### Multiplex qPCR Assay

The qPCR assay is designed to amplify and detect the target exons of the DMD gene. The assay uses specific primers and probes for each target exon. The sequences and concentrations of the primers and probes are as follows:

- **DMD exon 44:**
  - Forward primer: 5′- TAC CTG CAG GCG ATT TGA C -3'
  - Reverse primer: 5′- CAC CCT TCA GAA CCT GAT CTT T -3'
  - Probe: 5′- FAM—AAA TTC CTG AGA ATT GGG AAC ATG—BHQ -3'
  - Concentration: 300 nmol/L (primers), 130 nmol/L (probe)

- **DMD exon 46:**
  - Forward primer: 5′- TTT ATG GTT GGA GGA AGC AGA -3'
  - Reverse primer: 5′- AAT GGG CAG AAA ACC AAT GA -3'
  - Probe: 5′- YakimaYellow—AAC CTG GAA AAG AGC AGC AAC T—BHQ -3'
  - Concentration: 300 nmol/L (primers), 90 nmol/L (probe)

- **DMD exon 50:**
  - Forward primer: 5′- CTG AGT GGA AGG CGG TAA AC -3'
  - Reverse primer: 5′- TCT CAC CCA GTC ATC ACT TCA -3'
  - Probe: 5′- ROX—ACTTCAAGAGCTGAGGGCAAAG—BHQ -3'
  - Concentration: 300 nmol/L (primers), 80 nmol/L (probe)

- **DMD exon 52:**
  - Forward primer: 5′- AAT ACA CAA CGC TGA AGA ACC C -3'
  - Reverse primer: 5′- TTG TGT GTC CCA TGC TTG TT -3'
  - Probe: 5′- Atto 647 N—CGC TGC CCA AAA TTT GAA AAA—BHQ -3'
  - Concentration: 300 nmol/L (primers), 150 nmol/L (probe)

- **DMD exon 54:**
  - Forward primer: 5′- TCT ATA GCA GTT GGC CAA AGA C -3'
  - Reverse primer: 5′- TCA TGG TCC ATC CAG TTT CA -3'
  - Probe: 5′- Atto 700—AAT ATC AAT GCC TCT TGG AGA AGC—BHQ -3'
  - Concentration: 300 nmol/L (primers), 180 nmol/L (probe)

The qPCR reactions are performed in a total volume of 25 µL, containing 1 µL of DNA, 5× Takyon master mix (Eurogentec), and the specified primers and probes. The reactions are run on a CFX96 Real-Time PCR System (Biorad) under the following conditions: 95°C for 5 minutes, followed by 40 cycles of 95°C for 30 seconds and 61°C for 75 seconds.

### Interpretation of Results

To interpret the qPCR results, a normalized fluorescence ratio (NFR) is calculated for each target exon. The NFR is obtained by dividing the endpoint fluorescence of each sample by the median endpoint fluorescence of the corresponding exon of all samples in the same run. An NFR below 0.2 is considered indicative of a deletion.

### Confirmation of Positive Results

Positive results, indicating the presence of a deletion in one or more of the target exons, are confirmed using a second-tier molecular test, such as Multiplex Ligation-Dependent Probe Amplification (MLPA). MLPA testing is performed on a fresh EDTA sample to confirm the deletion and to establish the precise genotype, determining whether the patient is eligible for exon-skipping therapy.

### Kits and Reagents

The invention also encompasses kits and reagents for performing the qPCR assay. The kits include the necessary primers and probes for amplifying and detecting the target exons of the DMD gene, as well as the master mix and other reagents required for the qPCR reactions. The kits may also include instructions for performing the assay and interpreting the results.

### Applications

The method of the present invention is particularly useful for newborn screening (NBS) programs to identify DMD patients who can benefit from exon-skipping therapies. Early identification of these patients allows for the initiation of treatment at a young age, which can significantly improve outcomes. The method is also applicable in clinical settings for the diagnosis and management of DMD patients.

## EXAMPLES

### Example 1—DMD Genotyping Assay

#### Materials and Methods

**Sample Collection and DNA Extraction:**
Blood from DMD and control samples was collected as dried blood spots (DBS). DNA was extracted from a 3.1-mm DBS using a standard protocol. The extracted DNA was not quantitated, and 1 µL of the DNA was used for the qPCR assay.

**Multiplex qPCR Assay:**
The qPCR assay was performed using the primers and probes listed in Table 3. The reactions were run on a CFX96 Real-Time PCR System (Biorad) under the specified conditions. The NFR was calculated for each target exon to interpret the results.

**Confirmation of Positive Results:**
Positive results were confirmed using MLPA on a fresh EDTA sample.

#### Results

The qPCR assay was performed on 120 samples, including 51 male patients with a clinical DMD phenotype, 50 female carriers of a DMD deletion, and 19 normal individuals. The assay correctly identified all subjects with either the presence or absence of amplification of the target exons. The NFR below 0.2 was found to be discriminant between the deleted group and the carrier and control groups. The assay achieved 100% sensitivity and 100% specificity in the population studied.
