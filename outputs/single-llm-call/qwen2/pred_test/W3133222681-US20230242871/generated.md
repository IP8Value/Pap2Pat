## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Primordial Germ Cells (PGCs) are essential for the continuation of the species, as they give rise to eggs and sperm. Understanding the developmental origins and specification of PGCs is crucial for reproductive biology and the treatment of infertility. While the developmental origins of mouse PGCs have been extensively studied, the same cannot be said for human PGCs due to ethical and technical challenges in accessing early post-implantation human embryos. Consequently, much of the knowledge about human PGCs is inferred from studies in related species and in vitro differentiation of human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs).

Recent advancements have enabled the differentiation of hPSCs into PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) in 3D cultures, providing valuable insights into early human germline specification. However, these methods are complex and often yield heterogeneous cell populations, making it difficult to study the precise mechanisms of PGCLC specification. There is a need for a simplified and efficient method to generate human PGCLCs in vitro, which can facilitate further research and potential clinical applications.

## BRIEF SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides a simplified monolayer system for the differentiation of human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) into PGC-like cells (PGCLCs). The method involves a two-phase differentiation process where hPSCs are first exposed to primitive streak-inducing signals for a short duration, followed by the inhibition of WNT signaling and the addition of PGC-specifying signals. This method significantly enhances the efficiency of PGCLC specification and allows for the purification of PGCLCs using specific cell-surface markers.

Key aspects of the invention include:
1. Temporally dynamic activation and inhibition of WNT signaling to enhance PGCLC specification.
2. Identification of a unique cell-surface marker profile (CXCR4 + PDGFRα - GARP -) for the purification of PGCLCs.
3. Demonstration of the transcriptional similarity between in vitro-generated PGCLCs and in vivo human fetal PGCs.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE INVENTION

### Definitions

- **Primordial Germ Cells (PGCs):** Early germ cells that give rise to eggs and sperm.
- **Pluripotent Stem Cells (PSCs):** Cells that can differentiate into any cell type in the body.
- **PGC-like Cells (PGCLCs):** Cells generated in vitro that exhibit characteristics similar to PGCs.
- **Monolayer Culture:** A method of culturing cells in a single layer on a flat surface.
- **WNT Signaling:** A signaling pathway involved in various developmental processes, including cell fate determination.
- **BMP Signaling:** A signaling pathway that plays a role in the differentiation of various cell types, including PGCs.
- **CXCR4:** A chemokine receptor expressed on PGCLCs.
- **PDGFRα:** Platelet-derived growth factor receptor alpha, a marker for mesodermal cells.
- **GARP:** Glycoprotein A repetitions predominant, a marker for mesodermal cells.

### Methods

The invention provides a method for differentiating human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) into PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) in a monolayer culture. The method comprises the following steps:

1. **Phase 1: Posterior Epiblast Induction**
   - hPSCs are cultured in a monolayer and exposed to primitive streak-inducing signals for 12 hours. These signals include:
     - 100 ng/mL Activin
     - 3 μM CHIR99021 (WNT agonist)
     - 10 μM Y-27632 (ROCK inhibitor)
   - The cells are cultured in aRB27 basal media, which consists of Advanced RPMI 1640 medium supplemented with 1% B27 supplement, 0.1 mM non-essential amino acids (NEAA), 100 U/ml Penicillin + 0.1 mg/ml Streptomycin, and 2 mM L-Glutamine.

2. **Phase 2: PGCLC Specification**
   - After 12 hours, the cells are washed and treated with the following signals for 24 hours:
     - 40 ng/mL BMP4
     - 1 μM XAV939 (WNT inhibitor)
     - 10 μM Y-27632 (ROCK inhibitor)
   - Subsequently, the cells are treated with:
     - 100 ng/mL SCF
     - 50 ng/mL EGF
     - 1 μM XAV939 (WNT inhibitor)
     - 10 μM Y-27632 (ROCK inhibitor)
   - Finally, the cells are treated with:
     - 40 ng/mL BMP4
     - 100 ng/mL SCF
     - 50 ng/mL EGF
     - 1 μM XAV939 (WNT inhibitor)
     - 10 μM Y-27632 (ROCK inhibitor)

### Cell Compositions

The method generates a population of cells that includes PGCLCs. These PGCLCs can be purified using the cell-surface marker profile CXCR4 + PDGFRα - GARP -. The purified PGCLCs express hallmark PGC markers such as NANOS3, SOX17, PRDM1 (BLIMP1), and TFAP2C (AP2γ), and do not express markers of endoderm or mesoderm.

### METHODS OF USE

The PGCLCs generated using the method of the invention can be used for various purposes, including:
- **Research:** To study the molecular mechanisms of human PGC specification and early germline development.
- **Infertility Treatments:** To develop in vitro methods for generating human eggs and sperm for infertility treatments.
- **Reproductive Technologies:** To advance reproductive technologies such as in vitro fertilization (IVF) and assisted reproductive techniques (ART).

### EXAMPLES

#### Example 1: Temporally Dynamic WNT Activation Enhances PGCLC Specification

hPSCs were cultured in a monolayer and exposed to primitive streak-inducing signals (100 ng/mL Activin, 3 μM CHIR99021, 10 μM Y-27632) for 12 hours. Subsequently, the cells were treated with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours, followed by 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for an additional 24 hours. Finally, the cells were treated with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours. The resulting cells were analyzed for the expression of PGCLC markers using flow cytometry and immunostaining. The results showed a significant increase in the percentage of NANOS3-mCherry + PGCLCs compared to control conditions without WNT inhibition.

#### Example 2: Purification of PGCLCs Using Cell-Surface Markers

hPSCs were differentiated into PGCLCs using the method described in Example 1. The resulting cells were stained with antibodies against CXCR4, PDGFRα, and GARP, and analyzed by flow cytometry. The CXCR4 + PDGFRα - GARP - fraction was isolated using fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS). The purified cells were analyzed for the expression of PGCLC markers using qPCR and immunostaining. The results confirmed that the purified cells expressed high levels of NANOS3, SOX17, PRDM1, and TFAP2C, and did not express markers of endoderm or mesoderm.

#### Example 3: Transcriptional Similarity Between In Vitro PGCLCs and In Vivo Fetal PGCs

hPSCs were differentiated into PGCLCs using the method described in Example 1. The resulting cells were subjected to single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) to analyze their gene expression profiles. The scRNA-seq data were compared to published scRNA-seq data of human fetal germ cells (FGCs) from week 5-26 human fetuses. Hierarchical clustering and pairwise Pearson's correlation coefficient analysis showed that the in vitro-generated PGCLCs were transcriptionally similar to early-stage FGCs (FGC1), which represent early-stage PGCs.

## EMBODIMENTS

### EMBODIMENTS

1. **Embodiment 1: Method for Differentiating hPSCs into PGCLCs**
   - A method for differentiating human pluripotent stem cells (hPSCs) into PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) in a monolayer culture, comprising:
     - Culturing hPSCs in a monolayer and exposing them to primitive streak-inducing signals (100 ng/mL Activin, 3 μM CHIR99021, 10 μM Y-27632) for 12 hours.
     - Washing the cells and treating them with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours.
     - Treating the cells with 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for an additional 24 hours.
     - Treating the cells with 40 ng/mL BMP4, 100 ng/mL SCF, 50 ng/mL EGF, 1 μM XAV939, and 10 μM Y-27632 for 24 hours.

2. **Embodiment 2: Purification of PGCLCs Using Cell-Surface Markers**
   - A method for purifying PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) from a mixed cell population, comprising:
     - Differentiating hPSCs into PGCLCs using the method of Embodiment 1.
     - Staining the cells with antibodies against CXCR4, PDGFRα, and GARP.
     - Isolating the CXCR4 + PDGFRα - GARP - fraction using fluorescence-activated cell sorting (FACS).

3. **Embodiment 3: Use of PGCLCs in Research**
   - A method for using PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) generated using the method of Embodiment 1 in research, comprising:
     - Analyzing the molecular mechanisms of human PGC specification and early germline development.
     - Studying the transcriptional and functional properties of PGCLCs.

4. **Embodiment 4: Use of PGCLCs in Infertility Treatments**
   - A method for using PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) generated using the method of Embodiment 1 in infertility treatments, comprising:
     - Developing in vitro methods for generating human eggs and sperm.

5. **Embodiment 5: Use of PGCLCs in Reproductive Technologies**
   - A method for using PGC-like cells (PGCLCs) generated using the method of Embodiment 1 in reproductive technologies, comprising:
     - Advancing in vitro fertilization (IVF) techniques.
