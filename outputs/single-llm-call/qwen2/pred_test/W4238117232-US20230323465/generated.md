# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to a method and kit for the preoperative diagnosis of thyroid nodules using a panel of specific gene isoforms. More specifically, the invention provides a method for detecting and quantifying the expression of five specific gene isoforms in fine needle aspiration (FNA) samples to distinguish between benign and malignant thyroid tumors, including the recently reclassified non-invasive follicular thyroid neoplasm with papillary-like nuclear features (NIFTP).

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

Thyroid cancer incidence is rapidly increasing worldwide, particularly in the United States, where the prevalence has nearly quadrupled over the past 15 years, primarily due to the increased incidence of papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC). Thyroid nodules are detectable by ultrasound in over 50% of the adult population, and fine needle aspiration (FNA) cytopathology is the most accurate means of pre-operative diagnosis. However, up to 30% of FNA samples result in indeterminate cytology, complicating the diagnosis and management of thyroid nodules.

The American Thyroid Association has recently reclassified a previously considered malignant subgroup of follicular variant of papillary thyroid carcinoma (FVPTC) to the non-invasive follicular thyroid neoplasm with papillary-like nuclear features (NIFTP), an indolent neoplasm with questionable malignant potential. NIFTP cannot be differentiated from invasive FVPTC by cytopathology alone, requiring histopathologic evaluation for diagnosis. This reclassification has further complicated the use of existing molecular tests, which often report NIFTP as "positive" or "suspicious" for malignant disease.

Several molecular diagnostic tests, including RNA-based gene expression and multi-panel mutation genotyping analyses, are currently available for clinical use. However, these tests suffer from relatively low specificity and positive predictive value (PPV), particularly after the introduction of the NIFTP subtype. There is a critical need for a more accurate and reliable method to preoperatively diagnose thyroid nodules, especially to distinguish NIFTP from malignant PTC and FVPTC.

The present invention addresses the aforementioned needs by providing a method and kit for the preoperative diagnosis of thyroid nodules using a panel of specific gene isoforms. The method involves detecting and quantifying the expression of five specific gene isoforms in FNA samples to distinguish between benign and malignant thyroid tumors, including NIFTP.
