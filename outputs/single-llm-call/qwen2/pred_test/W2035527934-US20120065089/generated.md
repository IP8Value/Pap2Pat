# DESCRIPTION

## TECHNICAL FIELD
The present invention relates to a method and system for diagnosing cholangiocarcinoma (CC) using bile specimens. More specifically, the invention involves the use of a combination of Wisteria floribunda agglutinin (WFA) and a monoclonal antibody (mAb MY.1E12) to detect sialylated MUC1, a glycoprotein marker that is highly expressed in CC. The invention provides a high-sensitivity and high-specificity diagnostic tool that can distinguish CC from benign bile duct diseases and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC).

## BACKGROUND ART
Cholangiocarcinoma (CC) is a malignant tumor arising from the bile duct epithelium. Early and accurate diagnosis of CC is crucial for effective treatment, but current diagnostic methods often lack sufficient sensitivity and specificity. Commonly used serological markers, such as carbohydrate antigen 19-9 (CA19-9), mucin 5AC (MUC5AC), and Mac-2-binding protein, have not proven satisfactory for early detection. Biliary cytology, although routinely performed, also has limitations in sensitivity.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

### Object to be Solved by the Invention
The primary object of the present invention is to provide a robust and reliable method for the early and accurate diagnosis of cholangiocarcinoma (CC) using bile specimens. The method aims to overcome the limitations of existing diagnostic tools by offering higher sensitivity and specificity.

### Means to Solve the Object
The invention achieves the aforementioned object through the following means:
1. **Lectin Microarray Analysis**: Differential glycan profiling using lectin microarray to identify Wisteria floribunda agglutinin (WFA) as a specific lectin for CC.
2. **Histochemical Analysis**: Confirmation of WFA-reactive glycans in cancerous lesions of CC using histochemical staining.
3. **Double-Staining Analysis**: Colocalization of WFA-reactive glycans and sialylated MUC1 using fluorescence double-staining.
4. **Pulldown Assay**: Enrichment of sialylated MUC1 and WFA-reactive glycans from bile samples using biotinylated WFA and streptavidin beads.
5. **Sandwich ELISA**: Development of a sandwich ELISA system combining WFA and mAb MY.1E12 to detect sialylated MUC1 in bile specimens.

### Effect of the Invention
The invention provides a high-sensitivity and high-specificity diagnostic method for cholangiocarcinoma (CC) using bile specimens. The method effectively distinguishes CC from benign bile duct diseases and hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC), offering a significant improvement over existing diagnostic tools. The WFA-MY.1E12 sandwich ELISA system demonstrates superior performance with a sensitivity of 90.0% and an area under the ROC curve (AUC) of 0.86.

## MODE FOR CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION
The invention involves the following steps:

1. **Lectin Microarray Analysis**:
   - Formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue sections from cholangiocarcinoma (CC) and normal bile duct epithelium (BDE) are deparaffinized and fluorescence-labeled with Cy3-succimidyl ester.
   - The labeled tissue extracts are applied to a lectin microarray slide, and fluorescence signals are measured.
   - Data analysis identifies WFA as the lectin with the highest signal intensity in cancerous lesions compared to normal BDE.

2. **Histochemical Analysis**:
   - Tissue sections are stained with biotinylated WFA and detected using Histofine Simple Stain MAX-PO.
   - WFA staining is observed in cancerous lesions of CC with high frequency and intensity, confirming the presence of WFA-reactive glycans.

3. **Double-Staining Analysis**:
   - Fluorescence double-staining is performed using biotinylated WFA and mAb MY.1E12, followed by Alexa Fluor 488-labeled anti-mouse IgG antibody and Cy5-SE-labeled anti-biotin mAb.
   - Colocalization of WFA-reactive glycans and sialylated MUC1 is confirmed, indicating that sialylated MUC1 carries WFA-reactive glycans.

4. **Pulldown Assay**:
   - Bile samples are centrifuged, and the supernatants are incubated with biotinylated WFA preconjugated streptavidin beads.
   - The enriched fractions are analyzed by western blotting using mAb MY.1E12, confirming the presence of sialylated MUC1 carrying WFA-reactive glycans.

5. **Sandwich ELISA**:
   - Flat-bottomed 96-well streptavidin-precoated microtiter plates are treated with biotinylated WFA for immobilization.
   - Bile samples are incubated in the wells, followed by mAb MY.1E12 and horseradish peroxidase (HRP)-conjugated anti-mouse IgG.
   - The optical density (OD) is measured at 450 nm to quantify the presence of sialylated MUC1 carrying WFA-reactive glycans.

## EXAMPLE 1
**Lectin Microarray Analysis**:
Formalin-fixed, paraffin-embedded tissue sections from 45 cases of CC and 38 cases of normal BDE were deparaffinized and fluorescence-labeled with Cy3-succimidyl ester. The labeled tissue extracts were applied to a lectin microarray slide, and fluorescence signals were measured. The data analysis revealed that WFA showed the highest signal intensity in cancerous lesions compared to normal BDE, with a T/N ratio of 4.6 (P < 0.0001).

## EXAMPLE 2
**Histochemical Analysis**:
Tissue sections from 90 cases of CC, 25 cases of normal BDE, 25 cases of HCC, and 10 cases of HCC-ICC were stained with biotinylated WFA. WFA staining was observed in 92.2% of CC cancerous lesions and 80.0% of ICC elements of HCC-ICC, with much less frequency and intensity in normal BDE (32.0%).

## EXAMPLE 3
**Double-Staining Analysis**:
Fluorescence double-staining was performed on ICC tissue sections using biotinylated WFA and mAb MY.1E12. The results showed that WFA-reactive glycans and sialylated MUC1 colocalized in the apical surface of epithelial cells in cancerous lesions.

## EXAMPLE 4
**Pulldown Assay**:
Bile samples from 5 hepatolithiasis patients and 5 CC patients were centrifuged, and the supernatants were incubated with biotinylated WFA preconjugated streptavidin beads. Western blotting using mAb MY.1E12 confirmed the presence of sialylated MUC1 carrying WFA-reactive glycans in all 5 CC samples.

## EXAMPLE 5
**Sandwich ELISA**:
Flat-bottomed 96-well streptavidin-precoated microtiter plates were treated with biotinylated WFA. Bile samples from 30 CC patients and 38 patients with benign bile duct diseases were incubated in the wells, followed by mAb MY.1E12 and HRP-conjugated anti-mouse IgG. The OD values were significantly higher in CC patients (P = 0.0004), with a sensitivity of 90.0% and an AUC of 0.86.

### EXAMPLE 6
**Comparison with Conventional Methods**:
The WFA-MY.1E12 sandwich ELISA system was compared with a conventional antibody-antibody sandwich system (MY.1E12-MY.1E12). The WFA-MY.1E12 system showed superior performance with a sensitivity of 90.0% and an AUC of 0.86, while the conventional system had a sensitivity of 56.7% and an AUC of 0.75.

### EXAMPLE 7
**Validation in Combined HCC-ICC**:
The WFA-MY.1E12 sandwich ELISA system was tested on 10 cases of combined HCC-ICC. The system accurately distinguished ICC from HCC elements with 100% accuracy, demonstrating its potential for differential diagnosis.

### EXAMPLE 8
**Further Validation**:
A larger cohort of patients with various benign bile duct diseases, such as primary sclerosing cholangitis and primary biliary cirrhosis, was evaluated using the WFA-MY.1E12 sandwich ELISA system. The results confirmed the high sensitivity and specificity of the method in distinguishing CC from benign diseases.
