# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD

The present invention relates to the field of biotechnology and immunology, specifically to the development and use of anti-TIGIT antibodies for therapeutic purposes. These antibodies are designed to block the interaction between TIGIT and its ligands, thereby enhancing immune responses and providing therapeutic benefits, particularly in the treatment of cancer.

## BACKGROUND

TIGIT (T cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains) is a member of the Ig superfamily receptors and is expressed on activated CD8+ and CD4+ T cells, natural killer (NK) cells, and regulatory T cells (Tregs) in both mice and humans. TIGIT binds to poliovirus receptor (PVR/CD155) with high affinity and shows relatively weaker binding to poliovirus receptor-like 2 (PVRL2/CD112). TIGIT can inhibit T-cell and NK-cell functions through various mechanisms, including delivering direct inhibitory signals, out-competing DNAM-1/CD226 for PVR and PVRL2 binding, augmenting Treg immunosuppressive functions, and promoting tolerogenic dendritic cells (DCs) through interactions with PVR on DCs.

The expression of TIGIT is upregulated in the tumor microenvironment in multiple malignancies, and its expression on tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs) has been associated with poor survival. TIGIT is often co-expressed with PD-1 and other inhibitory receptors, such as TIM-3 and LAG-3, on exhausted CD8+ T cells and Tregs in tumors. High TIGIT expression on tumor-infiltrating Tregs has been linked to poor clinical outcomes in melanoma following anti-PD-1 or anti-CTLA-4 therapies. Therefore, targeting TIGIT, and further in combination with other immune checkpoint blockade therapies, may augment immune responses and achieve optimal anti-tumor efficacy.

Several anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibodies (mAbs) have entered clinical stages, utilizing different Fc formats. Some, like Tiragolumab, Vibostolimab, BGB-A1217, and EOS-448, adopt wild-type human IgG1, an Fc format with full effector functions, while others, like COM902 and ASP8374, utilize IgG4, an IgG type with weak effector functions. BMS-986207 and AB154 are engineered with effector function-disabled Fc, and SGN-TGT harbors enhanced Fc functions via afucosylation. These discrepancies raise the question of whether effector function-competent Fc is necessary for a TIGIT blockade antibody to achieve optimal anti-tumor efficacies.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention provides a novel anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibody, BGB-A1217, which is designed to block the interaction between TIGIT and its ligands, PVR and PVRL2. This antibody is characterized by its high affinity binding to TIGIT and its ability to enhance immune responses, particularly in the context of cancer therapy. The invention further includes a pair of anti-TIGIT antibodies with the same variable regions but different Fc forms: BGB-A1217 (with wild-type IgG1 Fc) and BGB-A1217MF (with an Fc lacking FcγR binding capacities). Comparative characterizations of these two mAbs indicate that effector function-competent Fc is necessary for BGB-A1217 to elicit optimal anti-tumor efficacy in pre-clinical models, through mechanisms such as Treg reduction, NK cell and monocytes activation, and trogocytosis.

The invention also encompasses methods of using BGB-A1217 and anti-PD-1 antibodies in combination to synergistically activate T cells in vitro and inhibit tumor growth in mouse models. Additionally, the invention provides pharmaceutical compositions and kits containing the anti-TIGIT antibodies for therapeutic use.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Definitions

- **TIGIT**: T cell immunoreceptor with Ig and ITIM domains, a member of the Ig superfamily receptors.
- **PVR**: Poliovirus receptor, also known as CD155.
- **PVRL2**: Poliovirus receptor-like 2, also known as CD112.
- **Fc region**: The crystallizable fragment of an antibody, responsible for effector functions.
- **FcγR**: Fc gamma receptors, a family of receptors that bind the Fc region of IgG antibodies.
- **ADCC**: Antibody-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, a mechanism by which antibodies recruit immune cells to kill target cells.
- **Trogocytosis**: The process by which a cell removes membrane fragments from another cell, often mediated by FcγR binding.
- **Treg**: Regulatory T cells, a subset of T cells that suppress immune responses.
- **NK cells**: Natural killer cells, a type of lymphocyte involved in innate immunity.
- **PD-1**: Programmed cell death protein 1, an immune checkpoint receptor.
- **PD-L1**: Programmed cell death ligand 1, a ligand for PD-1.

### Anti-TIGIT Antibodies

BGB-A1217 is a humanized monoclonal antibody derived from a murine clone (mu1217) generated by hybridoma fusion, targeting the extracellular domain (ECD) of human TIGIT. The variable regions of the heavy and light chains of mu1217 were sequenced, and the murine framework regions were replaced by closely homologous human germline IgG sequences. The final form of the humanized anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibody was selected as the clinical candidate, referred to as BGB-A1217.

BGB-A1217 binds to human TIGIT with high affinity, as determined by surface plasmon resonance (SPR) assay (KD = 0.135 nM) and cell binding assay (EC50 = 0.54 nM). It completely blocks the interaction of TIGIT with its ligands PVR (IC50 = 4.53 nM) and PVRL2 (IC50 = 1.69 nM) in a cell-based ligand competition assay.

### Anti-PD1 Antibodies

Anti-PD1 antibodies, such as BGB-A317, are designed to block the interaction between PD-1 and its ligands, PD-L1 and PD-L2. These antibodies are used in combination with anti-TIGIT antibodies to enhance immune responses and achieve optimal anti-tumor efficacy. BGB-A317 is a humanized monoclonal antibody that binds to human PD-1 with high affinity and blocks the interaction with PD-L1 and PD-L2.

### Further Alteration of the Framework of Fc Region

To investigate the role of Fc effector functions in the anti-TIGIT antibody's activity, a pair of anti-TIGIT antibodies with the same variable regions but different Fc forms was generated: BGB-A1217 (with wild-type IgG1 Fc) and BGB-A1217MF (with an Fc lacking FcγR binding capacities). BGB-A1217 binds to FcγRI with high affinity and also has significant binding to FcγRIIA, FcγRIIB, and FcγRIIIA. In contrast, BGB-A1217MF completely loses binding to these FcγRs.

### Antibody Production

BGB-A1217 and BGB-A1217MF are produced using standard recombinant DNA technology. The variable regions of the heavy and light chains are cloned into expression vectors, and the vectors are transfected into mammalian cell lines, such as Chinese Hamster Ovary (CHO) cells. The cells are cultured under controlled conditions to produce the antibodies, which are then purified using affinity chromatography and other purification techniques.

### Methods of Detection and Diagnosis

The anti-TIGIT antibodies can be used in various methods for detecting and diagnosing TIGIT expression and TIGIT-ligand interactions. These methods include enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA), flow cytometry, and surface plasmon resonance (SPR) assays. For example, the binding of BGB-A1217 to TIGIT can be detected using flow cytometry by staining TIGIT-over-expressing cells with the antibody and a secondary antibody conjugated to a fluorescent dye.

### Methods of Treatment

The anti-TIGIT antibodies, particularly BGB-A1217, can be used to treat various diseases, including cancer. The antibodies can be administered to patients to block the interaction between TIGIT and its ligands, thereby enhancing immune responses and reducing tumor growth. The antibodies can be used alone or in combination with other therapeutic agents, such as anti-PD-1 antibodies.

### Pharmaceutical Compositions and Formulations

The anti-TIGIT antibodies can be formulated into pharmaceutical compositions suitable for various routes of administration, including intravenous, subcutaneous, and intramuscular injection. The compositions may include pharmaceutically acceptable carriers, excipients, and stabilizers. For example, the antibodies can be formulated in a buffered saline solution containing a stabilizer such as human serum albumin (HSA) or trehalose.

### Pharmaceutical Compositions and Kits

The invention also provides kits containing the anti-TIGIT antibodies for therapeutic use. The kits may include one or more vials of the antibody, a buffer solution for reconstitution, and instructions for use. The kits may also include other components, such as syringes, needles, and diluents.

## EXAMPLE

### Example 1: Generation of Anti-TIGIT Monoclonal Antibody

BGB-A1217 is a humanized monoclonal antibody derived from a murine clone (mu1217) generated by hybridoma fusion, targeting the extracellular domain (ECD) of human TIGIT. The variable regions of the heavy and light chains of mu1217 were sequenced, and the murine framework regions were replaced by closely homologous human germline IgG sequences. The final form of the humanized anti-TIGIT monoclonal antibody was selected as the clinical candidate, referred to as BGB-A1217.

### Example 2: Cloning and Sequence Analysis of TIGIT Antibodies

The variable regions of the heavy and light chains of BGB-A1217 were cloned into expression vectors. The vectors were sequenced to confirm the correct insertion of the variable regions. The sequences were aligned with known human germline sequences to ensure the humanization process was successful.

### Example 3: Affinity Determination of Purified Murine Anti-TIGIT Antibodies by SPR

The binding kinetics of BGB-A1217 to TIGIT were characterized by surface plasmon resonance (SPR) assay using a BIAcore™ T-200 instrument. Anti-human Fc antibody was coupled to a CM5 biosensor chip, and 10 nM of BGB-A1217 was flowed over the chip and captured by the immobilized anti-human Fc antibody. Serial dilutions of human TIGIT with a His tag were injected, and changes in SPR signal were detected and plotted against time. The association rates (K on) and dissociation rates (K off) were calculated using the one-to-one Langmuir binding model, and the equilibrium dissociation constant (KD) was determined as the ratio of K off/K on.

### Example 4: Humanization of the Murine Anti-Human TIGIT mAb mu1217

The variable regions of the heavy and light chains of mu1217 were humanized by replacing the murine framework regions with closely homologous human germline IgG sequences. The humanized variable regions were cloned into expression vectors and transfected into mammalian cell lines, such as CHO cells. The cells were cultured, and the antibodies were purified using affinity chromatography.

### Example 5: Binding Activities of Different Versions of 1217 to Native TIGIT

The binding activities of BGB-A1217 and BGB-A1217MF to native TIGIT were evaluated using a cell binding assay. TIGIT-over-expressing cells were stained with the antibodies and a secondary antibody conjugated to a fluorescent dye. The binding was detected by flow cytometry, and the EC50 values were determined.

### Example 6: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies Block the Interactions of TIGIT with its Ligands PVR and PVR-L2

The ability of BGB-A1217 and BGB-A1217MF to block the interactions of TIGIT with its ligands PVR and PVRL2 was evaluated using a cell-based ligand competition assay. Serial dilutions of the antibodies were mixed with TIGIT-mIgG2a and incubated with PVR or PVRL2-over-expressing cells. The binding was detected by flow cytometry, and the IC50 values were determined.

### Example 7: Activation of CMV-Specific Human T Cells by Anti-TIGIT Antibodies

The ability of BGB-A1217 to enhance the activation of CMV-specific human T cells was evaluated using a co-culture assay. Peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs) from HLA-A2.1+ healthy donors were stimulated with CMV pp65 peptide and co-cultured with pp65-pulsed HCT116 cells in the presence of BGB-A1217. IFN-γ production was measured by ELISA.

### Example 8: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies Enhanced NK Cell-Mediated Cytotoxicity

The ability of BGB-A1217 to enhance NK cell-mediated cytotoxicity was evaluated using an ADCC assay. NK92MI/CD16a-V158 cells were co-cultured with TIGIT-over-expressing BW5147.3/TIGIT cells in the presence of BGB-A1217. Cytotoxicity was determined by lactate dehydrogenase (LDH) release assay.

### Example 9: Anti-TIGIT Antibodies can Reduce the Surface Expression of TIGIT Receptor Via FcγR-Mediated Trogocytosis

The ability of BGB-A1217 to reduce the surface expression of TIGIT receptor via FcγR-mediated trogocytosis was evaluated using a trogocytosis assay. Jurkat/TIGIT/DNAM-1 cells were pre-incubated with CF633-labeled BGB-A1217 and co-cultured with HEK293 cells expressing different FcγRs. The reduction in TIGIT surface expression was detected by flow cytometry. The roles of FcγRs in trogocytosis were determined using human serum blockade or FcγR blocking antibodies.
