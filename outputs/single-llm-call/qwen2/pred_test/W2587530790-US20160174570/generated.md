# DESCRIPTION

## FIELD

The present invention relates to the field of agricultural biotechnology and, more specifically, to methods and compositions for enhancing seed germination and plant growth through the use of beneficial endophytic fungi. The invention provides novel compositions and methods for improving the vigor, uniformity, and stress tolerance of plants, particularly in the context of climate change and environmental stressors.

## BACKGROUND

Global food production faces significant challenges due to climate change, extreme weather conditions, and increasing demand. The Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) predicts a substantial increase in the need for cereals by 2030, and China's food import requirements are expected to rise by 30-40% in the coming years. Traditional agricultural practices, such as breeding and soil management, may not be sufficient to meet these demands. Therefore, innovative approaches are necessary to enhance crop resilience and productivity.

One promising avenue is the exploration of the symbiotic relationship between plants and their associated microbiome, particularly endophytic fungi. These fungi, which live asymptomatically within plant tissues, have been shown to confer various benefits to their hosts, including improved germination, enhanced nutrient uptake, and increased resistance to abiotic stressors such as drought and heat. Despite the potential of these microbial partners, their role in seed biology and plant development remains underexplored.

The present invention addresses this gap by providing novel compositions and methods that leverage the beneficial effects of endophytic fungi to improve seed germination and plant growth. This approach offers a sustainable solution to the challenges faced by modern agriculture, particularly in the context of climate change.

## SUMMARY

The present invention provides novel compositions and methods for enhancing seed germination and plant growth through the use of beneficial endophytic fungi. The compositions include seeds coated with or otherwise treated with endophytic fungi, formulations containing these fungi, and populations of seeds and plants that exhibit improved vigor, uniformity, and stress tolerance.

In one aspect, the invention provides a method for enhancing seed germination and plant growth, comprising treating seeds with a composition comprising one or more beneficial endophytic fungi. The method can be used to improve the germination rate, vigor, and stress tolerance of the resulting plants, particularly under adverse environmental conditions such as drought and heat.

In another aspect, the invention provides a seed composition comprising seeds coated with or otherwise treated with one or more beneficial endophytic fungi. The composition can be used to produce populations of plants with improved uniformity and stress tolerance.

In yet another aspect, the invention provides a formulation comprising one or more beneficial endophytic fungi, suitable for use in treating seeds or applying to plants. The formulation can be applied to seeds before planting or to growing plants to enhance their growth and stress tolerance.

The invention also provides populations of seeds and plants that exhibit improved uniformity and stress tolerance as a result of treatment with the compositions and methods described herein. These populations can be used in agricultural fields to improve crop yield and resilience.

Finally, the invention provides a commodity plant product derived from plants grown from seeds treated with the compositions and methods described herein. The commodity plant product can be used for various purposes, including food, feed, and industrial applications.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION

### Novel Compositions and Seeds

The present invention provides novel compositions and seeds that incorporate beneficial endophytic fungi to enhance seed germination and plant growth. The compositions can be in the form of seed coatings, seed treatments, or formulations that can be applied to seeds or growing plants.

#### Seed Coatings

The seed coatings of the present invention comprise one or more beneficial endophytic fungi. The fungi can be selected from a variety of species known to confer benefits to plants, such as improved germination, enhanced nutrient uptake, and increased resistance to abiotic stressors. Examples of suitable endophytic fungi include, but are not limited to, *Piriformospora indica*, *Trichoderma harzianum*, *Fusarium oxysporum*, and *Penicillium expansum*.

The seed coatings can be prepared by mixing the endophytic fungi with a carrier material, such as a polymer, clay, or other suitable matrix. The mixture can then be applied to the seeds using standard coating techniques, such as fluid bed coating, drum coating, or pan coating. The coated seeds can be stored and transported in a manner that maintains the viability of the endophytic fungi.

#### Seed Treatments

In addition to seed coatings, the compositions of the present invention can be used to treat seeds before planting. The treatment can involve soaking the seeds in a suspension of the endophytic fungi, or applying the fungi to the seeds using a spray or dusting method. The treated seeds can be planted immediately or stored for later use.

#### Formulations

The invention also provides formulations containing one or more beneficial endophytic fungi, suitable for use in treating seeds or applying to growing plants. The formulations can be in the form of suspensions, emulsions, powders, or granules, and can be applied to seeds or plants using standard agricultural equipment.

The formulations can include additional components to enhance their effectiveness, such as nutrients, plant growth regulators, or other beneficial microorganisms. The formulations can be designed to release the endophytic fungi over time, ensuring sustained benefits to the plants.

### Methods

The present invention provides methods for enhancing seed germination and plant growth through the use of beneficial endophytic fungi. The methods can be applied to a variety of crops, including cereals, pulses, oilseeds, and vegetables.

#### Method for Enhancing Seed Germination

The method for enhancing seed germination comprises treating seeds with a composition comprising one or more beneficial endophytic fungi. The treatment can be performed using seed coatings, seed treatments, or formulations as described above. The treated seeds can be planted in soil or other growing media, and the resulting plants will exhibit improved germination rate, vigor, and stress tolerance.

#### Method for Enhancing Plant Growth

The method for enhancing plant growth comprises applying a formulation containing one or more beneficial endophytic fungi to growing plants. The formulation can be applied to the soil, roots, or leaves of the plants using standard agricultural techniques. The application can be performed at various stages of plant growth, from seedling to maturity, to ensure sustained benefits.

### Formulations/Seed Coating Compositions

The formulations and seed coating compositions of the present invention can be tailored to meet the specific needs of different crops and growing conditions. The compositions can include a variety of components to enhance their effectiveness, such as:

- **Endophytic Fungi**: One or more beneficial endophytic fungi, selected based on their ability to confer specific benefits to the target crop.
- **Carrier Material**: A matrix or medium in which the endophytic fungi are suspended or embedded, such as a polymer, clay, or other suitable material.
- **Nutrients**: Essential nutrients that support the growth and activity of the endophytic fungi, such as nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium.
- **Plant Growth Regulators**: Compounds that promote plant growth and development, such as auxins, cytokinins, and gibberellins.
- **Other Beneficial Microorganisms**: Additional microorganisms that complement the activity of the endophytic fungi, such as mycorrhizal fungi or nitrogen-fixing bacteria.

The compositions can be formulated to release the endophytic fungi and other components over time, ensuring sustained benefits to the plants. The release can be controlled by the choice of carrier material and the inclusion of controlled-release agents.

### Populations of Seeds

The present invention provides populations of seeds that have been treated with compositions containing beneficial endophytic fungi. These populations exhibit improved uniformity and stress tolerance compared to untreated seeds. The populations can be used in agricultural fields to improve crop yield and resilience.

The populations can be produced by treating a large number of seeds with the compositions described herein, either as a batch process or on a continuous basis. The treated seeds can be planted in the field, and the resulting plants will exhibit consistent and improved performance.

### Increased Uniformity in Populations of Plants/Agricultural Fields

The use of the compositions and methods of the present invention can lead to increased uniformity in populations of plants and agricultural fields. Plants grown from treated seeds exhibit more consistent germination rates, growth patterns, and stress tolerance, resulting in a more uniform and productive crop.

The increased uniformity can be attributed to the consistent and reliable benefits provided by the endophytic fungi, which help to mitigate the effects of environmental stressors and promote optimal plant growth. This uniformity can lead to improved crop management and higher yields.

While the primary goal of the invention is to increase uniformity and consistency in plant populations, there may be instances where decreased uniformity is desirable. For example, in certain breeding programs, it may be useful to introduce variability to select for specific traits. The compositions and methods of the present invention can be modified to achieve this goal by using a diverse mix of endophytic fungi or by applying the compositions in a selective manner.

### Commodity Plant Product

The present invention also provides a commodity plant product derived from plants grown from seeds treated with the compositions and methods described herein. The commodity plant product can be used for various purposes, including food, feed, and industrial applications.

## EXAMPLES

### Example 1

**Preparation of Seed Coatings Containing Endophytic Fungi**

A seed coating composition was prepared by mixing *Piriformospora indica* spores with a polymer matrix. The mixture was applied to wheat seeds using a fluid bed coating technique. The coated seeds were then stored in a cool, dry place until ready for planting.

### Example 2

**Enhancement of Seed Germination Using Seed Coatings**

Wheat seeds coated with *Piriformospora indica* were planted in a controlled environment. The germination rate, vigor, and stress tolerance of the resulting plants were compared to those of uncoated seeds. The coated seeds exhibited a significantly higher germination rate and improved stress tolerance under drought conditions.

### Example 3

**Preparation of a Formulation for Treating Growing Plants**

A formulation containing *Trichoderma harzianum* spores was prepared by mixing the spores with a nutrient solution and a controlled-release agent. The formulation was applied to the soil around growing wheat plants using a standard irrigation system.

### Example 4

**Enhancement of Plant Growth Using a Formulation**

Wheat plants treated with the *Trichoderma harzianum* formulation exhibited improved growth and stress tolerance compared to untreated plants. The treated plants showed increased biomass, higher chlorophyll content, and better resistance to drought and heat stress.

### Example 5

**Production of a Population of Treated Seeds**

A large batch of wheat seeds was treated with a composition containing *Fusarium oxysporum* spores using a drum coating technique. The treated seeds were planted in a field, and the resulting plants were monitored for uniformity and stress tolerance. The treated seeds produced a more uniform and resilient crop compared to untreated seeds.

### Example 6

**Increased Uniformity in a Field of Wheat Plants**

A field of wheat plants grown from seeds treated with *Piriformospora indica* exhibited increased uniformity in terms of germination rate, growth pattern, and stress tolerance. The plants showed consistent performance across the field, leading to improved crop management and higher yields.

### Example 7

**Decreased Uniformity for Breeding Purposes**

A population of wheat plants was grown from seeds treated with a diverse mix of endophytic fungi, including *Piriformospora indica*, *Trichoderma harzianum*, and *Fusarium oxysporum*. The resulting plants exhibited decreased uniformity, which allowed for the selection of plants with specific traits, such as drought resistance or increased yield.

### Example 8

**Harvest of a Commodity Plant Product**

Wheat plants grown from seeds treated with *Piriformospora indica* were harvested and processed into a commodity plant product. The product was tested for quality and yield, and was found to meet or exceed industry standards.

### Example 9

**Application of the Invention in Different Crops**

The compositions and methods of the present invention were applied to a variety of crops, including corn, soybeans, and tomatoes. In each case, the treated seeds produced plants with improved germination, growth, and stress tolerance. The results demonstrated the broad applicability of the invention across different crop types.
