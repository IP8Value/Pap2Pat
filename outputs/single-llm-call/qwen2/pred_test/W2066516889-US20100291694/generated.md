# DESCRIPTION

## BACKGROUND OF THE INVENTION

The complement system is a crucial component of the innate immune response, playing a vital role in the defense against pathogens and the clearance of immune complexes and apoptotic cells. However, dysregulation of the complement system has been implicated in a wide range of diseases, including autoimmune disorders, ischemia-reperfusion injury, and age-related macular degeneration. The complement system is activated through three pathways: the classical pathway (CP), the lectin pathway (LP), and the alternative pathway (AP). Each pathway converges at the cleavage of C3, a central component of the complement system, into C3a and C3b. C3b, in turn, forms the C3 convertase, which is essential for the amplification of the complement cascade.

The development of selective inhibitors of the complement system has been a focus of extensive research. Traditional inhibitors, such as natural regulators and monoclonal antibodies targeting serine proteases, have shown promise but often suffer from limitations such as broad-spectrum inhibition and unfavorable pharmacokinetics. The present invention addresses these challenges by providing a novel antibody, S77, that selectively targets C3b, a key activator of the complement system, thereby inhibiting the alternative pathway without affecting the classical pathway. This selective inhibition offers a significant therapeutic advantage in managing complement-mediated diseases while preserving the host's ability to mount an appropriate immune response.

## SUMMARY OF THE INVENTION

The present invention relates to a novel antibody, S77, that selectively binds to C3b, a cleavage product of the complement protein C3. S77 exhibits high affinity and selectivity for C3b, iC3b, and C3c, but not for the unactivated form of C3. The antibody effectively inhibits the binding of complement factors H (fH) and CR1 to C3b, as well as the cofactor activity of these factors in factor I (fI)-mediated cleavage of C3b to iC3b. Additionally, S77 prevents the formation of the alternative pathway C3 convertase by inhibiting the binding of factor B to C3b, thereby blocking the generation of C3a and C3b. Furthermore, S77 inhibits the binding of C5 to C3b, preventing the formation of the C5 convertase and the subsequent cleavage of C5 into C5a and C5b. Importantly, S77 selectively inhibits the alternative pathway of complement activation without affecting the classical pathway, making it a promising therapeutic agent for treating complement-mediated diseases.

## DETAILED DESCRIPTION OF THE PREFERRED EMBODIMENT

### Definitions

For the purposes of this invention, the following terms are defined as follows:

- **C3**: A complement protein that is a central component of the complement system. It is cleaved into C3a and C3b during complement activation.
- **C3b**: A cleavage product of C3 that forms the C3 convertase and is involved in the amplification of the complement cascade.
- **iC3b**: A further cleavage product of C3b that is generated by factor I (fI) in the presence of a cofactor such as factor H (fH) or CR1.
- **C3c**: A fragment of C3b that remains after the cleavage of iC3b.
- **Factor H (fH)**: A plasma protein that acts as a cofactor for factor I (fI) in the cleavage of C3b to iC3b and regulates the alternative pathway of complement activation.
- **CR1**: A complement receptor that acts as a cofactor for factor I (fI) in the cleavage of C3b to iC3b and is involved in the regulation of the complement system.
- **Factor B (fB)**: A complement protein that, when bound to C3b, is cleaved by factor D to form the C3 convertase.
- **C3 Convertase**: A complex that cleaves C3 into C3a and C3b, amplifying the complement cascade.
- **C5 Convertase**: A complex that cleaves C5 into C5a and C5b, leading to the formation of the membrane attack complex.
- **Alternative Pathway (AP)**: One of the three pathways of complement activation, characterized by the spontaneous hydrolysis of C3 and the formation of C3 convertase.
- **Classical Pathway (CP)**: One of the three pathways of complement activation, initiated by the binding of C1q to immune complexes or pathogen surfaces.
- **S77**: A novel antibody that selectively binds to C3b, iC3b, and C3c, but not to the unactivated form of C3, and inhibits the alternative pathway of complement activation.

### MODES OF CARRYING OUT THE INVENTION

The present invention provides a novel antibody, S77, that selectively targets C3b, a key activator of the complement system. The antibody is generated using phage display technology, which allows for the identification of antibodies that distinguish between C3 and C3b. S77 is affinity-matured to enhance its binding affinity and selectivity for C3b. The crystal structure of S77 in complex with C3b reveals the structural basis for its selective recognition of C3b and not C3. S77 binds to an epitope formed by the MG6 and MG7 domains of C3b, which are reoriented during C3 activation, creating a binding site that is not accessible in the unactivated form of C3.

#### Generation of S77

S77 is generated using a human synthetic phage antibody library that displays bivalent Fab fragments on M13 phage. The library is subjected to subtractive phage panning to counter-select phage antibodies that bind to C3. Phage that bind specifically to C3b are identified, and the Fab sequences are grafted onto the human IgG1 framework of herceptin. The sequences are expressed in mammalian cells, and the resulting antibodies are purified using protein A columns. The selected antibody, YW144.2.45, is affinity-matured using combinatorial libraries to improve its binding affinity and selectivity for C3b. After several rounds of sorting, the antibody YW144.2.45.S77 (referred to as S77) is selected for further analysis due to its high affinity and selectivity for C3b.

#### Structural Basis of S77's Selectivity

The crystal structure of S77 in complex with C3b is solved at 3.1-Å resolution. The structure reveals that S77 binds to an epitope formed by the MG6 and MG7 domains of C3b. The reorientation of these domains during C3 activation creates a binding site that is not accessible in the unactivated form of C3. The interface between C3b and the Fab buries a total of approximately 1930 Å² of solvent-accessible surface. The bulk of these interactions is mediated by the heavy chain CDRs, with additional contributions from the light-chain CDRs. A notable hydrophobic interaction exists between Phe-898 of C3b and a hydrophobic pocket formed by residues from CDR-H3 and CDR-L2 of the Fab. This interaction, along with other hydrophilic interactions, contributes to the high affinity and selectivity of S77 for C3b.

#### Functional Effects of S77

S77 inhibits the binding of fH and CR1 to C3b, as well as the cofactor activity of these factors in fI-mediated cleavage of C3b to iC3b. S77 also prevents the formation of the alternative pathway C3 convertase by inhibiting the binding of factor B to C3b. This inhibition prevents the generation of C3a and C3b, thereby blocking the amplification of the complement cascade. Furthermore, S77 inhibits the binding of C5 to C3b, preventing the formation of the C5 convertase and the subsequent cleavage of C5 into C5a and C5b. Importantly, S77 selectively inhibits the alternative pathway of complement activation without affecting the classical pathway, making it a promising therapeutic agent for treating complement-mediated diseases.

### Example 1

**Preparation of C3b and C3 Fragments**

C3b and its fragments, including iC3b and C3c, are prepared as follows:

1. **C3b Preparation**: C3 is incubated with CVF, fB, and fD in a 10:10:1 molar ratio at 37 °C for 1 hour in the presence of 10 mM MgCl₂. The C3b fragments are isolated using a strong anion exchanger Mono Q 5/50 and a Superdex S-200 10/300 GL gel filtration column (Amersham Biosciences) to achieve a purity of greater than 95% as confirmed by Coomassie Blue-stained gel.
2. **iC3b and C3c Preparation**: C3b is incubated with fI and fH in a 1:10:10 molar ratio at 37 °C for 1 hour. The reaction is diluted with a 0.05× volume of 20 mM Tris, pH 8.0, containing 5 mM EDTA and loaded onto a Mono Q column (Amersham Biosciences) equilibrated in the same buffer. Protein is eluted with a 0.0 to 0.5 M NaCl gradient. The identity of C3c and iC3b fragments is confirmed by Edman degradation.

### CRIg Variants

CRIg (Complement Receptor of the Ig superfamily) is a known inhibitor of the complement system that binds to C3b, iC3b, and C3c. While CRIg and S77 share binding affinity for these C3 fragments, they recognize different sites on C3b. CRIg binds primarily to residues of the C3b β-chain, whereas S77 binds predominantly to residues from the α-chain. The binding sites for CRIg and S77 are located on the same face of C3b, opposite the CUB and TED domains, which mediate opsonization. This suggests that the molecular surface of the face of C3b, opposite the TED domain, contains the binding surface for substrates C3 and C5.

### Example 3

**Binding Specificity and Affinity of S77**

The binding specificity and affinity of S77 are determined using surface plasmon resonance (SPR) and enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA). S77 is shown to bind to C3b, iC3b, and C3c with high affinity, ranging from 1.2 to 2.2 nM. S77 does not bind to C3, C3dg, or other complement proteins, confirming its selectivity for C3b and its cleavage products. The high affinity and selectivity of S77 are attributed to the specific interactions between the Fab and the MG6 and MG7 domains of C3b, as revealed by the crystal structure.

### Example 4

**Inhibition of Complement Activation by S77**

The ability of S77 to inhibit complement activation is assessed using various assays:

1. **fH and CR1 Binding Assays**: S77 is shown to inhibit the binding of fH and CR1 to C3b, as well as the cofactor activity of these factors in fI-mediated cleavage of C3b to iC3b. This inhibition is confirmed using ELISA and cofactor activity assays.
2. **C3 Convertase Assay**: S77 prevents the formation of the alternative pathway C3 convertase by inhibiting the binding of factor B to C3b. This inhibition is demonstrated using a C3 convertase assembly assay on zymosan particles.
3. **C5 Convertase Assay**: S77 inhibits the binding of C5 to C3b, preventing the formation of the C5 convertase and the subsequent cleavage of C5 into C5a and C5b. This inhibition is confirmed using a C5 convertase assembly assay on zymosan particles.
4. **Hemolytic Assay**: S77 selectively inhibits the alternative pathway of complement activation in human serum, as demonstrated by a hemolytic assay using C1q-depleted serum. S77 does not inhibit the classical pathway of complement activation in fB-depleted human serum.

### Example 5

**Species Cross-Reactivity of S77**

The species cross-reactivity of S77 is evaluated by testing its binding and inhibitory activity in serum from various species, including baboon, cynomolgus, and rhesus monkey. S77 is found to be specific for human C3b and does not inhibit complement activation in serum from these non-human primates. The lack of cross-reactivity is attributed to the presence of a more hydrophobic Phe residue at position 897 in rhesus C3b, which replaces the hydrophilic His residue in human C3b. This substitution disrupts the hydrogen bonding network between S77 and C3b, preventing the antibody from recognizing rhesus C3b and inhibiting complement activation.
